## 2. What was read on Qwen

- **Re-gating share on p-9707d6ff, saved updates (P3.1, E132).** All six states 0.991-0.9992 (old decomposition 0.993-0.9995); range 0.0083; random changes also near the ceiling. Figure `experiments/qwen_regating_new/figures/share_new_old.png`; summary `experiments/qwen_regating_new/results/summary.json` (qwen-lead clone path in cell). Log-odds version (P3.2, E144, post hoc): every update *lowers* the log-odds of its own trained answer (mean -1.1 to -8.0 per token), random changes too (-5.0 to -7.1), so no share can be scored. Figure `experiments/qwen_regating_new/figures/amend1_gains.png`.
- **Instrument check (P3.3, E152;** `reports/analyses/2026-11-11_analysis_e152-qwen-instrument-check.md`, figure `experiments/qwen_instrument_check/figures/step1.png`). Every saved update leaves >= 0.97 of what a random change of the same norms leaves outside the subcomponents. Least-squares split recovers 0.89 of a change planted on often-on subcomponents (chance 0.06) but 0.29 on rarely-on ones; the re-gating share separates planted kinds at every size, so it does respond to a built change. Filed outcome: no call.
- **Share on the pair library, p-9707d6ff (P3.8, E170/E174;** `reports/analyses/2026-11-13_analysis_p3-8-library-share.md`, figure `experiments/qwen_library_share/figures/share_gaps.png`). Every side 0.990-0.9998; random changes scaled to the same output change 0.992-1.009; largest gap 0.0032; all six cells inside the random band. Saturated.
- **Frozen-neuron decomposition (P3.9, E175/E179;** `reports/analyses/2026-11-13_analysis_p3-9-frozen-neuron-share.md`, figure `experiments/qwen_library_share/figures/share_gaps_two_decomps.png`). Off the ceiling: sides 0.921-0.995, random 0.81-1.06. Three of six cells leave the band (known minus invented late 0.0493 vs band 0.0231), but the gold twin (built as a near-null) is one of them with a sign flip between saves, so the band is judged too narrow. MLP-only on frozen vs whole update on p-9707d6ff: the two rows differ in sites as well as decomposition.
- **Share reference (P3.10 E181, repaired P3.11 E186;** `reports/analyses/2026-11-13_analysis_e181-qwen-share-reference.md`, `reports/analyses/2026-11-17_analysis_e186-share-reference-repair.md`, figures `experiments/qwen_share_reference/figures/share_reference.png`, `experiments/qwen_share_reference_repair/figures/share_reference.png`). E186 (finer sizes, six random draws): 14 comparable, 5 above, 7 inside, 2 below: mixed. The most-used-neuron sets (plainest switching-off) read inside or below the band on 4 of 5 row sets. Bands widened to 0.046-0.154 because the three new random draws read lower than the old three on every row set (draws share one direction across row sets; candidate reason only, `reports/digests/2026-11-17_digest_qwen-lead.md`). Fine-tunes 26 above, 10 inside. Premise check doubtful (`~/commute-data/premise/P3.11.md`).
- **Cheap readings (P3.6 E162/E166, P3.7 E169, compare-lead;** `reports/analyses/2026-11-12_analysis_p3-6-qwen-cheap-readings.md`, `reports/analyses/2026-11-12_analysis_p3-7-library-cheap-readings.md`). Only effective rank separates an arm contrast beyond seed spread (RL 1.5 seed ranges lower than its null; known pool lower than never-solved pool). Top-neuron overlap separates neither, though all 19 updates keep fewer top neurons than random (RL 0.89 vs 0.997). - **Size-matched (P2.28, E187;** `reports/analyses/2026-11-16_analysis_p2-28-size-matched.md`, figure `experiments/size_matched/figures/size_matched.png`). Shrinking the larger side to equal update size: on Qwen the share's separation goes from 3 of 6 cells to 1 (the 3/6 and the 0.049 facts gap are P3.9's frozen-neuron values), the surviving gold-late cell shrunk by only 3%; top-neuron overlap reverses on copy vs cipher.

**Does it repeat the 67M?** No. On the 67M the share is higher on the reuse side of all nine built pairs and survives size matching (claims table, `docs/research_log/parts/sections/claims.html`, E133, E187). On Qwen p-9707d6ff the share is saturated for real and random changes alike; on the frozen-neuron control it moves but neither separates pairs robustly nor reliably reads a built switch-off.

## 3. RL vs SFT

- Behaviour: RL raises held-out GSM8K from 0.782 to 0.882/0.884 at step 75 (E167). Pass rate did not grow with size (0.848-0.906 across all reads, steps 50-400).
- The shuffled-reward null collapsed at both rates tried (3e-6 within ~30 steps, pass 0.039/0.000; 1e-6 seed 0 at step 90, seed 70 stopped at step 20 by a filed check). Cause not checked. So **no RL-vs-working-null comparison at a readable size** (P3.4 closed "no call"). The earlier working null (E69, lower rate, step 100, below readable size: RL 0.690x) read 0.870/0.794 vs RL 0.881/0.877; claims table: "qualified, size depends on how answer format is graded".
- Own-samples SFT: pass 0.818/0.814 at step 75, output change only 0.046-0.053x masked error (0.216 at step 250) despite update norm 0.626-1.051 comparable to RL's (E167). A reference at RL's step, not a size-matched arm.
- Placement: every arm sits outside the subcomponents as a random change does (outside-share ratio 0.979-0.993 on p-9707d6ff, E167/E178); every update smaller than the decomposition's leftover on all 196 matrices.
- On the *old* decomposition E104 found RL reads like its null on three readings; own-samples differed on two, explained by which matrices and by size (`reports/analyses/2026-11-06_analysis_e104-d3-gsm8k.md`; decisions.md line 163). Not re-run on new (DECOMPOSITIONS.md).
- Effective rank: RL lower than its null by 1.5 seed ranges (P3.6) — the only Qwen RL contrast that separated.

## 4. The earlier carrying ("commuting") line, 67M

Question (GOALS.md): can a fine-tune's δW be carried onto the decomposition (subcomponents, Γ, Δ) so it stays faithful, without dumping δW into Δ (do-nothing)?
- **Rescaling split is dead.** A finished update is 0.05-0.06% in the span of the rank-1 subcomponents vs 0.035% for random (E1, `experiments/TRIED.md` row E1); as a state it keeps P(target) 0.012-0.016 vs W₀ 0.012 (E16).
- **Outside share.** Fine-tune's outside-both block / null 0.18 -> 0.41 rising with training; LoRA at null (0.54-1.07); LoRA does not line up better than full fine-tune (E14, `reports/analyses/2026-10-20_analysis_lora-split.md`, `experiments/lora_split/figures/outside.png`). On Qwen every update is placed like random (above).
- **No one-shot split keeps behaviour, faithfulness and Δ together** (E16, `experiments/split_comparison/figures/`; `reports/analyses/2026-10-21_analysis_split-comparison.md`). Least-squares split is complete through Δ but fails the adversary on the fine-tune (PGD ratio 6.96 at step 10); scaled adjoint split passes (0.92-1.06) but keeps less behaviour as the update grows (P 0.93/0.60/0.10 at steps 10/100/1000). Component training is the only operation with both (PGD 0.97-1.12, P >= 0.994, Δ <= 2e-8).
- **Teacher training works on narrow tasks** (E34, `reports/analyses/2026-10-23_analysis_teacher-training.md`, figures `experiments/teacher_training/figures/behaviour.png`, `faithful.png`, `gap_damage.png`). Train alive (U,V) on KL(fine-tuned ‖ student), Γ and Δ frozen: all six teachers pass at steps 100 and 1000, P 0.92-1.00, gap <= 3.6% of W₀'s; also from Pile text only (0.90-0.98), even with every trigger row removed (0.94/0.89). Holds under a second batch order and an 8x longer adversary (Amendment 4). Extends to a key-to-value cipher (E42). Caveat: a pass means "no harm seen"; a teach-nothing student reads above a real student under the adversary, within noise (Amendment 8, `reports/analyses/2026-11-02_analysis_e34-amend8-base-control.md`).
- **Fails on a broad fine-tune** (E37, lowercase style): behaviour kept 0.998 but harness PGD 2.20 (fail), still about half of do-nothing's 4.87 (`reports/analyses/2026-10-26_analysis_teacher-spread.md`). E127: placement on the subcomponents decides faithfulness; teacher training hides most of the broad change's fragility (`reports/analyses/2026-11-06_analysis_e127-ls-start-faithfulness.md`).
- Status: the user takes teacher training as the answer; re-runs on new decomposition and Qwen teacher training stopped (plan 2026-11-09). Untested on Qwen.
