# P2.71: every paper in the lit review, read properly

Every paper the project's literature notes name, plus every entry in the draft's bibliography, was re-read from the
paper itself (abstract, method and results at least) by parallel reader agents, and given one tagged entry with a
"relevance to our paper" paragraph. The ranked digest lists what the paper must act on: missed baselines, realistic
known-answer cases, definitions, findings for and against ours, and citation fixes.

- Entries: `lit_review/digest/papers/` (one per paper), index `lit_review/digest/master_list.tsv`, by theme
  `lit_review/digest/index.md`.
- Ranked digest: `lit_review/digest/lit-digest-2026-11-24.md` (also in whip-lead's folder), top ten in
  `lit-digest-2026-11-24-top10.md`.

Most important for the paper:
1. The VPD paper itself proposes diffing a fine-tune by its subcomponents; the draft's "first" sentence should cite it
   as the proposal we test.
2. An independent audit of the released 67M decomposition finds its labels faithful only jointly, a possible confound
   for the re-gating share.
3. Lee et al. (DPO on toxicity, GPT-2) is a realistic reuse case made by an additive offset, which our gain-only kept
   clause may misread: the best outside test of our readings.
4. Missed baselines that run on our 67M model: the delta crosscoder (built for narrow fine-tunes) and WeightWatch (the
   singular vectors of the weight difference).
5. Realistic known-answer candidates: Chhabra et al. (IOI fine-tunes, circuit-level labels), Drake and Eberstadt
   (same data, LoRA reuses and full fine-tuning builds), Soligo et al. (open-weights emergent misalignment).

Not read: two papers (abstract only) and two books.
