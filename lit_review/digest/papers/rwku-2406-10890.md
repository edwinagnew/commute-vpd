# RWKU: Benchmarking Real-World Knowledge Unlearning (Jin 2024)

**What it does.** A benchmark for unlearning knowledge of 200 famous people from LLaMA3-8B-Instruct and Phi-3 without
access to a forget or retain corpus. Forgetting is probed by fill-in-the-blank, QA, nine adversarial attack types and
four membership-inference attacks; retention by neighbour knowledge, general and reasoning ability, truthfulness,
factuality and fluency. Baselines: in-context unlearning, RepE, GA, DPO, NPO, rejection tuning, full and LoRA.

**Key result.** Unlearned models still leak the target under fill-in-the-blank and adversarial probes even when QA
looks forgotten; almost all methods fail under MIA; LoRA "unlearns less ... and forgets less" than full fine-tuning;
fine-tuning only early layers unlearns better without harming neighbours.

**Quote.** "although the unlearned model (especially after RT) may forget how to utilize the knowledge, it can still be
detected through knowledge memorization probes" (Section 5)

**Relevance to our paper.** Purely behavioural benchmark; it bears on our paper only as one more instance of a
fine-tune suppressing rather than removing knowledge, which the draft already covers with Deeb and Roger. Not needed.
