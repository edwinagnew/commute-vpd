# Why research personas despite RL scaling? (Nardo 2026)

**What it does.** A short opinion post. Starting from the observation (citing Sam Marks's "Thoughts on the persona
selection model", 2026-09-24) that large-scale RL seems to shape agents' motivations and "wash out" the persona set
by pretraining and early post-training, it lists the reasons various groups (Anthropic, Arcadia, Truthful AI, CLR,
Resolution, Geodesic, Forethought) might still study personas.

**Key result.** No results. The list of motivations, with comments disputing whether RL must break down the persona
prior (1a3orn: which RL does so is an empirical question) and adding "to prove that personas break down under RL".

**Quote.** "RL seeming to shape much of the motivations and behaviour of the agents, \"washing out\" their initial personas" (post opening)

**Relevance to our paper.** Context only: it voices the view that heavy RL does more than select a pretrained
persona, the opposite of the Elicitation Hypothesis in its persona form. It gives no evidence. If the draft's planned
persona-selection paragraph cites Marks's persona-selection post, this is at most a pointer to the counter-view; the
Marks post itself is the citation to find.
