# Gaia2: Benchmarking LLM Agents on Dynamic and Asynchronous Environments (Froger 2026)

**What it does.** A benchmark of 1,120 scenarios in a simulated consumer mobile environment, built on the open-source
Agents Research Environments (ARE) platform. The environment changes independently of the agent, so scenarios test
time constraints, noisy events, ambiguity and multi-agent collaboration. Each scenario has a write-action verifier that
can also serve as an RL reward.

**Key result.** No model leads on every capability. GPT-5 (high) scores 42% pass@1 overall but fails time-sensitive
tasks, and Kimi-K2 leads the open models at 21%.

**Quote.** "no model dominates across capabilities: GPT-5 (high) reaches the strongest overall score of 42% pass@1 but fails on time-sensitive tasks" (Abstract)

**Relevance to our paper.** None. It is a benchmark, cited by Oh et al. for long-horizon agent coherence.
