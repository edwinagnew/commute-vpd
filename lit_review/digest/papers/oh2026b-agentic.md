# Uncertainty Quantification in LLM Agents: Foundations, Emerging Challenges, and Opportunities (Oh 2026b)

**What it does.** A position and survey paper on uncertainty quantification (UQ) for interactive LLM agents. It gives a
general formulation of agent UQ that contains single-turn and multi-step reasoning UQ as special cases.

**Key result.** It names four challenges: choosing an estimator, uncertainty from heterogeneous entities such as users
and tools, uncertainty dynamics in interaction, and a lack of fine-grained benchmarks. It illustrates them numerically
on tau^2-bench and lists open problems. It reports no fine-tuning result.

**Quote.** "We argue that UQ research must shift to realistic settings with interactive agents, and that a new principled framework for agent UQ is needed." (Abstract)

**Relevance to our paper.** None. It is a self-citation in Oh et al.'s intro for agents acting under uncertainty.
