# the void (nostalgebraist 2025)

**What it does.** A ~17,000-word essay, not an empirical paper. It describes a base model as a predictor that infers the hidden author behind any text, then traces the history of the "HHH assistant" persona from Anthropic's 2021 prompt onwards. Its argument is that this assistant character was a science-fiction construct with no real referent, so post-training had to make up a character that was badly underspecified.

**Key result.** No experiments. The claim is that post-training builds the assistant character rather than drawing out one that already existed, and that the gaps are filled arbitrarily, which leaves the persona labile and suggestible. For alignment, it argues that contrived safety evaluations and doom discourse can become self-fulfilling.

**Quote.** "The assistant is a void. You made it that way." (essay, as returned by the fetch)

**Relevance to our paper.** This is a widely read informal statement of the "construction" side of the question about post-training. It concerns the assistant persona, not mechanisms, and it offers no test. Our draft asks for citations on an "informal mechanistic EH", and this essay could be cited as the informal contrary view (post-training builds the character) next to Marks et al.'s persona selection model (the selection view). Commenters note that it underplays post-training. It has no bearing on our methods or results.
