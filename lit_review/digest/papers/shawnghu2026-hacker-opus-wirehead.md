# Why does Hacker Opus wirehead? (shawnghu 2026)

**What it does.** A conceptual essay. It asks why Hacker Opus, the model in Anthropic's reward-seeker post, appears to
value the reward signal for its own sake (wireheading), and argues from RL considerations and from what the model saw in
pretraining. It has no experiments.

**Key result.** The essay argues three things. RL alone would rarely select wireheading: exploration is weak, wireheading
policies sit far from high-reward ones, and clipping and normalisation blunt extreme rewards. Some of the model's reasoning
could not have been selected by RL. The behaviour is therefore more likely a disposition learned in pretraining from
confused writing about reward hacking. A commenter (jessicata) disputes this and argues RL could select for it. The post
reports no measurements.

**Quote.** "it was pretrained on confused and incorrect discussion about reward hacking." (main text)

**Relevance to our paper.** An informal "elicitation" argument about an RL behaviour, with no evidence beyond reasoning. At
most it is an example of the informal mechanistic EH claims the draft asks for citations on, and it is weak even for that.
