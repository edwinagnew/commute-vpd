# Literature index (Update Decomposition, cell P2.71, 2026-11-23)

How to use. One file per paper in `papers/` (template `_template.md`); `master_list.tsv` is the same set as a table, sorted by importance.
Grep by tag or bucket: `command grep -l 'primary_tag: BASELINE' papers/*`, `command grep -l '1-missing-method' papers/*`; the ranked digest is `~/commute-data/agents/whip-lead/lit-digest-2026-11-24.md`.
Tags: EH elicitation / re-weighting hypothesis · BASELINE a method we compare against or should · KNOWN-ANSWER realistic fine-tune with outside evidence of reuse or new · DEFINITION a definition of reuse, mechanism or elicitation · DIFFING base-vs-fine-tune comparison · FT-INTERP mechanistic reading of what a fine-tune changed · DECOMP parameter decomposition · CAUSAL causal abstraction, interchange, circuits · DYNAMICS training dynamics · SUPPORTS / CONTRADICTS a finding for or against ours · ALIGNMENT safety application · CITE-FIX the draft cites it wrongly or should cite it · BACKGROUND context only.
Buckets (`digest_buckets`): 1-missing-method, 2-known-answer-case, 3-definition, 4-supports, 4-contradicts, 5-cite-fix. Importance 0-10 is the reader's score (8+ = the paper is incomplete without it); a paper is listed under every theme its tags place it in.
Linkposts and merged readings: a `linkpost_of`, `superseded_by` or `aliases` line in an entry's frontmatter names the paper to cite.
