[5 cite-fix] **Bushnaq 2026 (2610.20748)** — "This could allow us to express each gradient update to a model as a combination of upweighting, downweighting, or modifying existing subcomponents, as well as creating new ones" — VPD's own Sec. 7.5 proposes our programme as future work. main.tex:220 says "To our knowledge, a decomposition has not been used to diff a fine-tune before". [4b contradicts] **Angsten 2026 (LessWrong audit of the released VPD)** — "The common theme throughout is that causal importance labels hold only as a joint configuration." — re-gating puts M's Γ masks on F's activations, which is a mixed configuration. This could confound the re-gating share and its saturation near 0.99 on Qwen. Check it before the re-gating share carries a headline.
3. [2 known-answer] **Lee 2024 (2401.01967)** — "DPO does not remove the capability of generating toxic outputs, but learns an “offset”, distributed amongst its layers, to “bypass” the regions that elicit toxicity." — GPT-2 medium + DPO on toxicity. The evidence is weight-level (toxic vectors kept, cosine > 0.99), plus a 7-neuron un-aligning that restores toxicity. That is close to mechanism-level. It is clear reuse made by additive offsets, not gains, so our gain-only kept clause may read it as new. It is a small open model, the best realistic test of ρ against outside evidence, and already cited.
4. [1 missing method] **Kassem 2026 (2603.04426)** — "Crosscoders ... consistently fail to recover causally relevant features in this regime. This failure is structural" — the Delta-Crosscoder is built for the narrow-fine-tune regime where our BatchTopK crosscoder fails its planted control. It runs on our 67M. Without it, "existing tools fail" rests on the weakest member of the crosscoder family.
5. [1 missing method] **Zhong 2026 (2508.00161)** — "the top singular vectors of the weight difference between a fine-tuned model and its base model correspond to newly acquired behaviors." — data-free SVD of ΔW. It runs as is on the 67M and Qwen, beside effective rank. It is the obvious weight-level baseline a reviewer will ask for, and the draft neither cites nor runs it (intruder dimensions, Shuttleworth 2024, 2410.21228, are its free companion).
6. [2 known-answer] **Chhabra 2025 (2503.01896)** — "task-specific fine-tuning enhances the underlying mechanisms of circuits without introducing novel mechanisms, even in longer training scenarios." — GPT-2 small on IOI with three fine-tunes: clean (amplify = reuse), corrupted (sign flip = changed) and retrain. Labels come from path patching, ablation, DLA and CMAP, which is circuit-level evidence. It is cheap to rebuild at heads or SAEs. Note that its "amplification" counts new heads copying an old role as reuse, and our per-variable clause would not: cite it and contrast.
7. [2 known-answer] **Drake 2026 (2607.04510)** — "full SFT ... builds a dedicated, localised solution for the narrow training task, while LoRA ... recruits the EM persona" — Qwen2.5-32B on insecure code: one dataset, two methods, opposite reuse labels. The evidence is weight enrichment, transplant and ablation, which is representational, not mechanism-level. It is the realistic version of Claim 1's "same behaviour, different mechanism", but at 32B it is out of our reach.
8. [4b contradicts] **Prakash 2024 (2402.14811)** — "Our findings suggest that fine-tuning enhances, rather than fundamentally alters, the mechanistic operation of the model." — the anchor reuse case (Llama-7B to Goat/FLoat, same circuit, faithfulness 0.88-0.97), already cited. The evidence is value-level: the circuit and functions are the same and the values better, from CMAP. Our "trained fine-tunes turn writes rather than scale them" predicts its Value Fetcher heads would read as turned. The paper should say whether that contradicts Prakash or only the by-value reading.
9. [5 cite-fix] **Wen 2026 (2506.14245)** — "a consistent and significant performance gap between the models across all values of K (up to 1024)." — once chain-of-thought correctness is checked, RLVR does extend the base model's boundary. That cuts against Yue 2025, which the draft relies on at main.tex:82 ("post-training cannot teach a model anything new") and main.tex:146. Cite both sides (also Liu 2025 ProRL, 2505.24864; Yuan 2026, 2509.25123).
10. [2 known-answer] **Soligo 2025 (2506.11618)** — "different emergently misaligned models converge to similar representations of misalignment" — Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct with rank-1 LoRA EM fine-tunes. An existing direction steers EM in the chat model, and ablating it removes EM across fine-tunes: activation and steering evidence of reuse. It is the open-weights version of Wang 2025 (2506.19823, cited at main.tex:205, closed GPT-4o), and the realistic reuse case nearest our Qwen line.
