On 2026-11-18 the user adopted the held-model wording of causal-rule retention as the working definition of reuse. Source: reports/analyses/2026-11-18_analysis_held-model-retention-run1.md (P1.26: E197, E201).

The wording is a cross-model interchange intervention: a change from M to F is reuse to the degree that it survives when every unit of F is interchanged for M's matching unit, held to its rule. The map class is a declared parameter: one gain per unit is the strict end, and the per-site linear map is the main reading. Fitting that map is a stitching-style step, not an interchange. A linear re-mix counting as reuse is the user's stipulation, not a finding.

**Why:** at P1.26 the per-site map read the built-new graft at 0.13 and every trained item at 0.56–0.999. Matched pairs differ by at most 0.011, so they support no claim.

**How to apply:** no new wording, cut-off or label without the user. The follow-ups are grafts at other sites and offsets, and the main reading on E187's size-matched pairs, each with its rules filed first.
