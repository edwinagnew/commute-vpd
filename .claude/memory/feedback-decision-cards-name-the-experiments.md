A decision card's "measured so far" must name each experiment in plain words: what was run, what we expected, what came out (at most two numbers), and a link to its report and figure. Never "none of it did" or a run of codes (E221, rival b, band, S3). Clearer is not longer: ~200 words, high level.

**Why:** the ask-56 card (twins + R saturation, 2026-11-20) was "one of the worst decision cards ive seen ... where are the results youre talking about? which experiment? what went wrong?"

**How to apply:** put this structure into every clerk brief for a card; check the card cold before filing. Extends [[feedback-decision-cards-cold-reader]].
