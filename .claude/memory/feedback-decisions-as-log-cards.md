When proposing work that needs the user's approval (new run cells included), write a decision card in the research log's pending decisions, in the format of `docs/research_log/README.md` → Pending decisions. Do not hand over proposed rows for `cells.tsv` to paste.

**Why:** user, 2026-11-09: "i dont like this way of presenting information and getting my approval. the cells are hard to read and do not justify their decisions. stick to the traditional method of adding decision card(s) to the research log."

**How to apply:** each card says the question in plain words, the evidence, what could go wrong, lettered options with cost and limits, what happens next, one recommendation. Why each choice was made goes on the card. See [[feedback-decision-cards-cold-reader]], [[feedback-pending-decisions-in-log]].
