The project's consolidated research log is the artifact "Update Decomposition" at
https://claude.ai/artifact/SvgkzUiESRkyE9GM65HCWu (created 2026-10-20). Source: `docs/research_log/parts/` (one file per section and per experiment card), built by `docs/research_log/build.py` into four pages since 2026-10-22 (main; experiments, glossary and changelog as separate pages, at the user's request to stop reading the whole log per update); layout, publish recipe (URL, figure files map) and update checklist in
`docs/research_log/README.md`. Sections: two-minute
intro and headline results, sub-questions Q1–Q9 with experiment codes E1–E12, apparatus, techniques (with a diagram of
the three maps), every experiment, every claim with status, timeline, gaps, backlog, mistakes, glossary, repo map,
changelog. Update it by republishing to that URL (read it first from a new session). The user modelled it on a
colleague's artifact: sticky left table of contents, readable prose, a claims-with-status table. An earlier Claude Doc
draft (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/93369049-1c64-46ee-b862-edef9d9c8354) was superseded and may be deleted.
