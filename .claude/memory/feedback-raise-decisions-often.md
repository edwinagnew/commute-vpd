Raise open scientific choices (scope of a hypothesis, how to read a failure) to the user more often, written like the
E28 pending-decision card: what it is about, what the evidence says, the options and what each changes.

**Why:** user, 2026-10-22: "im glad you raised decision to me ... it helps me understand a lot better about where we
are at currently ... raise these sorts of things more often, even if i just pass it straight back to you."

**How to apply:** when a result bears on a claim's scope or wording, put a card in the log's section 3 and tell the user
in chat, even inside approved classes; keep working on whatever doesn't wait on it. See [[feedback-pending-decisions-in-log]],
[[feedback-plain-approval-requests]].
