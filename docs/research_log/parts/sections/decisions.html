The search missed the clearest reuse case. What does the definition do now?

This is a question about whether the working definition's premise holds, so it is put without a recommendation.

What was done. The working wording of reuse (adopted tentatively on 2026-11-10, cut-offs 0.5 for the rewiring share and 0.8 for the re-weighting share) was to be tested on a built control. That needs a positive control beside it: a fine-tune the wording should call reuse. Three runs tried it today, all on the free cluster queue.

  • Sum parity, first run. Stopped at its first check: the plain 67M already reads "the sum of the last two numbers is odd" at 0.61 by a linear read, against a bar of 0.55 (shuffled labels 0.50).
  • Strongly taught original. The earlier exclusive-or fine-tune, which holds its variable at 0.9997, was pushed again. The outputs moved by only 0.12, under the 0.15 floor: they were already saturated. Stopped before any fine-tune.
  • Sum parity rerun, bar raised to 0.9 at your request. The positive control ran first: a fine-tune of the plain 67M that acts when the last number is odd. The plain 67M reads that bit exactly (1.0 at every site), and the fine-tune learned its target (its divergence from the target is 0.04 of a half-pushed model's). Yet the best rewiring share was 0.466 on seed 0 and 0.459 on seed 1, under 0.5. By the filed rule the built control is uninformative on this layout, and the plain and taught arms did not run.

What was found. The rewiring share is the part of the fine-tune's change in next-token output that is reproduced when an affine map of the original's activations is written into the original at one site: 0 is none, 1 is all. A low share on a case that is plainly reuse is not new: the earlier always-on fine-tunes read 0.45 to 0.58, while the table copies read 0.72. On this layout a low share cannot tell a built skill from a reuse the search missed, so your condition for revising the wording now bears on reuse as well as on built.

  • Option a: a free diagnosis on saved states of why the share stops near 0.47 on an exactly readable variable. Runs: reads of the saved control fine-tunes and the plain 67M only, no training, $0. It would split the miss into its parts, how much of the change the site carries and how much the map's leftover error costs, and try a richer map or another site. Already seen: at the input of block 3 the reversion is 0.72 and the map's mismatch 0.04, yet the share is 0.47; at the final residual the reversion is 0.93 and the mismatch 0.06, yet the share is 0.29. It tells us whether the miss is the search's limit or a property of what the fine-tune learned. Next: if a richer map or another site reads well above 0.5, the plain arm can be run against that control; if not, the choice comes back to b or c. Needs a new cell with 0 or 1 runs, which only you open.
  • Option b: you revise the wording, or its 0.5 cut-off. Runs: nothing until a revision is written; the change is yours alone. It tells us nothing new by itself, but sets what the later runs test. Next: the control and the built arms are read again under the revised wording, on saved states where possible.
  • Option c: close this step and write the gate page, due 2026-11-13. Runs: nothing. The page would say that the wording as it stands certifies neither reuse nor built on these layouts: the positive control read under the cut-off, and the earlier built attempts had no clean control. It tells the paper where the definition stands today. Next: the gate decides the definition part with that finding on the page.
