Two cheap readings together now call every built pair the way the share does. What should the comparison do before it says what the share adds?

What was done. On the nine built pairs of fine-tunes, each pair has a side built to reuse something the model already does and a side built to be new. We asked which side each cheap reading puts higher, and whether two readings together match the re-gating share's call. The first is the original model's prior: its log10 probability of the trained answer. The second is steering at strength 1: the fine-tune's mean change of activation added to the original model at its own size.

What was found. Together they call the share's direction on all nine pairs; each alone misses at least one (the comparison card). Where the prior is matched, steering carries the pair: the prior gaps are 0.12 and 0.20 decades, under the 0.5 bar, and steering separates the same pairs by 0.31 and 0.44 of the gain. Where steering gives no call, the prior carries it: on three pairs the prior gap is 3.2 to 3.7 decades. With steering's searched strength instead, three pairs have the two readings pointing opposite ways; that comes from how the strength is chosen, not from the new side changing the model more. This reads direction only, says nothing about how far apart the sides are, and nine pairs is a small set.

  • Option a: compare gap sizes from saved results. Costs nothing and needs no GPU. It asks whether the share's gaps across the nine pairs follow the prior's gaps, follow steering's, or carry something neither does. With nine pairs it is descriptive only. Next: if the gaps add nothing, option d; if they do, that is a lead for option b.
  • Option b: build new pairs where the prior and steering disagree, or where the prior is matched and steering is held near zero, and read the share there. The only option that could show the share adds information on direction. It needs new fine-tunes on the GPU, a new cell and a run budget from you; not yet costed. Next: a filed rule first, then the run.
  • Option c: effective rank of the update on the 24 refit 96-key pairs. Runs from saved states and fills a column still empty. Next: one more cheap reading in the table. Rank already agrees on at most three of nine pairs, so it is unlikely to change the picture.
  • Option d: stop and write the paragraph as it stands. On direction, the share agrees with prior plus steering on the built pairs. Its claim to add something rests on what it measures, which subcomponents are switched on, and not on a different call.

Recommendation: option a now, since it is free and quick, then option d if the gap sizes show nothing beyond the prior and steering. Option b only if you want the paper to claim the share adds information beyond the cheap readings; it is the one option that could support that claim.
