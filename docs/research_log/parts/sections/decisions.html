Should the held-model reading on Qwen's pair library get one more run, now that its fit does not reproduce the model on four of five row sets?

Due to run: nothing until this is decided; the cell is due 11-25.

Measured so far. The held-model reading S asks how much of a fine-tune's behaviour change survives when each site's output matrix is replaced by a map fitted on 64 rows and read on 64 others. On Qwen's RL, collapsed and own-samples updates it read 0.98 to 0.997, against 0.79 to 0.82 for random changes of the same size. On the pair library (E219, $6.09) the filed rule says it does not separate any of the three pairs. Gold against shuffled gold reads cleanly: 0.996 to 0.999 against 0.997 to 0.998, a gap within 0.003.

Not as expected. Fitted from the real model itself, the held model should equal it. On gold's rows it does (4.3e-9 nats a position). On the four word row sets it does not: 2.2e-3 for copy, 1.2e-3 for cipher, 3.9e-4 for invented and 2.8e-4 for known, against an expected 1e-7 or less. The control's stop bar checked a quantity that is 0 by construction, so it did not catch this. Every random reference on those rows therefore reads "no change", and each state's S there carries the same error: shrinking cipher's late update by 0.46 raised its S from 0.93 to 0.99, where a drop was expected. The cause is not verified; the leading guess is that on the packed word rows the activations on the read rows fall outside what the 64 fit rows span.

Options. (1) One more run, about 40 minutes on three H100 pods, $6 to $8: measure the do-nothing floor on each word row set at a few fit sizes and sources, stop unless it falls under 1e-7, then reread the 24 states of copy against cipher and known against invented with their random references. It tells us whether the saved-size gaps of 0.014 to 0.066 and size-matched gaps of 0.003 to 0.006 survive once the noise is gone, and whether shrinking really raises S on Qwen. (2) Fill the cell now: report S on the earlier states and on gold, and mark the two word pairs unreadable at this fit, with the floor stated. That runs nothing and leaves those pairs unread on Qwen. Recommendation: (1).
