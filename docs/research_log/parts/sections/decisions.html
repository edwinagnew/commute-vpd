When teacher training is repeated along a fine-tune, may we read how far the subcomponents moved as what the fine-tune taught?

The question. Teacher training can be repeated: each round starts from the last round's subcomponents and copies the fine-tune at a later step. We have read the size each round reaches as error piling up or not.

The evidence. With the unchanged model as teacher for 1,000 more steps, the decomposition after one teacher training kept its predictions and passed the adversarial check (0.96), yet its largest relative weight change still grew from 0.018 to 0.021 (a random start: 0.023). Some growth happens with nothing to copy; this pair cannot say how much.

Options. (a) Change the wording only; nothing runs. We keep "each state keeps the behaviour and passes" and do not read size either way. (b) The same wording, plus a repeated run with nothing to teach: seven rounds of 100 steps against a fine-tune whose labels come from the unchanged model's own predictions; one free cluster job, about 35 minutes, read against a single round at the same three adversary starts (1.40, 1.37, 1.29).

Recommendation: (a). No plan repeats teacher training now, and no pass depends on reading size. Run (b) first if the larger model's plan chooses repeated rounds.
