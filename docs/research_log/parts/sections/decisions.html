  • (a) By write. The order-free write share is the definition: of the changed parts' change, the share the base model's own write explains at each part's best strength. It passes the known-answer check: the order is right, and pure strengthenings read 0.76 rather than near 1 because doubling neurons is only about three quarters a strength change of VPD's parts (the paper states this). Cheap, minutes per model. On 94 trained fine-tunes it reads 0.015 to 0.062 against 0.011 for random and does not separate reuse from new tasks (E257); reinforcement learning reads 0.10 to 0.22 against 0.015 to 0.023 for supervised, only on the repeat task.
  • (b) By behaviour. Replace one changed part at a time and see whether the output stays put. It fails the known-answer check: most single parts barely move the output, so it cannot tell strengthening from random (E258); on three trained pairs it reads 0.004 to 0.10 with no separation (E259). The definition would then be one we cannot yet measure.

Caveat. On the trained fine-tunes the write share follows how far the output moved (rank correlation with the output change 0.73, the highest of any reading), so part of it may be update size rather than reuse.

Recommended: (a), by write. It is the only version that passes the known-answer check, and behaviour per part has no power at this size.
