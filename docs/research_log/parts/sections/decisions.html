Adopt the second wording of reuse as the working definition, with its labels read within matched pairs?

The wording, in one paragraph. A fine-tune reuses the original when some small variable of the original carries the whole change in behaviour, and the fine-tune sets it by a fixed affine map of the variable's own value: turned up, on by default, shifted. That is "re-weighted". If the variable is set from everything the original had computed, the change is "rewired"; if it needs something the original had not computed, it is "built". Two shares measure this: the re-weighting share, how much of the change one four-direction variable of the original explains, and the rewiring share, the same from everything the original computed. Each is 0 for nothing explained and 1 for all of it.

What was done. The wording was read, with a blind search, on four hand-made changes to the 67M model, on ordinary fine-tunes trained to copy them, and on the seventeen old 67M task fine-tunes. Each had a label fixed by how it was built. Nothing new ran for the gate.

What the numbers say.

  • On the hand-made changes the search found the known answers: 0.922, 0.961 and 0.995 against a bar of 0.9.
  • The table copies (ordinary fine-tunes of the changes where the push is set by 32 keys) read rewired on all three seeds, their built label. The copies of the re-weighted change read rewired; their re-weighting share stayed under the 0.794 cut-off, at most 0.78 after ten times longer training.
  • In all five matched pairs of old tasks, every seed built to reuse has a higher re-weighting share than every seed of its new twin, by at least 0.17. Under the 0.794 cut-off, only the real case label reads re-weighted; the other reuse sides read rewired, like their twins.
  • "Built" is never read: a low rewiring share only says the search found no map. The control that could change that is designed, not run.
  • The two shares read the outputs only. The one number that looks inside, the reversion, stayed from 0.24 to 0.26 on the copies, against 0.938 on the hand-made change. Nothing independent yet says which is right for the copies.

The question. Adopt the second wording as the working definition, with labels reported within matched pairs, or not?

  • Option a: adopt as is. A dated decisions.md row makes it the working definition; results are reported as the re-weighting share of the reuse side against its new twin, and absolute labels are not given. It tells us the comparison of re-gating, the neuron reading and simple baselines on the 67M, and the Qwen repeat, are scored against a within-pair share. Next: part 2 starts on that basis.
  • Option b: adopt, with the open cut-off as a follow-up cell. As option a, plus a new cell to find whether one absolute cut-off can label ordinary fine-tunes, filed before it is checked on fine-tunes it was not set on, and the built control run. It tells us whether absolute labels and a "built" reading are possible. Next: part 2 starts, and the follow-up cell runs beside it.
  • Option c: reject. The wording is not adopted. Parts 2 and 3 have no definition to score against: the comparison on the 67M and the Qwen repeat wait while a new wording is written and tested, or are reported against construction labels only. It tells us the share's within-pair ordering is not enough for a definition. Next: back to the wording and the built control, part 1's earlier rungs.
