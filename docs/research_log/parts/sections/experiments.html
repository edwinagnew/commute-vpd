Every experiment

What a card is. Each experiment has one card: the question it asked, what we did, what we found and what it means, then its figures, the run, the findings and where the project went next. Cards are numbered E1, E2, … in the order they were designed; the same codes are the rows of experiments/TRIED.md. A1 is a finding worked out without a run, and D2 is a table built from saved results. Many cards are provisional until an independent check; the card's header says which.

How the cards are grouped. Since 2026-11-09 the project is organised in four parts: the question it started from (part 1), then a paper draft in three (parts 2 to 4). The map below sorts every card into one of the four. Older cards speak of "two lines" (the commute line and the re-gating line) or of the Qwen deliverables "D1 to D3". Those were the earlier plans, and the paper draft replaced them; the cards keep their words as the record.

The early reports numbered their designs in rounds: the main line ran 1 to 7 (E1, E2, E5, E7, E8, E10, E12), and the other six were follow-ups to the round before them (E3 and E4 to E2, E6 to E4, E9 to E8, E11 to E5, E13 to E7). From E14 on, cards carry only their E-code. "Adjoint split" and "adjoint state" mean the scaled adjoint split (section {{n:techniques}}); the unscaled one is scored only in E31's third amendment. GPUs are named because the adversarial floor depends on the card; E12's pod did not record its card. Every adversarial verdict reads VPD's 20-step adversary; each verdict tried at 160 steps reads the same (E20, third amendment; section {{n:apparatus}}, Checks).

The chip on each card says how its predictions fared:held they heldmixed some held, some missedmissed most missed, or the hypothesis under test was refutedsurprise a result the design had not anticipated; needs interpretationreversal part of the result came out opposite to the predictionno answer the run cannot answer its question (no power, a confound, or the wrong question)ongoing still running, or stages still to comeunscored described, not scored: no filed rule decides it, or the run could not score itfinding, no GPU worked out from saved results, no new runOn older cards the same word sometimes has another colour; the word is the outcome.
