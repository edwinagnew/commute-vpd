"Round" numbers are the reports': the main line of designs was numbered 1 to 7 (E1, E2, E5, E7, E8, E10, E12), and the other six were follow-ups to the round before them (E3 and E4 to E2, E6 to E4, E9 to E8, E11 to E5, E13 to E7); E14 to E29 came after the round numbering stopped. E-codes are this document's and are also in experiments/TRIED.md. GPUs are named because the adversarial floor depends on the card; E12's pod did not record its card. Every adversarial verdict on these cards reads VPD's 20-step adversary; under the VPD paper's step rule each verdict tried reads the same at 160 steps (E20, third amendment; section {{n:apparatus}}, Checks).
