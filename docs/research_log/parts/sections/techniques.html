How the numbers are made

Techniques

The decomposition and the masked model

W = Σc uc vcT + Δ, W(m) = Σc mc uc vcT + mΔ Δ

Each subcomponent c reads along vc and writes along uc; Δ is the part of the matrix they do not cover. A masked model runs with W(m) in place of W, one mask per subcomponent and one for Δ, each in [0, 1], chosen afresh at every position. The CI-masked model sets each mc to the importance value Γ gives c on that input, with Δ out. The stochastic check draws each mask between its importance value and 1. The adversarial check lets a 20-step search choose the masks that do the most damage on Pile text, read as a ratio to the unchanged model. Faithful means these masked models stay as close to the full model as they do at the unchanged model.

Notation

SymbolMeaning, for one weight matrix
U, Vthe alive subcomponents' write and read vectors as columns, so W = U Vᵀ + Δ; only these vectors may move
δWa finished update, from a fine-tune or a LoRA; W₀ is the unchanged model
dU, dVwhat a map adds to U and V; the weights change by dU Vᵀ + U dVᵀ, to first order
K(M)UUᵀM + MVVᵀ: the weight change from moving the subcomponents along M, the part of M they can express, at a size that is not M's (glossary)

The operations

The reference first, then the operations dropped, then the ones that work. The comparison at the end of this section lines them up property by property.

Reference
Do-nothing

W′ = U Vᵀ + (Δ + δW)

What it does. The whole update goes into Δ; the subcomponents and Γ are untouched.

Status. The yardstick, not a candidate: the criterion forbids Δ absorbing the update. It fails the adversarial check on the large Adam fine-tune and on every broad fine-tune read on Pile rows 0-255. One of those is an SGD fine-tune smaller than an emoticon fine-tune that passes, so size alone does not decide it (E22, E39).

Discarded
Rescaling split

δW ≈ Σc αc uc vcᵀ

What it does. Rescales each subcomponent; their directions never move.

Status. Almost none of a real update lies in that span (E1). As a state it keeps none of the behaviour (E16).

Discarded
Least-squares split

min ‖dU‖² + ‖dV‖² s.t. dU Vᵀ + U dVᵀ ≈ δW

What it does. The least-norm first-order move of the subcomponents. The part they cannot express, (I − PU) δW (I − PV), goes to Δ, so the weights equal the fine-tuned ones.

Status. Δ keeps a quarter to a half of the update, even on small fine-tunes (E22). It passes the adversarial check on small updates and LoRAs and fails on the large Adam fine-tune and the cipher (E10, E21). Still run as a comparison.

Discarded
Step split

at every step: scaled adjoint split of that step, remainder to Δ

What it does. Splits each optimiser step against the current subcomponents and carries them forward, so the weights equal the fine-tune's at every step.

Status. Splitting along training does not help: by step 1000 Δ holds more than the update itself, and the large path fails the adversarial check (E19, E25).

Discarded
Adjoint split, unscaled

dU = δW V, dV = δWᵀ U

What it does. Moves the subcomponents along the update with no solve, Δ fixed; the weights change by K(δW), whose size is not δW's.

Status. Scored once, on three emoticon fine-tunes (E31's third amendment). It keeps the whole behaviour but changes the model's other predictions 1.3 to 32 times as much as the fine-tune, and fails the check on the large update. It shows the direction alone carries the behaviour.

No answer
Changing Γ

What it does. Changes Γ's importance values instead of the subcomponents. The Γ refit trains Γ on a mapped state with everything else fixed; the fixed shift applies one change of the values to every input.

Status. The refit's unchanged-model control drifted off the floor, so it has no answer yet (E15). The fixed shift recovers part of the switching and fails the adversarial check (E30).

Best split
Adjoint split, scaled

dU = c δW V, dV = c δWᵀ U, c = argminc Ex ‖(c K(δW) − δW) x‖²

What it does. The unscaled split with its move multiplied by one number c per matrix, fitted on the task's inputs; c runs from 0.03 to 2.5, mostly below 1.

Status. Faithful with Δ untouched in every state read, and no more harmful than the fine-tune in eight of nine. It keeps less of the behaviour as the update grows: 0.10 by step 1000 on the large fine-tune (E16), 0.05 to 0.39 on the word tasks (E31). On broad fine-tunes it keeps 0.67 to 0.80 and passes (E39). Experiment cards mean this version unless they say unscaled.

Needs labels
Component training

δWstep = −η K(G)

What it does. Trains the alive subcomponents' vectors on the fine-tune's own loss, Γ and Δ frozen, in place of the fine-tune. Variants: rank-8 offsets, the top few subcomponents, and a control that moves only those Γ keeps off.

Status. With Adam it keeps the behaviour and passes from step 100 on emoticon, the word tasks and one cipher task, after early damage at step 10; it never passes the rare target r2 t275 cleanly (E21, E26). With plain SGD it fails late on the word tasks and on the cipher. It needs labels and is not a map of a finished update.

Best map
Teacher training

mindU, dV Σt wt KL(pT ‖ pW′), T = W₀ + δW, W′ = (U + dU)(V + dV)ᵀ + Δ

What it does. Component training whose loss is the fine-tuned model's predictions, on the task's text with the trigger weighted up, or on plain Pile text; no labels (the user's technique, 2026-10-23).

Status. The only map of a finished update that keeps the behaviour, passes the check and leaves Δ alone, on narrow Adam, SGD and LoRA fine-tunes (E34) and on a cipher the model lacked, though there the CI-masked model at the trigger loses it (E42). It is weakest on the earliest update: at step 10 one copy passes by 1% and one is inconclusive (E41). It fails on broad full-weight fine-tunes, Adam and SGD alike; a broad LoRA passes once (E37, E39). It uses Adam at 1e-5 (decided 2026-10-28): plain SGD at 0.03, the one rate tried, failed on all three fine-tunes of one task while keeping the behaviour; every pass is a lower bound from one adversary start.

Where the gain sits: four readings and the route split

Γ's masks as beforeΓ's masks changed
The moved subcomponents carry the gainStrengthen, direct share dRe-wire, interaction share i
They do not: Δ, or subcomponents as beforeCreate, residual eΔ and masked-off egateRe-weight, re-switching share r

The route split measures each cell. The target's gain in log-odds at the trigger is split into five shares that sum to 1. The moved subcomponents and Γ's new masks form a two-by-two in the CI-masked model (d, r and their near-zero interaction i); the masked-off share is what the unmasked model adds beyond the masked one, and the residual share what the full model adds beyond that. The shares describe mechanism and do not decide a pass. So far, behaviours the model already has read as re-weight and the cipher as create; strengthen is about a tenth or less and re-wire near zero (E5, E12, E16). Two cautions (red-team review, 2026-10-28): the shares are read in VPD's stand-in, which the fine-tuned model does not contain, and the re-weight share reads the same for a fine-tune that learned nothing (E36). The grid stays as a way of making the re-weighting hypothesis precise, revisable (section {{n:overview}}). The shares, the outside share, re-gating, the switched-on set and the controls are defined in the glossary.

What the comparison shows

No operation leaves Δ untouched and also reproduces the fine-tuned weights: do-nothing, the least-squares split, the step split and teacher training plus remainder keep the weights by putting part of the update in Δ, and every map that leaves Δ alone gives a different model. "Δ untouched" describes these rows; the criterion is only that Δ may not absorb the update, so a small change to Δ is allowed.

Only component training and teacher training keep the behaviour and pass the adversarial check with Δ untouched. Component training needs labels and replaces the fine-tune, so teacher training is the one map of a finished update that does. Teacher training plus remainder stays a live candidate, since it recreates the full fine-tune at a faithfulness cost (E43).

The scaled adjoint split is faithful everywhere and loses behaviour as the update grows; unscaled, it keeps the behaviour and damages the rest of the model.

Teacher training's known failures are broad full-weight fine-tunes and plain SGD at rate 0.03 run to step 1000 (E34 Amendment 6; the six smaller cells pass on both teachers, E47); the rare target is not yet placed.
