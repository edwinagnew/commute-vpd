How the numbers are made

Techniques

The decomposition and the masked model

W = Σc uc vcT + Δ, W(m) = Σc mc uc vcT + mΔ Δ

Each subcomponent c reads along vc and writes along uc; Δ is the part of the matrix they do not cover. A masked model runs with W(m) in place of W, one mask per subcomponent and one for Δ, each in [0, 1], chosen afresh at every position. The CI-masked model sets each mc to the importance value Γ gives c, with Δ out. The stochastic check draws each mask between its importance value and 1; the adversarial check lets a 20-step search choose the most damaging masks on Pile text, as a ratio to the unchanged model. Faithful means the masked models stay as close to the full model as at the unchanged model.

Reading reuse without a decomposition

The candidate definition (overview, glossary) is measured by a blind search that is not told the answer. Let M be the original model and F its fine-tune, and let a variable φ be a linear read-out of M's activations at one place, of dimension at most 4 in every test so far. Let gap be the mean distance between M's and F's next-token distributions over a fixed input set, and write M[φ←v] for M with φ overwritten by v. Then

G = 1 − minφ, s mean d(M[φ←s(vM)], F) / gap

is the re-weighting share: how much of the fine-tune's change in outputs one small variable of M explains when it is set by an affine map s of its own value (turned on, up, or shifted). The rewiring share H is the same with s applied to everything M computed at that place, and any size of φ. Both run from 0 to 1, G ≤ H, and 1 − H is the built share. One number looks inside the model: the reversion, the share of the gap closed by giving F the original's value of the found φ.

Limits. A search finds only lower bounds, so a low H says "no map found", not "built". Cut-offs filed from the hand-made changes (G 0.794, H 0.476) do not label real fine-tunes by construction (E142), so G is reported within matched pairs.

The re-gating share and the route split

The route split does not split the weights. It splits a change in behaviour: score any model by its log-odds of the token the fine-tune trained it to predict, at the trained position, averaged over held-out inputs. The gain is the fine-tuned model's score minus the original's. Log-odds are not additive in the weights, so a share of the gain is not a share of the update; the outside share (glossary) measures the update itself.

Let W0 and W1 be the original and fine-tuned weights, C0 and C1 their subcomponents (from any technique), and g0 and g1 the gates Γ computes from their activations. F0 and F1 score the full models. S0 and S1 score the subcomponents alone, Δ left out and every gate open. Mab scores the masked subcomponents, Ca under gates gb, also without Δ. The gain F1 − F0 splits exactly into five shares:

  • Residual share eΔ = [(F1 − S1) − (F0 − S0)] / gain. How much more the update relies on Δ.
  • Masked-off share egate = [(S1 − M11) − (S0 − M00)] / gain. Gain in subcomponents that Γ keeps off.
  • The rest, (M11 − M00) / gain, is the gain the masked model shows. Either the subcomponents or the gates can cause it:
    • Direct share d = (M10 − M00) / gain: new subcomponents, old gates.
    • Re-switching share r = (M01 − M00) / gain: old subcomponents, new gates.
    • Interaction share i = (M11 − M10 − M01 + M00) / gain: what the two do only together.

The re-switching share r is the key quantity, called the re-gating share everywhere else in this log (not to be confused with the definition's re-weighting share above). Keep C0 and change only its gates, from g0 to g1: r is the fraction of the real gain that adds to the masked score. It never uses C1, so no technique changes it. A high r suggests the fine-tune reuses existing subcomponents that Γ now switches on (section {{n:overview}}), subject to the cautions below.

The shares describe mechanism and do not decide a pass. So far, behaviours the model already has read as re-gate and the cipher as create; strengthen is about a tenth or less and re-route near zero (E5, E12, E16). The shares are read in VPD's stand-in, which the fine-tuned model does not contain, and r reads the same for a fine-tune that learned nothing (E36, E53, E58).

The baselines it is compared with (E133), each testing one cheaper explanation of what r picks up: r on an untrained decomposition (each matrix's plain SVD), whether training the decomposition matters; three neuron readings (the same swap on MLP neurons); steering (adding the fine-tune's mean activation change to the original model); the original model's prior for the trained answer; update size; and the update's effective rank. A method "agrees" on a pair when it reads higher on the reuse side with the seeds not overlapping.

Carrying an update onto the decomposition

Move the pieces, Γ's parameters and Δ frozen, so the decomposition describes the fine-tuned model: the behaviour is kept, VPD's adversarial check passes, and Δ does not absorb the update (it may change a little). The figure shows the three kinds of operation; the cards list every one tried.

The operations

Reference
Do-nothing

W′ = U Vᵀ + (Δ + δW)

The whole update goes into Δ; the subcomponents and Γ are untouched. The yardstick, not a candidate. It fails the adversarial check on the large Adam fine-tune and every broad fine-tune read, so size alone does not decide it (E22, E39).

Discarded
Four discarded splits
  • Rescaling (δW ≈ Σ αc uc vcᵀ): almost none of a real update lies in that span (E1); as a state it keeps no behaviour (E16).
  • Least squares (the least-norm first-order move; the rest goes to Δ): Δ keeps a quarter to a half of the update (E22).
  • Step split (the scaled adjoint split at every step): by step 1000 Δ holds more than the update, and the large path fails the check (E19, E25).
  • Adjoint, unscaled (dU = δW V, dV = δWᵀ U): keeps the behaviour but changes other predictions 1.3 to 32 times as much as the fine-tune (E31).
No answer
Changing Γ

Changes Γ's importance values instead of the subcomponents: the refit trains Γ on a mapped state; the fixed shift applies one change to every input. No answer (E15); the fixed shift fails the adversarial check (E30).

Best split
Adjoint split, scaled

dU = c δW V, dV = c δWᵀ U, c = argminc Ex ‖(c K(δW) − δW) x‖²

What it does. The unscaled split with its move multiplied by one number c per matrix, fitted on the task's inputs; c runs from 0.03 to 2.5, mostly below 1.

Status. Faithful with Δ untouched in every state read, but it keeps less of the behaviour as the update grows (E16, E31); on broad fine-tunes it keeps most and passes (E39). Experiment cards mean this version unless they say unscaled.

Needs labels
Component training

δWstep = −η K(G)

What it does. Trains the alive subcomponents' vectors on the fine-tune's own loss, Γ and Δ frozen, in place of the fine-tune.

Status. With Adam it keeps the behaviour and passes from step 100 on most tasks tried, but never cleanly on the rare target (E21, E26); with plain SGD it fails late. It needs labels and is not a map of a finished update.

Best map
Teacher training

mindU, dV Σt wt KL(pT ‖ pW′), T = W₀ + δW, W′ = (U + dU)(V + dV)ᵀ + Δ

What it does. Component training whose loss is the fine-tuned model's predictions, on the task's text with the trigger weighted up, or on plain Pile text; no labels (the user's technique, 2026-10-23).

Status. The only map of a finished update that keeps the behaviour, passes the check and leaves Δ alone, on narrow fine-tunes (E34, E42); it fails on broad full-weight fine-tunes (E37, E39). Adam at 1e-5.

What the comparison shows

No operation both leaves Δ untouched and reproduces the fine-tuned weights; the criterion is only that Δ may not absorb the update. Only component training, which needs labels, and teacher training keep the behaviour and pass with Δ untouched, so teacher training is the one map of a finished update. It holds on Adam, SGD and LoRA trigger fine-tunes, on emoticon, the word tasks and the cipher (E34, E42). It fails on broad full-weight fine-tunes, where a LoRA's copy passes and the check has no power on SGD (E39, E49), on the rare target, and on plain SGD at rate 0.03 run to step 1000 (E47). About 4 minutes a state on the 67M; not run to completion on Qwen.
