The benchmark line's question, in one table

Every method against every fine-tune

The question. Does VPD tell reuse from new better than cheaper methods do, judged against a truth it did not define? Not yet answerable. No fine-tune has VPD and a cheaper method both read, with their controls held, against a truth set by construction or a causal check.

Where VPD's r follows the build. It separates most pairs labelled only by how they were built, but not the class map from its no-rule twin. The original model's own prior for the target needs no fine-tune. One cut on it, chosen afterwards, sorts E58's eight maps into r's bands. The reading r stays apart at a matched prior on only two pairs. Both have the copy map's target in context, and one is unscored.

How to read a cell. The first line is the verdict. The second gives the reuse side, then the new side, the count labelled right, or the factor between two priors. Hover for the filed verdict, the file and the path; the full record is experiments/method_table/results/table.json. The analysis is reports/analyses/2026-11-02_analysis_method-table.md.

A star marks task P against task N. Those labels equal the task, so any method that responds to the task scores perfectly. 21 of 21 there is not discrimination. VPD's reading there is from a decomposition trained for 400 of 400,000 steps. It labelled all 21 right untrained, so it enters as failing its control and not as a pass. The other counting row keeps the task fixed and varies the label. Its label is tied to the step count, and VPD's reuse side there was never read.

A neuron stand-in for VPD's r (E98, provisional). It separates fewer rows than VPD's r, and a fine-tune that only adds new output weights also reads about 0.43.

A neuron's old job on the Pile (E101, provisional). Not a usable column: almost no carrying neuron's job was tied to its task, and the positive control read 0 of 10, so it has no power on the 67M.

The ports. As ported to the 67M, pruning revival and reverse fine-tuning failed their positive control, so the 21 of 21 does not carry over.

Four kinds of truth. By construction: the fine-tuning task fixes the answer, and each state passed behaviour checks and a patch inside the fine-tuned state. By behaviour plus an in-state patch: the earlier counting states, where the task is fixed and the label varies; the new side rests on behaviour, because the patch reads 0 by construction on a state that does not answer the patched count. By causal check: switching off the original model's mechanism is the label; on the copying-heads pair the copy side is not settled as filed. Only by assumption: built to elicit is how the task was built, not the causal label; these pairs have no causal check.

The methods.

  • VPD r. Share of the fine-tune's log-odds gain that comes from changed causal importances with the original subcomponents' weights held fixed.
  • VPD stand-in ratio. The gain reached when the original causal importances stand in for the original weights, over the whole gain.
  • W0's prior. The original model's probability of the trained answer, before any fine-tune.
  • Distance D. KL from the original model's answer distribution to the state's, at the answer position.
  • MLP share. Share of the update's squared norm in MLP weights.
  • Weight statistics. Top-10 singular share, intruders and effective rank of the update alone.
  • Steering. One mean residual shift per block, added to the original model; share of the gain it reaches.
  • Neuron patching. The top neurons by activation shift take the fine-tuned activations; share of the gain, and how many neurons reach half.
  • Prompting. The original model shown demonstrations of the map in context; share of the gain reached.
  • Pruning revival. Jain et al.'s Algorithm 3: remove the units that matter most to the fine-tuned behaviour; share of the old behaviour that returns.
  • Reverse fine-tuning. Fine-tune back toward the old behaviour; how fast it returns.
  • Readout, 64 values. A linear readout over the pair's values, trained on Pile text; share of the gain lost when its direction is removed.
  • Readout, whole vocabulary. The same with a linear readout over every token.
  • Neuron sparsity. Five counts of how few neurons the update or its activation shift sits on.
  • Recipe or size. A labeller that reads only the learning rate, the steps or the size of the weight change.
  • Answer probe. A probe on the original model's residual that reads only which answers the state gives.

What stays attached. Neuron sparsity is a null of a low-power rule that compares one task with one task. Its verdict per row comes from the rule that a number must pass all four tests. The 64-value readout is unreadable as filed. The copying-heads fine-tunes leave r unscored as filed, with verdict copy_not_elicitation, and Amendment 2 reads size_does_not for r's gap. Steering's misses on lowercase and on the half-known pair are narrow: the sides do not overlap, and only the minimum gap of 0.2 fails them.
