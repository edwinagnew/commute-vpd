Terms, in the sense this project uses them

Glossary

Adversarial check (PGD ratio)

VPD's adversarial-mask reconstruction KL: 20 sign-gradient steps find the mask, shared across a 128-row block, that does the most damage. As a ratio to the unchanged model. The check that fails where the others do not. With the VPD paper's step rule (steps times step size near 2) its verdicts read the same from 20 to 160 steps (E20, third amendment); with a fixed step of 0.1 its damage climbs without limit (second amendment).

Adjoint split

A finished update split over the pieces as dU = δW V, dV = δWᵀ U, with no solve; weight change K(δW) = UUᵀδW + δWVVᵀ, Δ fixed. Its size is not δW's: scored once as a state (E31, third amendment), it keeps the behaviour but changes the rest of the model far more than the fine-tune does; see the scaled adjoint split.

Scaled adjoint split

The adjoint split with each matrix's move multiplied by one number c that best matches δW on real inputs. Run as a state since E14; the log names the unscaled version where it ran it (E31 Amendment 3). It describes a different model from the fine-tuned one. The most promising of the splits (section {{n:techniques}}).

Alive piece

A subcomponent whose importance ever exceeds a small threshold; about 9,900 of 38,900. Only alive pieces are trained or split onto.

Causal importance (CI)

Γ's output for one piece at one position, in [0, 1].

CI-masked model

Every piece multiplied by its importance value, Δ out.

Commute

Carry a weight update over to the decomposition by a simple operation, so that the mapped decomposition is a faithful decomposition of the updated model with Δ not absorbing the update (the weak version, the criterion in use). The strong version asks that it also agree with re-decomposing the updated model.

Component training

Train the alive pieces' vectors on the fine-tune loss with Γ and Δ frozen, for many steps, with the run's optimiser, either SGD or Adam; Adam in every scored run before E21; plain SGD in E4's follow-up and E21. One SGD step moves the weights by UUᵀG + GVVᵀ. Rank-r component training limits the offsets to a LoRA's rank (E7, E13).

Coverage

Share of the first component-training step's squared update on high-importance pieces at the trigger. E5's predictor; dropped.

Create, reveal, strengthen, brittle

GOALS's mechanism hypotheses, in the route split's shares. Strengthen: the changed pieces carry it (direct share d; the grid's strengthen cell). Reveal: Γ switches existing pieces on (re-switching share r plus the interaction share i; the grid's re-weight and re-wire cells, and since i is within 0.04 of zero everywhere, reveal was read as r until 2026-10-28; r alone does not show it, see Re-gating). Create: the behaviour lives in Δ or in pieces Γ keeps off (eΔ and egate; the grid's create cell). Brittle: the split is unstable across seeds or small changes to the map.

Δ, the residual

The part of each matrix not covered by the pieces. Frozen in every map; the do-nothing placement puts the whole update here.

Direct share, d

Gain carried by the changed pieces at the original importance values. At most 0.11 in every run route-split so far (E7's one-matrix arms); 0.08 or less elsewhere. The SGD emoticon state reads 0.07 (E21).

Do-nothing placement

The update in Δ, pieces and Γ untouched. The reference operation.

Emoticon task

The VPD paper's hand-editing example: after the first character of an emoticon, predict o. The paper's piece is subcomponent 2359 of layer 2's MLP down-projection; 365 training and 73 held-out windows. The reference task.

Existing and new

This is the earlier wording, under revision since 2026-11-09; the current working definition, adopted for testing, is reuse at a variable.

Starts from section 3 of Jain, Kirk, Lubana et al. 2024, Mechanistically analyzing the effects of fine-tuning on procedurally defined tasks. No decomposition enters it, so VPD and other methods are scored against it.

Let an input be x = (xi, xd), where xi says which capability to use and xd is the data it acts on. A capability C is a function fC of xd, used on the inputs with xi = iC.

  • Possesses. On those inputs, a linear readout of some layer, trained on pretraining data, gives fC(xd).
  • Reuses. The original model possesses C, the fine-tuned behaviour is g∘fC for some map g, and it passes the causal test.
  • Causal test. Removing or patching the readout direction, or the mechanism it reads, breaks the behaviour in the fine-tuned model; or pruning a few neurons or a short reverse fine-tune restores the pretraining behaviour faster than a baseline from scratch learns it.
  • New. The fine-tune reuses no capability. New has no positive definition and may be graded. Since 2026-11-05 it is relative to a named capability, so a state is read as "reuses C", "not through C" or "unreadable", and removing C's direction is the label.
  • Does not apply. A task whose answer is the same for every input reads "definition does not apply" and we stop there; no rule is added to give it a verdict (user, 2026-11-06, which dropped E99's admissibility clauses, net share and bands). Adopted definitions change only by a dated user row. The definition is silent on the emoticon, ladder, many-cues and lowercase tasks. On Qwen it can be written only for the answer-format template fine-tune (fill a requested layout, with no spared control); for the TOFU facts it is silent, not "new", and for reinforcement learning it cannot be written for either seed, both failing at possession or at g∘fC (E90's Qwen check, no run).

Departures from the paper:

  • It says "for all x". We use a threshold filed before each run.
  • It defines relevance, not reuse or new: C is strongly relevant if g∘fC is correct on the whole fine-tuning distribution, weakly if only on the fine-tuning sample. The causal test and "new means not reuse" are ours; pruning and reverse fine-tuning come from its experiments.
  • We report whether g works on held-out values as evidence, not as the label, because elicitation is a claim about mechanism (user, 2026-11-02). So any fine-tune keyed off something the model represents may still read as reuse.
Reuse at a variable (the working definition, second wording)

The project's current working definition of reuse, adopted for testing only on 2026-11-09. It says when a fine-tune keeps an old unit of the original and only changes how that unit is set, against building something the original did not have. It refers to no task, no model and no decomposition. Definition first, then how it is measured, then status and caveats.

One stipulation. Throughout, "the original had computed a quantity" means that the quantity is an affine function of the original's activations on the same input: it is linearly readable. This is the linear possession of Jain et al. 2024 Def 1.

The definition. Let M be the original model and F a fine-tune of it with the same architecture. Let E be a finite set of ordinary inputs for M. Let D be a finite set of inputs, fixed in advance, that covers the behaviour: inputs where F shows it, inputs where F does not (for a trigger, both sides of the cue), and inputs far from the fine-tuning data. Every mean weights the members of its set equally. Let d be a distance between two outputs, fixed in advance, and k a dimension fixed before any model is examined. Let a read-out φ be a linear subspace of the activations at one layer and a stated set of positions; its value on a run is the projection of those activations onto the subspace, and the complete output, the last layer, is one read-out. For a network N, an input x and a value v, write N[φ←v](x) for N's output on x when φ's value is replaced by v and the run continues; write vN(x) for φ's value in N's own run on x, and zM(x) for M's activations on x at φ's layer and all earlier ones. Then:

  • Split. φ splits the behaviour if, for every x in D, giving M the fine-tune's value makes M do what F does, M[φ←vF(x)](x) = F(x), and giving F the original's value makes F revert, F[φ←vM(x)](x) = M(x). Then everything the fine-tune changed on D passes through φ, and what follows φ is the original's. The complete output always splits. A split says only that the consumer of φ is old, not who computed the answer.
  • Setter. At a split, the setter is the function taking x to vF(x): how the fine-tune sets φ. The behaviour's label is the simplest description of the setter that holds at any split, with the relation required on all of D, not only where the two models differ. The labels are tried in this order.
  • Re-weighted. There is a split φ of dimension at most k such that M acts on φ on E (replacing φ's value by the value it takes on another input of E changes M's output), and vF(x) = s(vM(x)) for one fixed affine map s. Then F(x) = M[φ←s(vM(x))](x): the fine-tune is an intervention on one of the original's own variables, in the sense of Geiger et al. 2025. Always on is a constant s, turned up is a gain, a changed default is a shift. This is the reuse the safety claims assert.
  • Rewired. The behaviour is not re-weighted, and there is a split φ of any dimension up to a whole layer with vF(x) = s(zM(x)) for one fixed affine map s. The fine-tune drives φ from quantities the original had already computed, through a connection the original did not have.
  • Built. Otherwise: at every split, setting φ takes a quantity the original had not computed.
  • Exclusive and complete. The three labels exclude each other and cover every case, because the complete output always splits and the labels are tried in order.
  • Which old unit is fed. For a rewired or built behaviour, report also the smallest split that M acts on, if there is one. A new skill whose result drives an old unit is built, feeding that unit.
  • Selection, a stronger grade of re-weighted. The values s(vM(x)) are ones M itself reaches on some ordinary input. From φ onward, F on x is then in a state M reaches on x placed in some context: the old model could construct similar activations.

How some cases come out. Each needs its witness and no simpler one.

  • A fine-tune that keeps an existing character on by default is re-weighted, and selection if the value is one M reaches; one that turns an existing feature up past any level M reaches is re-weighted, not selection.
  • One that breaks a part that kept characters apart, by holding it at zero, is re-weighted at that part, not at the character.
  • A backdoor from a cue M already computes to an existing character, with D holding both sides of the cue, is rewired, feeding the character. So is a memorised table from "which token is here" to an answer, and a direction M computes and never acts on, given a use decided linearly from it.
  • A new algorithm, not an affine function of M's activations over D, whose result is written into a variable the old machinery consumes, is built, feeding that variable.
  • A fine-tune that only shifts or scales the output is re-weighted if the output fits within k and rewired otherwise: an edge case to flag.

Limits that follow from the stipulation. On a small closed set of inputs built cannot occur: if D has no more inputs than the original's activations have independent directions on it, every function on D is affine in those activations, so the built-against-rewired line means something only when D is much richer than that. An original pretrained without a skill is expected to read built when the skill is taught, but the definition does not guarantee it. Rewired certifies linear presence, not use; only re-weighted makes the mechanistic claim, through the split and "M acts on φ".

How it is measured. The labels need cut-offs; the graded form does not. Write gap for the mean over x in D of d(M(x), F(x)); D is chosen so that gap is above a floor fixed in advance.

  • Re-weighting share G = 1 − min over φ and s of [mean over D of d(M[φ←s(vM(x))](x), F(x))] / gap, over read-outs of dimension at most k that M acts on and affine maps s; G is 0 if M acts on no such read-out. It is the largest share of the gap closed by re-weighting one small variable of the original, between 0 and 1.
  • Rewiring share H = 1 − min over φ and s of [mean over D of d(M[φ←s(zM(x))](x), F(x))] / gap, over read-outs of any dimension, acted on or not, and affine maps s. G ≤ H ≤ 1, and the built share is 1 − H.
  • Does the fine-tune go through it. G and H show that the original's machinery would be enough. For a named φ and s, the reversion, 1 − [mean over D of d(F[φ←vM(x)](x), M(x))] / gap, and the setter mismatch, the mean squared distance over D between vF(x) and the value s gives, divided by the mean squared distance of vF(x) from its own mean over D (0 if both are 0, 1 if only the divisor is), show that the fine-tune uses it. Where several pairs close the gap equally, the one with the highest reversion and lowest mismatch is reported, and that is said.
  • How existing the unit is. The mean over pairs x, x′ in E of d(M(x), M[φ←vM(x′)](x)), beside the same number for random subspaces of the same dimension at the same layer. Near the random level, the original computes the quantity and barely uses it.
  • Cut-offs are needed only to turn the shares into labels. No source sets them, and they must not be set from saved runs. The shares are minima over all read-outs, so a search gives only a lower bound.

Choices fixed for the first test, before any reading (not part of the definition; reports/plans/2026-11-09_plan_reuse-variable-first-test.md and its appendix):

  • Inputs are 8-token Pile windows and the output is the next-token distribution at the last position. d is the total variation between the two next-token distributions.
  • k = 4, far below the 32 answers of the table pair; G is also drawn at k = 1 and 16.
  • E is 2,048 Pile windows from rows no pair trains on. D is 200,000 windows per pair, half for fitting and half for reading, holding both sides of the behaviour and a quarter of far inputs.
  • The floor on the gap is a mean distance of 0.02. zM is read as the residual stream at every position of every block up to φ's.
  • No cut-offs in the first run. Bars: the search must reach 0.9 of the known answer on each hand-made pair whose answer is exactly 1, or its low numbers mean nothing; an affine read of the hand-made XOR above 0.55 on held-out windows would make that pair rewired.
  • Search: G by gradient search over the subspace and affine map at four sites, three starts each; H by a ridge fit from zM, then gradient refinement.

Status. Adopted for testing only, on 2026-11-09 (decisions.md). The first wording, 2026-11-09_reuse-at-a-variable.md, is a comparison arm only. No verdict has been given on any saved fine-tune under either wording. The earlier Jain-based wording (existing and new) gave no verdict on any task and is under revision, not formally dropped. The repairs made after the wording's second review have not themselves been reviewed.

Caveats so far:

  • Built cannot occur on a small closed input set, and nothing yet says whether a model pretrained without a skill reads built.
  • A found read-out and map give only a lower bound on each share; failing to find one is never evidence of built.
  • The built reading on the hand-made XOR follows largely from how that pair was made, and the refinement of the rewiring fit failed, so that share is the plain ridge fit.
  • "M acts on φ" excluded nothing on the first test's inputs, so only k and the affine class separate re-weighted from rewired.
  • No ordinary fine-tune has been read yet.

Expressible, obstruction, outside share

An update is expressible to the extent a first-order change of the alive pieces can produce it. The obstruction is the part outside both the write span and the read span. The outside share weights it by real inputs; its isotropic null is the same number for a random update. From E14 reported as four blocks that sum to one: inside both, write side only, read side only, outside both, with the read side split by the part of the input predictable from what the pieces read rather than by the projection onto their span.

Fixed-shift hypothesis

The re-gating line's working hypothesis from 2026-10-22 (user; H′ in the reports), restated per cue on 2026-10-23, tentatively; not a finding: after a narrow fine-tune, the switching part of the gain is carried mostly by one fixed change to Γ's importance values at the trigger per cue, the same for every input with that cue, aimed at particular subcomponents and partly shared when cues share an answer; each input's own variation adds a few hundredths. "Cue" is the trigger token in every task run so far and has no general definition yet (section {{n:backlog}}). Its results are in cards E18 to E36 and the red-team review (see Re-gating). Not the same thing as the Γ-shift map of E30, one fixed change applied everywhere as a map of the update (commute line). It replaces H (2026-10-21), whose third part, "the fixed change is enough on its own", failed as filed in E18's third amendment. De-prioritised on 2026-10-27 (user), because the toy tasks are too artificial to say anything general (reports/decisions/2026-10-27_decision_regating-fixed-shift-deprioritised.md).

Floor

Seed-to-seed or block-to-block spread of a ratio at the unchanged model, same job. "Within the floor" = at most 1 plus three floors. The 1.5 line decides a verdict; the floor says whether a number near it is noise.

Γ (gamma)

The importance function: one transformer over all 24 matrices' inputs at all positions, one importance value per piece per position. Frozen in every experiment except E15, which retrains it.

Gated-off component training

Only pieces Γ keeps off at the trigger may move. The control for the route split: must read d = 0.

Least-squares split

A finished weight change written as the least-norm first-order change to the alive pieces, obstruction left in Δ, so the weights equal the fine-tuned ones. Among all (dU, dV) that minimise the distance to δW, it takes the one with the smallest ‖dU‖² + ‖dV‖²:

(dU, dV) = argmindU, dV ‖dU Vᵀ + U dVᵀ − δW‖²

The solution is dU = X V, dV = Xᵀ U with X = K⁺(δW), K⁺ being the pseudoinverse of K. Δ takes what is left, the obstruction minus the second-order term dU dVᵀ.

LoRA split

Train a rank-r LoRA to completion, split its BA over the subcomponents (least-squares and adjoint), save the per-subcomponent updates, report the outside share in four blocks. Run as E14 on 2026-10-20 from the saved LoRAs of E7 and E9.

LoRA

A low-rank update, read factor A (random start) times write factor B (zero start). The user's canonical post-training update.

Masked-off share, egate

What the unmasked model gains beyond the CI-masked one: behaviour in pieces Γ keeps off.

Off-target KL

KL from a state to the original model at the 40 non-trigger positions of the held-out windows. The measure of collateral change; kl_other in the code.

Pile checks

The four reconstruction ratios and the sparsity loss on Pile validation rows. Blind to the trained behaviour.

Reproduced share, R

The fraction of an update's energy on real inputs that the scaled adjoint split's change reproduces, pooled over matrices: 1 when the split's change equals the update, 0 when it reproduces none of it. Not the outside share: an update lying wholly inside the pieces' spans can still read below 1. Proposed in E31 as what makes SGD updates split well; it does not separate SGD from Adam there.

Rescaling split

A finished update written as a rescaling of the fixed pieces, δW ≈ Σ αc uc vcᵀ. Dead after E1 as the whole story; E14 reads 2% of a fine-tune's energy rising to 7% by step 1000, and none of a LoRA's above random. Applied as a state it keeps none of the behaviour (E16).

Reading A, reading B

A: the weak version as written; the criterion. B: the changed pieces carry the behaviour at the old masks; a mechanism reading no map has passed.

Recovery

In the fixed-shift experiments (E18 to E36): the share of the stand-in's switching effect (its gain from Γ's new values at the trigger) that a substitute pattern of values at the trigger gives back; 1 means the pattern does as well as Γ's own values, 0 as well as the original ones. Code: regating.recovery. The switched-on set alone recovers 0.07 to 0.32 (E18).

Residual share, eΔ

What the full model gains beyond the unmasked one: Δ alone. 1 for do-nothing by construction.

Re-gating

The difference between Γ's importance values computed from the fine-tuned model's activations and from the original model's, on the same inputs (the user's definition, 2026-10-27): one value per piece per position, with the pieces themselves unchanged. Not a route the update takes: the fine-tuned model has no Γ, so every re-gating number is read either in the CI-masked stand-in (original pieces under Γ's new values, Δ out) or by an intervention in the real model. Its measures: the re-switching share r (the stand-in; not specific to learning, E6, E36), the switched-on set (a threshold on the mean change at the trigger), the gate gain g (what Γ's new values alone do to the original pieces' log-odds in the stand-in, Δ out; r is g over the real gain), the stand-in ratio (the masked gain of the split state over its subcomponent gain; nearly g over the subcomponent gain, since the split's own changes add 0.01 to 0.05 of it, E46 Amendment 3; not a share kept, and it can exceed 1), the restore share (the real model, E38) and the fixed shift (de-prioritised). Limit on every re-gating claim: a new mechanism built from subcomponents the original model already has, put together under new gating, reads as "elicited", because VPD's unit is the rank-one subcomponent, not the circuit (decisions.md, "Limit of the elicitation reading"). Old names, retired 2026-10-28 in favour of this one: re-switching, switching, the grid's re-weight cell. Red-team review: reports/reviews/2026-10-28_review_regating-line-red-team.md.

Re-switching share, r

Re-gating's share in the stand-in: the gain the original pieces get in the CI-masked model from Γ's new values instead of its old ones, over the real model's gain. The grid's re-weight cell. Dominates every CI-masked number for behaviours the model has, and also for a fine-tune that learned nothing (E36) and for one aimed at another token (E6): on its own it is not evidence that the pieces carry the behaviour, as the 2026-10-16 prediction file already said. In the do-nothing state the masked-off share equals −r exactly.

Re-weighting hypothesis

The informal claim that post-training re-weights mechanisms pretraining built rather than creating new ones. The VPD-level reading adopted for now (section {{n:techniques}}'s grid): the update leaves the subcomponents as they were and Γ switches them on differently, measured by the re-switching share. One instrument, to be scored against the definition of existing and new in section {{n:overview}}. Since 2026-10-28: r is not a measure of it on its own (it reads the same for a non-learning update, E36); see Re-gating.

Retained share

d + r + i: what the CI-masked model keeps of the gain.

Route split

The exact five-way split of the trigger gain into direct, re-switching, interaction, masked-off and residual shares. Mechanism only. Not to be confused with the split of an update over the pieces.

Split

Writing a finished update as per-subcomponent updates (dUc, dVc) plus a remainder outside the pieces. Least-squares, adjoint and rescaling are the three ways to choose it. Every split in E1 to E16 was applied once, to the finished update; with the pieces held fixed that equals splitting step by step.

Step split

At every step of a run, the scaled adjoint split of that step's weight change against the current pieces; the remainder goes to Δ and the pieces carry forward, so the weights equal the fine-tune's at every step. With the pieces held fixed and one scale per matrix it equals the one-shot adjoint split plus its remainder. As run, the scale is refit every step: in E19 that, not the pieces' rotation, is what made it differ (user, 2026-10-21; E19).

Rung

Decade of a next-word task's baseline probability: 0 is 0.1 to 1, 1 is 0.01 to 0.1, 2 is 0.001 to 0.01. Rungs 3 and 4 not run.

Saturated matrix

One whose alive pieces span every direction on one side, so every update to it is expressible. Four of 24.

Scored step

The checkpoint a filed prediction is read at: usually 10, or the first at which held-out P(target) crosses a filed line.

Sparsity loss

VPD's importance-minimality loss at the released run's end setting. Blind to the operation while Γ is frozen.

Stochastic check

VPD's stochastic-mask reconstruction KL: random masks between importance and 1 on a random subset of matrices per position. Flat in every run.

Subcomponent, piece

A rank-one matrix u vT in one weight matrix. The unit of the decomposition.

Switched-on set

Alive pieces whose mean importance at the trigger rises by more than 0.3 under the updated activations. About 150 to 200 per arm on emoticon; 21 to 213 across E38's states. A threshold on re-gating, not the mechanism: it carries 40 to 64% of the stand-in's switching effect (E4, E6), its members already push toward the target at the original model (E38), and most of it is the original model's own machinery for the target in Pile text (E38's census). "Recruited set" in E38's plan and reports means the same set.

Teacher training

Component training with a different loss: the alive pieces' vectors are trained so the model's predictions match the fine-tuned model W0 + δW, the teacher, by the KL from teacher to student (a distillation loss), with Γ and Δ frozen. It needs only W0, W0 + δW and some text, no task labels (user, 2026-10-23; E34).

Trigger, target

The position where the task applies and the token taught there.

Unmasked model

Every piece fully on, Δ out. Says whether the pieces alone reproduce the model.

VPD

Goodfire's parameter decomposition method (Bushnaq, Braun, Clive-Griffin et al., 2026), used unchanged.

W0, W + δW, D, D′

Original weights, fine-tuned weights, released decomposition, mapped decomposition.

Weak and strong versions

Weak: D′ is faithful to the updated model with Δ not absorbing the update. Strong: D′ matches a fresh re-decomposition. Only the weak version is in play.

Windows

41-token Pile contexts holding one trigger, split into training and held-out sets.

K(M), what the subcomponents can express

K(M) = UUᵀM + MVVᵀ is the weight change from moving the subcomponents by dU = M V, dV = Mᵀ U: the part of M they can express, at a size that is not M's. M is the gradient in component training and δW in the adjoint splits. One plain-SGD step of component training changes the weights by −ηK(G), the adjoint split of the fine-tune's own step −ηG. The adjoint split applies K once, to a finished update, so what K distorts stays; component training applies it to each step's gradient at the current weights, so later steps correct it. Under Adam, component training's step is not a multiple of K(G).

Interaction share, i

The part of the route split's gain from the moved subcomponents and Γ's new masks acting together, beyond what each gives alone. The four readings' re-wire cell. Within 0.04 of zero in every run.

Four readings: strengthen, re-wire, create, re-weight

Where an update's gain can sit, on two axes (section {{n:techniques}}). Strengthen: the moved subcomponents carry it at the old masks, measured by d. Re-wire: moved subcomponents that Γ also switches differently, measured by i. Create: the subcomponents and masks as before, so the gain is in Δ or in subcomponents Γ keeps off, measured by eΔ and egate. Re-weight: the subcomponents as before, Γ switches them on differently, measured by r; the re-weighting hypothesis in VPD terms. Fixed on 2026-10-20 so that the readings have names; revisable. Not the same list as the 2026-10-27 decision's four readings of "understand re-gating" (recruitment test, routing trace, piece census, refit agreement), three of which ran as E38's stages.

Controls

Every ratio has a floor measured at the unchanged model in the same job. Every plan names what each metric is blind to and a control that must fail or must read zero: gated-off component training for the route split (must read d = 0); the large Adam fine-tune left in Δ, or its least-squares split, for the adversarial check (must fail; small fine-tunes and LoRAs pass, so they are not failing controls); do-nothing under the original masks for trigger-local faithfulness.
