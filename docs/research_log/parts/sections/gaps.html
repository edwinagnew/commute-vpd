What a critical reader should raise

Gaps and inconsistencies

The criterion and the comparisons

The pass line has no anchor

"About as well" is read as an adversarial ratio under 1.5, a line inherited from E2's filing. On the L40 the unchanged model's own floor reached 21%, so the line sits inside the noise band and the two stated rules disagree there (1.5 against 1.63). Two of seven ladder verdicts sit on the rules (E11): " we" then " just" straddles the line across seeds, and " will" then " have" passes the line but sits just over its card's floor.

The reference fails the pass check

Do-nothing changes nothing in the decomposition and still fails the adversarial check on the large Adam fine-tunes (2.18 on emoticon) and on every broad fine-tune read on Pile rows 0-255 (the only rows read for do-nothing), because the adversary can mask Δ and Δ now holds the update; small fine-tunes' do-nothing passes (E21, E22, E39). So the 1.5 line mixes how much of the update sits outside the pieces with whether the pieces are fragile, and every adversarial verdict should be read against the same update's do-nothing while the criterion is anchored on the ratio to the unchanged decomposition, with do-nothing reported beside every verdict (decided 2026-10-29).

"Δ not absorbing δW" has no threshold

The split LoRA's residual share (0.22 to 0.31 of the behaviour, half the energy) is judged by feel. The split fine-tune's 0.09 was read as acceptable, 0.22 as "the open reading". GOALS fixes no number.

Component training is not "the same fine-tune"

Adam on the pieces at another learning rate, half the weight movement, and on the ladder tasks about twice the off-target damage at the same step, which the adversarial excess orders with. On emoticon the sign flips (a third of the damage). Unexplained.

Measurement

Γ reads the text after the trigger, and we feed it 41 tokens

The 67M Γ attends in both directions and was trained on 512-token rows, so on 41-token windows its value at the trigger depends on the 20 tokens after it and on the window's short length; a code comment said otherwise. Comparisons between models probably stand (the change in Γ's values comes almost entirely from the trigger's own inputs; E32's ceiling reads 1.00 to 1.04), but absolute window levels and rung-0 retained shares are exposed. One pass of at most 30 minutes on saved windows would settle it. Qwen's Γ is causal, so it does not carry over. reports/reviews/2026-10-27_review_gamma-bidirectional-windows.md

Nothing measures faithfulness at the trigger with power

Pile checks see the old model's account; the task-local adversary saturates. On a cipher the model lacked, the CI-masked model's probability at the trigger shows what the Pile checks miss (E42), and it now counts as the behaviour kept by the parts Γ switches on, on the task's own text (decided 2026-10-29); its reference, the unchanged decomposition's own masked fidelity on cipher windows, is still to be read.

The refit's adversary is weaker than the harness's attacker

Both refit recipes take the released Γ off its own adversarial floor on the unchanged model, and the refit's attacker plateaus at 0.42 nats where the harness's reaches 0.70. Which one the released run's adversary resembled is not on disk, so every refit arm is unreadable until a matched attacker exists (E15).

The same state reads differently across runs

The fine-tune's step-10 least-squares state read 5.24 on an RTX A5000, 6.96 on two H100 runs and 5.41 on the L40 (E14, E15, E16, E19). Whether the card or the re-run differs is not established.

Four checks, not five

The sparsity loss follows the weights only while Γ is frozen. Reading A was recorded as five checks; the correction is proposed and not yet in the decisions file.

Seeds

E9: one LoRA seed. E10: one bijection and one adversary seed per state. E5: optimiser-seed noise on emoticon only. Component training on r0, r1 and r2 now has two batch orders, which agree (E26).

Sparse or SGD component training never tried off emoticon

E4 found sparse component training faithful (0.98 at the trigger against 0.64 for the diffuse arm); E11's failures are all the diffuse Adam arm. Plain SGD has since run on the ladder and the cipher (E21); the sparse arms have not.

The adversary's masks are not shown worst-case

Every adversarial verdict reads VPD's check after 20 sign steps. Longer searches read the same (E20, E23), but the masks found are not the worst possible: keeping the alive block whole beats the 20-step search at the original model (1.08 against 0.66 nats), and on a fine-tune left in Δ the reading depends on the random start, 1.6 from one and 9.2 from another (E34). The user kept the paper's one start and 20 steps (2026-10-27), so every reading is a lower bound; the row set also matters (E39 Amendment 3).

Most failures rest on two large updates

E14 to E20 measured their failures on an Adam 3e-5 fine-tune and on Adam component training at step 10. A fine-tune 4.7 times smaller passes (E21), and small fine-tunes were tested since (E22 to E25).

Re-gating's share is not a measure of learning

The share reads 0.51 to 0.61 for every arm including nulls (E6), 0.82 for a fine-tune that made held-out text worse (E36), and 0.12 to 0.80 across tasks (E10). It is set by what the existing gates can route at a given trigger and format, not by whether anything was learned.

Re-gating has one definition now, and no vetted measure

Since 2026-10-28 the glossary defines re-gating once (what Γ says differently on the fine-tuned model). No measure of it has passed its nulls: the share reads the same for a non-learning update (E36); the switched-on set's members already push toward the target at the original model (E38); and the decisive leak tests (Γ's change applied out of context, a Γ from another VPD seed, a Γ refitted on the fine-tuned model) never ran. "Reveal" and "create" readings therefore rest on the route split, calibrated on E12's anchors, not on a mechanism. The reset plan's stage 0 is the first attempt at a vetted measure.
