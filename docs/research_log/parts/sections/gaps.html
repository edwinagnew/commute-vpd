What a critical reader should raise

Gaps and inconsistencies

Commute: the criterion

The pass line has no anchor

"About as well" is read as an adversarial ratio under 1.5, a line inherited from E2's filing. On the L40 the unchanged model's own floor reached 21%, so the line sits inside the noise band and the two stated rules disagree there (1.5 against 1.63). Two of seven ladder verdicts sit on the rules (E11).

Commute: measurement

The 67M's trigger-level measurements are weak

Its Γ attends both ways and sees 41-token windows (absolute levels exposed, E32); no check measures faithfulness at the trigger with power (E42); and the refit's attacker is weaker than the harness's, so refit arms are unreadable (E15).

The adversary's masks are not shown worst-case

Every adversarial verdict reads VPD's check after 20 sign steps from one start. Longer searches read the same (E20, E23), but on a fine-tune left in Δ the start matters, 1.6 against 9.2 (E34): every reading is a lower bound.

Reuse against new (parts 1 to 3)

Every label is by construction

"Reuse" and "new" sides are so because of how each pair was made. No definition is adopted, its cut-offs do not label real fine-tunes (E142), and "built" has never been read, so every comparison measures agreement with the builder's intent.

The re-gating share and the prior are not yet told apart

The original model's probability for the trained answer agrees with the share on every pair where both were read; two pairs and an untested prior-plus-steering combination are the only separation (E133). The share also reads high for a fine-tune that learned nothing (E36).

Both shares read outputs, not mechanism

The re-weighting share drops when a fine-tune copies a change imperfectly (E139), and the re-gating share is read in VPD's masked stand-in, which the fine-tuned model does not contain. Only the reversion looks inside, and it disagrees with the share on the imitators.

Part 2 is unchecked

Every E133 column is provisional, with no independent check, and the share was never computed on the four 96-key pairs' neuron and untrained-decomposition columns.
