What a critical reader should raise

Gaps and inconsistencies

The criterion and the comparisons

The pass line has no anchor

"About as well" is read as an adversarial ratio under 1.5, a line inherited from E2's filing. On the L40 the unchanged model's own floor reached 21%, so the line sits inside the noise band and the two stated rules disagree there (1.5 against 1.63). Two of seven ladder verdicts sit on the rules (E11): " we" then " just" straddles the line across seeds, and " will" then " have" passes the line but sits just over its card's floor.

The reference fails the pass check

Do-nothing changes nothing in the decomposition and still fails the adversarial check on the large Adam fine-tunes (2.18 on emoticon) and on every broad fine-tune, because the adversary can mask Δ and Δ now holds the update; small fine-tunes' do-nothing passes (E21, E22, E39). So the 1.5 line mixes how much of the update sits outside the pieces with whether the pieces are fragile, and every adversarial verdict should be read against the same update's do-nothing until the criterion has an anchor (pending decision).

"Δ not absorbing δW" has no threshold

The split LoRA's residual share (0.22 to 0.31 of the behaviour, half the energy) is judged by feel. The split fine-tune's 0.09 was read as acceptable, 0.22 as "the open reading". GOALS fixes no number.

Component training is not "the same fine-tune"

Adam on the pieces at another learning rate, half the weight movement, and on the ladder tasks about twice the off-target damage at the same step, which the adversarial excess orders with. On emoticon the sign flips (a third of the damage). Unexplained.

One optimiser behind every result

From E4 to E20 every component-training run used Adam, so those failures were Adam's until SGD was tried (Q11): the verdicts move with the rate and the step as well as the optimiser (E21, E26). Where it stands is in section {{n:next}}.

Step or run

The least-squares split is applied one-shot to a 10-step update, a first-order map on a non-small change; only component training matches the "continuous" framing. The per-step split, E19, matches it too and does worse than the one-shot least-squares split, so the step-or-run question is answered for the adjoint form only. Along small fine-tunes the step split passes the adversary but still leaves more behaviour in Δ than least squares, except on SGD at step 1,000 (E25).

Measurement

Γ reads the text after the trigger, and we feed it 41 tokens

The 67M Γ attends in both directions and was trained on 512-token rows, so on 41-token windows its value at the trigger depends on the 20 tokens after it and on the window's short length; a code comment said otherwise. Comparisons between models probably stand (the change in Γ's values comes almost entirely from the trigger's own inputs; E32's ceiling reads 1.00 to 1.04), but absolute window levels and rung-0 retained shares are exposed. One pass of at most 30 minutes on saved windows would settle it. Qwen's Γ is causal, so it does not carry over. reports/reviews/2026-10-27_review_gamma-bidirectional-windows.md

Nothing measures faithfulness at the trigger with power

Pile checks see the old model's account; the task-local adversary saturates. On a cipher the model lacked, the CI-masked model's probability at the trigger shows what the Pile checks miss (E42), but it has no reference or line yet, and whether it should count is a pending decision.

The refit's adversary is weaker than the harness's attacker

Both refit recipes take the released Γ off its own adversarial floor on the unchanged model, and the refit's attacker plateaus at 0.42 nats where the harness's reaches 0.70. Which one the released run's adversary resembled is not on disk, so every refit arm is unreadable until a matched attacker exists (E15).

The same state reads differently across runs

The fine-tune's step-10 least-squares state read 5.24 on an RTX A5000 (E14), 6.96 on two H100 runs (E15, E16) and 5.41 on the L40 (E19); its do-nothing state 2.29 against 2.52. That failed E16's reproduction gate. Whether the card or the re-run differs is not established; the test is one saved update read on both cards.

The half-revert statistic has no functional positive control

E17's control rewrote component training's change as cancelling pieces with 64 times the energy in weight space, and the half revert moved only from 0.31 to 0.45: directions a training step did not choose are nearly inert on Pile, so weight-space and functional cancellation come apart. The half-revert readings stand as readings; a control that cancels in effect, not in norm, is still to be built. The adversary's own revert does not depend on it.

Component training's inside-the-pieces control, to second order

E16's control expected component training's change to lie inside the pieces; rank-8 at step 1000 put 0.0119 outside both sides against a limit of 0.01. The expected source, the second-order term dU dVᵀ, was not computed, and the filed alternative, a bug in the block code or the training hook, was not checked.

Four checks, not five

The sparsity loss follows the weights only while Γ is frozen. Reading A was recorded as five checks; the correction is proposed and not yet in the decisions file.

Seeds

E9: one LoRA seed. E10: one bijection, one adversary seed per state, and E7's floor from another card. E5: optimiser-seed noise on emoticon only. The Δ-share rise at the plateau and the 32-pair failure rest on single runs. Component training on r0, r1 and r2 now has two batch orders, which agree (E26).

Sparse or SGD component training never tried off emoticon

E4 found one-, ten- and six-piece component training faithful, with CI-masked probability 0.98 at the trigger against 0.64 for the diffuse arm; E11's failures are all for the diffuse Adam arm. A cheap, untested question. Plain SGD has since run on the ladder and the cipher (E21); the sparse arms still have not.

The adversary's masks are not shown worst-case

Every adversarial verdict reads VPD's check after 20 sign steps. With the paper's step rule the search barely grows to 160 steps, and every verdict reads the same there (E20, E23). But the masks found are not the worst possible: keeping the alive block whole beats the 20-step search at the original model (1.08 against 0.66 nats), and on a fine-tune left in Δ the reading depends on the random start, 1.6 from one and 9.2 from another (E34, Amendment 5). The user decided on 2026-10-27 to keep the paper's one start and 20 steps, so every reading is a lower bound.

Most failures rest on two large updates

E14 to E20 measured their failures on an Adam 3e-5 fine-tune and on Adam component training at step 10. A fine-tune 4.7 times smaller passes (E21), small fine-tunes were tested since (E22, E25, E23, E24), and broad fine-tunes fail at small sizes too (E39). Untested beyond the large update: E15's refit.

Two readings of re-switching

E6: the share is "not target-specific" (0.51 to 0.61 for every arm, nulls included). E10: it runs 0.12 to 0.80 across tasks. Reconciled as: set by what the existing gates can route at a given trigger and format, not by which target is learned there. Easy to trip on.
