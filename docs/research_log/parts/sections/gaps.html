What a critical reader should raise

Gaps and inconsistencies

The criterion and the comparisons

The pass line has no anchor

"About as well" is read as an adversarial ratio under 1.5, a line inherited from E2's filing. On the L40 the unchanged model's own floor reached 21%, so the line sits inside the noise band and the two stated rules disagree there (1.5 against 1.63). Two of seven ladder verdicts sit on the rules (E11).

The reference fails the pass check

Do-nothing changes nothing in the decomposition and still fails the adversarial check on the large Adam fine-tunes (2.18 on emoticon) and every broad fine-tune, because the adversary can mask Δ (E21, E22, E39). So the 1.5 line mixes how much of the update sits outside the pieces with how fragile they are; the criterion is now the ratio to the unchanged decomposition, do-nothing beside every verdict (decided 2026-10-29).

"Δ not absorbing δW" has no threshold

The split LoRA's residual share (0.22 to 0.31 of the behaviour, half the energy) is judged by feel. The split fine-tune's 0.09 was read as acceptable, 0.22 as "the open reading". GOALS fixes no number.

Measurement

The 67M's trigger-level measurements are weak

Its Γ attends both ways and sees 41-token windows (absolute levels exposed, E32); no check measures faithfulness at the trigger with power (E42); and the refit's attacker is weaker than the harness's, so refit arms are unreadable (E15).

The adversary's masks are not shown worst-case

Every adversarial verdict reads VPD's check after 20 sign steps from one start. Longer searches read the same (E20, E23), but on a fine-tune left in Δ the start matters, 1.6 against 9.2 (E34), so every reading is a lower bound.

The record

Re-gating has one definition now, and no vetted measure

No measure of re-gating has passed its nulls: the share reads 0.51 to 0.61 for every arm including nulls (E6) and the same for a non-learning update (E36) and, on Qwen, for random changes (E57; its gates differ from the 67M's); the switched-on set's members already push toward the target at the original model (E38). "Reveal" and "create" readings rest on the route split, not on a mechanism.
