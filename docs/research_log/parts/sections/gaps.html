What a critical reader should raise

Gaps and inconsistencies

The criterion and the comparisons

The pass line has no anchor

"About as well" is read as an adversarial ratio under 1.5, a line inherited from E2's filing. On the L40 the unchanged model's own floor reached 21%, so the line sits inside the noise band and the two stated rules disagree there (1.5 against 1.63). Two of seven ladder verdicts sit on the rules (E11).

The reference fails the pass check

Do-nothing changes nothing in the decomposition and still fails the adversarial check on the large Adam fine-tunes (2.18 on emoticon) and every broad fine-tune on Pile rows 0-255, because the adversary can mask Δ (E21, E22, E39). So the 1.5 line mixes how much of the update sits outside the pieces with how fragile they are; the criterion is now the ratio to the unchanged decomposition, do-nothing beside every verdict (decided 2026-10-29).

"Δ not absorbing δW" has no threshold

The split LoRA's residual share (0.22 to 0.31 of the behaviour, half the energy) is judged by feel. The split fine-tune's 0.09 was read as acceptable, 0.22 as "the open reading". GOALS fixes no number.

Component training is not "the same fine-tune"

Adam on the pieces at another learning rate, half the weight movement, and on the ladder tasks about twice the off-target damage at the same step. On emoticon the sign flips (a third). Unexplained.

Measurement

Γ reads the text after the trigger, and we feed it 41 tokens

The 67M Γ attends in both directions and was trained on 512-token rows, so on 41-token windows its value at the trigger depends on the 20 tokens after it. Comparisons between models probably stand (E32's ceiling reads 1.00 to 1.04); absolute window levels are exposed. reports/reviews/2026-10-27_review_gamma-bidirectional-windows.md

Nothing measures faithfulness at the trigger with power

Pile checks see the old model's account; the task-local adversary saturates. On a cipher the model lacked, the CI-masked model's probability at the trigger shows what the Pile checks miss (E42); its reference, the unchanged decomposition's masked fidelity on cipher windows, is still to be read.

The refit's adversary is weaker than the harness's attacker

Both refit recipes take the released Γ off its adversarial floor on the unchanged model, and the refit's attacker plateaus at 0.42 nats where the harness's reaches 0.70, so every refit arm is unreadable until a matched attacker exists (E15).

The same state reads differently across runs

The step-10 least-squares state read 5.24 on an RTX A5000, 6.96 on two H100 runs and 5.41 on the L40; whether the card or the re-run differs is not established.

Seeds

E9: one LoRA seed. E10: one bijection and one adversary seed per state. E5: optimiser-seed noise on emoticon only.

Sparse component training never tried off emoticon

E4 found it faithful (0.98 at the trigger against 0.64 for the diffuse arm); plain SGD has since run on the ladder and the cipher (E21), the sparse arms have not.

The adversary's masks are not shown worst-case

Every adversarial verdict reads VPD's check after 20 sign steps. Longer searches read the same (E20, E23), but on a fine-tune left in Δ the reading depends on the random start, 1.6 from one and 9.2 from another (E34). The paper's one start and 20 steps were kept (2026-10-27), so every reading is a lower bound.

Re-gating's share is not a measure of learning

The share reads 0.51 to 0.61 for every arm including nulls (E6), 0.82 for a fine-tune that made held-out text worse (E36), and 0.12 to 0.80 across tasks (E10). It is set by what the existing gates can route at a given trigger and format, not by whether anything was learned.

The record

Lost or unsaved data

E2's states never saved; E5's for emoticon only; E7's final results file rebuilt from the log; E8's read-side jobs have no result file. E14's per-subcomponent updates were deleted with the pod; the split stage is to be rerun.

Re-gating has one definition now, and no vetted measure

No measure of re-gating has passed its nulls: the share reads the same for a non-learning update (E36) and, on Qwen, for random changes (E57); the switched-on set's members already push toward the target at the original model (E38). "Reveal" and "create" readings rest on the route split, not on a mechanism.
