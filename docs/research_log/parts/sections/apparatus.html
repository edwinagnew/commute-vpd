The fixed apparatus

Model and decomposition

Model
Goodfire's released 4-layer Pile transformer (67M parameters) and their released VPD decomposition, via the pinned param-decomp package at commit 74146b5. A testbed.
Decomposed
24 weight matrices: the attention projections and two MLP matrices in each of four blocks; everything else frozen.
Pieces
About 38,900 rank-one subcomponents, about 9,900 of them alive (ever important). Four matrices (the MLP matrices of layers 0 and 3) are saturated: any update to them is expressible by construction.
Γ
One network shared across all 24 matrices. It reads every matrix's input at every position and outputs an importance value in [0, 1] per piece per position, so an edit in layer 2 moves importance values in layer 0. Its attention is not causal. Frozen in every map so far.

Updates

Fine-tune
Adam, learning rate 3e-5, batch 32, on the 24 decomposed matrices, with a KL penalty to the original model at the non-trigger positions.
LoRA
Learning rate 1e-3. Rank 8 on all 24 matrices (E8, E9), or ranks 1 to 32 on the paper's one matrix (E7).
Component training
The alive pieces' vectors trained with Adam (plain SGD only in E4 and E21, Q11), Γ and Δ frozen. Learning rate 1e-5 whenever all alive pieces train; the restricted variants picked theirs between 3e-5 and 1e-3.
Update size
Largest per-matrix ratio of update norm to weight norm, about 0.01.

Checks

Pile checks (VPD's own)
  • Stochastic-mask KL: random masks between each piece's importance value and 1.
  • Adversarial-mask KL (PGD): 20 sign-gradient steps of a mask shared across a 128-row block. The check that fails where others do not.
  • CI-masked KL: every piece at its importance value, Δ out.
  • Unmasked KL: every piece fully on, Δ out.
  • Sparsity loss: VPD's importance-minimality loss.

Pile validation rows 0 to 255, each number a ratio to the unchanged model in the same job. The adversarial verdict is one line, inherited from E2: a ratio under 1.5 passes. "Within the floor" means at most 1 plus three times the adversary's seed spread at the unchanged model; between that band and 1.5 it reads "at the line". On an L40 the band can reach 1.63, so the line is called unanchored (section {{n:gaps}}). At 160 steps every ratio tried moves by a tenth or less (E20).

Task-local checks
  • The target's log-odds gain over the original model at the trigger, on held-out windows.
  • The route split: which of five routes carries that gain (section {{n:techniques}}).
  • The off-target KL: KL to the original model at the other 40 positions of the window.

Protocol

Predictions
Filed before any GPU run, with rule and threshold, never edited afterwards; corrections are dated reports. Verdicts: pass, fail, null, neither.
Compute
MATS cluster L40s first, RunPod otherwise. The adversary's seed spread depends on the GPU (about 4% on a 4090 or L40S, up to 21% on an L40), so adversarial ratios are compared only within one card.
