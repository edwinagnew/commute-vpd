What's next

The project is now one paper in three parts, done one after another. First a working definition of when a fine-tune reuses something the model already had; then, on the small 67M model, a comparison of ways to measure that; then the key findings repeated on the larger Qwen3-1.7B. You decide from one page at each gate: on 2026-11-13 whether to adopt the definition, on 2026-11-18 the comparison and what re-gating measures, and on 2026-11-25 the larger-model table.
