Is component training's failure the operation's or Adam's?

What. Q11, and through it Q1: compare SGD and Adam on the same tasks, both as component training's optimiser and as the fine-tune optimiser whose update the splits consume. Report: reports/analyses/2026-10-22_analysis_sgd-vs-adam.md.

Where it stands. Neither the optimiser nor the size decides alone (E21). A small fine-tune passes under do-nothing and the least-squares split whichever optimiser made it. Adam component training passes at steps 100 and 1,000 on three tasks, while SGD fails the two word tasks at step 1,000 (E26, E23). The scaled adjoint split keeps less of an SGD update's behaviour as it grows (E31). Teacher training works with plain SGD at every smaller rate and step tried (E47).
