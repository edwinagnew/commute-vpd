Is component training's failure the operation's or Adam's?

What. Q11, and through it Q1, a priority subgoal since 2026-10-21 (user): compare SGD and Adam on the same tasks, both as component training's optimiser and as the fine-tune optimiser whose update the splits consume. Report: reports/analyses/2026-10-22_analysis_sgd-vs-adam.md.
