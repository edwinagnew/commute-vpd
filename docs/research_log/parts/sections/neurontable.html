Cheaper methods beside the decomposition

Baselines

Why baselines. The re-gating share needs a trained decomposition, which is expensive. Each baseline here is a cheaper explanation of what the share picks up: if a baseline separates reuse from new as well, the decomposition adds nothing. All of them have been run on the 67M's built pairs; the card E133 holds one short block per baseline (what it asks, how, what it found, its caveat, where the report is). Labels are as built, so none of this is a verdict on reuse.

  • Which updates pass the least-squares split's faithfulness table (E93) is ordered by update size, by gain and by the carrying-neuron share, and not by VPD's share left in Δ or by steering.
