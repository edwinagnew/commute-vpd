Does training TOFU's invented facts in five wordings make Qwen3-1.7B answer them when asked a rephrased question it never saw?

2026-11-04 · cluster L40 ($0) for the unchanged model and the wordings, RunPod H100 (about $1.81 and $5.40) for training and reads · experiments/qwen_tofu_forms · ended, provisional (no independent check)

The question. The Qwen comparison needs one update that teaches content the model lacks (anchor 2). E81 fine-tuned on TOFU's question-answer pairs and the model repeated trained answers but gave little of them back when asked in other words. You chose to try training each fact in several wordings, as Allen-Zhu and Li report helps.

What we did. We took the 800 TOFU rows that carry a held rephrased question (40 invented authors), wrote four more wordings of each question and answer, and kept 787 rows, five wordings each. We fine-tuned for five epochs, then ten as the plan's one amendment, and read greedy answers on three sets: TOFU's own rephrased question, never trained; the trained wording; and 400 rows about 20 authors never trained, as a floor. The score is new-word recall, the share of the reference answer's words absent from the question that the answer contains. The filed test called it answered when rephrased recall reached halfway from the floor to the trained wording.

What we found. Both runs missed. Rephrased recall read 0.532 after 5 epochs and 0.554 after 10, against a line of 0.602, while the trained wording read 0.993 and 1.000 and the never-trained authors 0.21 and 0.20. That is above E81's 0.377, but E84 also holds fewer facts, sees each five times as often and ran on another card, so the rise is not credited to wording alone. Doubling the training moved rephrased recall by only 0.02. Read by hand, many rephrased answers give the right fact in other words and some are confident inventions; that is an impression, not a count.

What it means. This update holds new facts memorised and carries them only partly to rephrasings; no cause is named for the gap. It stands at the filed label "not answered" with the plan's amendment used, so what stands as anchor 2 was your choice. You chose the 5-epoch update, labelled "new facts memorised, carrying only partly to rephrasings"; a rule filed beforehand then moved the label (E88). The unchanged model was never read on the H100, so no comparison across the two cards has a same-card control.
