On Qwen3-1.7B, do two cheap readings of a saved update tell reinforcement learning from its null, or one maths pool from another?

2026-11-12 · cell P3.6, 2 of 2 runs used (E162 run 1, E166 run 2), provisional, no independent check · cluster job 59473, $0 · experiments/qwen_cheap_readings

The question. Part 4 repeats the 67M comparison of cheap readings with the decomposition's readings on Qwen (section {{n:next}}, cell P3.6). Two readings need no decomposition at all. Applied to the 19 saved Qwen3-1.7B updates, can either tell the reinforcement-learning run from its shuffled-reward null, or the maths pool the unchanged model passed from the pool it never solved?

What we did. The effective rank counts how many directions an update spreads over (1 for a rank-one change, k for k equal singular values). The top-set overlap takes the 480 most strongly firing MLP neurons (7.8 per cent of the width) at each answer position of each block and asks how many stay on top after the update (1 means all, 0 means none). Each reading sits beside a random change of the update's own size per matrix, the floor, which is the mean of four draws for the overlap as you chose on 2026-11-12 after run 1's two draws missed their filed tolerance. A contrast counts when the gap between two sides' means exceeds the wider side's seed range, on two seeds a side.

What we found. Only the effective rank separates an arm contrast: RL reads 1,362 against the null's 1,381, a gap of 1.5 seed ranges, and the passed pool reads 1,313 against 1,357. The overlap separates neither (0.03 and 0.01 seed ranges; RL and null both 0.89). Yet all 19 updates keep fewer top neurons than their random floor, for example RL 0.89 against 0.997 and the pools 0.62 against 0.979, and all 19 are lower in rank than a random change (1,058 to 1,443 against about 1,815).

What it means. A real update moves the top-firing neurons far more than a random one of the same size, as on the 67M model, but the overlap does not see the reward: RL and its null move the top set alike. It is a description, not evidence of reuse, and no Qwen update is labelled reuse or new. The pools differ in update size by about 5 per cent, so their gaps may follow size; the premise check said doubtful for this reason.
