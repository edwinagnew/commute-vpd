Does the reuse score read the built labels at the base-only sparse-autoencoder latents?

2026-11-21 · cell P1.40 run 2 of 2, sae-lead, no independent check · experiments/sae_r_a

The question. Under the second wording of reuse (wording A), the score R is the share of a fine-tune's circuit that can take the original model's mechanism, each part times one positive gain, and keep the behaviour within a tenth of how far the fine-tune moved it. E242 asked this at VPD's subcomponents; this asks it at the base-only sparse autoencoders (SAEs) of E234, a baseline featurization. Two rivals: a new edit keeps its lookup where the original mechanism cannot write it, so it reads below every reuse edit (rival a); or the SAE puts everything it lacks into one remainder variable, so a new edit reads as high as reuse (rival b).

What we did. On the attention-output and MLP-output SAEs we read R for six hand-built edits: four reuse edits (a two-fold and a ten-fold edit, a moved one and a rebuilt circuit) and two new edits (a few-neuron and a thin lookup). Each item was read on two halves of its task windows, and the split-half difference sets the noise band (0.11).

What we found. R reads 0.999 for the two-fold edit, 0.85 for the ten-fold edit and 0.69 for the rebuilt circuit, against 0.57 for the thin new edit and 0.52 for the few-neuron one; the moved edit changes no behaviour, so it is not read. The order holds, and the gap between the lowest reuse item and the highest new one is 0.12 against a band of 0.11, so rival a holds, narrowly. Every search hit its cap of 2,000 evaluations, so all scores are lower bounds. The share of the behaviour left in the remainder is 0.89 on the two-fold reuse edit too, so the remainder does not separate new from reuse.

The key tasks. We also read all 15 key-task fine-tunes (five tasks, three seeds each, every update scaled to one common size: copy, top, mid, a cipher and a prior-matched cipher). All 15, the cipher included, read R 0.989 to 0.999, so wording A at SAE latents calls every key task reused. The cipher reads lowest (task mean 0.9898, against 0.9982 to 0.9989 for the rest). The reason is that each must-keep set is mostly one remainder per site, so the score sits at its ceiling and cannot tell kinds of task apart.

What it means. At SAE latents the built labels come out in the expected order, but by a gap barely above the noise band and with capped searches, so this is a weak read. The built order rests on latents at untouched sites: over changed members only, the rebuilt circuit reads 0.220, below the thin new edit at 0.422 and the few-neuron one at 0.377.
