Does the reuse score read the built labels, the nine trained pairs and random updates at the base-only sparse-autoencoder latents?

2026-11-21/22 · cell P1.40 runs 2 and 3 (E251), sae-lead, no independent check · experiments/sae_r_a, experiments/sae_r_a_random

The question. Under the second wording of reuse (wording A), the score R is the share of a fine-tune's circuit that can take the original model's mechanism, each part times one positive gain, and keep the behaviour within a tenth of how far the fine-tune moved it. E242 asked this at VPD's subcomponents; this asks it at the base-only sparse autoencoders (SAEs) of E234, a baseline featurization. Two rivals: a new edit keeps its lookup where the original mechanism cannot write it, so it reads below every reuse edit (rival a); or the SAE puts everything it lacks into one remainder variable, so a new edit reads as high as reuse (rival b).

What we did. On the attention-output and MLP-output SAEs we read R for six hand-built edits (four reuse, two new; the split-half difference sets a noise band of 0.11), for 15 key-task fine-tunes, and for the 76 fine-tunes of E187's nine trained pairs, each pair's larger update scaled down to the other's size, the same fine-tunes E248 read at VPD. As a reference, E251 read one random update of each key fine-tune's size.

What we found. On the built edits R reads 0.999, 0.85 and 0.69 for three reuse edits against 0.57 and 0.52 for the two new ones; the gap of 0.12 is barely over the band, so rival a holds narrowly. On every trained fine-tune it reads near one: the 15 key tasks 0.989 to 0.999, and in the nine pairs the reuse side reads higher in 9 of 9 but by only 0.0001 to 0.030, all inside the band, where VPD separates the same nine pairs by 0.11 to 0.32, each outside its own band of 0.024. Random updates of the same size read 0.48 to 0.54, so near one is not automatic; but they move the output over a thousand times less than the fine-tunes, and every random search hit its cap.

What it means. At SAE latents the built labels come out in the expected order, barely, and the reading cannot sort trained fine-tunes into reused and new: a trained update's write outside the base dictionary is one remainder variable per site, so R is pinned near one. The remainder share does split the pairs seed by seed (reuse 0.54 to 0.85, new 0.34 to 0.50), so the new tasks do write more outside the dictionary; R does not see it. The built order itself rests on latents at untouched sites: over changed members only, the rebuilt circuit reads 0.220, below the thin new edit at 0.422 and the few-neuron one at 0.377. Still open: a random update moved as far as a key fine-tune.

Run
Six built items, fifteen key items, 76 pair fine-tunes and 17 random updates on E234's saved SAEs, one job per item (cluster jobs and eight H100 pods); scored by the cell's filed rule.
Found
  • Built edits in the expected order by 0.12 against a band of 0.11; over changed latents only the order holds too (0.220 against 0.422 and 0.377).
  • All 15 key items read 0.989 to 0.999 and the nine pairs' gaps are 0.0001 to 0.030, so R at SAE cannot sort trained fine-tunes; VPD separates all nine pairs by 0.11 to 0.32.
  • Random updates read 0.48 to 0.54, but they move the output over a thousand times less and every search was capped, so the random reading is a lower bound and not yet a floor.
