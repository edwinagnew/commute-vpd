Do the second wording's labels on the 17 old 67M tasks follow how the tasks were built?

2026-11-10 · provisional, no independent check, labels are provisional · cluster debug jobs 57762, 57772 to 57774 and RunPod pods commute-E142-1 to -6 · experiments/old_task_labels

The question. The working definition of reuse (part 1) is under revision, and the user adopted a second wording for testing. It worked on four hand-made changes whose label is known by construction. Do the labels it gives follow how the old 67M tasks were built, some to reuse something the model does, some to be new?

What we did. We took the saved fine-tunes of the 17 old tasks, two seeds a task, and read each with the blind search and the filed rule from the hand-made pairs. The search measures two shares of the change in outputs, where 0 is nothing explained and 1 is all of it: the re-weighting share (one 4-dimensional variable set by an affine map of its own value) and the rewiring share (the same variable set by a map of everything the original computed there). Both at or above their cut-offs reads re-weighted; rewiring only reads rewired; a rewiring share under its cut-off reads no map found, a limit of the search. Cut-offs 0.79 and 0.48 are copied from the hand-made pairs. Inputs were cut to 8 tokens.

What we found. The labels do not follow construction. Of 16 two-seed tasks, 11 read rewired, built to reuse or built to be new alike; only the real case label reads re-weighted, and the two text ciphers, lowercase and the emoticon read no map found. The re-weighting share itself does order every matched group by construction: reuse sides read 0.68 to 0.83, new sides 0.26 to 0.57. The filed cut-off sits above every reuse side but the case label, so the ordering does not become a label.

What it means. With these cut-offs the second wording does not yet separate strengthening from new capability on real tasks, though the share carries the construction. Moving the cut-off is a definition change only the user makes; you chose that the gate page reports the share within matched pairs and lists an absolute cut-off as open; adopting the wording is a card in section {{n:decisions}}. In 10 of 33 readings the found rewiring share is under the found re-weighting share, a search limit.

Run
A control first: a zero change through the loader moved no output, the loader matched the old evaluation path, and an earlier fine-tune re-read matched its shares to six decimals. Then 33 fine-tunes, 9 items on cluster debug and 24 on six RunPod pods; cost not recorded in the report.
Found
  • Labels as filed: 11 of 16 two-seed tasks rewired, reuse and new alike; 12 of 17 task predictions hit, the summary point (labels do not follow construction) held.
  • The re-weighting share orders all four matched groups with the reuse side higher; the real case label and its twin are the only matched pair the label separates. Only the copy-against-lookup, case and repeat pairs were read on the same inputs; the key-table pairs were read on separate inputs drawn the same way.
  • Long-text tasks read on 8 tokens may explain the no-map-found labels; the emoticon's rewiring share is near zero beside a high re-weighting share, which the definition rules out.
