On trained fine-tunes, how much of each changed circuit part does the base model's own part explain?

2026-11-21 · cell P1.50 (filled), definition-lead, descriptive, no independent check · experiments/rb_states

The question. You chose to count reuse only over the circuit parts a fine-tune changed, each weighted by how much it changed (option b). On the hand-built edits that reading put the two pure strength edits at 0.76 and the new and random edits near 0.01. Does it still put reuse above new on real fine-tunes, and does it move with how the fine-tune was trained?

What we did. For each changed part of the circuit we asked what share of its change the base model's own part explains when only turned up or down. A random update reads about 0.011; the do-nothing reads 1. We read 142 trained updates of the 67M model: 94 supervised fine-tunes (nine task pairs, copy against lookup at their own sizes, five key tasks), 21 repeat-text updates trained by reinforcement learning or by supervised training, and 45 updates on addition, brackets and sequence tasks.

What we found. Every supervised fine-tune reads 0.015 to 0.062, near the random update and far below the built reuse edits at 0.63 to 0.76; the side built to be new reads higher in six of nine pairs. On repeated text reinforcement learning reads 0.17 (size-matched) and 0.11 (divergence-matched) against 0.016 to 0.021 for supervised twins and 0.037 for shuffled rewards. On the three procedural tasks every trained arm reads 0.037 to 0.075, reinforcement learning within 1.5 times supervised, and matched random updates read 0.009 to 0.013.

What it means. This reading does not reproduce on trained fine-tunes the order it gave on the built edits, so it cannot yet say which fine-tunes reuse the base model. It separates reinforcement learning from supervised training on repeated text but not on the procedural tasks, so what moves it is neither the pair label nor the training route alone. All 66 fresh searches for the changed parts hit their 2,000-evaluation cap, a limit on the repeat and procedural readings. No reuse or new verdict is given.
