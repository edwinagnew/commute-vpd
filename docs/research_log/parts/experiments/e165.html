Can we build fine-tune pairs of Qwen3-1.7B whose answer, reused or new, is known by construction?

2026-11-12 · cell P3.5, 3 of 3 runs used (E160 rehearsal, E164 second run, E165 third run), provisional, no independent check · about $70 (E164) and $37 (E165) · experiments/qwen_pair_library

The question. Part 4 reads Qwen3-1.7B fine-tunes with the decomposition (section {{n:next}}, cell P3.5), but a reading of a real fine-tune has no answer key. Can we make pairs of updates where one side only reuses what the model can already do and the other teaches something it cannot, so a later reading has a known answer to be scored against?

What we did. Two built pairs, each training the same inputs to two targets: copy a word against spell it through a fixed letter substitution (the cipher), and answer questions about real facts against questions about invented people. Two twins, each a task trained once with its real answers and once with the answers shuffled to other prompts: gold maths solutions and an earlier answer template. Three seeds a side, every update saved at two matched sizes and at its last step. The size is the output change, the KL divergence from the unchanged model to the fine-tuned one on 128 of the side's own rows, as a multiple of the decomposition's own error (the masked error). An accuracy of 0.9 on trained items counts as learned.

What we found. At the recipe of the third run (peak rate 1e-5, 300 steps), every built side learns its trained items by the last step. Copy then reads 1.00 on 256 unseen words on all three seeds, and cipher 0.01 to 0.02 with 1.00 on trained words, so the cipher was memorised word by word, which is the label we built. At the matched sizes, though, the new sides have not learned yet: cipher reads 0 at 5 and at 15 times the error, and invented facts read 0.04 to 0.05 at 5 times. At the last step the new side's weight change is about twice the reused side's.

What it means. Only the gold twin sets two learned updates beside each other at a matched size; the built pairs are matched where the new side has not learned, and learned where the sizes differ. The second run (peak 2e-6, 100 steps) taught no new side at all: the learning rate was too low, checked and not a bug. The cell's own premise check reads doubtful, since the "new" label describes the recipe and the readings of part 2, not the library, are what make no call. All of this goes to the 2026-11-18 gate; no decomposition reading of the library has been run.

Run
E164 trained the twins and a first built pair at the low rate on pods (about $70); E165 retrained both built pairs at 1e-5 for 300 steps, three seeds (about $37). The saved states of the third run are on W&B only and none has been pulled back.
Found
  • Copy against cipher splits as built: held-out gap 0.97 to 0.99 on all three seeds, far above the bar of 0.3; the facts pair does not split on its unseen phrasing, which tests recall across wordings rather than reuse.
  • Pairs fit to read on the filed rule: copy and cipher, facts, and the gold twin on all seeds; the template twin on none, as neither side reached 5 times in 100 steps.
  • Shuffled gold answers still cut held-out gold loss from 2.50 to 0.81 to 0.83 nats a token, against 0.51 for the real answers, so much of what the gold update learns is format.
