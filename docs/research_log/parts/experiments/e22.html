Do small fine-tunes stay faithful when placed at later steps?

2026-10-22 · RunPod H100 · experiments/small_finetune_splits

Yes, at every step. Two small emoticon fine-tunes, left in Δ or split over the subcomponents, read as faithful as the original model at steps 10, 100 and 1,000, even after the SGD one grew larger than the large Adam update that fails (first figure). No placement keeps everything: least squares leaves a quarter to two-fifths of the change in Δ, and the adjoint split loses behaviour, much less for SGD (second figure). The rise of the outside share along training is Adam's, at both sizes (third figure). So the fine-tune leg of "no frozen-Γ map stays faithful" rests on the one large Adam update. The SGD adjoint split's kept behaviour held on a second emoticon seed (0.57 against small Adam's 0.21 at step 1,000) but not on two word tasks, where an SGD fine-tune at a three times higher rate lost it like the large Adam one.

Run
Three emoticon fine-tunes of all 24 decomposed matrices, seed 0, 1,000 steps: SGD at rate 0.01 and Adam at 3e-6 (E21's matched small pair, size about 0.0023 at step 10, the largest relative change of any matrix) and the large Adam 3e-5 (0.0113). Each placed by do-nothing, the least-squares split and the scaled adjoint split at steps 10, 100 and 1,000, and read on the Pile adversarial ratio (256 rows; this card's floor gives pass up to 1.40, fail from 1.61), the held-out probability of the target and the share left in Δ. Along nine checkpoints, E14's outside-both and rescaling shares over a random update's. Checks passed: the original model at its known values, step 10 reproduced E21 and E16, the large series reproduced E14. Pod 2,267 s of run, $4.55.
Found
  • Every small-update state passes at every step, 0.98 to 1.10. The SGD fine-tune at step 1,000 passes at size 0.0122, past the large one's failing 0.0113 at step 10 and past E21's SGD size cut of 0.009.
  • The large update: do-nothing 2.52 at step 10 (fail), 1.14 at 100, 1.50 at 1,000 (inconclusive); least squares 6.96, 1.48 and 2.11. Every adjoint state passes.
  • Least squares leaves 0.25 to 0.30 of the change in Δ for SGD and 0.32 to 0.39 for Adam 3e-6, rising slowly with step.
  • Surprise: the adjoint split was filed to lose behaviour at each step. For SGD the target's probability rose from 0.71 to 0.75, then fell only to 0.61 at step 1,000, where Adam 3e-6 reads 0.24. Cause not worked out. On a second emoticon seed the same holds, 0.57 against 0.21, every state faithful with Δ fixed and the pass unchanged by an attack eight times longer. On " which" then " was" and " it" then " is", an SGD fine-tune at rate 0.03 loses it like the large Adam update (0.07 and 0.26 against 0.05 and 0.23); its update there grew nearly to the large one's size, so task and rate are not yet separated. The share of the update the split reproduces does not explain the difference.
  • Outside-both share over the random value: SGD stays at 0.03 to 0.06, against 0.17 to 0.40 for the large update. Adam 3e-6 rises to 0.38.
  • The rescaling share over the random value climbs 5.8-fold for the large update only (2.1 to 12.2); SGD ends at 2.2, Adam 3e-6 stays near 2.
