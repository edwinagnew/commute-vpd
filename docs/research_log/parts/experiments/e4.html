Concentration test, with two amendments

2026-10-15 · RunPod RTX 4090 · experiments/concentration_test

A handful of pieces can learn the behaviour, but under the original masks no arm's pieces carry it: everything runs through Γ switching existing pieces on.

Run
Component training with only some pieces free: all (Adam; plain SGD at rates 0.3 to 30), the top 1, 10 or 100 by first-order importance, the paper's six emoticon pieces. Amendment 1 re-read the trigger with Γ's masks fixed to the original model's. Amendment 2 asked which pieces switch on.
Found
  • One piece, ten, or the six siblings each learn with small off-target damage, Pile ratios at most 1.08, and CI-masked probability 0.98 at the trigger; the diffuse Adam arm reads 0.64, do-nothing 0.53.
  • Plain SGD on the vectors: no rate in the grid learned without large off-target damage, so the runs after this used Adam. Contradicted on 2026-10-21: the grid started above the working range (Follow-up, below).
  • Surprise: Γ's masks alone move the CI-masked model most of the way (do-nothing 0.53 against 0.004 at the original model).
  • Amendment 1: under the original masks the siblings reach 0.37 of the target, every other arm at most 0.19. Two filed predictions reversed.
  • Amendment 2: every update switches on largely the same 150 to 200 pre-existing pieces (Jaccard 0.59 to 0.88); the set carries about half the re-switching effect; each piece pushes weakly. First evidence for "reveal".
Bears on
Q5, Q7
Moved on
The trigger-local control had no power as filed; fixed by amendment 1. Re-switching was now known to dominate every CI-masked number, which forced the route split before the ladder could be scored.
Follow-up
2026-10-21 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/sgd_grid · reports/2026-10-21_analysis_sgd-grid.md. At rates from 0.003 to 0.1 plain SGD learns within the off-target limit (figure). Its update is still diffuse: reverting the top ten pieces removes 0.16 of the gain, so E4's P1 scores fail, as filed. Both numbers were measured at a smaller size of change than Adam's, so no comparison with Adam is drawn here. The matched comparison and the state's faithfulness ran in E21: the SGD state passes the Pile adversarial check, and at matched size Adam's change is about seven times more diffuse.
