E268mixed

Does the write share keep its readings when it is taken over the variables the definition calls needed, not over the mean-ablation circuit?

2026-11-23 · cell P1.54, definition-lead, red-team: holds with caveats · experiments/kstar_needed

The question. Every saved write share so far was taken over the smallest circuit that keeps the behaviour when everything else is mean-ablated. The frozen definition scores reuse over a different set, the needed variables: those whose value, swapped between two inputs, changes the output. This was the one gap between the write share and the definition, and the cell asked whether the built-edit order and the trained readings stand over the needed set (no verdict filed).

What we did. On the 73 saved states of the 67M model (seven built edits, 66 trained or random) we found each state's needed set by swapping one variable at a time between 256 sampled input pairs, then re-read the write share over it: the share of the changed variables' change that lies along the base model's own write at one positive gain. It reads 1 when every changed variable keeps the base model's mechanism up to a gain. Checks ran first: a variable swapped with itself changed no output (largest logit change 5.1e-5, the rounding floor) and do-nothing read 1.

What we found. The built-edit order is unchanged: the two gain edits read 0.761 over the needed set and at most 0.762 over the old set, the rebuilt edit 0.623 against 0.631, and the new and random edits 0.011 to 0.019 on both. Most trained readings stay within the 0.04 seed spread, but repeat-task reinforcement learning falls on five of its six states, and the ratio of its mean to supervised fine-tuning drops from 5.1 to 2.6 (KL-matched run) and from 10.8 to 9.3 (size-matched run).

What it means. The write share keeps the one ordering checked against edits with a known answer. The reinforcement-learning fall sits on the most confident states, whose needed sets are smallest and most under-sampled (one has 182 needed variables against 8,295 over the old set), so it is provisional and the paper should not quote it as a change in the reading. One collapsed addition state has an empty needed set and reads 1 vacuously, which is not a reading of reuse.

Run
Five RunPod pods on 2026-11-22, about $74, one run of four allowed; 256 sampled input pairs per state.
Found
  • The built-edit order stands over the needed set: gain edits 0.761, rebuilt 0.623, new and random edits 0.011 to 0.019.
  • Supervised, shuffled-reward and guarded reinforcement learning and every random update stay within the spread; repeat-task reinforcement learning falls, on the most under-sampled needed sets.
  • The needed set was still growing between 192 and 256 pairs on 71 of 73 states, so it is a lower bound; its size tracks how confident the model is (rank correlation -0.82 with the margin).
