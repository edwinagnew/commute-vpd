On Qwen3-1.7B, can any test under masks or the adversary tell a good placement of a fine-tuning update from a bad one?

2026-10-29 to 11-04 · MATS cluster L40s, $0; one RunPod A100, one RunPod H100 and two Vast H200 pods, about $10 in all · experiments/qwen_maps, qwen_pass_test, qwen_fixed_masks, qwen_diagnose, qwen_transmission, qwen_open_points, qwen_masked_teacher, qwen_masked_read, qwen_d1_adversary

The question. The commute line asks whether a finished fine-tuning update can be carried onto a decomposition's subcomponents so that the decomposition stays faithful and the changed model still does what the fine-tuned one does. On Qwen3-1.7B the first job was to find a test that can fail a bad placement of a real update and pass a good one. A test that passes everything, or nothing, cannot grade a placement. Nine jobs tried every masked reading we had, then masked training, then VPD's own adversary.

What we did. The decomposition writes each of Qwen's 196 weight matrices as a sum of subcomponents plus a leftover, Δ. Its importance function, Γ, reads activations and sets a mask per token: how much each subcomponent is switched on. The masked model runs on the masked subcomponents with Δ off. Nothing was trained except in one job. We took saved updates (mainly ten steps of fine-tuning on maths answers, plus a gold-solutions and a template fine-tune) and placed each in several ways. Do-nothing leaves the whole update in Δ and must fail. The often-on and rarely-on halves put it on the half of each matrix's subcomponents Γ switches on often, or rarely. A random change of the update's size is a second bad placement. The least-squares and adjoint splits are the two closed-form maps. We scored each by its distance (KL per token) from the fine-tuned model, under masks Γ makes on the original model, on the fine-tuned model or on the state itself. We traced where a change is lost in the masked forward. We trained the subcomponents against the fine-tuned model with a masked term in the loss. Last, we ran VPD's adversary, which searches for the masks that hurt a state most; it passes at 1.40 times the unchanged decomposition's damage or less.

What we found. No test here works. If Γ reads each state's own activations, its masks rebuild the update, so do-nothing passes (first figure). If the masks come from the original model, they fail bad placements but pass nothing: even the whole update added unmasked closes 0.03 to 0.14 of the gap, against 0.85 needed (second figure). A change is lost at the masks at and after it, whatever the change; masking 12 matrices instead of all 196 lets far more through (third figure). The clean model is not to blame: a random change of the update's size moves it at most a two-thousandth as much as the update does. Training with a masked term did lower the masked distance, from 1.33 to 0.28, but at 3.4 times the update's squared size (fourth figure). Under each state's own masks, the masks did nearly all the work: do-nothing read lowest of all (fifth figure). The adversary passed all four maps, do-nothing included, on two seeds whose spread (18%) covers every gap.

What it means. On this update, every masked reading either passes do-nothing or cannot pass the whole update, and the adversary passes everything. So no placement on Qwen was graded under masks, and nothing here bears for or against the weak version. Why the masks drop a change is not shown; whether masking every matrix at once is the wrong forward stays open. The project moved D1's test to the clean model (E75). On 2026-11-09 the paper plan put further tests of the carrying map out of scope.

Found
  • Every masked reading of a placement either passes do-nothing (masks from the state's own activations) or cannot pass the whole update (masks from the original model), so none grades a placement.
  • Under all-matrix masks almost any change is lost at the masks at and after it, though the clean model responds strongly to the update; fewer masked matrices let much more through.
  • Masked teacher training lowers the masked distance at three times the update's squared size, and VPD's adversary passes all four maps, do-nothing included, inside its seed spread.
