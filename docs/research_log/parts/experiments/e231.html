Can strength alone do the built reuse tasks but not the built new ones?

2026-11-22 · cell P2.46 (filled), no independent check · experiments/gain_only

The question. The results section says that on built reuse pairs fine-tuning changes when and how strongly existing mechanisms act. Its literal test at the level of neurons and heads: can a change of strength alone do what each side's full fine-tune did?

What we did. We froze the 67M model and trained one multiplier per MLP neuron and one per attention head (12,288 and 24 over four layers), starting at 1, on each task of the nine built pairs and four real-against-twin pairs, three seeds, with the reference fine-tune's own data, loss and batch (Adam at 3e-3, as in IA3; the rate was not swept). Each state was exported as a weight change. We kept the state whose output change (KL to the original) first reached the full fine-tunes' mean, and report the reach: its gain on the trained answer over the full fine-tunes' mean gain. A second matching kept the state whose gain reached theirs. The control checked first: gains of 1 change nothing.

What we found. The control passed. We filed that reuse sides would reach at least half the full gain and new sides less than half on all but two of nine pairs; it held on three, with both sides reaching on four and neither on two, and on none of the four twins. New sides reached 0.65 to 0.74 on three pairs, beyond their reuse partners (copy maps 0.50 against random maps 0.65). At matched gain, eight of nine pairs had both sides past half, but only 9 of 81 states actually reached the gain, so that reading is confounded by the step cap. The log-odds share, not filed, still put every reuse seed above every new seed on all nine pairs (reuse 0.52 to 1.02, new 0.15 to 0.35).

What it means. At neurons and heads, strength alone reaches the new tasks about as well as the reuse tasks, so the literal "how strongly" reading does not tell them apart and the card's stop rule applies. The share's ordering survives updates that are pure gain changes on both sides, so it comes from the task in the original model, not from how the update is parametrised. The rate was not swept, so a better rate could change every reach.

Run
Three RunPod H100 pods, 78 gain-only runs, about $13.1 in all.
