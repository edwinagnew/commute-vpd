With the LoRA on all 24 matrices, its split state passes the Pile adversary where the fine-tune's fails fivefold in the same job. That reverses E7 and our filed prediction: a LoRA's read side is random, so we expected its split to be at least as fragile. The two were scored at different steps here; E14 and E16 repeat it at matched steps on one card, and the split LoRA stays far below the split fine-tune (E16's first figure, left panel).
