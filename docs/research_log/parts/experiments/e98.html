On the 67M, can a reading of neurons, with no decomposition, tell reuse from new as well as VPD does?

2026-11-05 to 11-06 · provisional; the last job's independent check holds with caveats · cluster L40 debug jobs, $0 · experiments/neuron_r, experiments/carrying_jobs, experiments/neuron_vpd_count

The question. VPD is only worth its cost if it tells a fine-tune that reuses something the model already does from one that adds something new better than a cheap method does. The cheapest rival is the model's own MLP neurons, which need no decomposition. Earlier neuron patches had tracked how far training had got, not what kind of update it was. So we asked whether some neuron reading separates the 67M's built pairs of fine-tunes the way VPD's readings do.

What we did. We tried three neuron readings on saved fine-tunes of the 67M, with no new training. Let the re-gating share be the part of a fine-tune's gain recovered when the unchanged model keeps its weights and only changes which parts are switched on. The first reading takes the same idea on neurons: the share of the gain carried by the original neurons firing differently, beside VPD's own share on 56 fine-tunes, with a control that adds only new output weights. The second asks what the neurons that carry each fine-tune did before training: a writer agent gave each one a job from the Pile text where it fires hardest, and a judge said whether that job relates to the task. The third takes VPD's switched-on set (parts whose importance rises by more than 0.3) and the same number of neurons, on copy maps, random maps, lowercase and two ciphers, and reads whether the set repeats across seeds and how much gain it carries. Every reuse or new label is an assumption from how the task was built.

What we found. The neuron share separates lowercase from ciphers and copying from lookup, but not copy maps from random maps, and reads elicit and cipher alike, where VPD's share separated all six scored rows. The output-only control reads 0.43, near most real states, so a high neuron share is not evidence of reuse. The Pile-job reading has no power: its positive control tied 0 of 30 task-picked neurons to their task, and those neurons turned out to do formatting. Beside the same count of neurons, VPD's switched-on set repeats across seeds by class (0.81 and 0.85 on copy maps, first figure) and the neurons' does not. Neither set carries much of the gain (second figure). At full width VPD's share orders the tasks as built, and the neuron share reverses on the maps (third figure).

What it means. On the 67M no neuron reading we tried replaces VPD's reading. VPD adds two things: a switched-on set that recurs and a full-width share that orders the tasks as built. Its thresholded set, though, is not where the gain lives. That full-width share is what is now called the re-gating share. E133 has since carried this comparison over nine built pairs with three forms of neuron reading beside steering, update size and the prior. There the share reads the reuse side higher on all nine, and neurons agree on at most three, so this line's answer stands and E133 is where it is now kept. The labels are still how each task was built, not a verdict of reuse; that waits on the definition.

Run
Three experiments, all reading saved 67M fine-tunes on single L40s in the cluster's debug partition, $0, plus agent calls for the neuron writer and judge. They were first logged under their own codes, now folded into this card:
  • the neuron share beside VPD's share on 56 fine-tunes (was E98, experiments/neuron_r; job 52786 after 52747 stopped at its first stage);
  • the neurons' old jobs on the Pile, with a sharper judge on a second pass (was E101, experiments/carrying_jobs; jobs 52876, 52885, 52944);
  • VPD's switched-on set against the same count of neurons on eight map fine-tunes, lowercase and two ciphers (was E117, experiments/neuron_vpd_count, table in experiments/neuron_vpd_table; job 54427, about 4 minutes, and a follow-up read, job 54503).
Found
  • The neuron share separates fewer pairs than VPD's share, and a fine-tune that only adds output weights reads 0.43, so it cannot mark reuse by itself.
  • Reading what a carrying neuron did on the Pile labels nothing on this model: the positive control failed under both judges.
  • VPD's switched-on set repeats across seeds by class where neurons' does not, and its full-width share orders the tasks as built where the neuron share reverses; the set itself carries under 0.02 of the gain.
