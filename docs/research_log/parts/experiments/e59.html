Is E54's "half-known" label independent of the decomposition: does a linear readout trained on ordinary text hold the half-known map's answers before the last block, and not the cipher's?

2026-11-02 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, $0 (job 48279, stopped at stage 0) · experiments/jain_readout

The question. The reuse line (Q5) calls a key-to-answer map "half-known" because the original 67M model ranks the answer 2nd to 20th after the key (E54). That label comes from the model's output, the same thing the map was built on, so a sceptic can say the re-gating share only reads that. The user approved (2026-10-30) an independent label from Jain et al. 2024: a linear readout from a block's residual stream to the vocabulary, trained on Pile text and never on the task tables.

What we did. We coded one readout per block, trained on rows of the Pile disjoint from the task windows, and filed three predictions before running: the half-known map's answers are readable at an early block (2 or earlier) and the cipher's at none; removing the readout's direction at that block takes away more of the half-known fine-tune's gain than the cipher's (on the twelve saved E54 and E56 states, read in log-odds net of the original model after the code review); and the last block's readout reproduces the construction. Stage 0 comes first: the saved states and rates must reproduce, and the last-block readout must come within 0.5 nats of the model's own loss.

What we found. Stage 0 stopped the job: the last-block readout's held-out loss is 0.71 nats above the model's (4.14 against 3.43), over the 0.5 limit, so none of the three predictions was read. Its training loss was below the model's (3.2), so the readouts overfit: 4,000 steps of 96 windows drew 15.7M tokens from 4.2M distinct ones, each fitted about 3.7 times.

What it means. Nothing yet bears on the label. The cause is the data, not the schedule, so the amendment (Fable's advice, a dated change to stage 0 only) draws fresh rows (16.8M tokens, each seen under once), keeps the schedule, and replaces the guessed 0.5-nat limit by a measured one: within 0.1 nats of the model's own unembedding with its final norm's scale frozen at its mean. The rerun is the same $0 job.
