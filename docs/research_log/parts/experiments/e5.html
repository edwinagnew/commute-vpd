run 2026-10-19 · RunPod RTX 4090 (named in the pod's smoke log) · experiments/task_ladder

We predicted that tasks whose gradient lands mostly on subcomponents Γ marks as important ("coverage") would keep more of the behaviour in the decomposition. Coverage predicts nothing, because the moved subcomponents carry under 8% of the gain on the target on every task. What carries it is Γ switching existing subcomponents on, by an amount set by the task.

Run
Six next-word tasks at three decades of baseline probability (two per rung, high and low coverage), plus emoticon. Arms: do-nothing fine-tune, Adam component training, gated-off component training. Route split at the trigger; optimiser-seed noise on emoticon.
Found
  • Both filed predictions null: coverage does not predict retention; rarity matters at rung 2.
  • Direct share 0.03 to 0.08 on every task.
  • Re-switching share agrees within 0.16 across fine-tune, all-piece component training and gated-off component training. It orders by rung (0.75 to 0.92 at rungs 0 and 1; 0.3 to 0.6 at rung 2), not by coverage.
  • Where re-switching is small, the masked-off share takes over, not the direct share.
  • The gated-on positive control did not learn in 10 steps. The task-local adversarial numbers have no power (seed spread 55 to 88 nats against gains of 4 to 15).
  • Pile checks on two tasks only: " will" then " be" reads 1.09 on the adversary and " we" then " just" 1.53. The first is the seventh next-word verdict behind Q1's "3 of 7", tabulated on E11's card.
