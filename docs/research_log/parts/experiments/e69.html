Qwen3-1.7B: what does a first reinforcement-learning update look like next to a fine-tune on the model's own correct answers?

2026-11-03 · rehearsed on the MATS cluster L40 ($0, jobs 50067-50266); paid run on one H100 approved by you on 2026-11-03, $33 · experiments/qwen_rl

The question. The Qwen deliverable D2 asks for the same table for a supervised fine-tune and a reinforcement-learning update of the same model, and no reinforcement-learning update of Qwen3-1.7B existed. This run makes the first one, so that qwen-decomp-lead can map and test it.

What we did. We trained the unchanged model on GSM8K school-maths problems three ways, 100 steps each, with 64 problems and 8 sampled answers per problem at a learning rate of 1e-6: reinforcement learning that rewards right answers (GRPO), the same loop with the rewards shuffled within each problem (the null, which should learn nothing), and supervised fine-tuning on the model's own correct answers. The change in the 196 decomposed weight matrices is saved at steps 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100. Nothing here applies a map or reads the decomposition.

What we found. All three arms are scored. Reinforcement learning gains 9.0 points (standard error 1.1) on the GSM8K test set and 7.1 (1.2) on GSM1K under the drop rule; fine-tuning on its own correct answers gains 2.6 (0.7) and 1.2 (0.9) and moves the model far less (KL 0.0017 against 0.194). The null missed its filed limit: shuffled rewards gained 5.6 points (1.2) on the test set against a filed limit of 1.5, so the filed early-signal ratio also missed at step 100 (8.69 against 5.10, needing 3 times). On capped answers the null reads +1.7 and -1.9 against RL's +9.0 and +6.4; on whole answers +4.9 and +3.0 against +9.4 and +7.8.

What it means. RL's update cannot yet be called learning, since the null gains too and the cause is not established. D2's table reads RL, the null and the supervised arm side by side. The report and notebook are due; this card is provisional until they exist and are checked.
