Qwen3-1.7B: what does a first reinforcement-learning update look like next to a fine-tune on the model's own correct answers?

2026-11-03 · rehearsed on the MATS cluster L40 ($0, jobs 50067-50266); paid run on one H100 approved by you on 2026-11-03 and running · experiments/qwen_rl

The question. The Qwen deliverable D2 asks for the same table for a supervised fine-tune and a reinforcement-learning update of the same model, and no reinforcement-learning update of Qwen3-1.7B exists yet. This run makes the first one, so that qwen-decomp-lead can map and test it.

What we did. Nothing has run at full size. The plan trains the unchanged model on GSM8K school-maths problems three ways, 100 steps each, with 64 problems and 8 sampled answers per problem at a learning rate of 1e-6: reinforcement learning that rewards right answers (GRPO), the same loop with the rewards shuffled within each problem (the null, which should learn nothing), and supervised fine-tuning on the model's own correct answers to the same problems. The change in the 196 decomposed weight matrices is saved at steps 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100. Nothing here applies a map or reads the decomposition. Every stage was rehearsed at small size, with saves, a resume and uploads, and the plan was reviewed by a fresh agent.

What we found. No result yet. The rehearsal gave the costs: about 113 seconds of sampling and 34 seconds of training per reinforcement-learning step on the L40, and a first step that moves each weight by about the learning rate, as expected. The paid run is estimated at 8 to 10 hours on an H100, $32 to $40; the worst case on the plan's decision tree is $44 to $55.

What it means. The run is over the 6-hour mark that makes a run need your go-ahead; you approved it on 2026-11-03 on one H100, and it is running. Reinforcement learning runs alone first; if it gains under 0.01 at 1e-6, it repeats at 3e-6 before the null and the supervised arm start.
