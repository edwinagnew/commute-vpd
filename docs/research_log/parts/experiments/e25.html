The step split along the small fine-tunes' paths

2026-10-22 · RunPod 4090 · experiments/step_split_small

Along two small emoticon fine-tunes the step split passes the adversarial check at every step, where E19's large path failed at step 1,000 (first figure). So that failure belongs to the large update. The step split still leaves more of the behaviour in Δ than the least-squares split in five of six readings; the exception is SGD at step 1,000 (second figure). Surprise: Δ's share of the update was filed lower for SGD than for Adam and came out higher at every step, in directions the task's inputs barely use (third figure).

Run
E19's step split (each optimizer step's weight change split by the scaled adjoint split against the current subcomponents, the scale refit each step, the remainder to Δ) along E21's small emoticon fine-tunes, SGD 0.01 and Adam 3e-6, seed 0, 1,000 steps, with a twin whose subcomponents are held at the original model's. The step split, least-squares and do-nothing states at steps 10, 100 and 1,000 through the Pile adversary (256 rows; pass up to 1.45, fail from 1.55 on this card). Every check passed, including step-10 reproduction of E21. Pod run 2,491 s, about $0.82.
Found
  • All 18 small-path states pass, 0.98 to 1.18. E19's step-split fail at step 1,000 (2.67) does not appear on either small path.
  • Behaviour with Δ out: on the Adam path the step split keeps 0.15 to 0.22 less than least squares at every step. On the SGD path it keeps less at steps 10 and 100 and more at 1,000, 0.99 against 0.92.
  • Surprise: Δ's plain share was filed lower for SGD at every step and came out higher at every step, by 0.34 to 0.43 of the update. The input-weighted shares agree within 0.07. Cause not established.
  • Moving the subcomponents' vectors changes nothing: carried and held twins agree within 0.004; the largest vector change is 0.034, a quarter of E19's.
  • The per-step refit's push into Δ is weak on small paths: weighted share at step 1,000 of 0.64 against 0.52 (SGD) and 0.58 (Adam) for one scale, where E19 had 1.38 against 0.69.
