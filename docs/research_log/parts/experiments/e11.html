2026-10-19, rerun 2026-10-20 · RunPod L40S; cluster L40 · experiments/reading_a_coverage

Adam component training's decomposition fails VPD's adversarial check on three of five ladder tasks, and leaving the update outside the pieces is more robust on four of five.

Run
The ladder's step-10 component training and fine-tune regenerated on the five ladder tasks without Pile numbers (one at three seeds), a gate tying them to the ladder's curves, all five checks with same-job floors. Emoticon rerun on the same harness the next day.
Found
  • Component training's adversarial ratios on the five ladder tasks: " it" then " is" 2.73, " which" then " was" 2.24, " will" then " have" 1.16, " in" then " Fig" 6.74, " we" then " just" 1.38 to 1.55 over three seeds. With emoticon (1.03, E2 and the rerun) and " will" then " be" (1.09, E5) that is the seven-task count: 3 hold, 1 at the line, 3 fail.
  • Three of five filed predictions failed, all one way: component training's pieces are less robust than filed, on common-word tasks and against do-nothing alike.
  • Stochastic, CI-masked and unmasked checks mostly within the floor: blind to what the adversary finds. Sparsity loss moves under 1%.
  • Patterns, not causes: the excess orders with component training's off-target KL, which is 1.8 to 2.5 times the fine-tune's at the same step; the fine-tune alone breaks 1.5 on three tasks.
  • Emoticon rerun reproduced E2 (1.03, 2.18) and found the unchanged model's own adversary spread can reach 21% in one job.
