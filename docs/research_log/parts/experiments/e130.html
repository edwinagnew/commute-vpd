Does teacher training's placement shrink the excess of every lowercase copy it makes?

2026-11-07 · cluster debug L40, job 55000, about 11 minutes, $0 · experiments/copy_protection · provisional, not independently checked

The question. E127 found that on two lowercase copies, the places teacher training put each copy's weight change hid most of its excess under the adversarial check (about 2.8 and 4.1 times less than the same change left in Δ). Is that a constant of the recipe, or does it depend on which fine-tune is copied? Two copies cannot tell.

What we did. We took two more lowercase copies made by the same recipe, one of the full Adam fine-tune stopped at step 20 and one of a rank-8 LoRA fine-tune trained to step 2,400. We read each copy twice under the check, on Pile rows 0-255: as teacher training placed it, and with its weight change moved into Δ. A ratio of 1 means as hard to break as the released decomposition; E39's fail line is 1.61. The multiplier m is the placed excess over the excess in Δ: under 1 placement protects, over 1 it adds excess.

What we found. The step-20 Adam copy reads 2.84 placed and 2.17 in Δ, so m is 1.58 and placement adds excess. The step-2,400 LoRA copy reads 1.53 placed and 3.17 in Δ, so m is 0.25, a fourfold cut as before. Over the four lowercase copies, m runs from 0.24 to 1.58, one seed each. The reversal rests on one block of 128 rows.

What it means. Teacher training's protective placement is not a constant of the recipe; its loss has no mask or adversary term, so nothing in it asks for protection. This weakens E127's accounting split for the broad Adam copy and leaves open why some copies are protected. A carrier for broad updates needs a placement that protects by design. Row set 256-511 is the next test.
