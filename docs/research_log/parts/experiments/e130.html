Does teacher training's placement shrink the excess of every lowercase copy it makes?

2026-11-07 · cluster debug L40, jobs 55000 and 55031 (Amendment 1), about 24 minutes, $0 · experiments/copy_protection · provisional, not independently checked

The question. E127 found that on two lowercase copies, the places teacher training put each copy's weight change hid most of its excess under the adversarial check (about 2.8 and 4.1 times less than the same change left in Δ). Is that a constant of the recipe, or does it depend on which fine-tune is copied? Two copies cannot tell.

What we did. We took two more lowercase copies made by the same recipe, one of the full Adam fine-tune stopped at step 20 and one of a rank-8 LoRA fine-tune trained to step 2,400. We read each copy twice under the check: as teacher training placed it, and with its weight change moved into Δ. First on Pile rows 0-255, then, because that result rested on one block, again on rows 256-511. A ratio of 1 means as hard to break as the released decomposition; E39's fail line is 1.61. The multiplier m is the placed excess over the excess in Δ: under 1 placement protects, over 1 it adds excess.

What we found. On rows 0-255 the step-20 Adam copy reads 2.84 placed and 2.17 in Δ, so m is 1.58 and placement adds excess; the step-2,400 LoRA copy reads 1.53 and 3.17, so m is 0.25. On rows 256-511 the Adam copy's m is 0.78, partial protection and 0.02 under the 0.8 line, from a coarse read, so its reversal does not replicate. Over four 128-row blocks its m is 0.62, 1.75, 0.60 and 0.97, while the LoRA copy reads 0.23 to 0.28 on every block.

What it means. Teacher training's protective placement is not a constant of the recipe, but how far the step-20 copy loses it is not settled: it runs from partial protection to none depending on the rows, and the LoRA copy stays protected. Its loss has no mask or adversary term, so nothing in it asks for protection. This weakens E127's accounting split for the broad Adam copy; "function-dependent" stands only as "not constant", and placement adding excess is not established. A carrier for broad updates needs a placement that protects by design.
