Does the update chosen as Qwen's example of new content hold up when checked?

2026-11-05 · cluster L40 ($0) · experiments/qwen_anchor_checks · ended, hand count checked by a third reader

Since then. The 2026-11-09 paper plan (a definition of reuse, re-gating against baselines on the 67M, then Qwen3-1.7B) replaced the framing in deliverables and parallel lines used here.

The question. The Qwen comparison needs one update that teaches content the model lacks. You chose the 5-epoch fine-tune on TOFU's invented-author facts in five wordings (E84), labelled "new facts memorised, carrying only partly to rephrasings". Does that label survive a blind count of right answers and four cheaper reads?

What we did. We drew 100 rows and shuffled three arms of each into a sheet of 300 answers: the unchanged model on TOFU's rephrased question, the updated model on the trained wording, and the updated model on the rephrased question. Two fresh readers marked an answer right if every fact asked for was stated correctly, in any words. A third reader marked all 300 blind and sealed the marks. We also read both models on one card, dropped the suffix "Answer in one sentence.", counted known facts on 217 real-fact questions, and trained both example updates at a second seed. A last step split the real-fact answers at both seeds into author questions and others, with no training.

What we found. The readers marked 0.75 of the updated model's rephrased answers right, against a line of 0.60 filed before any reading; the unchanged model read 0.205, the trained wording 1.00. By that rule the label moves to "new facts memorised, carrying to rephrasings for most facts", and the rule replaces the label you chose. It means most rephrased questions get at least one correct fact, not most facts in each answer. Word recall misses its own line at both seeds (0.53 and 0.54 against about 0.60), so the two measures disagree and both stand.

What it means. The new facts held by the filed rule's count; known facts about authors did not. On 95 author questions, answers containing the fact fell about thirty points at both seeds, and at least 18 and 11 answers name an invented author (lower bounds). The whole-set fall is 7.4 points at the first seed, under the 10-point limit filed for that update only, and 11.1 at the second, so the label stands and every comparison quoting this update states the limit. The rise on other questions is at least partly longer answers. "Pull" is a description of this, and no cause is named. The update is also 37 times the template fine-tune's size.
