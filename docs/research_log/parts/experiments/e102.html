On Qwen3-1.7B, do the neurons that carry a fine-tune already fire at the task's answers, and does a system turn already move them?

2026-11-05 · cluster L40 debug, $0 · experiments/qwen_carrying · stage A provisional, stage B coded and due to run

The question. The neuron baseline (E98, E101) could not label a fine-tune as reuse or new on the 67M, because its agent-written jobs and the task shared no vocabulary. On Qwen3-1.7B the tasks are natural language, so two questions need no agent and no decomposition: were the carrying neurons already firing at the task's answers, and do they overlap the neurons a system turn already moves?

What we did. For each saved Qwen fine-tune we took the 1,024 neurons of the unchanged model that carry its gain when they fire differently with their weights kept (the E98 cell). We read their overlap with the neurons a system turn moves, and their firing at the answers as a share of their own usual firing, each against a random change of the template's output size. The template fine-tune (reuse by construction) and the TOFU fine-tune on invented author facts (new) were read at the same weight size.

What we found. The control passed. The template's carrying neurons overlap the system turn's more than the random change's do, 0.056 against 0.035, but the TOFU fine-tune at the same size overlaps almost as much, 0.051, so real updates share these neurons whatever they teach. The firing share sat at 0.50 for every set, so that reading has no spread. Seeds share 0.95 of their carrying sets, so the sets belong to their update. VPD's direct share, read by another lead, tells the two apart more (0.013 against 0.031) but in the opposite direction to the labels.

What it means. This neuron reading marks a real small update, not reuse, so it is not a bar VPD must beat on the reuse-or-new question; the report is provisional. What the carrying neurons do is still open, and stage B asks it: an agent describes each neuron's job from its corpus text, and a judge ties each description to one of four Qwen tasks, chance one in four. A control comes first: neurons picked at biography answers must be tied to the author-facts task above random before anything else is read. Then the template's neurons are set against the matched TOFU state's on the layout task, and the full TOFU state's on author facts.

Run
Stage A: two cluster debug jobs (53025, 53029), 723 s and 411 s, $0. Stage B: its 770-neuron pool is built (26 mixed batches), rules filed in predictions_stageB.json; 78 agent calls and one short debug job are due.
Found
  • Prompt overlap: template 0.056 and 0.057 against 0.035 for the random change (the filed rule holds), TOFU matched 0.051 and 0.052, so most of the lead is shared.
  • Firing at the answers: 0.50 for every set, inside the random draws (the filed template rule misses).
  • The sets are specific to their fine-tune, but the across-row-halves overlap is not a test of that: the random change reads 0.61 and 0.86.
