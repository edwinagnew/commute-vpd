On Qwen3-1.7B, do the neurons that carry a fine-tune already fire at the task's answers, and does a system turn already move them?

2026-11-05 · cluster L40 debug (planned), $0 · experiments/qwen_carrying · coded, jobs due to run

The question. The neuron baseline (E98, E101) could not label a fine-tune as reuse or new on the 67M, because its agent-written jobs and the task shared no vocabulary. On Qwen3-1.7B the tasks are natural language, so two questions need no agent and no decomposition: were the carrying neurons already firing at the task's answers, and do they overlap the neurons a system turn already moves?

What we did. Not yet run. For each saved Qwen fine-tune we take the 1024 neurons of the unchanged model that carry its gain when they fire differently with their weights kept (the E98 cell), and read two things against a random change of the template's output size: their firing at the answers as a share of their own usual firing, and their overlap with the neurons a system turn moves. The control that a random change does not overlap comes first and stops the job if missed.

What we found. Nothing yet.

What it means. If the template fine-tune (reuse by construction) passes and the random change does not, a neuron-level reader needs no decomposition on Qwen; if the control fails, the neuron reading is dropped for this question. The agent-judged job reading runs only if the control passes.
