What is the fixed change of Γ's values made of?

2026-10-22 · RunPod RTX 4090 · experiments/shift_anatomy

One change per key. On the copy task and the 32-pair cipher, a separate fixed change for each key gives back most of what each input's own values give, where one change for all inputs gave back almost nothing (first figure). The change sits in the MLP matrices (third figure). It is not one direction with a varying strength, not one fixed move of what Γ reads (second figure), and not the key word's own subcomponents on ordinary text.

Run
E28's eight do-nothing states, regenerated exactly: five word tasks and three key-value tasks, ten-step Adam fine-tunes, seed 0, read at the trigger on about 100 held-out inputs. Every number is a share of what each input's own updated values give back. Arms: one change per key against random groups of the same sizes; one direction with a per-input strength, before Γ's clip; one block of matrices at the fixed change with the other at the input's own values; Γ re-run with its input at the trigger moved by the mean change; and on the copy task, each key's change against that word's subcomponents on Pile text. Emoticon ran first and reproduced E28 exactly. 118 s, $0.21.
Found
  • One change per key: 0.84 on the copy task and 0.82 on the 32-pair cipher; random groups 0.07 and 0.15. Each key's change rests on 9 to 15 training inputs.
  • Attention's change barely matters on any task. The MLP carries it, and on the copy task its single change gives back only 0.46. Our filed lean, a fixed "spot the cue" part in attention, missed.
  • Moving Γ's input by one fixed amount works on word tasks and gives back 0.03 on the copy task.
  • Per-input strength gives back 0.05 on the copy task, but its word-task control failed, 0.54 and 0.63 against 0.7: on those tasks a change before the clip does worse than the plain change after it.
  • The copy keys' changes do not match their Pile signatures, −0.02 against a control of 0.73; our lean missed.
