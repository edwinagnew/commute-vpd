Qwen3-1.7B: does retraining the answer-format template with only the saving changed give the same run, with its update kept in full precision?

2026-11-03 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, $0 (jobs 50875, 50876) · experiments/qwen_anchor1 · fast lane, no independent check

Since then. The 2026-11-09 paper plan (a definition of reuse, re-gating against baselines on the 67M, then Qwen3-1.7B) replaced the framing in deliverables and parallel lines used here.

The question. D2 and D3 need the template fine-tune (the model learns to give, unprompted, the answers it gives when a prompt asks for the chat format; anchor 1, behaviour the model already produces) through the same split readings as the RL and fine-tuning updates. The earlier run (E61) saved its update rounded to bf16 at four steps, and the readings refuse rounded files.

What we did. We trained the same recipe again with the same weights, batches and rate, and changed only what is saved: the update in fp32 at steps 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100. The earlier run is the control; a wrong recipe or file would show as a gap to it.

What we found. The retrain reproduces the earlier run. At step 100 the four numbers agree within two parts per million (filed tolerance 1 percent) and all 256 generated answers are byte for byte the same; 92.6 percent follow the template and strict accuracy is 62.1 percent (first figure). The files are the update the run made, and no step differs by more than 14 parts per million.

What it means. The template fine-tune now exists in full precision at eight steps and can go through the split readings; nothing here says how it will read. Step t has t − 1 moving steps and a cosine schedule, where RL's runs hold a constant rate, so compare by label and say so.
