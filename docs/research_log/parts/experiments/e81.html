Does fine-tuning Qwen3-1.7B on TOFU's invented authors teach content it lacks, so that rephrased questions are answered?

2026-11-04 · MATS cluster, $0 · experiments/qwen_tofu · stopped for your card, no notebook yet

The question. The Qwen comparison needs one update that teaches content the model does not have. We chose TOFU, a public set of questions about invented authors. The unchanged model turned out not to be at chance on it (0.41 against 0.17 by picking one of six answers), so you chose to keep TOFU and measure learning from where it starts.

What we did. We fine-tuned for about 35 minutes at each of two learning rates, 1e-5 and 2e-5, and read the last step on four things: the multiple-choice share (bar 0.588), recall of the answer's words when the question is asked in other words, the same in the trained wording, and real facts as a control.

What we found. Neither rate meets the filed recall test: 0.455 and 0.517 on rephrased questions. The model repeats trained answers (0.757 and 0.956 of their new words in the trained wording, 0.23 for authors it never saw), but only 0.30 and 0.38 come back when the question is rephrased. The multiple-choice pass (0.77) says little, since 0.65 appears with no question at all. Real-fact multiple choice falls from 0.79 to 0.43, largely because answers grew from 2.5 to 13 words.

What it means. The update holds new facts in memorised form that carries over only partly to rephrasings. Whether to use it as that, or train on several rephrasings, is your Blocking card in section {{n:decisions}}. At the first 200 steps the update holds wording and little content.
