Where in the masked forward of Qwen3-1.7B is a fine-tuning update lost?

2026-11-03 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, $0 (jobs 50507, 50510, 50533, 50546) · experiments/qwen_transmission

The question. With all matrices masked, E72 found the whole update shifts the logits very little (Q1; D1 is a map on Qwen3-1.7B that keeps the decomposition faithful for a real update, judged by a test that can fail a bad placement), and E73 showed the clean model does respond to it. We asked where in the masked forward a change is lost, and whether it is lost for the update alone or for any change.

What we did. Nothing was trained. We added a change as an unmasked term at its matrix, with the original model's masks fixed, and read transmission (KL of the masked forward over KL in the clean model; 1 means the masked forward responds as the clean model does, 0 that it ignores the change) and amplitude (the masked logit shift's size over the clean one's). Beside each block's real change we put a Gaussian of the same clean KL, and the last matrix alone was the control. Two amendments separated masks upstream from masks at and after the change, froze later matrices at their clean output, and repeated the read under VPD's stochastic masks.

What we found. With all 196 matrices masked by the original model's masks, the whole update shifts logits by 0.03-0.13 of its clean amplitude (0.095 and 0.032 under fixed masks, 0.13 and 0.051 under stochastic masks); the last matrix alone passes at 0.99 and 0.75, so the term does enter (first two figures). A change is lost mostly at the masks at and after it, for the update and a Gaussian alike, and the masked residual stream sits 25% to 73% of its norm from the clean one at every layer. The gap between update and Gaussian is about amplification through later blocks, but a Gaussian still passes 3.1 to 6.5 times the update's share with later blocks intact (third figure).

What it means. Why the masks lose the update is not shown: four alternatives stay open, among them that all-matrix masking is not VPD's training forward (partial routing was never read) and that the masked stream may be a different network reaching the same logits. A change expected to pass at an early block has no positive control, so E77 adds one, and the whole update under partial routing.

Run
Reads on saved states, 32 rows on two updates, four short jobs on one L40, $0; one stopped at its control on the template and was amended after seeing the values.
Found
  • The whole update reaches the logits at amplitude 0.095 and 0.032 under fixed masks, and stochastic masks are no better (0.013 and 0.0011 in KL), against a lean of 0.05 to 0.25 or more.
  • Block 0's change did not recover with later sites unmasked (0.057 and 0.034 kept), and a Gaussian did not track the update within a factor 3.
  • One temperature removes about half of the gold-data updates' KL from W0 and 15% on the template.
