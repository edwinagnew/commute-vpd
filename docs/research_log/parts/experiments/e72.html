Can any test on Qwen3-1.7B that fixes the masks give a placement of a fine-tuning update room to pass?

2026-11-03 · MATS cluster L40, normal QoS, $0 (jobs 50428, 50441) · experiments/qwen_fixed_masks

The question. D1 is a map on Qwen3-1.7B that keeps the decomposition faithful for a real update, judged by a test that can fail a bad placement (Q1). The last job (E68) found that under the original model's masks even the whole update added back closes little of the gap, so the test could fail a placement but not pass one. We asked whether masks made on the fine-tuned model, or no masks at all, give a test that can pass.

What we did. Nothing was trained. On three saved fine-tuning updates we scored seven states under masks Γ gives on the fine-tuned model and holds fixed: doing nothing, the whole update added unmasked, the often-on and rarely-on halves, a random change and the two splits. In the clean model with no masks we scored W0 plus each part of the update (the least-squares split's in-span part, its remainder, each half) against the fine-tuned model, and we read what a random change does under the original model's masks.

What we found. With all 196 matrices masked by the original model's masks, the whole update shifts logits by 0.03-0.13 of its clean amplitude, about what 1-3% of it does in the clean model; why the masks lose it is not shown. Under masks made on the fine-tuned model it moves the gain by at most 0.04 of the fine-tune's, so there is no room to grade a placement (first figure). In the clean table the in-span part reads 0.003-0.009 nats from the fine-tuned model and the remainder 0.22-0.53, but the gap between the in-span part and the remainder is about what size gives: the update rescaled to those norms reads 0.001-0.003 and 0.09-0.21 (second figure).

What it means. No masked test here can yet pass a placement, so by your decision the clean-model table is D1's test and teacher training with masks in its loss is the map. That table does not test masks, and the filed label that the in-span part "carried" the gain stands only as a filed label with the size reading beside it; size-matched references are being read (E75). Why the masks lose the update is the open question, and E74 follows it through the forward.
