Do five readings inside the Qwen decomposition tell the template fine-tune from the TOFU facts fine-tune, beyond what update size does?

2026-11-06 · cluster L40 debug, seven jobs, $0 · experiments/qwen_d3_rows · provisional, not independently checked

The question. The third deliverable ({{n:next}}) asks for readings that tell apart two known kinds of update: one that only brings out a behaviour the model has, and one that teaches content it lacked. Its two examples are the template fine-tune (100 steps, weight norm 0.49) and the TOFU facts fine-tune on fictional authors. Do five readings of where an update lands in the decomposition, and what the moved subcomponents do, separate the two by more than their own seeds differ, and is that more than update size alone does between random changes?

What we did. We split each fine-tune over the subcomponents by least squares, two seeds each. TOFU was read at the step matched to the template in weight norm (before it has learned the facts: recall 0.16 to 0.17 against the untrained model's 0.18), just past its learning line, and at its full update, whose norm is 37 times the template's but whose output size is about five times. Two random changes (a Gaussian one and one that moves only the alive subcomponents) stood beside them at two sizes matched on output. The five readings: the share of the update's energy outside the subcomponents; the share of the split on subcomponents already switched on; the direct share (what the moved vectors carry by themselves under held masks); the credit under masks; and the overlap of the subcomponents an update switches on with think mode's, beside a no-think set matched on answer length. A gap counts when it exceeds the larger seed spread.

What we found. At the full update all five separate the two fine-tunes. Three are not reproduced by size between the random changes: outside share (0.12 for the template against 0.18 for TOFU), placement excess (-0.008 against 0.015) and think overlap (3.7 against 3.0 fold over chance). Direct share depends on the rows: it separates on each fine-tune's own rows and not on the shared maths rows. Credit has no random under masks, so size is unread. Filed prediction 4, that placement does not count, reversed: placement separates the fine-tunes at both sizes, most strongly at TOFU's step before it learns the facts, where it reads 0.16 against the template's -0.008, above even the alive-subcomponents-only random's 0.09. We cannot say why. The think overlap is not about thinking: the no-think set overlaps the template's set more (4.2 to 4.4) than think mode does (3.7). Of eight filed rules, four held and four missed: placement, direct share at TOFU's matched step, the think-minus-no-think gap and the fold range.

What it means. The decomposition sees the two fine-tunes differently on three readings that size does not reproduce, so those three go to the maths arms as descriptions to compare against. They are not a verdict on which update teaches content the model lacked: the two differ 37 times in weight size, and TOFU may be pulling the model towards its training's names (the share of author answers that state a fact fell from 0.70 to 0.38 to 0.41). Each reading carries that caveat. The size check is weak, since the two random kinds differ more from each other than the sizes do.

Run
Seven cluster debug jobs on one L40 each, $0, about 5.2 h of card (control 52779 first and alone: it reproduced every stored reference on the E40 step-10 state within its bands). One-line filed rules in predictions.json, written before any read.
Found
  • Outside share, placement excess and think overlap separate the fine-tunes at the full update and are not reproduced by size between the random changes; they go to the maths arms.
  • Placement reversed the filed lean (placement does not separate): it separates at both sizes, most at TOFU's pre-learning step (0.16).
  • Direct share depends on the rows; credit has no random under masks. Both are printed only.
  • Think overlap is not thinking: the matched no-think set overlaps the template's set more than think mode does.
Bears on
The third deliverable: three descriptions of where a real update sits, with the 37-times size gap and the TOFU names caveat. They do not say whether an update teaches content the model lacked. VPD's adversary was not run.
