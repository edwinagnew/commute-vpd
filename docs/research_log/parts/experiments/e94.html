Do five readings inside the Qwen decomposition tell the template fine-tune from the TOFU facts fine-tune, beyond what update size does?

2026-11-06 · cluster L40 debug, nine jobs, $0 · experiments/qwen_d3_rows · provisional, not independently checked

The question. The third deliverable ({{n:next}}) asks for readings that tell apart two known kinds of update: one that only brings out a behaviour the model has, and one that teaches content it lacked. Its two examples are the template fine-tune (100 steps, weight norm 0.49) and the TOFU facts fine-tune on fictional authors. Do five readings of where an update lands in the decomposition, and what the moved subcomponents do, separate the two by more than their own seeds differ, and is that more than update size alone does between random changes?

What we did. We split each fine-tune over the subcomponents by least squares, two seeds each. TOFU was read at the step matched to the template in weight norm (before it has learned the facts: recall 0.16 to 0.17 against the untrained model's 0.18), just past its learning line, and at its full update, whose norm is 37 times the template's but whose output size is about five times. Two random changes (a Gaussian one and one that moves only the alive subcomponents) stood beside them at two sizes matched on output. The five readings: the share of the update's energy outside the subcomponents; the share of the split on subcomponents already switched on; the direct share (what the moved vectors carry by themselves under held masks); the credit under masks; and the overlap of the subcomponents an update switches on with think mode's, beside a no-think set matched on answer length. A gap counts when it exceeds the larger seed spread. Amendment 1 then read the same three on baseline-lead's known-facts fine-tune (E103): TOFU's task, format and recipe, on facts the model already knows, seeds 42 and 142. Each of its states is placed between the template (0) and TOFU (1), at the template's weight norm and at its end.

What we found. At the full update all five separate the two fine-tunes. Three are not reproduced by size between the random changes: outside share (0.12 for the template against 0.18 for TOFU), placement excess (-0.008 against 0.015) and think overlap (3.7 against 3.0 fold over chance). Direct share depends on the rows: it separates on each fine-tune's own rows and not on the shared maths rows. Credit has no random under masks, so size is unread. Filed prediction 4, that placement does not count, reversed: placement separates the fine-tunes at both sizes, most strongly at TOFU's step before it learns the facts, where it reads 0.16 against the template's -0.008, above even the alive-subcomponents-only random's 0.09. We cannot say why. The think overlap is not about thinking: the no-think set overlaps the template's set more (4.2 to 4.4) than think mode does (3.7). Of eight filed rules, four held and four missed: placement, direct share at TOFU's matched step, the think-minus-no-think gap and the fold range. The known-facts fine-tune reads like TOFU, not the template. At matched size it sits 0.90 of the way to TOFU on outside share (noise 0.027) and 0.65 on placement (noise 0.035); think overlap is no call there, because the two anchors do not separate. At its end outside share (0.73) and think overlap (0.79) are also on TOFU's side, and placement is no call, its seeds disagreeing. All three leans filed for it held. The sets of subcomponents switched on tell known from fictional facts beyond the seed null (overlap 0.63 to 0.66 between them against 0.86 to 0.89 between seeds, at matched size), but the fictional set is about a quarter larger and the two differ in training rows.

What it means. The decomposition sees the two fine-tunes differently on three readings that size does not reproduce, so those three go to the maths arms as descriptions to compare against. They follow the training task and format, not whether the content was new: a fine-tune built like TOFU on facts the model already knows reads like TOFU. They are not a verdict on which update teaches content the model lacked: the two differ 37 times in weight size, TOFU may be pulling the model towards its training's names (the share of author answers that state a fact fell from 0.70 to 0.38 to 0.41), and a new circuit built from subcomponents the model already has reads as brought out, not taught, to VPD. At the end, the known-facts outside share divided by its null sits on the template's side (0.60 against 0.58 and 0.64). The size check is weak, since the two random kinds differ more from each other than the sizes do.

  • Placement reversed the filed lean (placement does not separate): it separates at both sizes, most at TOFU's pre-learning step (0.16).
  • Direct share depends on the rows; credit has no random under masks. Both are printed only.
  • Think overlap is not thinking: the matched no-think set overlaps the template's set more than think mode does.
  • A known-facts fine-tune reads like TOFU on outside share and placement at matched size, so the readings follow task and format, not new against known content.

They do not say whether an update teaches content the model lacked, and the known-facts pair shows they do not see "already known". VPD's adversary was not run.
