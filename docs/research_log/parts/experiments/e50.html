In the real fine-tuned model, does putting back the pieces Γ switches on separate a fine-tune of a behaviour the model had from one it lacked?

2026-10-29 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS · experiments/restore_anchors

The question. Line 1 (Q5). On E12's toy tables the stand-in ratio separates copying (about 0.9) from random maps (about 0.4), but it is read in Γ's stand-in model, which the fine-tuned model does not contain. Does a number read inside the real fine-tuned model separate them too?

What we did. E12's four tables, two copy (repeat the key) and two random-map (give a fixed word for each key), each fine-tuned at two seeds. At the key position we put back the original model's subcomponents on a set of pieces and measured the restore share, the part of the fine-tune's gain (13 to 21 nats) taken back. Sets: every alive subcomponent, Γ's switched-on set, a ranking by pull on the answer, and 20 random sets. The kinds separate if the copies read at least 0.3 above the random maps. A follow-up (Amendment 1) asked whether the every-alive number can read low at all, using hand-made writes that add the answer's unembedding row directly; it also split Γ's set into its core and the rest, and showed the original model a demonstration of each pair.

What we found. They do not separate. Every alive subcomponent took back 0.76 to 0.78 of the gain on the copies and 0.66 to 0.73 on the random maps; Γ's set took back 0.14 to 0.19 and 0.10 to 0.20; random sets at most 0.11. The follow-up showed the every-alive number can read low: the hand writes read −0.16 to −0.32 (figure 2), so it is powered and measures how much of a gain the original weights relay. Both kinds relay about 0.7, alike within the bar, the copies consistently a little higher. The original model performs neither behaviour, even when shown the pair.

What it means. Nothing filed separates the kinds inside the fine-tuned model, and Γ's set is closed as evidence of kind on the 67M. The toys cannot show kind in the real model, so they cannot vouch for the stand-in ratio either; it is the line's only number that separates them and has never been tested on an anchor the original model really performs.
