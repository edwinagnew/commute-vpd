On the Qwen states where VPD has a reading, do four cheap numbers already sort the same verdicts?

2026-11-06 · provisional, independent check holds with caveats · two cluster debug L40 jobs (54382, 54383), $0 · experiments/qwen_comparator_table, table in experiments/neuron_vpd_table

The question. The Qwen half of your baseline retarget: set cheap comparators beside every VPD row of the Qwen table, so that where a cheap number reproduces a VPD verdict, the verdict is read as saying less. E117 is the 67M half.

What we did. No decomposition was run. On every saved Qwen state that already has a VPD row (E93, E94, E104, E107) we read four numbers: update size (weight change over the unchanged model's weight norm), the gain in log probability of the trained answer with the unchanged model's rank of it, a steering share (one mean-difference vector added at one block, against a random vector of the same norm), and the carrying-neuron share of E102. That is 180 cells; 32 do not apply because their states are built inside a VPD job.

What we found. Update size and gain each order E93's pass and fail exactly: the smallest passing update is 3.7 parts in ten thousand and the largest failing one 2.8, with gain 0.67 against 0.60. Where VPD tells a pair apart the cheap numbers do too, often by more; against the shuffled-reward null no column counts at both steps. Only the known-facts arm at its end of training reads differently: VPD's outside share and think overlap, 0.73 and 0.79, put it near the fictional-facts arm and no cheap column agrees; the rows differ by arm, and the carrying share and VPD's end placement read as unreadable. A carrying share above 1 is a hybrid-model gain larger than the fine-tune's, not a bug.

What it means. On Qwen the decomposition's verdicts are matched or beaten by cheap numbers on every pair but one, so its added value is shown on the 67M only so far. Caveat: the comparators are read on each arm's own rows, so part of every gap between arms is the rows (the independent check's verdict is holds with caveats). Next would be the comparators on VPD's shared maths rows.

Run
Two cluster debug L40 jobs (54382, 54383) reading the saved states, $0; controls reproduced E92's and E102's stored values exactly.
Found
  • Update size, gain and the carrying share each sort E93's pass and fail exactly, so that table says less than its verdicts suggest.
  • Gain, steering and carrying share separate every pair VPD separates, though the arms are read on different rows.
  • Only the known arm at its end differs: VPD's outside share and think overlap (0.73, 0.79) say fictional-facts and no cheap column agrees; E94 already read this as task and format, not novelty.
