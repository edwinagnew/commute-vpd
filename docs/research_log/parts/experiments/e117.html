On the 67M re-gating states, does the decomposition's reading add anything over the same reading done on neurons?

2026-11-06 · provisional · one cluster debug L40 job (54427), about 4 min of run, $0 · experiments/neuron_vpd_count, table in experiments/neuron_vpd_table

The question. Your 2026-11-06 baseline retarget asks that, on states where VPD already has a reading, the same reading is done on neurons and set beside it. A VPD reading that a cheap number reproduces says less than its number suggests (E92). This is the small-model half; E118 is the Qwen half.

What we did. Eight saved fine-tunes of the 67M (copy maps and random maps, two seeds each), plus lowercase and two ciphers, with no training. For each we took the switched-on set: the subcomponents whose causal importance at the read position rises by more than 0.3, and, for neurons, the same number of MLP neurons whose firing rises most. We read three things on each set. Seed agreement is the overlap of the set between the two seeds, 1 meaning identical. Restore share is the part of the fine-tune's gain that disappears when the set alone is put back to the unchanged model's values. Re-switching share is the part recovered when the unchanged model gets only the set's change. Each is shown beside random sets of the same size and beside the full-width version.

What we found. VPD's set repeats across seeds by class, 0.81 and 0.85 for copy maps against 0.55 and 0.66 for random maps, and the same count of neurons does not (0.38 and 0.59 against 0.32 and 0.23). Neither method's set carries much gain, 0.0005 to 0.019 of it recovered by VPD's against 0.27 to 0.80 at full width, and neither separates the maps on restore or re-switching. At full width VPD's re-switching share puts copy maps 0.47 above random maps and lowercase 0.59 above the ciphers; the neuron share reverses on the maps. Lowercase and the ciphers switch on no subcomponent at all.

What it means. On the 67M the decomposition adds two things over neurons, a set that recurs and a full-width share that orders the tasks as built, but its 0.3-threshold set is not where the gain lives. The classes are assumptions from how each task was built, and the full-width gaps were controls, not filed panels. It makes no statement under the frozen reuse definition. The Qwen half (E118) found the cheap numbers already sort most of VPD's verdicts.
