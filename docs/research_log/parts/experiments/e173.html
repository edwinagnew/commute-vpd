When the third wording of reuse is measured, what does it say about built edits and real fine-tunes on the 67M?

2026-11-13 · cell P1.19, 3 of 4 runs used, provisional, no independent check · cluster debug L40, under 10 min a job, $0 · experiments/soft_intervention (runs also numbered E176, E177)

The question. You proposed a third wording of reuse, for testing only and not adopted: a fine-tune reuses something the model already had when it is the original with only one variable's rule changed, where the variable is named on the original before the fine-tune is looked at. Part 2 asked whether that wording, measured, gives a verdict on hand-built edits, the memorised fact and its twin, and the old matched pairs.

What we did. No training. Each item's variable was named by hand from its task (a push direction, or the answer tokens' output rows). For each item we measured the share of the change closed when the plain model is given the fine-tune's value of that variable (carry) and when the fine-tune is given the plain model's value (reversion). Where a variable carries the change we then asked how much a trigger or a per-token table explains of how it moved. Items: eight hand-built edits where the right answer is known, the memorised fact and its twin, and four old matched pairs, two seeds a side.

What we found. Every hand-built edit read the grade it was built to have at all three bars (0.8, 0.9, 0.95), with carry and reversion within 2e-5 of 1. No fine-tune had a named variable that carried its change: carry and reversion were at most 0.33 one way or the other on all twenty, against a bar of 0.8 or more. Read descriptively, the side built to reuse an old variable has the larger share explained by its trigger in every pair (the twin 0.75 against the fact's 0.002).

What it means. The wording can be measured, and the measurement can tell a re-setting, a new trigger and a new computation apart on built edits. On real fine-tunes, with variables named by hand, it gives "no carrying variable among the named ones", so no reused-or-new verdict on any of them; whether that is the wording's answer or the naming's is open, since only one variable per item was named. The wording is not adopted and decisions.md is unchanged.
