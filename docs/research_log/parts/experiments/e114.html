Which parts of the 67M does the case-label fine-tune's gain run through, and is it fed through unchanged parts or written into changed ones?

2026-11-06 · provisional · one cluster debug L40 job (54238), about 12 min, $0 · experiments/case_locator

The question. E112 built the case-label pair to the adopted definition's shape (a real fine-tune and a shuffled-label twin that can only memorise), and the definition's three causal checks read the real one "not through the capability". Does the part-by-part search used for E97 and E100 find a head or MLP that the real fine-tune needs and the twin does not, and does the weight-side number from E100 place it with "fed" or with "written" fine-tunes?

What we did. On E112's four saved states (real and twin, two seeds each) we switched off each of the 24 attention heads and 4 MLP blocks in turn, replacing its output with the original model's average, and recorded the share of the fine-tune's gain that was lost. A part counts as located only if it takes at least 0.55 of the real gain on both seeds while taking at most 0.20 of the twin's. We also set back each part's own weight change, one at a time, and added the 28 shares: E100 found this "sum of single reverts" at most 0.40 on copying fine-tunes ("fed") and at least 0.62 on key-table and cipher fine-tunes ("written"). Two planted edits with known answers were read first, and read their whole gain at the edited MLP.

What we found. No part is located, in either unit: both arms need the same early parts (block 1's MLP takes about 0.7 of the gain on all four states), and the two parts that favour the real arm, block 3's MLP (0.49 and 0.46 against the twin's 0.15 and 0.16) and head (1,2), each fall under the 0.55 bar. The sum of single reverts separates the arms: 0.37 and 0.48 for the real fine-tune (E100's copying side) and 0.72 and 0.68 for the twin (its key-table side).

What it means. The part-by-part search agrees with the definition's "not through the capability" and finds no case-specific part. The sum points the other way, toward the real fine-tune using existing structure, but the twin was trained ten times longer and its update is about three times the real one's (summed norms 13.3 and 12.6 against 4.6 and 4.7), and E100's size check covered only a 5% spread, so this pair cannot separate the sum from update size and the sign is weak. The two readings are for baseline-lead to weigh; E114 adds no rule. Provisional: two seeds, one task, no independent check.
