Qwen3-1.7B: is E76's masked fall agreement or softness, and can a masked read credit a placement?

2026-11-04 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, $0 (jobs 51117, 51161, 51179) · experiments/qwen_masked_read · provisional: one update, one seed, 16 rows; independent check of the last amendment done

The question. D1 is a map on Qwen3-1.7B that keeps the decomposition faithful for a real update, judged by a test that can fail a bad placement (Q1). E76's masked term lowered the masked KL far, but that KL direction also rewards softer outputs. Is the fall agreement with the fine-tuned model, and does a low reading under the state's own masks credit a placement at all?

What we did. We read the saved changes from E76 against the fine-tuned model's clean output, on eight maths rows and eight web-text rows, under masks frozen at the original model's and under masks recomputed from each state's own forward. Do-nothing (the fine-tuned weights with the update left in Δ and nothing moved) was read the same way, and so was the original model's weights under the clean-term change's masks, with nothing placed. We also read two bad placements, the clean-term change with its columns permuted and E76's random-teacher control.

What we found. Recomputed masks did nearly all the work, whichever weights sat under them. The original model's unchanged weights under the clean-term change's masks read 0.094, against 1.334 under its own masks and 0.078 for the change itself; do-nothing reads 0.066, the lowest of all. Under frozen masks the all-masked and routed changes read mixed, neither agreement nor softness, and their fall does not hold on web text (first and third figures).

What it means. A low reading under the state's own masks can penalise harm but cannot credit a placement, so it is no test for D1, and the clean-term change has shown only clean agreement at 0.52 of the update's squared size. The clean-model table cannot fail do-nothing either, because do-nothing is the fine-tuned model's clean behaviour by construction. D1 has no test here that fails a bad placement near the fine-tuned model; that is the pending decision in section {{n:decisions}}.
