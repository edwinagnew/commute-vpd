Does the fixed change follow the cue, or the answer?

2026-10-22 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/many_cues

The cue. We mined 32 cue words the model already uses before " of". Trained each toward its own answer, one change per cue gives back 0.90 and one shared change 0.04. Trained all toward " of", one change per cue gives back 1.06 and one shared change 0.55 (first figure). Surprise: we leaned that one shared answer would make one shared change enough. Cues that share an answer share about half their change (second figure).

Run
From Pile rows, " of" had the most cue words the model already uses: 137 qualify, and the 32 most frequent were kept, about 47 training and 6 held-out inputs each. Three do-nothing states of E28's ten-step fine-tune: the 32 cues each toward its own other target with a similar starting probability; the same cues all toward " of"; and " will" → " have" as the one-cue control. Each read with one shared change, one change per cue and random groups, plus another input's values from the same or a different cue. Control first; it reproduced E28. Cluster job 29625, 197 s.
Found
  • Different answers: per cue 0.90, shared 0.04, random groups 0.04.
  • One answer: per cue 1.06, shared 0.55, random groups 0.54. Filed rule: "per cue only" on both, so the combined reading is "follows the cue", reversing our lean "follows the answer".
  • The shared half is real: fourteen times the different-answers state's 0.04. Different cues' changes overlap 0.46 of the within-cue ceiling with one answer, 0.16 without.
  • The different-answers fine-tune was weaker, reaching a held-out target probability of 0.56 against 0.81.
