Do steering and a neuron reading separate the built pairs where the re-gating share does?

2026-11-10 · provisional, no independent check · cluster debug L40, job 57802, $0 · experiments/holes_fill

The question. Part 2 asks whether a cheaper reading tells apart the two built sides of a task pair as the re-gating share does. The share is the part of a fine-tune's gain that comes from changing only which of the original subcomponents the decomposition switches on. On seven built pairs with saved fine-tunes, the neuron reading had never been taken and steering existed only in an older, weaker form. Do they separate the sides where the share does?

What we did. On every saved fine-tune of those pairs, two to eight seeds a side, we read steering and the neuron reading on the rows the share was read on. Steering adds the fine-tune's mean change in the block input to the original model, pooled over all training windows or one vector per key, with layer and strength tuned. The neuron reading keeps each block's top neurons. A pair separates when the gap between the sides is at least 0.1 of the gain and wider than the range inside a side. Sides as built; no verdict on reuse.

What we found. The share separates every pair. Steering separates two in the share's direction, copy vs random maps and copy vs cipher, and three the other way, with the side built to be new higher. The per-key vector reads close to the whole gain on both sides of every key-value pair, because each key fixes its answer. The neuron reading stays under 0.05 of the gain on every fine-tune, so it makes no call.

What it means. On these pairs neither activation reading reproduces what the share separates. Pooled steering reverses on both pairs built at equal update size, which this run does not explain. Per-key vectors are no test on lookup tables, and the neuron reading as built cannot compare the sides here.

Run
One debug job on the L40, about 12 minutes, $0, reading 56 saved fine-tunes. All controls passed first: a zero update read exactly 0, and the share and an earlier neuron reading reproduced exactly.
Found
  • As filed, steering separates fewer pairs than the share: two in its direction against six, with three the other way.
  • As filed, the neuron reading writes almost nothing, so it makes no call, and per-key vectors read above pooled ones on all 44 key-value fine-tunes.
  • Per-key random vectors read below zero on the reuse sides of three pairs, so that reading's null is off there.
