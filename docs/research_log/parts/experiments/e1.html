2026-10-14 · RunPod L40S · experiments/transport_test

The rescaling split of a fine-tune onto the existing pieces explains neither the parameters nor the behaviour.

Run
Two fine-tunes (emoticon; continued pretraining). Three splits onto the fixed pieces: the rescaling split by least squares, a fitted rescaling of every alive piece, and sparse write or read edits of the top-k pieces against k random ones.
Found
  • 0.06% of the emoticon update's energy lies in the span of the fixed pieces (0.05% for continued pretraining, the arm the prediction was filed for), against 0.035% for a random direction. Filed: ten times random. Reversed.
  • Ten random pieces with free write vectors reproduce the behaviour as well as the ten the rescaling split picks: no power at that size. Reversed.
  • The paper's emoticon piece ranks second in its matrix at step 10, with three of five siblings in the top eight, then disperses by step 30.
  • Stochastic faithfulness stayed within 0.94 to 1.02 under every transport.
  • The continued-pretraining fits failed to optimise (learning rate too large), found by the same-day audit.
