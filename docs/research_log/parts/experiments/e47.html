Do five claims a red team doubted still hold when each is re-read from several attacker starts, with the missing controls?

2026-10-29 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS · experiments/claim_tests

The question. The commute line looks for a simple map that carries a fine-tune onto the decomposition's pieces and stays faithful, without Δ, the weights outside the pieces, absorbing the update (Q1). Whether the optimiser matters is a side question (Q11). The best map so far is teacher training: the pieces are trained so the decomposition predicts like the fine-tuned model, with Γ and Δ frozen (E34). An independent red-team review then found five claims around it under-supported, mostly because each rested on one random start of the attacker.

What we did. Faithfulness is read by the adversarial ratio. An attacker searches over masks of the pieces for 20 steps to change the model's output on 256 Pile rows; the ratio is the damage it finds on a state over the damage on the unchanged decomposition, from the same random start, so the unchanged one reads 1. How much that unchanged reading moves between starts, f, sets the lines: pass at or under 1.5/(1+f), fail at or over 1.5(1+f), inconclusive between. On Pile rows 0-255 f is 0.21, giving pass 1.24 and fail 1.81; on rows 256-511, read only for claim (ii), it is 0.035, giving 1.45 and 1.55. Do-nothing, the reference, leaves the whole fine-tune in Δ. Each claim was re-read from two to four starts, after the unchanged decomposition and a state that must fail. The claims: (i) teacher training passes where do-nothing fails; (ii) a rank-8 LoRA's pass is not just its low rank; (iii) the scaled adjoint split, a one-shot formula, keeps less behaviour as the update grows, whatever the path; (iv) teacher training plus remainder, the uncopied rest of the fine-tune added to Δ, is worse than do-nothing; (v) teacher training needs Adam. The user decided the wording of (ii), (iv) and (v) the same day (below).

What we found. Claim (i) holds: on two tasks teacher training passes from every start, while do-nothing fails at its worst start (first figure). Claim (v) is rescoped: plain SGD passes at the three smaller settings on both teachers and fails at rate 0.03 for 1,000 steps (second figure), but a later check (E49) showed the test cannot fail SGD-trained updates on these rows, so SGD is unresolved. The LoRA passes on one of two row sets here (E49 adds four more blocks), and plus remainder is not shown worse than do-nothing (third and fourth figures). At the same update size, the largest relative change of any weight matrix, a faster run keeps 0.08 to 0.09 more under the split, on two seeds (fifth figure).

What it means. Teacher training stays the map for the weak version. "Needs Adam" is rescoped to the Adam full-matrix fine-tune, whose teacher training fails on all 8 Pile blocks read; the rank-8 Adam-trained LoRA passes (counted for rank 8 only, a rank sweep is in the backlog); plain SGD is unresolved. Teacher training plus remainder reproduces the fine-tuned weights at a faithfulness cost (2.5 and 1.9 times on the check, E43), a live candidate under the Δ criterion, not a verdict that it is worse than leaving the update in Δ. The caveat is breadth: one model and decomposition, two to four starts per state, only the adversarial check. One choice still waits for the user: how to word the split's loss of behaviour.

Run
The same 67M model and decomposition as E34 to E43, re-read in eleven jobs on the MATS cluster's L40 at no charge, each starting with the unchanged decomposition and a state that must fail. The last job (t4c) added teacher training's own step-10 state beside claim (iv)'s two.
Found
  • Teacher training passes from all four attacker starts on both tasks where do-nothing fails, and plain SGD passes at the three smaller settings on both teachers, but the check has no power on SGD-trained updates there (E49), so whether it needs Adam is unresolved; the Adam full-matrix fine-tune's teacher training fails on all 8 Pile blocks read.
  • Plus remainder is not shown worse than do-nothing: barely apart at step 1,000, and at step 10 its place against the two references flips with the start, which we did not expect and cannot yet explain; it stays a live candidate at a faithfulness cost.
  • The rank-8 LoRA passes on Pile rows 256-511 and is inconclusive on rows 0-255, with rank 128 climbing on the second set only (its pass counts at rank 8); at matched size the faster split run keeps 0.08 to 0.09 more on both of two seeds, where we expected no difference.
