The Qwen neuron control's size-matched reading depends on the random draw

2026-11-27 · cell P3.15 (filled), descriptive, red team passed · experiments/qwen_neuron_draws · three H100 pods, $32

The question. Part 3 repeats the neuron control on Qwen3-1.7B: each fine-tune's re-gating share (the part of its behaviour change carried by changed causal importances rather than by weight change inside the subcomponents) is set beside a random change to the same weights, scaled to the same KL divergence from the base model. The appendix sentence rested on one random draw per side. Does it survive more draws?

What we did. We drew two more random changes per side with the same recipe, for three pairs (copy against cipher, known against invented facts, real against shuffled maths solutions), each at the last save and at the save where the update was five times its first size. Before reading, the unchanged model had to read no change, the do-nothing state had to read a share of 1, and draw 0 had to reproduce its saved share; all held on all three pods. The size-matched reading scales the larger side's update down to the smaller side's size, then compares the two sides' gap across three fine-tune seeds with the random gap and a small seed-noise band.

What we found. At the saved sizes every fine-tune still reads above all three random changes. Once sizes are matched, which pair stands apart depends on the draw. Real against shuffled maths at the last save clears the band on draws 0 and 1 but not on draw 2, where the random gap is -0.006 against seed gaps of 0.004 to 0.006. Both copy-against-cipher saves clear it on draw 2 only, the one draw whose random gaps sit near zero. Known against invented facts clears it on no draw.

What it means. The random reference moves much more between draws than the seed noise used in the band (on copy against cipher, a range of 0.20 in the random gap against 0.002), so the neuron control does not separate the Qwen pairs at matched size in any stable way. The unmatched picture stands. The appendix sentence should be written per pair and per draw. A band built from the draw-to-draw spread needs about ten draws per side and a new cell.

Run
Three H100 pods, one per pair, about five minutes per random read and 36 reads in all, $32 of an $80 cap (four pods were lost to the idle watchdog during the W&B fetch). Draw 0 is the earlier draw, unchanged.
Found
  • Unmatched, every fine-tune seed reads above every random draw on all six sides; the closest is invented facts, 0.921 against 0.914.
  • Real against shuffled maths at the last save: all three seed gaps lie above all three random gaps, and it clears the band on two draws; the other five comparisons clear it on one draw or none.
  • Random shares spread widely on the copy side, 0.81 to 0.96.
