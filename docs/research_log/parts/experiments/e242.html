Under wording A, does R read every built reuse edit above both built new edits at VPD?

2026-11-20/21 · cell P1.46, definition-lead, no independent check · experiments/kstar_vpd_wording_a

The question. You adopted wording A on 2026-11-20: a fine-tune reuses the base model to the extent that the base model's mechanisms, each scaled by one output gain, can stand in for the fine-tune's at the variables the behaviour runs through, while the output stays within a tenth of the fine-tune's distance from the base model. The reuse score R is the largest share of the circuit's variables for which that stand-in works. Before reading any trained fine-tune, it had to pass the known-answer test on hand-built edits: every reuse edit above both new edits.

What we did. On the 67M model we read R at the VPD level on six built edits with known labels: reuse edits (a gain of 2 and a gain of 10 on some parts, a rebuilt circuit, a moved circuit), new edits (a computation written thinly over many parts, one written into a few parts), and random updates of matching size. A moved edit that changes the output by less than the measured noise floor is not read.

What we found. The filed rule failed, by a gap of -0.12. The gain-of-2 edit reads 1.00, the gain of 10 reads 0.976 and the rebuilt circuit 0.86, while the thin new edit reads 0.98 and the few-parts new edit 0.89. Random updates read low, 0.29 and 0.30, as filed. The share of changed parts that can stand in is 0.82 for the thin new edit and 0.62 for the rebuilt reuse edit, so the thin edit is not just diluted by untouched parts in the changed-only count.

What it means. Wording A as counted at VPD does not put every built reuse edit above both new edits, so no trained fine-tune can be read as reuse against new under it yet. An independent premise check doubts that the VPD pieces separate these built edits at all, so the failure may belong to the featurization and not to the wording (check: ~/commute-data/premise/P1.46.md). The pairs, copy against lookup and key-task stages held (they were conditional on a pass); a diagnosis of the thin edit (E247) and a pending decision for you followed, and the held stages were then read as descriptions (E248).
