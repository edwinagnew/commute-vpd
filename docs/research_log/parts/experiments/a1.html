Why the adversary breaks decompositions the random masks pass

2026-10-20 · from existing numbers · reports/analyses/2026-10-20_analysis_adversarial-vs-stochastic.md

VPD's adversarial check fails component training on three of five ladder tasks while the random-mask check barely moves. The adversary needs a selective mask: turning every subcomponent down together costs almost nothing, while its chosen mask does fifty to three hundred times the damage. That points at cancellation: the update is spread over many subcomponents whose changes only add up correctly together.

Found
  • The random-mask check masks about half the matrices at a time with random strengths, so a weakness that needs several specific subcomponents masked together is rarely sampled.
  • On " which" then " was": every subcomponent at its importance value costs 0.017 nats more than at the original model; the adversary's mask costs 0.87.
