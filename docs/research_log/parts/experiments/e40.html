Qwen3-1.7B: what does a fine-tune on GSM8K's solutions do to the existing decomposition, and can teacher training copy it?

2026-10-28 · RunPod · experiments/qwen_sft

The first run read the unchanged decomposition and the do-nothing state at steps 1 and 10, not yet teacher training. Doing nothing passes the web-text test at both steps, though the model's predictions on maths text have moved a lot. The fine-tune was not small at its first step, so a rerun with the standard warm-up recipe (Amendment 1) was planned; the Qwen re-plan holds it until simple maps of this update have been read (E44).

Run
The first run (no warm-up, about $13.00): the unchanged decomposition read on the model's own maths answers and on web text; a full-weight fine-tune of Qwen3-1.7B on GSM8K's human-written solutions at a constant 1e-5, with the update to the 196 decomposed matrices saved at small step counts; the do-nothing state scored by the paper's adversary at steps 1 and 10 only, because the run was stopped before step 100 for the recipe fix. Plan: reports/plans/2026-10-28_plan_qwen-sft.md.
Found
  • The control reproduced the web-text reading exactly. On the model's own maths answers the adversary reads 4.24 nats, 2.5 times its web-text reading, so maths text is scored against the unchanged decomposition's own maths reading.
  • 494,598 of 688,128 subcomponents are alive, which does not fit one H100 for teacher training. It goes to an H200 or B200 with the method unchanged.
  • The fine-tune moves the model's predictions on maths text by 0.11 nats per token at its first step. By step 100 accuracy has fallen from 0.788 to 0.627 on GSM8K and from 0.778 to 0.584 on GSM1k, a paired fall of 0.145 on the 1,248 problems kept at both. Doing nothing reads 1.05 at step 1 and 1.18 at step 10, so it passes.
