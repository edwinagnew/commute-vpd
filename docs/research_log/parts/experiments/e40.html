Qwen3-1.7B: what does a fine-tune on GSM8K's worked solutions do to the existing decomposition, and can teacher training copy it?

2026-10-28 · RunPod H100 · experiments/qwen_sft

The question. Every result so far came from a 67M-parameter test model with narrow fine-tunes. Q9 asks whether they carry over to a larger model with a real post-training update. This is the first update on Qwen3-1.7B, whose decomposition into pieces our port reproduces: a fine-tune on the human-written solutions of GSM8K, a set of school maths word problems. The plan was to read the unchanged decomposition and the do-nothing state first, then try teacher training, the map that copied narrow fine-tunes on the 67M model (E34, teacher training on narrow fine-tunes).

What we did. First we checked the unchanged decomposition on web text and on the model's own maths answers. The check is the paper's adversary, which searches for 20 steps over ways of switching pieces off for the one that most damages the model's predictions. Then we fine-tuned every weight of the 196 decomposed matrices (seven per layer) for 100 steps at a constant rate with no warm-up. Do-nothing leaves the whole weight change in Δ, the part of each matrix the pieces do not cover, and touches no piece. Its adversarial ratio is the adversary's damage on web text over the unchanged decomposition's on the same rows and start; at or under 1.5 passes, above 1.5 means the test sees the update.

What we found. The unchanged decomposition reproduced its earlier web-text reading, and the adversary did 2.5 times as much damage on maths text (first figure). Judged by the gold solutions' loss, the fine-tune was large from its first step: step 1 alone gave most of the 100 steps' fall in their log-loss, though its change to predictions on maths text was still small then, 0.11 nats per token (second figure), and accuracy fell on both maths test sets (third figure). Do-nothing still passed the web-text test, at 1.05 at step 1 and 1.18 at step 10 (fourth figure). Teacher training did not run: 494,598 of the 688,128 pieces are alive (importance above 0.01 somewhere on web or maths text), too many to train on one H100.

What it means. Do-nothing passed a test that reads web text only, so the pass says nothing about maths text, where the update acts. By that first step the fine-tune had already given 80% of its 100-step fall in the gold solutions' log-loss, so a pass on web text may say little about this kind of update. The fine-tune is also not the small update the project studies. The plan's reasoning, not tested here, is that at a constant rate with no warm-up Adam's first step moves every weight by the full rate. A rerun with the standard warm-up and teacher training on a larger card were planned. The Qwen re-plan holds both until E44, the next Qwen experiment, has asked whether the pass test can fail a bad placement, and has read simple splits of this update.

Run
One RunPod H100 run, one seed, about $13.00: the unchanged decomposition read on web text and maths text, a 100-step fine-tune of Qwen3-1.7B on GSM8K's solutions at a constant 1e-5 with the weight change saved at eight steps, and do-nothing scored at steps 1 and 10. The fine-tune ran all 100 steps and its accuracy was read at step 100, but the run was stopped before do-nothing was scored at step 100, for the recipe fix; teacher training never started, because the alive pieces need 73 GB of trainable offsets. Plan: reports/plans/2026-10-28_plan_qwen-sft.md.
Found
  • The control reproduced the earlier web-text reading, and on the model's own maths answers the adversary did 2.5 times as much damage, so maths text is scored against the unchanged decomposition's own maths reading.
  • On one seed, the fine-tune without warm-up was large at its first step by the gold-solution loss, giving 1.57 of the 1.98 nats its gold log-loss fell by step 100, (its KL to the start on maths text was only 0.11 then), and lowered accuracy by step 100, from 0.788 to 0.627 on GSM8K.
  • Do-nothing passed the web-text test at steps 1 and 10 (ratios 1.05 and 1.18, pass line 1.5); that test reads web text only, do-nothing was not scored on maths text, and step 100 was not read.
