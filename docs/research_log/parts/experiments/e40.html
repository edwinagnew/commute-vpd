Run
The first run (no warm-up, about $13.00): the unchanged decomposition read on the model's own maths answers and on web text; a full-weight fine-tune of Qwen3-1.7B on GSM8K's human-written solutions at a constant 1e-5, with the update to the 196 decomposed matrices saved at small step counts; the do-nothing state scored by the paper's adversary at steps 1, 10 and 100.
