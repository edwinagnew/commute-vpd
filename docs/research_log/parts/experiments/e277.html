Does the write share over the needed set still sort reuse from new when the variables are neurons?

2026-11-23 · cell P1.56, definition-lead, red team: separates built edits, orders no trained update · experiments/needed_neurons

The question. The definition's reading, the write share (how much of a fine-tune's change at the variables it needs is a pure turning up or down of the original model's own output), was taken with the decomposition's parts as variables. Neurons are the featurization many interpretability readers have in mind, so the same reading at neurons is a natural alternative to plug in (part 1, claim 1).

What we did. On the 67M model a neuron is a variable on a token only when its output is above its 97th percentile on Pile text; everything else an MLP site writes on that token is one remainder variable per site, and each of the 24 attention heads is a variable (12,316 variables, fixed from the original model). A variable is needed when swapping its value for the one it takes on another input changes the fine-tune's top token (sampled on 256 input pairs). We scored the built edits (the control), E229's 20 fine-tunes against matched random updates, the E62 copying and lookup pair, and the realistic panel of 10 readable tasks against the raters' tiers. The identity swap flipped no token and the do-nothing read exactly 1.

What we found. The edits built to strengthen existing computation read 1.00 and the other built edits 0.005 to 0.048, so they separate, but that gap rests on edits that change nine variables. Every trained fine-tune reads above its own random update (1.5 to 10.3 times, at 0.014 to 0.038), yet the reuse-minus-new gap per pair (-0.008 to 0.013) is about the spread between two seeds, E62 copy and lookup read 0.018 and 0.015, and the panel shows no order with the tiers (rank correlation -0.35, p 0.23, against -0.70 at the decomposition's parts). The four whole-MLP remainders carry 0.53 to 0.80 of the write change, so the reading is mainly theirs.

What it means. At neurons the write share tells an update from noise, not reuse from new. One head does separate copying from lookup (layer 2 head 4 keeps 0.09 to 0.14 of its change as a rescaling against 0.015 to 0.020) but it is under 1 percent of the write change, which is why the pooled share does not see it. No reuse or new verdict is given under any wording.

Run
Four pods plus a cluster make-up re-read of two panel items (agreement to 2e-5 relative); descriptive, no filed bar.
Found
  • Built strengthening edits separate from every other built edit (0.98 above the highest), as at the decomposition.
  • No trained update orders reuse above new: fine-tune pair gaps sit inside the seed spread, E62 reads 0.018 against 0.015, panel rank correlation -0.35.
  • Whole-MLP remainders carry most of the write change, so this is mainly their reading; Category and conll are too small to read (needed set under 100).
