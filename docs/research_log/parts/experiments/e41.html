Can teacher training write a fine-tune stopped early into the pieces, and keep up when it is applied again and again along training?

2026-10-28 · RunPod H100 · experiments/teacher_training

The question. Teacher training became the commute line's map in E34 (Q1): it trains the decomposition's pieces, with Γ and the residual Δ frozen, until the decomposition predicts what a fine-tuned model predicts. E34 tried it only on finished, 1,000-step fine-tunes. The project wants to read an update continuously along training (Q8), so the map must also work on the small updates of the first steps, and must not pile up error when it is applied again at each checkpoint.

What we did. We took the Adam fine-tunes on two trigger tasks, emoticon (an emoticon is followed by the letter o) and ` which` then ` was`, stopped them at steps 10, 100 and 1,000, and teacher-trained the pieces against each. Teacher training ran on the task's own text, or on ordinary Pile text. A chain starts each teacher training from the previous checkpoint's result instead of the unchanged decomposition: first three links of 1,000 student steps, then seven links of 100 steps from step 1 to step 1,000. The reference is doing nothing: the fine-tune's whole weight change left in Δ. Faithfulness is read by the adversarial ratio. An adversary searches for the masks over the pieces and Δ that do the most damage on Pile text; the ratio divides that damage by what it does to the unchanged decomposition. 1 means no change. The lines sit 7.5% either side of 1.5, that 7.5% being how much the unchanged decomposition's own reading moves across adversary starts on this GPU: 1.40 or less passes, 1.61 or more fails, and in between is inconclusive.

What we found. The check can tell a map from doing nothing only where doing nothing does not pass: at step 10, where it fails, and at step 1,000, where it is inconclusive. At step 10 teacher training sits at the pass line. On emoticon three batch orders gave one pass and two inconclusive readings, none failing (first figure). In one run on emoticon, the chain of short links passed at the two checkpoints the check can see, clearly at step 1,000 and near the line at step 10; at the other five a pass is no evidence (second figure). Every teacher-trained state kept the target probability at the trigger (third figure). The seven-link chain of 100-step links ended within the size and damage limits set before it ran, narrowly: damage 1.20 times a single teacher training's against a limit of 1.25. The earlier three-link chain of 1,000-step links had grown further; the two differ in link count as well as link length (fourth figure).

What it means. On one emoticon run, teacher training applied every 100 steps passed at the two checkpoints the check can read and stayed inside its size and damage limits, both by small margins, but its weakest point is the smallest update, at step 10. That rests on one fine-tune of one task, one seed and one adversary start per reading, so whether the step-10 spread is batch order or the adversary is untested. The user accepted that the step-10 state sits at the pass line, and left carrying the 100-step rhythm to the larger model to the Qwen plan.

Run
E34's Adam fine-tunes of all 24 matrices were rebuilt, teacher-trained at steps 10, 100 and 1,000 and in a three-link chain (43 minutes); then the emoticon step-10 state at two more batch orders, and the seven-link short chain. Three RunPod H100 jobs, about $3, $1.1 and $2.1, every reading on one 20-step adversary start over 256 Pile rows, controls first and all passing.
Found
  • Every teacher-trained state keeps the behaviour with Δ untouched, at 0.90 or more; from Pile text the fine-tunes stopped at steps 10 and 100 were reproduced better than the finished one.
  • At step 10, the one step where leaving the update in Δ fails, emoticon teacher training on task text read one pass and two inconclusive at three batch orders, never failing, and removed 64% to 82% of the excess doing nothing shows; the ` which` task, at one batch order, passed by about 1%.
  • On the emoticon fine-tune, teacher training applied every 100 steps from step 1 to step 1,000 kept the target probability at all seven checkpoints, passed where the check can tell (at step 10 by only about 5% under the line, 1.32 against 1.40, on one run and one adversary start), and ended with damage 1.2 times a single 1,000-step teacher training's, inside the limit; the chain of 1,000-step links had ended at 1.5 times.
