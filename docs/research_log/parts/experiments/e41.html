Does teacher training copy a fine-tune stopped early, and can it be applied again along training?

2026-10-28 · RunPod H100 · experiments/teacher_training

Partly. Copies of the fine-tune stopped at steps 10 and 100 keep its behaviour with Δ untouched, but the adversarial check can tell a copy from doing nothing only at step 10, and there the reading sits at the pass line: on emoticon, three copies trained at different batch orders gave one pass and two inconclusive readings, none failing (first and fourth figures). A chain of three copies along the fine-tune ended within the filed tolerances of a single copy, with a larger weight change (third figure).

Run
E34's Adam fine-tune of all 24 matrices on emoticon and on " which" then " was", regenerated and stopped at steps 10, 100 and 1,000. Each copied by teacher training from the unchanged decomposition (E34's recipe, Γ and Δ frozen, 1,000 steps), from task text on both tasks and from Pile text on emoticon. A chain warm-starts each copy from the previous one: step 10, then 100, then 1,000. Every state read through the Pile harness (one 20-step adversary start, 256 rows) beside its do-nothing state. Controls first, all passed. One H100 job of 43 minutes, about $3.
Found
  • Every copy keeps the behaviour with Δ untouched, and from Pile text the fine-tunes stopped at steps 10 and 100 are copied better than the finished one (0.998 against 0.90), against a filed low lean.
  • At step 10, the one step where leaving the update in Δ fails the check, the emoticon copy reads 1.55, 1.28 and 1.51 at three batch orders: one pass and two inconclusive, none failing, each removing 64% to 82% of the excess doing nothing shows. The copies agree on every other number and differ by up to a fifth in what the adversary finds on one block of 128 Pile rows; with one adversary start each, whether that is batch order or the adversary is untested. Two filed predictions failed: a pass at order 0 (E41), and a spread within 11% (Amendment 1).
  • The chain stayed within the filed tolerances of one copy on every scored number, but its weight change and its damage elsewhere grew relative to the one-shot copy's from the second link to the third in all three groups, which was not filed.
