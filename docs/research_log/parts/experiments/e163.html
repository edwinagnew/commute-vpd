Can reinforcement learning on Qwen3-1.7B grow its update to a readable size while the model still improves?

2026-11-12 · run 1 of 3, provisional, no independent check · five RunPod pods (four H100, one H200) and one cluster job (59301), $50.82 · experiments/qwen_rl_readable

The question. Part 4 reads a reinforcement-learning update beside supervised fine-tunes on Qwen3-1.7B (section {{n:next}}, cell P3.4). The saved reinforcement-learning updates from E69 change the model's output about as much as the decomposition's own error does, so any reading of them sits in the noise. Is there a training recipe whose update is five or ten times that error while the model still answers held-out maths well?

What we did. We ran E69's reinforcement-learning loop on school maths (GSM8K) from the unchanged model at three peak learning rates (1e-6, 3e-6, 1e-5), one seed. The output change is the KL divergence from the unchanged model to the trained one, per token, on 128 maths rows; the decomposition's own error on those rows is 0.0932 nats per token, and the multiple is the ratio. A state counted only if its held-out pass rate was at least 0.842 (the unchanged model reads 0.782) and at least 0.9215 of its answers fit the generation window. Two controls ran at E69's rate: the same loop with shuffled rewards, and a plain fine-tune on the model's own answers.

What we found. No recipe reached five times with both bars held. The middle rate reached 2.65 times at step 150 (pass 0.891) and 2.89 times at step 200 (pass 0.850). The highest rate reached 5.23 times in 25 steps with pass 0.890, but only 0.829 of its answers fit the window, so the run stopped there. The repeat of E69 matched its update norm (0.352 against 0.351) and pass rate at step 100, but its output change was 0.048 where the saved E69 update reads 0.064; the same measuring code on the saved E69 update gives 0.064, so the two are different updates of one norm.

What it means. Part 4 can read a reinforcement-learning update at about two to three times the noise floor; five times needs a longer run or a changed recipe, and what recipe the main run trains is a question for you. The shuffled-reward null at the same step moved the output more than the reinforcement-learning arm (0.78 times against 0.51) while losing accuracy, so the size of the output change alone cannot separate them. Output change varies up to about two times between runs at one step, so states are matched by step within a seed only.

Run
Five pods in parallel from the unchanged model: reinforcement learning at three rates, the null and the fine-tune at E69's rate. The measure was then checked on E69's saved updates on the cluster at no cost. About 66 s a step for the reinforcement-learning arm; $50.82 of the cell's $200 RunPod allowance.
Found
  • Best eligible state: 2.65 times at step 150 (peak 3e-6); the filed selection rule reads "not reached" for 5 and 10 times.
  • The high rate failed on answer length, not accuracy: 7.8 per cent of its answers were cut at the window, against at most 4 per cent elsewhere, and its pass rate never fell below the unchanged model's.
  • E69's rate grew slower than expected (0.51 times at step 100 and 1.36 at step 250, against 0.7 and 1.5 to 2.5), and the output change is not monotone along a run.
