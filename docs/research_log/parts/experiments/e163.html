Can reinforcement learning on Qwen3-1.7B grow its update to a readable size while the model still improves, and is it apart from a null?

2026-11-13 · runs 1 to 3 of 3, provisional, no independent check · RunPod H100 pods and cluster L40 reads · experiments/qwen_rl_readable (run 2 is E167, run 3 is E178)

The question. Part 4 reads a reinforcement-learning update beside supervised fine-tunes on Qwen3-1.7B (section {{n:next}}, cell P3.4). The saved reinforcement-learning updates from E69 change the model's output about as much as the decomposition's own error does, so any reading of them sits in the noise. Can the update be grown to a size the decomposition can see while the model still answers held-out maths well, and at that size is its pass rate above that of a null trained the same way with the rewards made meaningless?

What we did. The output change is the KL divergence from the unchanged model to the trained one, per token, on 128 maths rows; the decomposition's own error on those rows is 0.0932 nats per token, and the multiple is the ratio. Run 1 tried three peak learning rates on one seed. Run 2 trained the one you chose, 3e-6, to step 400 on two seeds, three ways: reinforcement learning on school maths (GSM8K), the same loop with the rewards shuffled among each problem's answers (the shuffled-reward null), and plain training on the model's own correct answers. States were saved where reinforcement learning crossed 2, 5 and 10 times the error, and the null and the fine-tune were saved at the same steps. Run 3 trained only the null, at a third of the rate (1e-6) where earlier nulls had held, to be stopped where its output change matched reinforcement learning's saved state. The held-out pass rate is greedy accuracy on 1319 GSM8K test problems; the unchanged model reads 0.782.

What we found. Reinforcement learning reached twice the error at step 75 on both seeds, and five times on seed 70 only, with the pass rate rising to about 0.88. But no working null exists to set that against: at 3e-6 the null's answers shrank from about 190 tokens to under 10 within 35 steps on both seeds, and at 1e-6 the seed-0 null held to about step 65 and collapsed by step 90, short of the target size, while the seed-70 null was stopped at step 20 by the filed check on its falling reward. The filed verdict is no call, and the cause of the collapse was not checked. Every update of every kind sits outside the decomposition's subcomponents as much as a random change of the same size does.

What it means. Part 4 has reinforcement-learning updates at two and five times the noise floor, read on the decomposition, but the behaviour question stays open: the gain from 0.78 to 0.88 was never compared with a working null. The three runs are spent, so the cell closes as read; a further null would need a new cell from you.
