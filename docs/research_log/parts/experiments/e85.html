Can VPD's adversary fail a bad state on Qwen3-1.7B's first update?

2026-11-04 · Vast H200 NVL, about $10.6 for the first read, about $7 for the controls · experiments/qwen_d1_teeth · provisional: the independent check covers the first read only

The question. D1 needs a test that can fail a bad placement of an update. VPD's adversary passed doing nothing on the first fine-tune's update (E80), so we did not know whether it can fail anything on this model.

What we did. We read the adversary on E80's rows, settings and code, with the unchanged model read first on the same host. First on two states built from the update: the weights plus a random change at the update's size, kept in Δ (the weights outside the subcomponents), and the update placed on the half of the subcomponents least often on, with the remainder in Δ. Then, after a review found neither state was known to be wrong, on two controls built to be wrong: the same random change at 100 times the size, and the unchanged weights with the rarely-on half's columns of U shuffled among themselves. The pass bar is a ratio of 1.40 to the unchanged model and the fail bar 1.61.

What we found. The adversary passed both states built from the update (random change 1.01, rarely-on placement 1.27 as a mean of two seeds; 1.16 at the seed the controls used). It failed both controls far past the bar, on one seed: the random change at 100 times reads 32.5 and the shuffled columns 11.3. So the test can fail on this model. But the shuffled columns differ from the right ones by 0.88 of the columns' own size, while the update moves them 0.0012 to 0.0036, so the failure came at 250 to 740 times the update's size. The adversary's search was still climbing at the filed 20 steps, and rounding of the readout to bf16 is not what limits it (a $0 check).

What it means. The adversary can fail states on this model, but only shown at sizes hundreds of times a real update's; whether it fails a bad placement at a real update's size is unmeasured, and a placement of that size passing does not make it good. The earlier reading that it had no power here is withdrawn. D1 is not judged by the adversary: you decided on 2026-11-05 that it is a stated limit beside the other checks.
