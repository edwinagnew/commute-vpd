With every matrix of Qwen3-1.7B masked, does a change the forward computes itself reach the logits, and does masking fewer matrices let the update through?

2026-11-03 · MATS cluster L40, normal QoS, $0 (jobs 50608, 50613, 50616) · experiments/qwen_open_points

The question. E74 found that adding the whole fine-tuning update on top of a masked forward hardly moves the logits (Q1; D1 is a map on Qwen3-1.7B that keeps the decomposition faithful for a real update, judged by a test that can fail a bad placement). Its independent check left three points open: is the loss about the update or about any small change, is masking all 196 matrices at once the extreme case, and does the update move the forward under masks made on the fine-tuned model?

What we did. Nothing was trained. On saved fine-tuning updates we added a small multiple of the masked forward's own output at one block (a change the forward computes itself) and read how much of it reaches the logits, as amplitude: the masked logit shift's size over the clean one's, where the clean model reads 1. We masked 196, 147, 98, 49, 12 or no matrices per position, and the library's random number, to see how fast the update gets through. We also took masks from one row and tokens from the next, and read how far the update moves the forward under masks made on the fine-tuned model.

What we found. With all 196 matrices masked by the original model's masks, Δ off, small changes in the stream barely reach the logits at early blocks, against our lean: the own-output change reads amplitude 0.05 to 0.11 at blocks 0 and 7, where we filed above 0.5 (first figure). With another row's tokens under them, those masks keep the output within about 0.5 nats of the masks' own row, and how much it still depends on its tokens is not measured (second figure). With 12 matrices masked, 0.14 to 0.67 of the update's effect passes, against 0.0004 to 0.013 with all 196 (third figure).

What it means. A masked loss term with all matrices masked can hardly teach early blocks, so E76 compares how many matrices its loss masks; that is an inference from forward reads, no gradient was read. Why the own-output change fails is unexplained. The independent check narrowed our provisional reading: neither "the masks carry the output" nor "the forward barely reads its input" is shown.

Run
Reads on saved states, 32 rows of 256 tokens, three jobs on one L40, $0; two stopped on filed rules and were amended, so both amendments are spent.
Found
  • The own-output change fails at early blocks (0.05 to 0.11 against a lean above 0.5), and the last-matrix control read 0.59, not near 1, because the final norm is read at two different points; no bug found.
  • Masking fewer matrices lets far more through: the ratio of 49 masked to 196 masked reads 9.65, 17.1, 13.4 and 29.6; the first cell gets no call.
  • Under masks made on the fine-tuned model the update moves the forward by 0.0005 to 0.009 of the clean model's own move, small as leaned.
