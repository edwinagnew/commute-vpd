At the template fine-tune's size, has each Qwen update learned what it is meant to teach?

2026-11-05 · cluster L40 ($0) · experiments/qwen_size_grid · provisional, not independently checked, no figure here

Since then. The 2026-11-09 paper plan (a definition of reuse, re-gating against baselines on the 67M, then Qwen3-1.7B) replaced the framing in deliverables and parallel lines used here.

The question. The Qwen comparison reads updates that teach content the model lacks beside the template fine-tune (the answer-format fine-tune), but the TOFU update ends 37 times larger. The amended plan leaned on the step where each arm matches the template fine-tune's size. Is there such a state for every arm, and has the TOFU update learned its facts there?

What we did. For every arm (both example updates at two seeds, reinforcement learning and its shuffled-reward null at two seeds, fine-tuning on the model's own correct samples, and the two earlier maths fine-tunes) we found the saved state nearest the template fine-tune's full update, in norm (0.487) and in KL to the unchanged model on maths rows (0.096). TOFU had no state near it, so both seeds were retrained and saved at steps 27 to 30, and then retrained again and saved at their first state past a learning line (0.585 of new words returned in the trained wording, halfway from the unchanged model's 0.179 to the full update's 0.99).

What we found. Every arm has a named state, but the five reinforcement-learning, null and own-samples runs stop short of the norm by step 100. At the template's size the TOFU update reads like the unchanged model on its facts (trained-wording recall 0.157 to 0.173 against 0.179), and its smallest saved state holding them, step 550 at one seed and step 500 at the other, is about 30 times the template's norm (31.9 and 30.2). Rephrased recall there is 0.43 to 0.44, against 0.17 unchanged and 0.53 for the full update.

What it means. The two examples cannot be compared at equal size while both have done their job, so the matched step is a size control only, and any claim that an update adds something new rests on the full update with its size stated. You chose this as a weak endorsement, not having fully followed the question. The first state past the line is not sharp: two cards read seed 42's step 500 at 0.583 and 0.570, either side of the line. Reads through the decomposition at these states are qwen-decomp-lead's.

Three cluster L40 jobs in debug, $0: the grid (1,051 s), the retrain of both seeds with answers (1,072 s), and the retrain to the first state past the line (1,324 s). Saved states are under experiments/qwen_size_grid/results/large/.
