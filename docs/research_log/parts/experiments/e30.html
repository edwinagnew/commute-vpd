Can one fixed change to Γ's values, applied everywhere, serve as the map?

2026-10-22 · RunPod 4090 · experiments/gamma_shift_map

No. Adding the fine-tune's fixed change to Γ's values at every position, with the subcomponents and Δ untouched, still reproduces the trigger behaviour as well as applying it at the trigger alone (first figure). But on Pile text an adversary then finds five to twenty-six times the damage it finds with no change, and the sparsity cost doubles or triples (second figure). One input-independent change is not a map; the change has to depend on the input.

Run
Three ten-step fine-tunes of all 24 matrices: emoticon by SGD at 0.01 and by Adam at 3e-5, and " which" then " was" (r1 t534) by SGD at 0.03, each with a null fine-tune to " http" at the same settings. The map keeps every subcomponent and Δ as for the unchanged model and adds the task's fixed change, the mean shift of Γ's values at the trigger, to Γ's output at every position. Scored against the fine-tuned model on 256 Pile rows beside four arms: no change, the null's change, the change shuffled, and do-nothing. Controls passed exactly: the new scoring path at no change matched the ordinary harness, and the three fine-tunes matched the earlier trigger readings. About $0.5.
Found
  • At the trigger the change applied everywhere recovers 0.75 on both emoticon updates and 0.44 on r1, as at the trigger alone. Filed, passed.
  • On the Pile the adversary's damage is 5 to 26 times the no-change reading on the three updates, and the sparsity loss 1.8 to 3.3 times the unchanged model's.
  • The null's change fails the adversary as badly; the shuffled change, at the same sparsity cost, barely moves it.
  • The CI-masked KL rises 12% and 33% on emoticon but falls 3% on r1, against a filed rise on all three.
  • A few hundred of the 9,881 alive subcomponents carry the change's effect at the trigger: the top 300 entries give 0.87 to 0.98 of it.
