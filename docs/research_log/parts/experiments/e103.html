With only the label changed, do the neuron readings tell a fine-tune on facts the model knows from one on facts it lacks?

2026-11-06 · provisional · six cluster debug jobs, $0 · experiments/known_answer_pair

The question. Every Qwen reading so far was scored against labels that came with four other differences (task, rows, recipe, size). If Qwen3-1.7B is fine-tuned on 217 real-person and world facts, which the unchanged model already picks at 0.79 in multiple choice, with the recipe of the fictional-author fine-tune, only the label should differ. Do the neuron readings of E102 (prompt overlap, engaged share) separate that pair beyond what the seeds and a Gaussian random change give?

What we did. We fine-tuned on the real facts with every answer written as a sentence, as the fictional run's are, and read the state whose total weight change matches the template fine-tune's (step 32 of 335), with the gain counted on answer words only. Then we repeated the known-facts run with a second data-order seed, so each kind of training has two seeds. We compared the four summary neuron readings, and the identity of the 1,024 neurons carrying each update (its carrying set), within each kind and across the two.

What we found. Both known-facts seeds read level with the fictional-facts update on all four summary neuron readings. Which neurons carry each update does separate the two kinds of training beyond seed noise: the two seeds of one kind share about nine tenths of their carrying neurons (0.86 and 0.90 on the real rows, 0.90 and 0.94 on the fictional rows), but a known-facts and a fictional run share at most 0.49 on the real rows and 0.64 on the fictional rows. The earlier apparent loss of real facts at the end of training was the end-of-turn token in a bare-phrase test, not lost facts.

What it means. A candidate neuron-level signal of reuse against new knowledge, and only a candidate: the two kinds also train on different rows, and the control with the same label on different rows is not run. E103 has used both amendments, so that control needs a new plan. qwen-decomp-lead's reads of these states inherit the caveat.
