Does the adjoint split of a plain-SGD fine-tune keep the behaviour beyond one task?

2026-10-22 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/adjoint_sgd

On emoticon yes, on two other tasks no. The adjoint split of an SGD fine-tune keeps most of the emoticon behaviour on a second seed, where the small Adam fine-tune's loses it, but on two word tasks it loses the behaviour as fast as the large Adam fine-tune's (first figure). Every adjoint state stays as faithful as the original model with Δ fixed (third figure). The share of the update the split reproduces does not explain the emoticon difference (second figure).

Run
Emoticon fine-tunes of all 24 decomposed matrices for 1,000 steps on two seeds: SGD at rate 0.01, Adam at 3e-6 (small) and at 3e-5 (large). On r1_t534_v369 (" which" then " was") and r0_t352_v310 (" it" then " is"): SGD at the rate a step-10 rule picked, 0.03 on both because 0.01 learned too little by step 10, and Adam 3e-5. Each update at steps 10, 100 and 1,000 placed by do-nothing and the scaled adjoint split, read on held-out probability of the target, the adversarial ratio (pass up to 1.24 on this card), Δ's share of the update and the reproduced share. A budget sweep to 160 adversary steps on the step-1,000 emoticon states. Controls passed: the original model at its known values, the seed-0 states matched the earlier emoticon run within 0.05, the large update's reproduced share matched the LoRA split study's (0.58, 0.42, 0.31). Six debug jobs, one a rerun of a crashed shard, $0.
Found
  • Second emoticon seed: SGD's adjoint state keeps 0.57 of the target probability at step 1,000, small Adam's 0.21 (seed 0: 0.60 and 0.24). Filed, passed.
  • Reversal of a filed prediction: on r1 and r0 the SGD adjoint state was filed to keep at least half the behaviour. It keeps 0.07 and 0.26, about the large Adam state's 0.05 and 0.23. Task and rate are confounded: there SGD ran at three times emoticon's rate, and by step 1,000 its update is nearly as large as the large Adam one's (largest relative matrix change about 0.03, against 0.012 on emoticon).
  • All 30 adjoint states pass the adversary, 0.93 to 1.08; Δ's share stays below 0.0001 in each.
  • The SGD emoticon state's pass holds at 40, 80 and 160 adversary steps (1.01, 1.00, 1.00). Filed, passed. The small Adam state's ratio to the unchanged model is 1.01 to 1.02 at the same budgets. That is inside the unchanged model's own seed noise of 1.13 to 1.21; reported, not scored.
  • The filed mechanism failed: SGD's reproduced share is 0.33 to 0.63, never the filed 0.8 or more, and below small Adam's at step 10. Kept behaviour ranks with it over all states (rank correlation 0.81, filed pass), but within one step only 0.62 to 0.69, so it mostly tracks training length.
