If the answers are made wrong but everything else is kept, do the carrying neurons stay where they were?

2026-11-06 · coded, not run · one cluster debug job, about 16 min, $0 · experiments/label_vs_style

The question. E103 and E105 found that which neurons carry an update separates fine-tuning on facts Qwen3-1.7B already knows from fine-tuning on invented authors, and that swapping the rows within the known facts barely moves the set. Answer style and topic (real people against invented authors) still differ between the two arms. Is the separation about the label, whether the model already holds the fact, or about style and topic?

What we did (planned). Train on the known arm's exact 217 real rows, five wordings and writer, with the true answer replaced by a wrong one taken from the TOFU data (seeds 42 and 142), so only whether the model holds the fact changes. Beside it, a topic split of the known rows (real-author rows against world-fact rows, two seeds). Both are read at the step where the update's size matches, on both probes. Controls run first: the gain and overlap control, the untrained model's level on the true sentence, and a check that the untrained model prefers the true sentence over the wrong one in at least 0.75 of rows, else stop.

What we expect. The wrong-answer arm reads "familiarity" if every overlap with the known arm stays within the rows-null band; the topic split should not move the set. Both are leans, filed before the run.
