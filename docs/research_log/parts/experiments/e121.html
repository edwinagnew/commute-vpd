Why does teacher training land far from the fine-tune's weight change?

2026-11-06 · provisional, no independent check · one cluster debug L40 job (54638), about 19 minutes, $0 · experiments/copy_gap_ladder

The question. E34 Amendment 10 found that on the emoticon task teacher training keeps the fine-tune's behaviour but its weight change sits at cosine 0.38 to 0.40 to the fine-tune's, where a rerun sits at 0.83. Which difference between teacher training and the fine-tune causes the distance?

What we did. We retrained the student six ways, changing one difference at a time: learning the fine-tuned model's outputs instead of the labels, the lower learning rate (1e-5 against 3e-5), training only the decomposition's U and V, plain SGD in place of Adam, and starting from the fine-tune's least-squares split. Each arm ran at two batch orders. All controls passed.

What we found. A plain student of the fine-tuned model lands at 0.87, as close as a rerun. Training only U and V drops it by 0.28 and the lower rate by 0.19. Teacher training's change needs its remainder (the target's probability falls to 0.28 without it), and its trigger logit change is further from the fine-tune's than a rerun's (0.64 against 0.86). Started from the least-squares split it stays at 0.81, with a gap of 0.0035 nats against 0.029.

What it means. Teacher training keeps the fine-tune's target probability by a different weight change and a different logit change, not by the fine-tune's change plus an idle part. Claims on teacher-trained decompositions stand for the target's probability, not for the update or the function. No reuse verdict is given.
