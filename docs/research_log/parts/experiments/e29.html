Are the subcomponents a fine-tune turns down the original model's machinery for the competing tokens?

2026-10-22 · RunPod RTX 4090 · experiments/regating_down

Not as a rule. The subcomponents whose importance the fixed change lowers at the trigger overlap the original model's machinery for the task's five competing tokens no more than a random set of the same size (first figure). Lowering them does take most of the competitors' probability on emoticon and on " will" then " be", but on " which" then " was" it adds two thirds to it (second figure). So "switch off the competitors" is not a general description of the turned-down half, and the fixed-shift hypothesis gains no mechanism sentence.
