Does re-gating follow whether the model's existing copying mechanism carries a new fine-tune, or only whether the answer was already likely?

2026-11-02 · MATS cluster L40 debug QoS, $0 (jobs 48507, 48541, 48698) · experiments/induction_cue

The question. You decided on 2026-11-02 that "elicitation" means the fine-tuned behaviour runs through a mechanism the original model already has, so re-gating's share r is a candidate to score against a causal check, not the definition (Q5). On the small model r follows how likely the original model already was to give the answer (E58), which cannot tell the two apart. This is the first test where "old mechanism" and "answer already given" come apart.

What we did. The small model already copies in context: after "A B ... A" it says B. We teach it to copy at a new marker, where it does not copy yet, and beside that teach a lookup at the same marker on the same text. We switch off the head the model copies with: the copy fine-tune should lose most of what it learned and the lookup little. Then we read r on both. A second, training-free read switched the same head off inside the masked model, the original pieces under the masks Γ gives, with the masks held as they were with the head on.

What we found. With the copying head off, the copy map lost 0.56, 0.55 and 0.47 of its gain (bar 0.5) and the lookup nothing, so the filed verdict reads copy_not_elicitation while the numbers say "about half", and r was not scored because one seed fell short of the bar. With the masks frozen, switching that head off costs the fine-tuned model about half its gain (0.47 to 0.56) but the masked model about a tenth (0.10 to 0.11). Unscored, r reads about 1.09 on the copy map against 0.40 on the lookup, while the two maps are close in prior (within a factor 1.3). Scaling each update to the other map's size barely moves that gap (0.691 to 0.686).

What it means. The masked read could not test whether r's gain depends on the old head: the frozen masks are the update's only way into that model, and the masked model never holds the fine-tuned head. So whether r reads the old mechanism on this map is untested. Update size does not explain the r gap over the small range read, and the copy map's target is in its context, so the gap is not credited to the mechanism either. Amendment 3, which recomputes the masks with the head off, is coded and running at $0. No stop has been taken on this line's 67M copying work. The granularity limit stands: a new circuit built from old pieces reads as elicited.
