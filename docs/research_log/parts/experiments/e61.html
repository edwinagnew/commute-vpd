Does the Qwen fine-tunes' direct share come from size alone, and do the fine-tunes learn when trained as they are read?

2026-11-02 · MATS cluster L40, $0 (jobs 48367 to 49673) · experiments/qwen_recipe

The question. On Qwen3-1.7B (Q3, Q9) the direct share (the part of the removable error that goes away by changing only the subcomponents, masks held) looked higher for real fine-tunes than for random changes (E57). Two audits said this may only be size, and that training rounded its weights so it may not have seen an update's first small steps, while every reading used full precision. The line needed a fine-tune that learns under the forward it is read with, and a direct share compared with random changes of the same size.

What we did. Saved states were first read three ways (full precision, training's rounded forward, rounded weights read in full precision). Then every fine-tune was retrained with a full-precision forward: human solutions to maths problems, the template task (a re-used skill), invented facts (a new one), and the own-answers run. A rehearsal on two rows ran every stage of the size test, with a resume, before the scored run. The scored run read the human-solutions fine-tune at step 8 and step 100 against two kinds of random change (Gaussian, and a change in the subcomponents that are alive), each scaled up to the fine-tune's size along a line in weight space. A rule filed first sorted the outcome into one line; an independent re-computation reproduced it.

What we found. Rounding hid the early update (own-answers step 4 reads 0.441 held-out loss in full precision, 0.055 as trained), and every retrained arm now learns: template 0.94 correct format at 3e-6 and 0.93 at 1e-6, invented facts 0.987 recall (4-shot 0.770) at 6e-6, human solutions loss drop 1.99 at 1e-5. On size, the finished maths fine-tune reads 0.038 above the alive random change at its size (0.077 at size 1.17); at step 8 there is no gap (0.026 at size 0.57, gap -0.004). Size alone cannot be said to explain the finished state: random changes scaled to its size read only 0.026 and 0.039. The filed line C stands; the check reproduced every number.

What it means. Final states are usable as readings, and the direct share separates a finished maths fine-tune from random changes of its size but not a part-way one. Three further reads (provisional, outside the filed rule) put the share at 0.0375 at size 0.76 (step 10), 0.0653 at 1.06 (step 20) and 0.0756 at 1.15 (step 50). The rule reads no call there: step 20 falls just under the 0.0669 that "follows size" needs and above the 0.06 that "follows training" needs. Size and training rise together along one run, so these reads do not separate them, and nothing here separates "already there" from "new".
