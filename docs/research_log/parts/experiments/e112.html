On a pair of 67M tasks with Jain et al.'s shape, does the real task read "reuses the capability" and its shuffled twin read "not through it"?

2026-11-06 · provisional · three cluster debug L40 jobs and two amendments, $0 · experiments/case_label

The question. You reset the new-against-reuse definition on 2026-11-06 to Jain et al. 2024's alone (E99 had piled extra rules on it). Their inputs are a marker plus content, and the answer is a function of the content; none of our old 67M tasks except copying has that shape. Does a pair built with that shape read as the definition says it should?

What we did. After a marker token the model outputs label word A if the word before it is capitalised and B if not (real), or A and B by a shuffled per-word list at the same marker (twin, so nothing in the original model predicts its labels). It uses 400 cased word pairs, 300 for training and 100 held out by pair, and two seeds per task. We read three things: a linear readout trained on Pile text (not on the task) must find the case in the original model, beside a control readout trained on random labels; the fine-tune must label unseen words right and agree with a threshold on that readout; and removing the readout's direction must take away the fine-tune's label gain (the bar: half of it). A random direction of the same size is the do-nothing reference.

What we found. The real fine-tune passes the first two tests and fails the causal one. The original model holds case at block 1 (readout 0.994 against a control at 0.524); the real fine-tunes label unseen words right 0.998 and 0.996 and agree with the threshold on 0.993 and 0.992. But removing the one direction took 0.08 and 0.09 of the label gain, and removing two directions at every position of blocks 1 to 3 took 0.19 and 0.21, against the bar of 0.50, so the real task reads "not through the capability" and our filed "reuses it" missed. The twin reads "not through it", as filed. A last try, erasing every linear trace of case (148 directions), stopped at its own control: the same number of random directions took 0.19 to 0.26 of the gain, and the erasure about doubled the original model's next-token loss.

What it means. The definition as adopted gives this pair no "reuse" label, though every test short of the causal one reads like reuse. We do not know why the share is low (case may be spread over many directions, or the fine-tune may rebuild it from the word's identity); no cause is tested. Both amendments are spent, so E112 is closed and the subject-number pair does not run. Provisional: two seeds, one pair, no independent check.
