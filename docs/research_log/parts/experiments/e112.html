Under Jain et al.'s reuse definition alone, does any 67M fine-tune read "reuses the capability", even on a pair built to its shape?

2026-11-06 · provisional, no independent check · cluster debug L40s, $0 · experiments/case_label and six more directories

The question. On 2026-11-06 you reset the reuse definition to Jain et al. 2024's alone, dropping the extra rules E90 had tested. It has three tests. Possession: a straight-line readout trained on Pile text recovers the capability in the original model before its last block, at 0.90 or better and clearly above a control readout. Carrying: the fine-tune answers right on inputs it never trained on. Causal: removing the readout's direction takes at least half of the fine-tune's gain. Jain's tasks have a marker plus content, with the answer a function of the content; none of our old tasks but copying has that shape. So does a pair built with that shape read "reuses" on its real side, and does any old task read at all?

What we did. We built two pairs of 67M fine-tunes, two seeds a side (E133 later added a third), each a real task against a twin that can only memorise. In the case-label pair, after a marker token the model outputs label word A if the word before it is capitalised and B if not; its twin outputs A or B by a shuffled per-word list, so nothing in the original model predicts its labels (400 cased word pairs, 100 held out by pair). The twin trained 300 steps against the real side's 30, so its update is about three times larger. In the repeat-label pair, the model outputs one word when the word before the marker already occurred earlier in the passage and another when it did not, a flag the original model's induction machinery already tracks; its twin again learns the words from a shuffled list. On the case-label pair we also tried wider removal, a full erasure of case, patching the readout to the opposite case, a head-by-head locator with the sum of single reverts, and the decomposition's readings. Beside the pairs we read all 26 old fine-tunes under the reset definition, and tested a natural task, picking " he" or " she" to match a first name.

What we found. No 67M fine-tune reads "reuses". Both built pairs pass possession and carrying and fail the causal test: removing the case direction takes 0.08 to 0.21 of the gain against the bar of 0.50 (first figure), and the repeat flag's direction 0.02 to 0.05, at every site tried. Patching the case readout flips the original model's next-block reading on 0.714 of windows, yet the real fine-tune's label flips on at most 0.014 (second figure). The twins read "not through it", as built. On the old tasks no readout passes possession, so every row reads "unreadable" or "does not apply" (third figure), and the pronoun task fails possession too. No head is needed by the real case-label side and spared by its twin; the sum of single reverts reads the real side fed and the twin written, but the twin's larger update may alone explain that (fourth figure). The decomposition's route split reads all eight states alike, real and twin.

What it means. Jain's definition gave no verdict on any 67M task: where every test short of the causal one looked like reuse, the causal one said no, and we do not know why (the capability may be spread over many directions, read at many sites, or rebuilt from the word itself; none is tested). That stalemate is why the definition has been under revision since 2026-11-09, with a second wording, reuse at a variable, tested from E137 on; the readings here hold under the first wording only. The two pairs outlive it: E133 reuses them, labelled as built, as real-against-twin rows, where the re-gating share does not separate either label pair.

Run
Seven experiments on the cluster's debug L40s, a few minutes to half an hour each, all $0; only the two pairs were fine-tuned, everything else read saved states. They were first logged under their own codes, now folded into this card:

  • the repeat-label pair, both readings and removal at four further sites, jobs 54841, 54877, 54880 and 54933 (was E125, experiments/fresh_reuse_pair).
Found
  • Both built pairs pass possession and carrying and fail every causal form tried (removal, wider removal, patching), so the real sides read "not through the capability", against what we expected.
  • No old 67M task passes possession, and neither does name gender before the last block, so Jain's definition labels no small-model task.
  • Neither the head locator nor the decomposition's route split separates a real side from its memorising twin; the sum of single reverts does, but the twin's update size confounds it.
