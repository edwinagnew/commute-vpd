Can a sweep over attention heads find the part a fine-tune runs through, without being told where to look?

2026-11-05 · cluster L40 debug, $0 · experiments/locate_mechanism · provisional, no independent check

The question. You decided that a fine-tune gets a label (new or strengthened) only through a located mechanism ({{n:decisions}}), and the prefix score that located the copying head earlier is the kind of nominator we cannot use on a broad task. So can a method that is not told where to look name the part, on the 67M?

What we did. We switched off each of the 24 attention heads in turn, in the original model and in the fine-tune, by replacing its output with the original model's average on natural text, and read what share of the fine-tune's gain (its log-odds of the target above the original's) goes away. We did this on the copy and lookup fine-tunes (three seeds) and on the lowercase and cipher fine-tunes (two seeds), then the best pair of heads, and scored three cheap ways of choosing parts to try: head salience, a probe per head, and the top 320 feed-forward units by a score from earlier work. No training.

What we found. On copying the sweep names head (2,4) on every seed: it takes 0.47 to 0.56 of the gain with lookup's best head at 0.13 to 0.15, and the original model's own copying falls with it, from 0.82 to 0.0095, so the part is shared. On lowercase no head and no pair is located: the best head takes 0.41 to 0.43 against the cipher control's 0.12 to 0.14, and the best pairs take 0.53 to 0.56 but are not spared on the control. About half of that best share is the original model's reading moving, not the fine-tune falling. None of the cheap nominators held up: head salience ranked the copying head 2, 2 and 19, the probe's head was first on all seeds by a margin of 0.01, and the feed-forward units took 0.39 to 0.45 on copying but 0.27 to 0.34 on lookup.

What it means. A sweep over heads works for a narrow copying behaviour, but E62 had already pointed at this head, so the control has few teeth. On lowercase the result is "unreadable at this grain", not "no mechanism": heads and pairs are the wrong unit for a broad update, and a new circuit built from old parts still reads as reuse. Fable advised stopping, so no amendment was spent; nothing waits on you.

Run
Two cluster L40 jobs in debug (52551, 52567), a few minutes each, $0. Each fine-tune's stored gain was re-read first, and the zero update read 0.
Found
  • The control passed: the sweep finds copying head (2,4) without the prefix score, as expected, and a weak control because earlier work pointed there.
  • Lowercase is not located at head or pair grain, and the best share is partly the original model moving; the cheap nominators (salience, probe, feed-forward units) missed or did not separate copy from lookup.
  • The original model's near-zero target probability makes a net share misleading, so the next task reports the fine-tune's own fall beside it.
Bears on
The strengthening-or-new label: a located mechanism exists for copying only; lowercase stays unlabelled.
