The step split along the fine-tune's path

2026-10-21 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/step_split

Splitting every step of the fine-tune, with the pieces carried forward, keeps the weights exact but leaves more of the behaviour in Δ than the least-squares split of the finished update, at every step checked. The pieces' movement changed nothing; refitting the split's scale every step made Δ's change larger than the whole update. That fine-tune was a large Adam one; along small fine-tunes the step split passes the adversary, still leaving more behaviour in Δ except for SGD at step 1,000.

Run
The emoticon fine-tune of E14 and E16 (Adam, 1,000 steps), on one L40. After every step, per matrix, the pieces take the scaled adjoint split of that step's weight change against their current vectors, with the scale refit every step; Δ takes the rest, so the weights equal the fine-tune's at every step. Beside it, the same split with the pieces held at the original model's, on energy shares only. Read at steps 10, 100 and 1,000 against do-nothing and the least-squares split of the same run: the route split, VPD's adversarial check on Pile, Δ's share of the update. The first run stopped at its control (the L40's floor, 0.209, above a line set on an H100); the line was amended to 0.25 before the rerun.
Found
  • The behaviour through Δ: 0.31, 0.37 and 0.61, against the least-squares split's 0.09, 0.15 and 0.31 (first figure). The step-10 value missed its filed ceiling of 0.3 narrowly.
  • The adversarial check: 1.34 and 1.15 at steps 10 and 100, inside the L40's wide band and better than both references at step 10; 2.67 at step 1,000, above the 1.63 line and the worst of the three.
  • Moving the pieces changed Δ's share by at most 0.01 (second figure). The largest change in any piece's vector, 13% by step 1,000, was the same with the pieces held, so it is the steps adding up, not the pieces turning.
  • Surprise: at step 1,000 Δ's change holds 1.38 of the update's input-weighted energy, twice the one-shot adjoint split's 0.69. No single step hands Δ more than half of itself, so the excess comes from how the per-step pieces add up. Its cause is not established; each matrix's scale per step was not saved.
  • The bookkeeping holds: the second-order term is under 1e-7 of a step, and Δ keeps the update's outside-both energy to 0.2%. The least-squares reference read 5.41 at step 10 on the L40 against E16's 6.96 on an H100, failing the reproduction gate on that one state.
