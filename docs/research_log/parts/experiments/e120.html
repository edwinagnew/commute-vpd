Can one real maths task give both kinds of update, one on problems the model already solves and one on problems it never solved?

2026-11-06 to 07 · provisional, no independent check · MATS cluster and one H100 pod · experiments/qwen_pass_pools

The question. The decomposition readings on Qwen3-1.7B (E93, E94) were calibrated on a template fine-tune and a fictional-facts fine-tune, which differ in task and format as well as in kind. A real task that supplies both kinds would calibrate them on one footing. Owned by qwen-rl-lead; the readings of the saved updates are qwen-decomp-lead's.

What we did. We sampled the unchanged model many times on GSM8K and MATH levels 3 to 5 problems and sorted them into two matched pools: problems it solves often ("known") and problems it never solved in 64 samples ("new", which does not mean the skill is absent). We fine-tuned on each pool's human-written solutions for 62 training problems with the recipe of the earlier GSM8K gold run, two seeds each, read the result on 15 held-out problems a pool and on GSM8K test, and saved the updates.

What we found. The pools came out at 77 a side, under the 100 planned (a grader fix returned solvable problems to "known", not to "new"). The new arm memorised its 62 problems and carried almost nothing: held-out pass@1 0.050 and 0.025 against a bar of 0.10. Both arms overfit at 103 passes over their problems: GSM8K strict fell from 0.784 to 0.42 to 0.55, and held-out known pass@1 from 0.97 to about 0.5. The new arm's update is about 5% larger (norm 3.61 and 3.63 against 3.44 and 3.43).

What it means. For the anchors, the two updates differ less in kind than planned: both are heavily overfit updates on a small set, so a reading that separates them may be reading difficulty or solution length. The saved updates go to qwen-decomp-lead with that caveat. Whether the damage comes from the 103 passes is untested; the backup about post-cutoff facts waits on the decision below.

Run
Sampling on an H100 pod, $15.72; four fine-tunes on a cluster L40 (jobs 54969, 55011, 55012, 55013), about 30 min each, $0; grading on CPU, $0.
Found
  • The new arm learned nothing that carries (0.05 and 0.025 against 0.10), all of it on one problem; the novelty check passed (0.256 of the new pool solved with thinking, bar 0.5).
  • The known arm was not the gentle update: GSM8K fell by 0.35, missing the filed rule within 0.05, and held-out gold loss rose in every arm.
  • Pools 77 a side, filed size missed; the new arm's larger update (3.61 against 3.44) holds in both seeds.
