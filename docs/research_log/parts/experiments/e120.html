Can one real maths task give both kinds of update, one on problems the model already solves and one on problems it never solved?

2026-11-06 · ongoing, coding, sampling next · MATS cluster and one H100 pod · experiments/qwen_pass_pools

The question. The decomposition readings on Qwen3-1.7B (E93, E94) were calibrated on a template fine-tune and a fictional-facts fine-tune, which differ in task and format as well as in kind. A real task that supplies both kinds would calibrate them on one footing. Owned by qwen-rl-lead; the readings are qwen-decomp-lead's.

What we did. Nothing has run yet. The plan samples the unchanged model many times on GSM8K and MATH levels 3 to 5 problems and sorts them into two matched pools: problems it solves often ("known") and problems it never solved in 64 samples ("new", not "absent"). Each pool's human-written solutions are then used to fine-tune with the recipe of the GSM8K gold arm, two seeds each, and the updates are saved for the decomposition readings.

What we found. No result yet.

What it means. If the pools hold, the GSM8K RL, own-samples, shuffled-reward and template states are read against anchors from their own task. If the new pool has under about 100 problems after the guards, or most of it is solved with thinking on, the plan falls back to its second option or goes to the user.
