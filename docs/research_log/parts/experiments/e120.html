Can one real maths task give both kinds of update, one on problems the model already solves and one on problems it never solved?

2026-11-06 · ongoing, sampling done, regrade and fine-tunes next · MATS cluster and one H100 pod · experiments/qwen_pass_pools

The question. The decomposition readings on Qwen3-1.7B (E93, E94) were calibrated on a template fine-tune and a fictional-facts fine-tune, which differ in task and format as well as in kind. A real task that supplies both kinds would calibrate them on one footing. Owned by qwen-rl-lead; the readings are qwen-decomp-lead's.

What we did. We sampled the unchanged model many times on GSM8K and MATH levels 3 to 5 problems and sorted them into two matched pools: problems it solves often ("known") and problems it never solved in 64 samples ("new", not "absent"). Each pool's human-written solutions are then used to fine-tune with the recipe of the GSM8K gold arm, two seeds each, and the updates are saved for the decomposition readings.

What we found. Sampling finished ($15.72). Each pool holds 78 problems, short of the 100 the plan asked for, so that size rule misses and stands. The novelty check passes: with thinking on, the model solves 0.256 of the "new" problems. A bug in how MATH answers were graded (choice letters and units) had wrongly put some solvable problems in the "new" pool; the regrade on CPU (Amendment 2) projects about 115 problems a side.

What it means. If the regraded pools reach about 100 problems, the four fine-tunes run and the GSM8K RL, own-samples, shuffled-reward and template states are read against anchors from their own task. If not, the plan falls back to its second option or goes to the user.
