Do the methods still separate reused from new fine-tunes when the tasks use real text?

2026-11-18 · cell P2.38, run 1 of 3 ·experiments/realistic_tasks

The question. The nine built pairs on the 67M are synthetic. You asked (2026-11-18) to try realistic tasks next, after the crosscoder was cleared (E210). Run 1 only checks that the proposed real-text tasks can be learned and mean what they should.

What we did. On the base model alone we checked each task's premise: topic (sports against business news) against a random coin label, French against Yoruba text, and lowercase against reversed word order. The label tasks were also probed for whether the label is readable from the base model's activations.

What we found. Topic, French against Yoruba and lowercase against reversed order passed and are kept; sentiment was dropped, since its probe read 0.658 against a bar of 0.7. All kept tasks are learnable at the built pairs' sizes. The coin task's held-out gain read 8.96 log-odds where about 0 was expected: roughly half of any label task's gain is learning to say a label word.

What it means. The label tasks will be scored on the two-label contrast, which removes that format gain. Run 2 (every method, equal size, three seeds) is in E211; this card stays ongoing until run 3 lands.
