Can the Qwen readings see a change whose placement we built in?

2026-11-11 · provisional, no independent check · cluster CPU job 58729 and debug L40 job 58847 (99 min), $0 · experiments/qwen_instrument_check

The question. Part 4 repeats the part 3 readings on Qwen3-1.7B (section {{n:next}}, cell P3.3). On Qwen the re-gating share read 0.99 on every update, random changes included (E132, E144). Is that because the readings cannot see where a change sits, or because the real updates sit nowhere in particular?

What we did. A check with changes whose placement we built in, on the new Qwen decomposition. Step 0 used only saved files: how much of a change the subcomponents of each matrix could hold at all. Step 1 planted two changes on the 28 down-projection matrices by scaling up the write vectors of 648 subcomponents each: one set switched on at over half of the unchanged model's maths positions (often-on), one never on there (rarely-on). A random change of the same size served as chance. Each was scaled to three output sizes, from the reinforcement-learning update's 0.064 nats per token up to the step-10 fine-tune's 1.64. The least-squares split then tried to find each plant again. The re-gating share is the part of the masked model's error that the changed model's own masks remove: it reads 1 when splitting adds nothing.

What we found. Step 0: real updates leave as much outside the subcomponents as random ones (0.97 to 1.005 of a random change's share), and every MLP matrix is fully reachable. Step 1: the split put 0.89 of the often-on change on the planted set (random change: 0.06) but only 0.29 of the rarely-on change (chance: 0.006); a third of the latter went to the often-on set. The share separated the two planted kinds at every size in 24 of 36 state pairs, but flipped direction between small and large sizes. By the filed rules the split gets no call, and the filed "none" lean for size was reversed: the share responds to placement, and size is not the limit.

What it means. The premise check is doubtful: planted changes sit inside the decomposition's basis and are found when often-on, while real updates do not look that way. So the 0.99 on real updates cannot yet be read as "blind"; it may be that they sit nowhere the readings can place them. No verdict on reuse. What part 4 repeats is the 11-18 gate's choice.

Run
One CPU job for step 0 and one debug L40 job for step 1, $0. Every control passed first: the unchanged model reproduced the earlier numbers, do-nothing read 1, the often-on set was all on and the rarely-on set not on.
Found
  • Real updates sit no more inside the subcomponents' reach than a random change does; the reinforcement-learning updates are smaller than the decomposition's own leftover, the two TOFU fine-tunes larger.
  • The split recovers 0.88 of the way from chance for the often-on plant and 0.29 for the rarely-on one; the already-on gap between them is 0.479, just under the filed 0.5, so no call.
  • The re-gating share reads 0.985 to 1.005 on all nine states, and separates the two kinds at every size, but not in a consistent order; the unmatched random change at the largest size is not read.
