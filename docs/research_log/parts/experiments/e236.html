Does R read a strength-only update as kept, and does the held model contain it?

2026-11-20/21 · cell P1.41, definition-lead, descriptive, no independent check · experiments/gain_only_r

The question. A pure strengthening, where only how strongly each neuron and head writes changes, is the clearest case of reuse. Part 1 needs a reuse score that reads it as kept. We expected R and the held model (the model that fits one gain per neuron and head to the fine-tune) to read such a state at least as kept as a full fine-tune of the same task.

What we did. On the 67M model we took E231's 81 strength-only states (one gain per neuron and head, trained to match each full fine-tune's output change; 27 tasks, seeds 0 to 2). Each was read beside the full fine-tune of the same task and seed, with E226's R unchanged (the squared-cosine grade, the reading before wording A) at VPD and at three coarser levels, and with the held model. The difference d is R of the strength-only state minus R of its full fine-tune.

What we found. The held model reads every strength-only state at 0.999998 or more, against 0.010 to 0.104 for the full fine-tunes; this is a positive control, since the family contains them by construction. At VPD, d is above zero on 0 of 49 pairs (median -0.016), although each strength-only state moved the output less. At heads and MLP layers, d is above zero on 57 of 57 (median 0.013); the two coarser levels split, 37 of 57.

What it means. At VPD the grade reads a pure strengthening as less kept: such a state changes fewer parts of the circuit (0.43 to 0.48 against 0.9999) but turns each further, so R there reads turned weights, not kept mechanisms. Whether wording A changes this was not read here. No verdict; the stop rule needed every level low, so it did not trigger.

Run
Nine RunPod H100 pods, after a $0 rehearsal on the cluster's debug queue; stage 0 was exact on every pod (do-nothing R = 1).
Found
  • The held model contains all 81 strength-only states (at least 0.999998), a control that held as it must.
  • R at VPD reads every strength-only state below its full fine-tune (0 of 49 above), while heads and MLP layers read every one above (57 of 57); the coarser levels have no clear direction.
  • The VPD gap is the grade, not output size: strength-only states turn each changed member further (mean grade 0.919 against 0.9989 on the cipher task).
