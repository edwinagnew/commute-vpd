If every trained fact is swapped for other known facts, do the neurons carrying the update change?

2026-11-06 · provisional, independent check holds with caveat · one cluster debug job (53480), $0 · experiments/rows_null

The question. E103 found that which 1,024 neurons carry an update (its carrying set) separates a fine-tune on facts Qwen3-1.7B already knows from one on invented authors, but the two also trained on different rows. If the label is held at "already known" and the rows are changed, does the carrying set move as far? That is the same-label, different-rows null E103 left open.

What we did. We split the 217 real-person and world facts into two disjoint halves and fine-tuned on each, with the recipe and answer form of E103's known-facts run, at two data-order seeds. We read each update at the step where its total weight change matches the template fine-tune's, and compared the carrying sets of the two halves, on the real rows and on the invented-author rows, with the overlap of two seeds on one half as the floor.

What we found. The two halves share 0.82 to 0.84 of their carrying neurons on the real rows and 0.88 to 0.89 on the invented-author rows, close to the 0.86 to 0.92 that two seeds of one half share, and far above the 0.49 and 0.64 that a known-facts run shares with a fictional one. The filed rule reads "label" on both row sets, against both filed leans (rows on the real rows, no call on the other): changing every row replaced about a sixth of the set, where changing one answer form had replaced about three tenths.

What it means. The separation in E103 is robust to which rows were trained and to the seed, so it is not a rows artefact. It is not yet a reading of known against new: the answer style and the topic or kind of entity (famous real people against invented authors) differ between the arms and remain live causes. The next step is a control that changes the label while holding topic and answer style (planned as E107).

  • The independent check re-derived every overlap and verdict; it rules out rows but not answer style, topic or entity type.
