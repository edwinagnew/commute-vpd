Do the share and the cheap readings still separate the pairs when the two sides are matched in update size?

2026-11-16 · cell P2.28, 1 of 1 runs used, provisional, no independent check · experiments/size_matched

The question. Part 3 compares the re-gating share with simple readings of a saved update on pairs of fine-tunes whose two sides differ by construction (section {{n:next}}, cell P2.28). Update size (the weight change as a share of the weights) differs between sides in many pairs. Does the share, or any cheap reading, still separate a pair when both sides change the weights by the same amount?

What we did. For each pair, every saved update of the side with the larger weight change was multiplied by one number, its partner's mean size over its own, and each reading was taken again on that side; the smaller side keeps its saved readings. The pairs are nine built pairs on the 67M model and, on Qwen3-1.7B, copy against cipher, known against invented facts and gold against shuffled gold at two saves each. A gap is one side's mean minus the other's; a cell counts as separated when the seed ranges do not overlap on the 67M, or when the gap clears its random-change band on Qwen. Effective rank cannot change under one scalar, so it is not drawn.

What we found. On the 67M the share still separates all nine built pairs; steering keeps seven, top neurons eight and neurons for half the gain five. On Qwen the share separates 1 of 6 pair-and-save cells (it was 3), and that one barely shrank. The top-neuron overlap reverses sign on copy against cipher at both saves.

What it means. On the 67M the share's separation of the built pairs is not update size. On Qwen the share's separation mostly goes away, but the premise check reads doubtful: a rescaled update is not a smaller learned one, so this cannot tell size from distortion. Description only; nothing is labelled reuse or new.

Run
Rescaling and re-reading of saved updates on the existing pairs, no new training; effective rank was checked unchanged on every state.
Found
  • The 67M share kept 9 of 9 built pairs; steering kept 7, top neurons 8 and neurons for half the gain 5.
  • The Qwen share kept 1 of 6 cells (it was 3); the kept one is gold at the late save, where the shrink factor was 0.97.
  • The Qwen top-neuron overlap reverses on copy and cipher at both saves, and Qwen steering stays unreadable on four cells.
  • Read beside the held-model reading (one cheap reading of the working definition) on the nine 67M pairs, the share's gaps are 0.39 to 0.70 while the held-model gaps are at most 0.023 saved and 0.033 matched. The two agree in sign on 8 of 9 pairs (not copy at a new marker against a lookup) but their rank correlation is 0.13 saved and -0.27 matched; one gain per unit agrees in sign on 8 then 9 of 9 (rank 0.52, 0.68). Descriptive, no labels.
