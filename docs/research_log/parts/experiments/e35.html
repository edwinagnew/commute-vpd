Can the per-cue change be predicted without naming the cue?

2026-10-26 · RunPod RTX 4090 · experiments/regating_cuefree

Largely, yes. Giving each input the mean change of the eight training inputs whose original importance values are nearest recovers 0.61 to 0.78 of the switching on the four 32-cue tasks, as much as one change per cue added the same way, 0.47 to 0.66 (figure). Those nearest inputs share the cue 62 to 71% of the time, against about 3% by chance, and restricting them to the same cue helps only where each cue has its own answer. Groups found by clustering and a fixed line per subcomponent fall short with many cues.

Run
E32's and E33's six do-nothing states regenerated, reproducing them within 0.0013. At the trigger, each held-out input gets its own original values plus a change fitted on training inputs: one change for all, one per cue word, the mean change of the 8 nearest training inputs by original values, k-means groups of the original values (8, 32, 128, with random groups as the null), and a least-squares line per subcomponent. The additive form is used because it can be carried to every position of a text; E32 and E33's averaged form reads higher. Amendment 1 counted how often the nearest inputs share the cue, the nearest rule within one cue, and duplicates. 58 s and 68 s.
Found
  • The nearest rule matches or beats one change per cue on three of the four 32-cue tasks and is 0.05 short on copy. The averaged per-cue form, 0.81 to 1.06, stays higher.
  • The nearest inputs share the cue 71% (copy, cipher) and 62% (the " of" cues) of the time, against a filed 90%. Restricted to the same cue they recover the same where all cues share " of", 0.78, and 0.23 more where each cue has its own answer.
  • Surprise: on the one-cue task the cue-free rules beat one shared change by 0.19 and 0.31, where no difference was filed. The change depends on each input's own values even with one cue.
  • k-means at 32 groups misses, 0.35 to 0.53, purity 0.40 to 0.57; 128 groups reach the per-cue level. The line works with few cues, 0.96 and 1.02, and partly with many, 0.37 to 0.80.
