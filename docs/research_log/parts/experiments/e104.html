Do the three readings that told the two example fine-tunes apart tell reinforcement learning from its shuffled-reward null?

2026-11-06 · cluster L40 debug, four jobs, $0 · experiments/qwen_d3_gsm8k · provisional, not independently checked

The question. The third deliverable ({{n:next}}) wants readings inside the decomposition that tell an update that strengthens what the model has from one that teaches content it lacked. E94 found three readings that separate its two example fine-tunes beyond what size does. Do those three, read on the maths updates the deliverable is about, tell reinforcement learning from the same loop with shuffled rewards?

What we did. We split each maths update of Qwen3-1.7B over the subcomponents by least squares and read the three numbers: the share of the update outside the subcomponents (against its exact null), the extra share of the split on subcomponents already switched on, and the overlap of the subcomponents the update switches on with think mode's (beside a no-think set matched on answer length). The arms were reinforcement learning (RL) on GSM8K and its shuffled-reward null, both at two seeds, and fine-tuning on the model's own correct answers (own-samples, one seed), each at steps 10 and 100. A gap counts when it exceeds the larger seed spread; own-samples borrows the null's spread. Three scaled copies test whether size alone gives a think gap.

What we found. RL reads like its null on all three readings at both steps. Own-samples differs from RL at step 100 on two: outside share (0.189 against 0.135 and 0.158) and think overlap (2.53 against 3.65 and 3.21). The outside-share gap is wholly which matrices the update sits in: as a fraction of its own null, own-samples reads 0.582 and RL 0.581 and 0.583, as does every maths state and the template. The think gap comes with an output size fifty times smaller, and the size check could not be matched, so it is described, not attributed. Placement missed its filed rule: RL seed 0 rises from 0.009 to 0.126 while seed 70 falls to -0.016, unexplained, so placement on RL reads nothing. TOFU shrunk to the template's size still reads 3.01, so E94's think gap is not size.

What it means. These readings give no handle yet on whether RL teaches something new: RL looks like a run that ignores the reward's content. Whether own-samples' think gap survives a size match, and whether it holds at a second seed, is the open point; a $0 size rematch (E104 Amendment 1) decides that before a second own-samples seed is asked of qwen-rl-lead.

Run
Four cluster debug jobs on one L40 each, $0, about 2.7 h of card (control 53267 first and alone: it reproduced E94's template values and scale invariance). Rules filed before the control ran.
Found
  • RL reads like its shuffled-reward null on outside share, placement and think overlap, at steps 10 and 100.
  • Own-samples differs from RL on outside share (through its null only) and think overlap (size unmatched); one seed.
  • Placement missed: the two RL seeds disagree by 0.14, unexplained; E94's think gap between the anchors is not size (3.01).
Bears on
The third deliverable: no evidence from these readings that the reward's content changes how the update sits in the subcomponents. Two of the three describe the update, not the split.
