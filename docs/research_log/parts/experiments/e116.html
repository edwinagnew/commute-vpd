The question. You froze the new-against-reuse definition on 2026-11-06 at Jain et al. 2024's, with held-out carrying and a direction-removal check, and asked for a sanity check before it labels the Qwen anchors: read every old 67M task under it, one column per part, and say whether it agrees with what we claimed earlier. E112 had read one new pair; this reads the old table.

What we did. On 26 saved fine-tunes and the original model, with no training, we named a capability for each task: the key's identity on the key tables, the copied word on in-context copying, and the original model's own next-word guess on the guess maps and text ciphers. Possession asks that a linear readout trained on Pile text recover it on the task's inputs at 0.90 accuracy, and beat a control readout that gives each token type a random value by 0.30, before the last block. Carrying asks that the fine-tune answer right on values it never trained on; it needs no readout. Lowercase, emoticon and the one-pair map have no content function, so they read "does not apply". The locator's part-level revert from E100 and E97 is copied beside, as the definition's "pruning a few neurons".

What we found. No readout passes possession, so every task reads "unreadable" or "does not apply" and no earlier claim is confirmed or contradicted. On the key tables the readout and its control are both perfect, since a relabelling of token types is read as well as the types. The copied-word readout is near chance over 178 words, the guess readouts peak at about 0.6, and on the text ciphers the control beats the readout. Carrying still reads: both copying fine-tunes answer unseen values right at 0.95 or more, while the cipher and the guess maps do not.

What it means. The unreadable rows are the finding, not a failure: they say what shape a task needs before this definition can label it. The capability must not be fixed by one token's type, its Pile readout must beat the per-type control on types it never trained on, with few values, and the task must define answers for unseen values. That is the input to step 2, the Qwen anchors. Provisional: one readout form, small held-out sets, no independent check.
