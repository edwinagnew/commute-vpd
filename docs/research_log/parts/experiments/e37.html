Can teacher training carry a broad fine-tune, one that changes text everywhere with no trigger, onto the decomposition faithfully?

2026-10-26 to 11-07 · RunPod H100 and MATS cluster L40 · experiments/teacher_spread, broad_fragility, row_blocks, damage_crossed, ls_start_faith, copy_protection

The question. Teacher training carries narrow fine-tunes onto the 67M decomposition faithfully (E34): it trains the subcomponents' read and write vectors, with Γ and Δ frozen, until the decomposition predicts what the fine-tuned model predicts. Post-training changes text everywhere, so the commute question needs the same on a broad update. We used E36's fine-tune, which teaches the model to write in lowercase and changes all 24 decomposed matrices.

What we did. We copied three lowercase fine-tunes made by different recipes: full-matrix Adam, plain SGD, and a rank-8 LoRA trained with Adam. Each copy is scored on the share of the fine-tune's held-out gain it keeps and on the adversarial ratio: an adversary searches over masks of the subcomponents and Δ for the largest damage on Pile text, over the damage at the unchanged decomposition. 1.40 or less passes and 1.61 or more fails. Do-nothing leaves the whole update in Δ and is the state the check must fail. We read eight blocks of Pile rows at up to three adversary starts, trained longer and from other starts, probed the failing copy with random moves and reverts, crossed damage against recipe, and read each copy's weight change both where teacher training placed it and moved into Δ.

What we found. It depends on the fine-tune. Every copy keeps the lowercasing. The LoRA's copy passes on all eight blocks; the Adam copy fails on all eight, between 1.66 and 2.20 (first and second figures). Plain SGD's copies pass almost everywhere, but its do-nothing passes too, so the check cannot fail SGD's update on these rows. Training four times longer, or starting from the adjoint split, which passes, still fails (third figure). Random moves of the same size pass, and the Adam copy's excess sits in its MLP changes acting together (fourth figure). Where the change sits matters: each copy's own change moved into Δ fails worse, 3.53 for Adam and 2.34 for the LoRA, so teacher training's placement hides much of the excess. For the LoRA copy it does so on every block; for an early Adam copy it ranges from partial to none with the rows (fifth figure).

What it means. Teacher training places a broad rank-8 LoRA update and not a broad full-matrix Adam one; plain SGD is unresolved for lack of power, not a pass. The broad copy's function is fragile under the check, and teacher training's placement protects it by accident, since its loss has no mask or adversary term. That is enough for the LoRA and not for Adam. Neither how spread out the update is nor how much it damages plain text separates the two, though both copies read lower at lower damage. VPD's own masked losses could not be tried as a fix: with nothing to copy they alone broke the check. A carrier for broad updates needs a placement that protects by design; the next read is where each change sits against Γ's importances.

Found
  • Every copy keeps the lowercasing; the rank-8 LoRA's passes on all eight row blocks, the full-matrix Adam one fails on all eight, and plain SGD's cannot be judged because the check cannot fail its update on these rows.
  • The Adam failure is not the update's size or spread; it sits in the MLP changes together and survives longer training, a passing start and lower damage on some blocks.
  • Where teacher training places the change hides 3 to 4 times the excess of the same change in Δ for the two saved copies, steadily for the LoRA and unsteadily for an early Adam copy; the least-squares start's placement does the reverse, reading above do-nothing.
