Can teacher training copy a fine-tune with no trigger?

2026-10-26 · RunPod H100 · experiments/teacher_spread

It keeps the behaviour but fails the adversarial check, and training longer does not fix it. A decomposition trained to imitate a fine-tune that lowercases text everywhere keeps all of its gain, yet the mask adversary finds 2.2 times the original decomposition's damage at 1,000 steps and again at 4,000, past the fail line (first and third figures). Surprise: the scaled adjoint split keeps most of the gain and passes, but a training run started from it climbs past the fail line within 1,000 steps.

Run
Teacher: E36's lowercase-text fine-tune of all 24 matrices, regenerated. Teacher training as in E34 (the alive subcomponents' read and write vectors trained to match the teacher's predictions, Γ and Δ frozen, Adam, 1,000 steps) on the teacher's own lowercase rows at two batch orders, and on the same documents with capitals kept. Scored beside do-nothing and the scaled adjoint split on held-out rows, with the harness adversary on 256 Pile rows. Controls first: the unchanged model, the regenerated teacher, do-nothing equal to the teacher; all passed. About 21 min, $1.40. Amendment 1: the same recipe continued to 4,000 steps, read at 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000, and the same training started from the adjoint split for 2,000 steps, with the same controls and the one-start adversary the user fixed; a third arm adding VPD's masked losses was not run, because its control (the recipe with nothing to copy) read 3.84, against a limit of 1.40. $2.27, H100.
Found
  • The trained decompositions match the fine-tune, as filed: they keep 0.998 of its gain and change ordinary Pile text as much as it does. Two batch orders land on nearly one weight change (cosine 0.93).
  • Every one fails the adversarial check, against the filed prediction, and more training does not help. The check reads 2.20, 1.89 and 2.23 at 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000 steps against a fail line of 1.61, while the gap to the fine-tuned model keeps falling. Started from the adjoint split (1.05), it reads 1.76 and 1.84 at 1,000 and 2,000. The filed plan had no branch for a falling loss with a flat check.
  • VPD's masked losses could not be tried: with nothing to copy the recipe alone read 3.84, more fragile than any trained decomposition, as E15's refit control did. That the check follows the change on ordinary text is untested: the masked control failed it with a thirtieth of the damage.
