Can teacher training copy a fine-tune with no trigger?

2026-10-26 · RunPod H100 · experiments/teacher_spread

It keeps the behaviour but fails the adversarial check, provisionally. A copy of a fine-tune that lowercases text everywhere keeps all of its gain, yet the mask adversary finds 2.2 times the unchanged model's damage, past the fail line (first figure). The copies were still learning, and no cause was tested. Surprise: the scaled adjoint split keeps most of the gain and passes.

Run
Teacher: E36's lowercase-text fine-tune of all 24 matrices, regenerated. Teacher training as in E34 (the alive subcomponents' read and write vectors trained to match the teacher's predictions, Γ and Δ frozen, Adam, 1,000 steps) on the teacher's own lowercase rows at two batch orders, and on the same documents with capitals kept. Scored beside do-nothing and the scaled adjoint split on held-out rows, with the harness adversary on 256 Pile rows. Controls first: the unchanged model, the regenerated teacher, do-nothing equal to the teacher; all passed. About 21 min, $1.40.
Found
  • The copies match the teacher, as filed. They keep 0.998 of its gain (0.92 from text with capitals) and change ordinary Pile text as much as it does. Two batch orders land on nearly one weight change.
  • Every copy fails the adversarial check, against the filed prediction: 2.20, and 1.88 at the second batch order, against a fail line of 1.61. Both halves of the rows agree. The copies' training loss was still falling by a third, so this reads an unfinished copy.
  • The scaled adjoint split keeps 0.80 of the gain, filed under half, and passes at 1.05. It barely changes ordinary text and matches the teacher's predictions far less closely. Across these states the adversarial ratio rises with the change on ordinary text, an untested alternative cause of the copies' failure.
