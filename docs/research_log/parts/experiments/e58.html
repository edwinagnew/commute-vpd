Does a fine-tune of the half-known key map on 24 of its 32 keys carry to the 8 keys it never saw, and do the route split's re-gating share and the stand-in ratio agree with that behavioural label?

2026-10-30 · MATS cluster L40 debug QoS, $0 (job 46266) · experiments/open_pair

The question. The re-gating line's "elicited" and "new" labels have all been set by how a task was built. No 67M fine-tune carried a label earned from behaviour alone, so the programme's second step was blocked. This experiment builds one at $0, following Fable's advice and your standing instruction to build the open pair ourselves.

What we did. E54's half-known map and its size-matched cipher (the same keys, values the model cannot know) were fine-tuned on 24 of the 32 keys, three seeds each. The label is behavioural: a state is "reuses" if the 8 unseen keys gain at least half as much as the trained ones, counting each answer's rise over the other values of the map, and "new" if that share is within three bootstrap errors of zero. Then the re-gating share r (the share of the gain recovered when no subcomponent changes and only Γ's switching does), the stand-in ratio (the masked gain over the subcomponent gain, no ceiling at 1) and steering were read on the same states and scored against the label.

What we found. The unseen keys gained nothing beyond a small boost: the label reads "new" on all three seeds (share about −0.03 against a "reuses" bar of 0.5), and "new" on all three ciphers, as it must. Yet r reads 0.76 to 0.79 and the ratio 0.93 to 0.95, "reuses", the same as when all 32 keys were trained, and still separate the map from the cipher (0.22 to 0.24, 0.30 to 0.34). So the filed prediction that the two would agree failed, for both readings. Steering's apparent win (5 of 6 states) is not a finding: its three elicit values sit on its own cut.

What it means. This is the first concrete case of the granularity limit: the map's values are words the model already says, so the subcomponents are old while the lookup is new, and r and the ratio read "old pieces" where the label reads "a rule generalises". That is a reading, not a proof; no 67M state has yet read "reuses" under this label, so it cannot yet count against the decomposition either. Which meaning of "reuses" the line wants is a pending decision, and the next $0 step, E58 Amendment 1, adds the label's positive control (a map whose rule the model has) and a rank-k readout.

Run
One cluster debug job on an L40 (job 46266), $0, about three minutes after a stopped stage-0 job (46259) that found the cipher raises its whole value set and moved the label to the value-specific gain; three seeds per map, saved changes under results/large/.
Found
  • The unseen keys' own values gained 0.5 to 0.9 log-odds against 1.6 to 1.8 for the trained values: label "new" on all three half-known seeds and all three ciphers, so the filed lean toward "reuses" missed.
  • r (0.76 to 0.79) and the stand-in ratio (0.93 to 0.95) still read "reuses", so the filed agreement prediction failed for both; both still separate the map from the cipher by 0.5 to 0.6 (the granularity limit, our reading).
  • Own value minus the 7 other unseen values: +1.1 on the half-known map, −0.1 to −0.2 on the cipher, so the update carries a small key-specific boost, about 6% of the trained keys' gain.
