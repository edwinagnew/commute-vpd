Does a fine-tune of the half-known key map on 24 of its 32 keys carry to the 8 keys it never saw, and do the route split's re-gating share and the stand-in ratio agree with that behavioural label?

2026-10-30 · MATS cluster L40 debug QoS, $0 (jobs 46266, 46332) · experiments/open_pair

The question. The re-gating line's "elicited" and "new" labels have all been set by how a task was built. No 67M fine-tune carried a label earned from behaviour alone, so the programme's second step was blocked. This experiment builds one at $0, following Fable's advice and your standing instruction to build the open pair ourselves.

What we did. E54's half-known map and its size-matched cipher (the same keys, values the model cannot know) were fine-tuned on 24 of the 32 keys, three seeds each. The label is behavioural: a state is "reuses" if the 8 unseen keys gain at least half as much as the trained ones, counting each answer's rise over the other values of the map, and "new" if that share is within three bootstrap errors of zero. The re-gating share r (the share of the gain recovered when no subcomponent changes and only Γ's switching does), the stand-in ratio (the masked gain over the subcomponent gain, no ceiling at 1) and steering were read on the same states and scored against the label. A follow-up (Amendment 1) read at the unseen keys which words the update pushed up, and gave the label a positive control: two maps the model can already apply at any key (a copy map, and a map of each key's own top word), same keys and seeds.

What we found. The unseen keys gained nothing beyond a small boost: the label reads "new" on all three seeds (share about −0.03 against a "reuses" bar of 0.5), and "new" on all three ciphers. Yet r reads 0.76 to 0.79 and the ratio 0.93 to 0.95, "reuses", and still separate the map from the cipher, so the filed prediction that the two agree failed. The follow-up showed why the label reads "new": at unseen keys the update pushed the 24 trained answers up and the model's own other preferences down, a lookup and not a sharpening. But the label then failed its own positive control: the copy map crossed the bar on one seed of three (share 0.42 to 0.55), and the rank-1 map could not be tested (one key with a value of its own), so a second filed prediction failed.

What it means. The held-out label has no working positive control on this model, so E58's disagreement counts neither for nor against the decomposition. r (0.76 to 0.85) and the ratio (0.93 to 1.11) stay level while behavioural carry runs from the copy map through the rank-1 map to the half-known map, and they separate only the cipher; so whatever they read, it is not held-out carry. The granularity limit applies throughout: a new circuit built from the model's existing pieces would also read as old subcomponents, so these readings cannot tell it from an old circuit. Which meaning of "reuses" the line wants is still a pending decision; the lead is consulting Fable on what follows.
