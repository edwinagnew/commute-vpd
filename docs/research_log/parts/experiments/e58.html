Does a fine-tune of the half-known key map on 24 of its 32 keys (and on 96 of 128) carry to the 8 keys it never saw, and do the route split's re-gating share and the stand-in ratio agree with that behavioural label?

2026-10-30 · MATS cluster L40 debug QoS, $0 (jobs 46266, 46332, 46383, 46398) · experiments/open_pair

The question. The re-gating line's "elicited" and "new" labels have all been set by how a task was built. No 67M fine-tune carried a label earned from behaviour alone, so the programme's second step was blocked. This experiment builds one at $0, following Fable's advice and your standing instruction to build the open pair ourselves.

What we did. E54's half-known map and its size-matched cipher (the same keys, values the model cannot know) were fine-tuned on 24 of the 32 keys, three seeds each. The label is behavioural: a state is "reuses" if the 8 unseen keys gain at least half as much as the trained ones, counting each answer's rise over the other values of the map, and "new" if that share is within three bootstrap errors of zero. The re-gating share r (the share of the gain recovered when no subcomponent changes and only Γ's switching does), the stand-in ratio (the masked gain over the subcomponent gain, no ceiling at 1) and steering were read on the same states and scored against the label. A follow-up read at the unseen keys which words the update pushed up, and gave the label a positive control: two maps the model can already apply at any key (a copy map, and a map of each key's own top word). Two later jobs trained the three maps on 96 of 128 keys, four times as many, with the cipher as the no-rule control, to see whether more keys let a rule carry and whether r and the ratio then move.

What we found. The unseen keys gained nothing beyond a small boost: the label reads "new" on all three seeds (share about −0.03 against a "reuses" bar of 0.5), and "new" on all three ciphers. Yet r reads 0.76 to 0.79 and the ratio 0.93 to 0.95, "reuses", and still separate the map from the cipher, so the filed prediction that the two agree failed. The follow-up showed why the label reads "new": at unseen keys the update pushed the 24 trained answers up and the model's own other preferences down, a lookup and not a sharpening. The label failed its own positive control at 24 keys (the copy map crossed the bar on one seed of three, share 0.42 to 0.55), so a second filed prediction failed. At 96 keys every map carried further: the copy map reads "reuses" (carry 0.82, the label's first working control, one seed), and the half-known map carries 0.21 of its trained gain at step 10 and 0.10 later, against nothing at 24 keys. r and the ratio stayed level (r 0.78 to 0.89 across maps whose carry runs 0.21 to 0.82).

What it means. The 24-key disagreement counts neither for nor against the decomposition, since the label then had no working control. At 96 keys r and the ratio stay level while carry rises, but the no-rule cipher's r also rose by 0.14 with the key count, so the filed test of whether they follow carry reads "untested", and no claim that they are blind to carry rests on it. The granularity limit applies throughout: a new circuit built from the model's existing pieces would also read as old subcomponents. Which meaning of "reuses" the line wants is still a pending decision.

Found
  • The unseen keys' own values gained 0.5 to 0.9 log-odds against 1.6 to 1.8 for the trained values: label "new" on all three half-known seeds and all three ciphers, a lookup and not a sharpening, so the filed lean toward "reuses" missed.
  • r and the stand-in ratio still read "reuses" (0.76 to 0.79, 0.93 to 0.95) and separate the map from the cipher, so the filed agreement prediction failed; they stay level across the copy, rank-1 and half-known maps (the granularity limit, our reading).
  • The label's positive control failed at 24 keys (copy map "reuses" on 1 of 3 seeds) and passed at 96 keys on one seed (carry 0.82); the half-known map is not a no-rule map at 96 keys (carry 0.21, then 0.10), and the cipher's r rose 0.14, so the test of whether r follows carry is untested.
