Qwen3-1.7B: does adding a masked term to teacher training make the decomposition copy a fine-tuned model under masks, and at what size of change?

2026-11-04 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, $0 (jobs 50915, 51024, 51046, 51051) · experiments/qwen_masked_teacher · provisional: one update, one seed, 200 columns per matrix; independent check done, no bug found

The question. D1 is a map on Qwen3-1.7B that keeps the decomposition faithful for a real update, judged by a test that can fail a bad placement (Q1). Masked tests had barely responded to any update (E74, E77), and you took teacher training with masks in its loss as the candidate map. Can the masks themselves be trained against, and does that cost a change far bigger than the update?

What we did. Teacher training makes small changes to about 200 often-on subcomponents per matrix so that the changed original model copies the fine-tuned model (the first fine-tune: ten steps on maths rows). We trained three ways for 1200 steps: with the clean loss alone, with a second loss read through the original model's masks on all 196 matrices at once, and with that second loss under the library's routing (a random number of matrices masked at each position). A control with a random teacher ran first. We read all of them on eight unseen maths rows. The direction of every KL below: the reference is the first argument, so KL(fine-tuned clean ‖ student masked) compares the masked student to the fine-tuned model's clean output; the original model's own masked score on the same rows is KL(original clean ‖ original masked), 0.042.

What we found. The masked term works far better than we expected. KL(fine-tuned clean ‖ student masked) falls from 1.33 to 0.28 with all matrices masked and 0.39 routed, against 1.13 for the clean loss alone; the all-masked arm still sits 6.6 times above 0.042 (first figure). It pays in size: the all-masked arm's change has 3.4 times the update's squared size and is still growing, the routed arm's 2.4 times, the clean-only arm's 0.52 times (second figure), and the all-masked arm puts 68 percent of it in the last seven blocks against the update's 25 percent (third figure). The filed label is "works" for both masked arms and it stands, but it says less than its name: its rules cannot see size, and the late-share rule also passes a random teacher.

What it means. This is progress towards D1's map, not the map. A masked term can be trained against, which we doubted, and early blocks are not deaf to a trained change, which narrows E77. E79 then read the fall under frozen masks as mixed, neither agreement nor softness, not holding on web text, and showed that a low reading under the state's own masks credits no placement. What D1's test should be is a pending decision (section {{n:decisions}}).

Found
  • The masked fall is large (1.33 to 0.28 and 0.39) against a lean that it would fail or barely beat the clean loss; both leans were false.
  • The change is 3.4 and 2.4 times the update's squared size, and a cap at 1.5 times would have kept most of the fall (73 percent of it arrives by 0.78 times).
  • Blocks 0-14 alone give 0.18 nats of fall at squared size 1.00, so early blocks respond; size or direction is unmeasured.
