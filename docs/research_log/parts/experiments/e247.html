Why does the thin new edit read as reused under wording A at VPD?

2026-11-21 · cell P1.47, definition-lead, diagnosis, no independent check · experiments/thin_edit_diagnosis

The question. Under wording A, the hand-built edit that writes eight new answers thinly into one layer read 0.98 in E242, above two reuse edits, although it is built as new. Either the output gains absorb the new computation, the change sits in few parts, or the built label is off.

What we did. We rebuilt E242's six items with its circuits, stand-in sets and fitted gains, which reproduced its readings exactly. For each item we set every gain to one and read how far that moves the fine-tune, read the share of the stood-in parts' change that the gains explain, and split each circuit into parts the edit never touched, changed parts the base mechanism stands in for, and changed parts that keep the fine-tune's mechanism. The SAE numbers are read from E245's files.

What we found. The gains are not the cause: they explain 0.016 of the thin edit's stood-in change (a random update 0.011, the gain of 2 at 0.73), and setting them to one changes the move by -1.6e-5. Instead 0.89 of the thin edit's circuit is untouched, against 0.63 for the rebuilt reuse edit, and wording A counts untouched parts as reused. Among changed parts alone the thin edit still stands in more than the rebuilt edit (0.82 against 0.62 at VPD, 0.42 against 0.22 at SAE).

What it means. Nothing here shows the built label off: wording A as counted is too lenient for a thin edit, because most of its circuit is never touched. Weighting changed parts by the change they carry inverts the order (thin 0.54, rebuilt 0.04), and counting changed parts only fails as above, so neither simple fix works. The gains-to-one test is weak on power (the gain-of-2 edit rose only from 0.078 to 0.13), and an independent premise check doubts the built label itself; the choice of what wording A does about untouched parts went to you as a pending decision.
