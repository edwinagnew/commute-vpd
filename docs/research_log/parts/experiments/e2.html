Split test (round 2)

2026-10-14 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/lift_test

On the emoticon task, component training keeps the decomposition faithful while it learns; the least-squares split of a finished fine-tune fails VPD's adversarial check fivefold by step 10.

Run
The obstruction of fine-tune and LoRA updates, Frobenius and input-weighted. The behaviour of the expressible part alone. The least-squares split of the finished fine-tune at steps 1 to 10. Component training, first at too large a learning rate, then at a matched update size.
Found
  • Component training at matched size: learns by step 3, a third of the fine-tune's off-target damage, all four Pile ratios 1.00 to 1.07 through 30 steps.
  • Least-squares split: carries the update (about nine tenths of the do-nothing gap recovered, unmasked) but its adversarial ratio climbs from 1.14 at step 1 to 5.41 at step 10; do-nothing reads 2.18.
  • Weighted by real inputs, about 30% of a fine-tune's update is outside the pieces (original weights 13%, random 60%), Adam or SGD alike. Dropping that part keeps the behaviour; the part alone also partly carries it.
  • Frobenius obstruction is useless: the original weights fail it too.
  • A LoRA's read side sits at its random initialisation, so its obstruction equals the null by construction.
