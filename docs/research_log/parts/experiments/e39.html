Why does teacher training on a broad fine-tune fail the adversarial check, and does a less spread-out update help?

2026-10-27 · RunPod H100 · experiments/broad_fragility

The question. Teacher training copies trigger fine-tunes well (E34). But on a broad fine-tune, one that makes the model write lowercase text everywhere, its copy failed VPD's faithfulness check (E37), which threatens it as the commute line's map (Q1). The check is the adversarial ratio: the damage an adversary searching over masks of the pieces can do at a state, over what it does at the unchanged decomposition. 1.40 or less passes and 1.61 or more fails. One guess was that Adam's fine-tune spreads its change over many weight directions, so a less spread-out update, plain SGD or a rank-8 LoRA, would copy faithfully.

What we did. We teacher-trained copies of SGD and LoRA fine-tunes of the same task, beside the Adam one. We then probed Adam's failing copy two ways. Random weight moves of the same size test whether size alone fails the check. Putting groups of pieces back to their original values shows where the excess lies. Amendments read new rows of Pile text and a second seed.

What we found. It depends on the fine-tune, and not on how spread out it is. The LoRA's copy passes (1.27, again at a second seed and on new rows). Adam's fails on every block of rows read (2.20, then 1.88). The SGD copies failed on the first 256 rows (2.07) but passed on the next 256 (1.18, 1.39); their failure sits on a single block of 128 rows, so SGD is unresolved (last figure). On Adam's copy, random moves of the same size pass, and the failure sits in the MLP changes acting together.

What it means. A broad fine-tune is not out of reach. Training the pieces on the task's labels reached 96% of the gain and passed, so a faithful state exists. The open question is whether teacher training finds it for every kind of fine-tune. The SGD verdict turns on which rows the adversary reads, so the next run, E49, reads many more row blocks at several adversary starts.

Run
Teacher training of an SGD (rate 0.01) and a rank-8 LoRA fine-tune of the lowercase task, beside E37's Adam fine-tune; random moves and group reverts on Adam's copy; amendments added a second seed, an SGD fine-tune cut to rank 8 and 256 new Pile rows. RunPod H100, three jobs.
Found
  • The LoRA's copy passes on two seeds and new rows, and Adam's fails every block. The SGD copies fail on one block of rows and pass elsewhere, so whether plain SGD's copy is faithful is open.
  • Neither how spread out the update is (its stable rank: 28.5 Adam, 3.5 SGD, 1.9 LoRA) nor the copy's damage on plain text predicts the verdict, except at the extremes.
  • Trained on the task's labels with SGD, the pieces reached 96% of the gain and passed at step 100 (1.28), so a faithful state exists for this task.
