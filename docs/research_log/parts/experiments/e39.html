Why does teacher training on a broad fine-tune fail the adversarial check, and does a less spread-out update help?

2026-10-27 · RunPod H100 · experiments/broad_fragility

Less spread out is not enough. A rank-8 LoRA fine-tune gives a trained decomposition that passes (1.27), but an SGD fine-tune with almost as concentrated an update (stable rank 3.5, against the LoRA's 1.9 and Adam's 28.5) fails (2.07) like Adam's (2.20). Random moves of matched weight size pass, but change the outputs far less, and on Adam's the failure sits in the MLP changes acting together. Amendment 2 found the LoRA's pass repeats, and that a rank-8 cap on the SGD fine-tune does not explain it.

Run
One H100 job on E37's trained decomposition of the lowercase fine-tune (stage 0 controls first, all passed): a random move of matched size per matrix, reverts by layer, by attention or MLP, and by Γ's importance halves, and teacher training on an SGD and a rank-8 LoRA fine-tune of the same task. Amendment 1 reran the SGD arm on rates 0.003 to 0.1, after the first grid lowered held-out likelihood at every rate; its controls reproduced the first run to every printed digit.
Found
  • The LoRA's trained decomposition passes at 1.27 where Adam's reads 2.20, against the filed prediction, and passes again at a second seed and batch order (1.27) and on 256 new Pile rows (1.21, 1.24), where Adam's still fails (1.88); SGD's fails at 2.07, and cutting the SGD fine-tune back to rank 8 after every step did not make it pass (2.12; one rate, one seed), so a rank-8 cap alone does not explain the LoRA's pass.
  • A random move of the same weight size passes (1.03 and 1.07, against 1.00 to 1.075 for the unchanged model), but does about a twenty-fifth of the trained decomposition's damage on plain text; random moves matched to that damage fail far worse (16.5, 27.2). Damage on plain text lines up with the verdict only at the ends: under 0.06 every state passes, over 0.2 every one fails, and between 0.09 and 0.13 the check runs from 1.27 to 2.70.
  • Trained directly on the task's labels, the subcomponents passed with plain SGD at step 100 (1.28, 96% of the fine-tune's gain), the state the gain rule chose; later steps were not read. With Adam the chosen state (step 1,000) failed at 7.24. So on this task the broad fail is not a fixed limit of the decomposition: a frozen-Γ state can reach the task's gain and pass. On Adam's teacher-trained decomposition, reverting all MLP matrices removes the whole excess (1.00) and reverting attention raises it (2.70).
