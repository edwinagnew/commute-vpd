Why does teacher training on a broad fine-tune fail the adversarial check, and does a less spread-out update help?

2026-10-27 · RunPod H100 · experiments/broad_fragility

The Adam fine-tune's trained decomposition fails the check on every block of Pile rows read (2.20, 1.88), a rank-8 LoRA's passes (1.27), and whether a plain SGD fine-tune belongs with Adam or the LoRA is unresolved. On the first 256 rows both SGD trained decompositions fail (2.07, 2.12), but on the next 256 both pass (1.18, 1.39), and the fail rests on one block of 128 rows. Random moves of matched weight size pass, but change the outputs far less, and on Adam's the failure sits in the MLP changes acting together. Amendment 3 (H100, $2.12, 2026-10-29) stopped there by its rule; the rest waits on a card.

Run
One H100 job on E37's trained decomposition of the lowercase fine-tune (stage 0 controls first, all passed): a random move of matched size per matrix, reverts by layer, by attention or MLP, and by Γ's importance halves, and teacher training on an SGD and a rank-8 LoRA fine-tune of the same task. Amendment 1 reran the SGD arm on rates 0.003 to 0.1, after the first grid lowered held-out likelihood at every rate; its controls reproduced the first run to every printed digit.
Found
  • The LoRA's trained decomposition passes at 1.27 where Adam's reads 2.20, against the filed prediction, and passes again at a second seed and batch order (1.27) and on 256 new Pile rows (1.21, 1.24), where Adam's still fails (1.88). The SGD ones failed on the first rows (2.07; 2.12 with the fine-tune cut back to rank 8) but pass on the new rows (1.18, 1.39), against the filed fail; their fail sits on rows 128-255 alone, where Adam's four blocks all fail. So "full-matrix fine-tunes fail, the LoRA passes" holds for Adam only; SGD is unresolved on the broad task.
  • A random move of the same weight size passes (1.03 and 1.07, against 1.00 to 1.075 for the unchanged model), but does about a twenty-fifth of the trained decomposition's damage on plain text; random moves matched to that damage fail far worse (16.5, 27.2). On the first rows, damage on plain text lines up with the verdict only at the ends: under 0.06 every state passes, over 0.2 every one fails, and between 0.09 and 0.13 the check runs from 1.27 to 2.70; how far that holds on other rows is unknown.
  • Trained directly on the task's labels, the subcomponents passed with plain SGD at step 100 (1.28, 96% of the fine-tune's gain), the state the gain rule chose; later steps were not read. With Adam the chosen state (step 1,000) failed at 7.24. So on this task the broad fail is not a fixed limit of the decomposition: a frozen-Γ state can reach the task's gain and pass. On Adam's teacher-trained decomposition, reverting all MLP matrices removes the whole excess (1.00) and reverting attention raises it (2.70).
