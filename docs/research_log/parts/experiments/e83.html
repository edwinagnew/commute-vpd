Do the decomposition's masks shrink a real update's effect on the logits on the 67M model too?

2026-11-04 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, about 4 minutes, $0 (job 51367) · experiments/logit_shift_67m

The question. Your one-off for Q3 of the update notes, which does not reopen the re-gating line. On Qwen3-1.7B the masks left only a tenth to a quarter of the first update's effect on the logits. Is that so on the 67M model as well?

What we did. We took two finished lowercase updates, an Adam fine-tune and a rank-8 LoRA, and added each to the 67M as an extra term on every matrix's input that the masks cannot touch. The bar is the size of the logit change that term causes under the masks, over its size with no masks, so 1 means the masks did nothing to it, summed over 256 Pile rows. We compared random masks on a random subset of matrices, as decomposition training draws them, random masks on all 24, and fixed masks from Γ on the unchanged model.

What we found. The masks shrink the effect to 0.55 and 0.57 on the random subset, about 0.3 on all matrices and 0.20 and 0.15 with fixed masks, in the order Qwen showed, but keeping about twice as much. All four controls held, including the last matrix alone, which read 1.02 and 1.12.

What it means. A real update's effect also shrinks under the masks on the small model, which bears on how to read faithfulness checks after an update. The bar is size only, so it says nothing about the direction or why. The comparison with Qwen crosses models, matrix counts and updates, so the gap is described and not explained.
