Do E21's ladder verdicts survive a second batch order?

2026-10-22 · RunPod H100 ×2 · experiments/ladder_second_seed

Yes. Retrained with a second order of training batches, component training repeats E21's pattern on both word tasks where it was claimed: Adam at rate 1e-5 stays at the original model's floor at steps 100 and 1,000, and plain SGD passes at step 100 and fails at step 1,000 at about three times the floor (figure). On the rare-target task neither optimiser gives a faithful state that keeps the target, as before. E21's one-seed findings belong to the recipe, not to one run.

Run
E21's recipe at batch-order seed 1, the only randomness in training: plain SGD at E21's rates (0.03 on r0 and r1, 0.1 on r2) and Adam 1e-5, 1,000 steps each, on r0 (" it" then " is"), r1 (" which" then " was") and r2 (" in" then " Fig"). Each state through the Pile check at steps 100 and 1,000. The adversarial ratio is the KL a 20-step mask adversary reaches at the trained state over what it reaches at the original model; 1 means nothing changed. The floor is the original model's spread over three adversary seeds in this job, 0.075, so a state passes at 1.40 or below and fails at 1.61 or above, and must keep the target's held-out probability at 0.9 or more. Controls first: the original model read its known values, and the first order, regenerated, reproduced E21 at step 1,000 within 5.4% on r0 and r1. Two pods (the first killed at a usage-limit halt during r1), about $6 in all.
Found
  • Adam 1e-5, second order, passes at both steps: 0.95 and 1.14 on r0, 0.98 and 0.96 on r1. Filed prediction: replicates.
  • SGD 0.03, second order, passes at step 100 (0.97, 1.05) and fails at step 1,000 (3.06, 2.91). Filed prediction: replicates. SGD's late fail is not seed noise.
  • At step 1,000 both optimisers reach the same size of change, about 0.017 to 0.019 (largest relative weight change over the 24 matrices), as with the first order. Damage elsewhere stays far under each task's limit.
  • Random masks see no difference: the stochastic ratio sits between 0.95 and 1.02 on r0 and r1. Only the adversary separates the optimisers.
  • r2, as expected: SGD fails at step 100 (5.9) and reads 52 at step 1,000 with the target's probability at 0.89; Adam's ratio stays low (1.31 to 1.36) while the target's probability falls to 0.80.
  • One control missed, not binding: r2's regenerated SGD state read 28% above E21's (48.8 against 38.0), same verdict. Second-order states came out bit-identical on the two pods.
