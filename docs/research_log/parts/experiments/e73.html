Is the clean Qwen3-1.7B sensitive to any change of that size, or only along a fine-tuning update?

2026-11-03 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS, about 17 min, $0 (jobs 50462, 50465) · experiments/qwen_diagnose

The question. The masked forwards of E72 barely responded to a real update (Q1; D1 is a map on Qwen3-1.7B that keeps the decomposition faithful for a real update, judged by a test that can fail a bad placement). Before blaming the masks we asked whether the rows, the model or the update was unusual in the clean model, with no masks at all.

What we did. Nothing was trained. In the clean model we added a fraction t of each of four saved updates to W0, a Gaussian and a shuffled update at the update's own norm, and one block at a time, and read W0's calibration on maths, FineWeb and template rows. KL(W0 ‖ W0 + change) is how far the change moves the model's output distribution.

What we found. Only the update moves the clean model. At its own norm a Gaussian or shuffled update moves W0 by 9e-6 to 4e-4 nats, while the update moves it by 0.27 to 0.80 (first figure). On the three gold-data updates a fraction of the update beats the whole of it on next-token loss, with the gain peaking at 1.37 to 1.98; on the template it climbs to 1 with no peak (second figure). W0 is overconfident on every set of rows, including FineWeb, by 1.10 to 2.04 nats.

What it means. W0 is not fragile in general, so the clean model's response to the update is not the odd part, and the masked forward's near-deafness (E74) is not explained by the rows. Gains above 1 are a shape of the update's own path, not of any placement; their cause was not read. Our leans that the path would be sharp, and that FineWeb would be calibrated, missed.

Run
Reads on saved states, four updates on 64 rows, about 17 minutes on one L40, $0; one earlier job stopped on a normaliser.
Found
  • A Gaussian or shuffled update at the update's norm moves W0 by at most 0.0005 of what the update does, so W0 is not fragile in general; expected.
  • The path is "between" (a tenth of the update gives 0.11 to 0.18 of the whole one's KL on the gold-data updates, 0.02 on the template), not the sharp shape we leaned to.
  • W0 is overconfident on all three sets of rows, against a filed lean that FineWeb would be calibrated.
