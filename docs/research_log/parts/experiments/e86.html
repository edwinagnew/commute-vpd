Does any other saved update's gain build gradually, so that "behaviour kept" can grade a placement?

2026-11-04 · MATS cluster L40, $0, three jobs · experiments/qwen_d1_fractions · provisional, no analysis report yet, not independently checked

The question. On Qwen3-1.7B's first fine-tune, a tenth of the update already keeps 93% of the gain, so "behaviour kept" cannot grade a placement there: one could lose most of the update and still read as keeping the behaviour. D1 needs a test that can fail a bad placement, so we looked for an update whose gain builds.

What we did. We read two other saved updates, the TOFU fine-tune (E81, step 1250) and the gold fine-tune (E61, step 100), with the first fine-tune as the control, run first and alone. For each we added a fraction t of the update to the original weights (t = 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1) and read how much of the gain was kept. We also ran the clean-model table, which splits the update by least squares, by the adjoint and by the often-on and rarely-on halves, and compares them with a random change and a sign-flipped one, and read the share of the update left in Δ.

What we found. TOFU's gain builds: a tenth of the update keeps 0.28 of the gain, a quarter 0.58, a half 0.84. Gold's barely does: a tenth keeps 0.83. The first fine-tune's does not, as before. On both updates the least-squares split leaves about 9-10% of the update in Δ and the adjoint split none, as on the first fine-tune.

What it means. TOFU's update is the candidate on which "behaviour kept" can grade a placement; the first fine-tune's cannot. Read provisionally: no report yet, no independent check; the decision it feeds is card decision-after-e76.
