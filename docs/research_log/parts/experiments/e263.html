Does the write-share reading of reuse (E_b) keep its order when the parts are sparse-autoencoder latents instead of VPD subcomponents?

2026-11-22 · cell P1.52, sae-lead, no independent check · experiments/sae_eb

The question. You chose the reading of reuse that counts, over the changed parts, how much of the fine-tune's change in what each part writes the original model's own write explains (E_b). At VPD it passes its controls: do-nothing 1, a doubled-gain edit 0.76, a random update 0.011. A definition should not depend on the featurization, so this asks whether the built order (reuse edits far above new edits and random updates) survives at the base-only sparse-autoencoder (SAE) latents of E234. Two rivals: the order holds, with reuse edits at least 0.2 above every new edit and random update (rival a); or E_b measures how well a featurization lines up with the update, and the order is lost (rival b).

What we did. One write-change pass on each of 114 saved states (six built edits, 17 random updates, the nine pairs' states, fifteen key-task states) with the SAE latents as the parts and each site's reconstruction error as one extra part, on the search already saved by E245 and E251. The do-nothing state was read first on every job: no change, E_b exactly 1.

What we found. The two gain edits read 0.969 and 0.947 (0.76 each at VPD); every new edit and random update reads at most 0.020. The rebuilt-circuit edit falls from 0.631 at VPD to 0.135, so the lowest reuse edit sits only 0.115 above the highest new or random one, under the 0.2 bar: rival b as filed, though the order is kept (rebuilt is about seven times the highest random). The eight reconstruction-error parts carry 0.83 of rebuilt's change; counting latents only, rebuilt reads 0.789 and the highest random 0.076. Trained pairs and key tasks read 0.028 to 0.082, and the nine pairs' reuse-minus-new gaps are within 0.03 of zero at both featurizations.

What it means. The reading gives the same order at both featurizations, but only when the featurization represents the changed writes: what the SAE cannot write sits in one remainder variable, which the original model's write does not explain, so it counts as new. The filed bar was missed by 0.085, and the cause is the remainder, not the latents. Still open: an SAE trained to reconstruct the fine-tuned model's writes would shrink the remainder (a new cell, not run).

Run
114 saved states, one write-change pass each, on cluster debug L40s (jobs 63389 to 63392, about 12 minutes, $0).
Found
  • Gain edits 0.97 and 0.95; new edits and random updates at most 0.020: the built order holds at SAE latents as expected.
  • Rebuilt reads 0.135 against 0.631 at VPD, so the filed 0.2 margin is missed (gap 0.115); the SAE remainders carry 0.83 of its change.
  • Over latents only, rebuilt reads 0.789 against 0.076 for the highest random; the nine pairs stay within 0.03 of zero gap at both featurizations.
