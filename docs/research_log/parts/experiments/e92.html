On Qwen3-1.7B, can cheap readings with no decomposition, neurons among them, tell reuse from new, and do they already give VPD's verdicts?

2026-11-05 to 11-06 · provisional; three of the later jobs' independent checks hold with caveats · cluster L40 debug jobs, $0 · experiments/qwen_comparators, qwen_carrying, known_answer_pair, rows_null, label_vs_style, larger_size, qwen_comparator_table

The question. On Qwen3-1.7B we want to know whether a decomposition can tell a fine-tune that reuses what the model has from one that adds something new. A separation means little if a cheap number with no decomposition already gives it. So we asked two things: does any cheap reading tell reuse from new by itself, and does it already sort the verdicts VPD gave on the same saved fine-tunes?

What we did. Every read used saved Qwen fine-tunes, labelled by how they were built. The template fine-tune teaches an answer layout the model already gives under a system turn (reuse). The fictional-facts fine-tune teaches invented authors' biographies (new). The known-facts fine-tune uses the fictional one's recipe on 217 real-person and world facts the model already answers. Most reads were at matched size, the step where the weight change equals the template's; one was at the end of training. The cheap readings were update size, the gain on the trained answer, and the steering share: how much of the gain one average activation difference recovers. The neuron readings centre on the carrying set: the 1,024 MLP neurons whose changed firing, with weights kept, carries most of the gain. We compared carrying sets across seeds, across disjoint halves of the known rows, and against a run with every known answer made wrong, so only agreement with the model differs. An agent-and-judge pass also asked what each carrying neuron did on corpus text. Last, we set all of this beside VPD's verdicts row by row.

What we found. The steering share flips direction with the training step, so it is a description of the output, not a reading of reuse. The carrying set overlaps a system turn's neurons almost as much for new facts as for the template, and the judge pass lacked power. The carrying set did separate known from fictional facts beyond seed noise, and swapping the trained rows barely moved it. But making every answer wrong did not move it either (first figure), so it reads source and style, not whether the model already held the fact. At the end of training two seeds no longer agree on their carrying sets (second figure), so that test had no power. Set beside VPD, neither method tells new from existing on these pairs (third figure). Update size and gain alone sort VPD's pass and fail on the first Qwen table, and every pair VPD separates the cheap numbers separate too, often by more (fourth figure).

What it means. On Qwen no cheap reading tells reuse from new, and none of VPD's verdicts there shows anything the cheap numbers miss, except one end-of-training read already explained as task and format. So VPD's added value over cheap numbers is a 67M finding so far. E133, the current comparison of the re-gating share against baselines, is on the 67M only and ran no Qwen pair. This card is therefore still the latest word on Qwen, and Part 3 starts from it: the cheap columns are the bar, and a Qwen pair read with the re-gating share has not been built. A caveat on every gap here: the arms were mostly read on their own rows, and topic and style differ between them.

Run
Seven experiments on single L40s in the cluster's debug partition, all $0, most reading saved Qwen states and three training new ones of a few hundred steps; agent calls wrote and judged neuron jobs. They were first logged under their own codes, now folded into this card:
  • update size, prior and steering share beside each Qwen row (was E92, experiments/qwen_comparators; three jobs, then 52467);
  • carrying-set overlap with a system turn, firing at the answers, and the neuron-job judge (was E102, experiments/qwen_carrying; jobs 53025, 53029, 53068);
  • the known-facts fine-tune at two seeds against the fictional one (was E103, experiments/known_answer_pair; jobs 53188, 53209, 53262, 53275, 53316, 53334);
  • disjoint halves of the known rows (was E105, experiments/rows_null; job 53480);
  • wrong answers on the known rows, a topic split, and VPD's switched-on set on the same states (was E107, experiments/label_vs_style; jobs 53575, 53822);
  • the wrong and true runs trained to their end (was E109, experiments/larger_size; jobs 54053, 54062);
  • four cheap numbers beside every Qwen state with a VPD row, 180 cells (was E118, experiments/qwen_comparator_table, table in experiments/neuron_vpd_table; jobs 54382, 54383).
Found
  • No cheap reading tells reuse from new on Qwen: the steering share flips with step, and the carrying set follows the data's source and style, not whether the model held the fact.
  • Once the facts are learned, two seeds share only about 0.65 of their carrying set, so the neuron reading has no power there.
  • Update size and gain sort VPD's first-table pass and fail exactly, and the cheap numbers separate every pair VPD separates; only the known-facts arm at its end reads differently.
