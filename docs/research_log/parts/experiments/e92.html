What do three cheap readings say beside each Qwen row of the new-against-existing comparison?

2026-11-05 · cluster L40 debug, $0 · experiments/qwen_comparators · provisional, no independent check; Amendment 1 included

The question. The Qwen comparison (D3) asks whether a decomposition can tell an update that reuses what the model has from one that adds something new. Without a cheap reading beside each row, a separation could be one that size alone or a steering vector already gives.

What we did. On saved Qwen states we read three things: how large the update is, how highly the unchanged model already ranks the answer the update trains, and how much of the gain one steering vector recovers (a random vector of the same norm is the control). Anchor 1, the answer-format template update, is read on held-out problems; anchor 2, the facts update, on its trained rows, at matched size and in full; the maths arms on gold solutions, not the model's own greedy answers.

What we found. One steering vector recovers 0.83 of the facts update's gain at the template's size and 0.065 at its own full size, on both seeds, against the template's 0.185 at a norm of 0.487, so the comparison changes direction with the step. At matched size the facts update gains about 2 nats on tokens the unchanged model rated very low, without ranking more right tokens first. Eight of the filed leans missed, among them the matched share and the matched gain, and the first-token anomaly is real. A second read with output entropy saved (Amendment 1) shows that at matched size both the facts update and its vector read as a flatter output: the unchanged model at equal entropy earns 1.29 of the vector's 1.73, and ranks barely move. At full size the vector's rise is no more than random vectors of its norm and variance share earn, and the template's share also falls with step over a size range the facts line barely shares.

What it means. The steering share is a comparator with its step and size beside it, not the definition of new against reuse, and a gain at matched size is not evidence of learning. No cheap number tells the template update from the facts update, and the steering share cannot be read across the two as new against old at either size; these reads describe the output's shape, not the cause in the weights. Every D3 cell of the comparator table is a gap until qwen-decomp-lead reads the decomposition's rows.

Run
Three cluster L40 jobs in debug, then Amendment 1's (job 52467), $0; the control (anchor 1's zero state and full update) ran first and alone.
Found
  • The steering share falls with step on both seeds (0.83, 0.23, 0.065; the middle values an unfiled addition), the reverse of the template at its size.
  • At matched size the facts update gains on very-low-rated tokens and loses on those ranked first, with no more answers ranked first.
  • At matched size the facts update and its vector read as a flatter output, and at full size the vector's rise is no more than matched random vectors earn, so the share does not separate new from existing.
  • Eight filed leans missed, including the matched share and gain.
