Why does the least-squares start read worse than do-nothing under the adversarial check?

2026-11-06 · provisional, no independent check · four cluster debug L40 jobs (54836, 54874, 54890, 54934), about 66 minutes, $0 · experiments/ls_start_faith

The question. E121 and E122 found that the least-squares split start, a state close to the fine-tune's weight change, reads above the do-nothing state (leaving the update in Δ) under the adversarial check, on both the emoticon and lowercase tasks. Is that excess caused by where the change sits (on the alive subcomponents) or by how the start's weights differ from the fine-tune's?

What we did. We read the start, then each teacher-trained copy's own weight change, in two places: on the alive subcomponents and moved into Δ. A hold that keeps the alive masks at 1 removes the adversary's choice of which subcomponents to switch off. Controls included do-nothing and the standard copy under the hold.

What we found. On both tasks the start's excess over do-nothing is all placement: its own weight change kept in Δ reads better than do-nothing (lowercase 2.95 against 4.79, the start itself 8.33). Holding the alive masks at 1 removed the excess but also changed the adversary (the unchanged model reads 1.60 against 0.69 nats) and, in Amendment 1, had no teeth: do-nothing held reads 1.19 on lowercase against a bar of 2.90, so that reading is unreadable. Amendment 2 reversed the filed lean: the lowercase copy's own change in Δ fails (3.53 against 1.91 on the subcomponents; copy from the start 3.96 against 9.40; rank-8 LoRA copy 2.34 against 1.33).

What it means. Placement cuts both ways: the least-squares split makes the excess about 3.8 times larger, teacher training makes it 3 to 4 times smaller, by accident (its loss has no mask term). The broad lowercase failure is a function fragile under the check that teacher training's placement mostly hides, enough for the LoRA copy but not full-matrix Adam. Whether fragility is the cause is not tested; one seed, one task.

Run
Four cluster debug L40 jobs, about 66 minutes in all, $0: the first run (two jobs), Amendment 1 (stopped on the emoticon copy control, most likely bf16 rounding, not rerun) and Amendment 2 (every control exact).
Found
  • The start's excess over do-nothing is placement, not the weight change: its own change in Δ reads 2.95 against do-nothing 4.79 on lowercase.
  • Holding the alive masks at 1 has no teeth (do-nothing held 1.19 against a bar of 2.90), so "the alive masks carry the excess" is unreadable under the hold.
  • Each copy's own change in Δ fails the check (standard 3.53, copy from the start 3.96, LoRA copy 2.34), the reverse of the filed lean of 1.24.
