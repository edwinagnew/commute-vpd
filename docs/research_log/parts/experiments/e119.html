The question. You asked by name where the twelve teacher-trained decompositions of E34 (two key rules and the emoticon task, each copied from an SGD and an Adam fine-tune) put the gain they show at the trigger: in Γ's switching, or in subcomponents Γ keeps off. That bears on whether these updates arrive as a change in which existing subcomponents are used.

What we did. E34 saved none of its copies, so we regenerated the six fine-tunes and twelve copies with the same seeds, and stopped if any copy's target probability on held-out windows fell more than 0.05 from E34's. The gain is the rise in the log-odds of the target token at the trigger. The re-switching share is the part of it that moving only Γ's masks to the new state's gives (0: none, 1: all). The masked-off share is the change in what the switched-off subcomponents contribute (0: none, 1: all). Labels use E12's filed lines: re-switching if the first is at least 0.6 and the second at most 0.25, masked-off the reverse (second at least 0.5, first at most 0.35), otherwise neither.
