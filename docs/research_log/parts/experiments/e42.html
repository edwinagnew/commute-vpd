E42surprise

Can teacher training copy a behaviour the model lacked, faithfully and with Δ fixed?

2026-10-28 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/teacher_training

Yes by the criterion in force, with one caveat. Copies of fine-tunes that teach the model a word cipher it lacked keep the cipher at its trigger, pass the harness's four reconstruction checks on Pile text and leave Δ untouched, on all three tasks (first figure). But on both random ciphers, the model built only from the subcomponents Γ marks important at the trigger keeps almost none of it (second and third figures). Surprise: a copy trained on plain Pile text alone reached 0.91 on the one-pair cipher, where we filed below 0.5.

Run
E10's cipher fine-tunes (all 24 matrices, Adam 3e-5, batch 32, seed 0) run to step 1,000 on three tasks: 32 random key-to-value pairs, one random pair, and an identity control in which each key is followed by itself, which the model half does already. Each copied by teacher training from the unchanged decomposition, Γ and Δ frozen, Adam 1e-5 for 1,000 steps: once on the task's 403 training windows, once on Pile rows that hold no marker. Read beside do-nothing and the least-squares split through the Pile harness (one 20-step adversary start, 256 rows), the route split at the trigger on 97 held-out windows, and the fine-tune's predictions in plain Pile text. Controls first, all passed. Three cluster jobs, about 22 minutes each, $0.
Found
  • The copy trained on task text keeps the cipher at its trigger (0.96 to 1.00) and passes the harness's four reconstruction checks on Pile text with Δ untouched, on all three tasks, as filed; VPD's sparsity loss was not read.
  • On both random ciphers, the model built from the subcomponents Γ marks important at the trigger keeps almost none of it (0.007 where the copy reads 0.96 on 32 pairs): the gain runs through subcomponents Γ keeps off, as filed and as with label-trained component training (E10). No map read so far with Γ frozen keeps the cipher there; three simpler masked versions broke their own nothing-to-copy control, so none was run on the cipher.
  • A filed prediction reversed: the copy trained on plain Pile text alone reached 0.91 on the one-pair cipher and passed, where we filed below 0.5. It sits just over the line, and a lower rerun would still contradict the filing; the fine-tune already shows that pair in plain text, untested as the cause. The random-mask term alone also failed its control (11.7), reversing a low-confidence lean towards a pass; why is not tested.

By the criterion in force (reading A) the copy passes; GOALS.md's mechanism section, marked as not deciding, would read its masked-off share as a fail, and on 2026-10-29 you decided that it counts: the pass adds behaviour kept by the parts Γ switches on, on the task's own text, as a ratio to the unchanged decomposition on the same rows, once its own masked fidelity on cipher windows is read. Amendments 1 and 2 (MATS cluster, $0) tried to fix the masked-off share by adding masked terms to teacher training, and each version was first run with nothing to copy: all four (both terms; the CI-masked term alone; the random-mask term alone; both at the cue only) broke the worst-case check on ordinary text, at 12 to 49 times the unchanged model against a fail line of 1.81, while the random-mask check stayed level, so no masked copy was made. Whether training with Γ fixed can keep the cipher in the parts switched on at the cue is therefore untested; one task, one batch order and one adversary start.
