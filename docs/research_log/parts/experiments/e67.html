Does a weight-side overlap share tell a fine-tune that learned nothing from one that learned, and how far do Γ's values move?

2026-11-02 · MATS cluster debug QoS, $0 (job 49342) · experiments/weight_side_read

The question. Re-gating (Q5) reads whether a fine-tune switches subcomponents on or off. The test r failed (E36) was a fine-tune that learned nothing; E67 asked whether a read from the weights, not the behaviour, passes that same null, and gives a first reading of your definition of re-gating, the difference in gating before and after fine-tuning, at every position.

What we did. Nothing was trained. On saved fine-tunes we gave Γ the fine-tuned model's activations while the subcomponents stayed as in the original model, and read two things on Pile rows. First, how much Γ's values change, per subcomponent and position. Second, an overlap share: how much of the switching this causes lines up with the update's own change to the matrix output (0 means orthogonal, 1 means it matches). The states were two copy fine-tunes, a fine-tune that learned nothing at steps 10 and 100, and a random update of the same size as the first copy fine-tune; an unchanged model first, which must read exactly 0 and did. The filed bars were guesses: the share keeps if the learned-nothing fine-tune reads at most 0.1 and both copy fine-tunes at least 0.4, and drops if it is within 0.15 of both.

What we found. The filed rule drops the share on these rows and states, and that is a limit of the test as much as a result: the shares read 0.02 to 0.06 (copy 0.056 and 0.043, learned nothing 0.031 and 0.022, random 0.001), so no ordering of them could have missed the 0.15 band, and every update was read off the text where it learned. The gap between the two copy seeds, 0.014, is about as large as the gap to the learned-nothing fine-tune at step 10. Γ's values move on every update, and half to two thirds as far on the fine-tune that learned nothing as on the copy fine-tunes (mean change 3.5e-4 and 4.9e-4 against 7.7e-4 and 7.4e-4).

What it means. The run shows neither that the share is informative nor that it is not: it has no positive control on real rows, and the copy fine-tunes were off task on every row read. The split side is not added and no weight-side column goes to the method table. The all-position reading of Γ's change stands by itself; it is not a causal check and says nothing about mechanism.

Run
One cluster debug job (49342, L40, 1 minute 29 seconds, $0) over seven reads on 128 Pile rows, plus 64 code-heavy rows where r failed for two of the states.
Found
  • The overlap share is far under the filed guess for every fine-tune (0.02 to 0.06 against a guess of 0.2 to 0.6), so the drop is the rule firing on small numbers, not a separation.
  • The random update reads about zero by construction, so reading above it is not a sign of learning; the learned-nothing fine-tune reads above it too.
  • Γ's values move on a fine-tune that learned nothing, half to two thirds as far as on the copy fine-tunes, so a moved Γ is not by itself evidence of learning on these rows.
