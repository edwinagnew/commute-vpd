Do five cheap readings tell the two sides of a Qwen3-1.7B fine-tune pair apart, beyond how big the update is?

2026-11-12 · cell P3.7, 1 of 2 runs used, provisional, no independent check · three pods (RunPod H100, Vast H100, Vast A100), 70 to 80 min each · experiments/qwen_library_readings

The question. Part 4 compares cheap readings of a saved update with the decomposition's readings (section {{n:next}}, cell P3.7). The pair library of cell P3.5 holds fine-tunes whose two sides differ by construction. On every pair, do the cheap readings differ between side A and side B by more than random weight changes would make them differ?

What we did. Four pairs, side A minus side B, three seeds paired. Copy against cipher and known against invented facts are built pairs; gold maths and an answer template are twins, each trained once with real answers and once with shuffled ones. Each pair is read at its last save (step 300 for the built pairs, 100 for the twins) and at the first save where the output change is five times the decomposition's masked error. Five readings: update size (the weight change as a share of the weights), the unchanged model's prior on the side's answers, steering (one vector, the update's mean shift of a block's input, added to the unchanged model), the effective rank of the update, and the top-neuron overlap (how many of the 480 most strongly firing MLP neurons per block stay on top). Each reading has a random floor band, from random changes at the library's smallest and largest size, and a zero update was read first.

What we found. Several gaps lie outside their bands. The overlap is higher on side A on copy minus cipher at both saves, on the facts pair at the matched save and on both twins late; the effective rank is lower on side A on both built pairs late. In every cell where the overlap passed its band, side A was also the smaller weight change, and at the last save cipher and invented facts are 2.0 to 2.4 times their partners. Steering has a band only on gold (±1.03, the fraction's whole range).

What it means. This describes the library and answers nothing yet: no gap is told apart from update size, and the bands are narrow by construction (a random change's overlap only falls from about 0.998 to 0.97 across the whole size range). Nothing is labelled reuse or new, and no share or verdict is given. The premise check for the cell reads doubtful.

Run
Three pods in parallel, one per group of pairs, each reading its pair's stages 0 to 2 (zero update, random floor, saved states); all 42 saved states matched the library's recorded checksums and weight-change norms.
Found
  • Overlap is higher on side A beyond its band in most cells, but side A is the smaller update in each of them; copy's own overlap falls from 0.77 at the matched save to 0.48 at the last, so a few hundredths between different sizes is not ruled out as size.
  • Effective rank is lower on side A at the last save on both built pairs, by far more than its band of 31, yet its sign is not tied to size across pairs: the template twin's smaller real side reads higher.
  • Steering on copy minus cipher reads +0.58 late and −0.33 matched, with no band to judge either; the prior columns disagree in sign on that pair.
