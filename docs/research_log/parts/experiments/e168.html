Does the re-weighting share tell a memorised fact from its reuse twin on the 67M?

2026-11-12 · cell P1.16, 1 of 2 runs used, provisional, no independent check · cluster debug L40, about 80 min, $0 · experiments/memorised_fact

The question. Part 2's working definition calls an update reused when its change is a re-weighting of something the model already computes. You asked whether a memorised fact, a random one-bit label for each number token, reads as new, beside a twin that learned "the last number is odd", which the plain model already holds. The tentative reading, "new means not re-weighted", and the adopted wording could label them differently.

What we did. Two fine-tunes of the 67M, memorised fact and odd twin, two seeds each, with the same inputs, push and prior; the fact's bit marks as many of the 100 number tokens as oddness does and agrees with it on 0.46 of them. Beside them, three edits written straight into the plain model: an exact planted odd edit (the positive control), a planted fact edit sized to the same gap, and a floor keyed on pairs of numbers that no single-token map can reach. The re-weighting share is the share of the gap between the two models closed by the best affine map of a four-direction variable from its own value (0 nothing, 1 all); the rewiring share is the same with the map reading everything computed before the site.

What we found. The memorised fact reads 0.10 and 0.12 on the re-weighting share, against its twin's 0.39 and 0.40, well past the filed smallest gap of 0.17 and in the order of all five older matched pairs. The planted edits do not separate: the planted fact edit reads 0.48 against the planted odd edit's 0.63, within 0.17, and that exact re-weighting reads only 0.63 where at least 0.9 was expected. The floor reads -0.15.

What it means. Under the adopted wording both fact seeds and both twin seeds read "no map found" (rewiring share 0.36 and 0.37 against 0.47 and 0.46), so the memorised fact is never labelled new, and the twin gets the same label. Under the tentative reading every item reads new, including the exact planted edit, so as an absolute label it cannot tell the fact from its twin; only the ranking within the pair does. Wording unchanged; whether the gap reflects the variable or how training implemented it is open.

Run
Six cluster debug jobs (59525-59527, 59533-59535), L40, about 80 min wall, $0; both fact seeds and both twin seeds learned the target at 300 steps. The rewiring shares of the planted odd edit and the floor are the earlier sum-parity run's readings (E157), not re-searched.
Found
  • The fine-tunes separate: fact 0.10 and 0.12 against twin 0.39 and 0.40, over a seed spread of about 0.04.
  • The planted edits are within 0.17 of each other (0.48 against 0.63), and the exact odd edit misses its expected 0.9 though it clears the stop at 0.5.
  • Adopted wording: "no map found" for fact and twin alike; tentative reading: every item new.
Bears on
Part 2: neither wording labels a memorised fact as new on this layout; the share's within-pair ranking holds again.
