Does the stand-in ratio tell a behaviour the original 67M model already performs (lowercase) from one it cannot (a 50-word cipher), fine-tuned on the same rows?

2026-10-29 · MATS cluster L40, $0 (first check on a RunPod 4090, $0.08) · experiments/performed_anchor

The question. The re-gating line's one number that might tell an existing behaviour's fine-tune from a new one's is the stand-in ratio, and it had been read only on toys the original model does not perform at all (E46, E50). The user chose (2026-10-29) to read it on a pair the original model really sorts that way: continuing lowercase Pile text in lowercase (performed), against a fixed swap of 50 common words (not performed).

What we did. First the original model alone: it prefers the lowercase form in lowercased text by 1.18 nats and the real word over the swapped one by 5.53, so the pair passed its go rule. Then we fine-tuned each task on the same 2,400 rows (Adam, 3e-5, 300 steps; two batch orders), split each finished update over the subcomponents by least squares, and read the stand-in ratio. That is the gain in the model rebuilt from the subcomponents under Γ's new masks, over the gain from the moved subcomponents alone. Near 1 means Γ's new switching explains the gain; the toys' copies read 0.7 to 0.8 and random maps 0.4. Controls were a KL-matched random update and a hand-made write that builds no mechanism.

What we found. The ratio separates the pair: lowercase reads 0.92 and 0.93 (the two batch orders), the cipher 0.26 on both, a gap of 0.65 against the 0.3 bar. The cipher stays low at the saved states that bracket lowercase's gain (0.28 to 0.38 against 0.92). The relay number (restoring the original pieces' outputs in the real fine-tuned model) does not separate them: 0.66 against 0.41 to 0.42. The hand-write check failed to reach its gain, so it is unread.

What it means. This is the first pair where the ratio separates a performed behaviour from a new one, but it is only one pair, and lowercase is also the broader update: it touches about twice as many positions and gains 4 nats per nat of damage elsewhere, against 11 to 15 for the cipher. So the ratio may read breadth, or the task, rather than the kind of update; no run here can tell them apart. A high reading still means only "uses pieces the original model has". The next test, approved by the user on 2026-10-29 (Amendment 1), swaps the breadth: lowercase of only 50 capitalised word types (performed but narrow) against a cipher of about 500 words (new but broad). If kind matters, the narrow lowercase reads high and the broad cipher low; if breadth matters, the reverse. A second decomposition seed and Qwen stage 1 were not taken now.

Run
Two cluster jobs on an L40, the fine-tunes 107 s and the readings 217 s, $0; up to 500 held-out positions per task; the cipher saved at steps 10, 100 and 300.
Found
  • The stand-in ratio separates lowercase (0.92, 0.93) from the cipher (0.26, 0.26), as leaned, and the cipher stays low at matched gain (provisional).
  • The relay does not separate them (0.66 against 0.41), as leaned; the size and stable rank of the update do not order like the ratio either (provisional).
  • The hand-write check did not reach its gain, so kind against task is unread, and breadth is fully confounded with kind.
