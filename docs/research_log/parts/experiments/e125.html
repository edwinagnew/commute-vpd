Can a fresh 67M pair be read "reuses C" by the definition and by the decomposition?

2026-11-06 · provisional, no independent check · four cluster debug L40 jobs (54841, 54877, 54880, 54933), $0 · experiments/fresh_reuse_pair

The question. No 67M task has yet been read "reuses C" by the frozen definition, so the decomposition's re-gating readings cannot be checked against such a label. E123 showed the case-label pair cannot supply one.

What we did. We taught the model one word when the word before a marker already occurred earlier in the passage and another when it did not, beside a twin that learns the two words from a shuffled per-word list. The model's induction machinery already tracks repeats, so the definition could read this pair on words the fine-tune never saw. We then read the same four fine-tunes by the route split and by the removal of the switched-on set.

What we found. The pair passed possession (the block-1 readout reads 0.949, its control 0.547) and carried to held-out words (0.97 and 0.96). But removing the readout direction took only 0.031 and 0.038 of the gain against the bar of 0.50, so the definition reads both arms "not through C". As on E123 the route split and the copies read masked-off on all four states and the removal "through" on all four (the twin highest), so no reading separates the real fine-tune from its memorising twin. Amendment 1 moved the removal to the repeated word's own position (blocks 0 to 2) or the trigger's block 2: 0.021 to 0.045 of the real fine-tune's gain against 0.50 (twin -0.002 to 0.009, random within 0.005).

What it means. The "wrong site" reason is ruled out for those four sites, so the "not through C" verdicts stand; whether the fine-tune barely uses the flag or reads it at many sites at once stays open. No 67M task yet reads "reuses C".
