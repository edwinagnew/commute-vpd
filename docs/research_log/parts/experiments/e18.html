Does one fixed change to Γ's importance values, the same for every input with a given cue, carry the re-gating part of a fine-tune's gain?

2026-10-21 to 10-26 · MATS cluster L40, RunPod RTX 4090 · experiments/regating_leak, regating_small, regating_tasks, regating_down, gamma_shift_map, shift_anatomy, many_cues, regating_cuefree

Since then. The user de-prioritised this hypothesis on 2026-10-27, as too artificial on toy tasks to say anything general, and the re-gating line was red-teamed and reset on 2026-10-28. Nothing here is a finding the project now builds on.

The question. On the re-gating line (Q5), a fine-tune's weight change is left whole in the residual Δ: the do-nothing placement. The subcomponents keep their original weights, and only the frozen importance function Γ reads the fine-tuned model. Γ gives each subcomponent a value from 0 (off) to 1 (fully on) at each position, and after the fine-tune it switches on subcomponents it kept off before. The switching effect is the rise in the target's log-odds at the trigger, the position just before the target, that comes from Γ's new values alone. Either the update switches on a fixed, reusable set of existing subcomponents, or Γ's values smuggle each input's own information into the forward pass, in which case re-gating describes Γ's wiring rather than the update. The fixed-shift hypothesis took the first side: one fixed change to Γ's values at the trigger carries most of the switching effect.

What we did. We fine-tuned the 67M model for ten steps on toy tasks, mostly on one seed, and replaced Γ's values at the trigger with test patterns. A pattern's share is what it gives back of the switching effect over what each input's own fine-tuned values give back: 0 is the original values, 1 the input's own. The fixed change is the mean of fine-tuned minus original values over training inputs; the average pattern gives every input the fine-tuned mean, less what the original mean gives. Tasks ran from word pairs the model already half predicts, such as which then was, to key-value tasks it lacks, where after a marker each of 32 key words is followed by itself (copy) or by a fixed random word (cipher). Later runs computed the change per key or cue word, predicted it without naming the cue, asked what the subcomponents it turns down do, and applied it at every position as a map, scored by VPD's adversary on Pile text.

What we found. On the seven tasks the model already does, one pattern for every input gives back most of the effect, and small updates that pass the faithfulness check read like the large one (first figure). On the key-value tasks one change fails, but one change per key gives back 0.84 on copy (second figure). With 32 cue words all fine-tuned toward " of", one change per cue still beats a shared one, so the change follows the cue, not the answer (third figure). A rule naming no cue, the mean change of the eight inputs whose original values are nearest, matches the per-cue change added to each input's own values, but not its averaged form (fourth figure). Applied at every position, the fixed change keeps the trigger behaviour but breaks the decomposition on ordinary text (fifth figure).

What it means. At the trigger, on one seed, one change per cue described re-gating on every toy task tried, and the change belongs to the task: moved to other subcomponents, it gives back nothing. But it is not a map, it gained no mechanism sentence (its turned-down half is not the competitors' machinery), and "cue" was defined only as the trigger token. The user judged the toy setting too artificial to generalise and turned the line toward what re-gating means. The nearest rule went on to a fine-tune with no trigger in E36, which stays its own card.

Run
Ten-step fine-tunes of the 67M model in the do-nothing placement, read at the trigger on about 70 to 190 held-out inputs per task, each run reproducing the one before within 0.002. Found
  • At the trigger, one fixed change per cue word gives back most of the switching effect on every toy task tried, word and key-value alike, at large and small update sizes; on one seed, with "cue" meaning the trigger token.
  • The change is the task's own: moved to other subcomponents it gives back nothing, and the half it turns down is not the machinery for the tokens the target must beat, though lowering it cuts their probability on some tasks.
  • Applied at every position it is not a map: the adversary finds 5 to 26 times the no-change damage on ordinary text, while the same values shuffled pass.
