If we set the case readout to the other case, does the case-label fine-tune's answer flip?

2026-11-06 · provisional · one cluster debug L40 job (54194), about a minute, $0 · experiments/case_patch

The question. E112 found that the case-label fine-tune on the 67M passes every test of the adopted reuse definition except the causal one: removing the case direction took at most a fifth of its label gain. The definition names a second causal form, patching the readout direction. Does patching give the label that removal did not?

What we did. On E112's four saved fine-tunes (real and shuffled-twin tasks, two seeds each) we moved one coordinate of the block-1 output, along the case readout, to the typical value of the opposite case, at the marker and at the word before it. First, on the original model alone, we checked that the edit reaches the next block: that block's own case readout has to report the flipped case on at least 0.70 of held-out windows. Then we counted how many of the fine-tune's labels changed sign, against a bar of one half, with random directions of the same step size as the do-nothing reference.

What we found. The edit works on the original model but the fine-tune ignores it. Block 2's case readout reports the flipped case on 0.714 of windows (0.115 unpatched, at most 0.134 under random edits), yet the real fine-tune's label flipped on only 0.002 and 0.014 of windows for the two seeds, against the bar of 0.50. The twin flipped on 0.002 and 0.008, as expected, and random edits flipped at most 0.004.

What it means. The adopted definition reads "not through the capability" for the third time on this pair, so the pair gives no outside "reuse" label. A fine-tune that carries perfectly to unseen words as a cut on a pretrained readout need not run through that readout; we do not know why (candidates: case also carried in other directions, later blocks restoring it, or a nonlinear read of the word), and none is tested. The case-label line is paused until you decide (your ask 6). Provisional: two seeds, one block, one direction per position, no independent check.
