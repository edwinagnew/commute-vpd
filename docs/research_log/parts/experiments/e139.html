Do ordinary fine-tunes that imitate the four hand-made changes read as the changes' labels?

2026-11-10 · provisional, no independent check · cluster debug L40, control job 57251, reruns jobs 57673, 57675, 57744, 57745 and 57821, amendment 1 job 57830, amendment 2 jobs 57831 to 57833, $0 · experiments/reuse_variable_finetune

Since then. On 2026-11-10 you adopted the second wording tentatively and rounded the cut-offs to 0.8 and 0.5. Re-labelled from saved results, the 300-step Always on copies now read no map found (one seed's rewiring share is 0.48), not rewired; the Table copies keep their label.

The question. The blind search of the second reuse wording recovered the labels of four hand-made changes (E137). A real fine-tune does not add one small piece; it moves every weight. If an ordinary fine-tune is trained to imitate a hand-made change, does the search read it with the change's label?

What we did. For each hand-made change, three seeds of an ordinary fine-tune of all weights of the 67M, 300 steps, trained to match the changed model's output. Each was read with the first test's two shares. The re-weighting share is how much of the change an affine map of the variable itself explains. The rewiring share is the same for an affine map of everything the model computed at that point. The label rule was filed first, with cut-offs halfway between the first test's readings. Re-weighted needs a re-weighting share of 0.794 or more. Rewired needs only a rewiring share of 0.476 or more; below that, no map is found. A change reads a label when 2 of its 3 seeds do. Two follow-ups looked at the Always on imitators, whose re-weighting share came out low. One read the hand-made change's own variable against the fine-tunes. The other retrained them for 3000 steps, to copy the change ten times more closely. Labels are provisional: no wording is adopted.

What we found. The fine-tunes that imitate the Table change read rewired on all 3 seeds, the change's own label; their rewiring share is 0.714 to 0.724 and their re-weighting share near zero. The fine-tunes that imitate Always on read rewired, not re-weighted: at 300 steps their re-weighting share is 0.627 to 0.638, and after 3000 steps it rose to 0.737 to 0.781, still under 0.794. The Always on shortfall tracks how closely the fine-tune copies the change's output, because the re-weighting share reads output only. The XOR was not learned and Cue was stopped by a wrong check, so neither has a reading.

What it means. On ordinary fine-tunes the second wording returned its change's label for Table only. For Always on the low re-weighting share comes from the imitation leftover, not from the fine-tune spreading the change over many weights: the share cannot rise above how closely the fine-tune copies the change, and the cut-off sits near the ceiling that leftover allows. Table's imitators copy the change about as closely (0.842 of the way) yet read near zero, so the leftover does not set their label. So on fine-tunes the share reads closeness as much as what happened inside the model, and only the reversion looks inside. The cell's runs are spent: no seed got close enough to say whether a closer Always on copy would cross, and Cue and XOR stay unread.

Run
Debug jobs on the L40, $0: the control alone first, which passed, then the reruns under the replacement early check, the last of them the three Table seeds. Then one read of the change's own variable against the Always on fine-tunes with no new search, and 3000-step retraining of the three Always on fine-tunes at a third of the first peak rate, read at 300, 1000 and 3000 steps.
Found
  • Table imitators read rewired on 3 of 3 seeds and Always on imitators read rewired, not re-weighted, at 300 and at 3000 steps (best re-weighting share 0.737 to 0.781, under the 0.794 cut-off), so only Table matched its change's label.
  • The change's own variable, which explains the hand-made change with 0.922, explained the fine-tunes no better than the search did (0.631 to 0.632): the low share is the imitation leftover, not a missing map. Both filed predictions for the 3000-step retraining are no call, because no seed got within 0.1 of the change's size (all ended at 0.192).
  • The share of the change undone by setting only the four found coordinates back (the reversion) stayed 0.243 to 0.260 on every Always on state, far below the hand-made change's 0.938. The XOR was not learned and every Cue seed was stopped at step 10 by a check of the lead's that compared two different directions of the divergence, so neither has a reading.
