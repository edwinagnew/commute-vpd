Do ordinary fine-tunes that imitate the four hand-made changes read as the changes' labels?

2026-11-10 · provisional, no independent check, results so far · cluster debug L40, control job 57251, rerun jobs 57673, 57675, 57744 and 57745, $0 · experiments/reuse_variable_finetune

The question. The blind search of the second reuse wording recovered the labels of four hand-made changes (E137). A real fine-tune does not add one small piece; it moves every weight. If an ordinary fine-tune is trained to imitate a hand-made change, does the search read it with the change's label?

What we did. For each hand-made change, three seeds of an ordinary fine-tune of all weights of the 67M, 300 steps, trained to match the changed model's output. Each was read with the first test's two shares. The re-weighting share is how much of the change an affine map of the variable itself explains. The rewiring share is the same for an affine map of everything the model computed at that point. The label rule was filed first, with cut-offs halfway between the first test's readings. Re-weighted needs a re-weighting share of 0.794 or more. Rewired needs only a rewiring share of 0.476 or more; below that, no map is found. A change reads a label when 2 of its 3 seeds do. Labels are provisional: no wording is adopted.

What we found. The fine-tunes that imitate the re-weighted change, Always on, read rewired. Their best re-weighting share is 0.627 to 0.638, well under 0.794. Their best rewiring share is just over its cut-off of 0.476 on two seeds, at 0.505 and 0.483. The third reads 0.450, just under, so 2 of 3 seeds read rewired. The XOR was not learned on any seed, so it has no reading. Every Cue seed was stopped by a check of the lead's that was wrong, and the Table seeds never ran.

What it means. The first real-fine-tune reading of the second wording does not match the label of the change imitated. It fits the alternative filed beside the prediction: a fine-tune spreads the change over many weights, so no four-direction map of the variable carries most of it. One change and a seed split this close are not yet general; whether the Table imitators read as their label waits on the decision in section {{n:decisions}}.
