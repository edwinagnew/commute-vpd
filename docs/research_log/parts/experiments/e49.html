On new Pile rows, does the adversarial check fail the SGD teacher-trained decompositions, as it fails Adam's?

2026-10-29 · MATS cluster L40, debug QoS · experiments/row_blocks

The question. Line 2 (Q1). On a broad fine-tune that lowercases text everywhere, teacher training's copy fails VPD's faithfulness check with Adam and passes with a rank-8 LoRA (E39). Plain SGD's copies failed on Pile rows 0 to 255 and passed on rows 256 to 511, with the failure on one block of 128 rows read at one adversary start. Either the start or the rows could decide that block, and four blocks were too few to place SGD.

What we did. We read the check on four new blocks of 128 rows, at three adversary starts each, for the teacher-trained decompositions of four lowercase fine-tunes (Adam, plain SGD, SGD held to rank 8, a rank-8 LoRA). The check is the adversarial ratio: the damage a 20-step mask adversary does at a state, over what it does at the unchanged decomposition, in the same job. 1.40 or less passes and 1.61 or more fails. We also read the do-nothing state of each fine-tune, which leaves its whole update in Δ and is the reference the check must fail, and the hard block (rows 128 to 255) at two more starts.

What we found. Adam's copy fails all four new blocks (1.66 to 2.06, one near the line) and the LoRA's passes all four (1.13 to 1.26), as filed. Both SGD copies pass all four (1.05 to 1.39), but plain SGD's do-nothing also passes all four (1.40 to 1.60), so the check could not fail SGD's update on these rows. On the hard block plain SGD passes at the two new starts (1.11, 1.24) and SGD held to rank 8 reads 1.00 and 1.60, so the rule reads "mixed".

What it means. Teacher training places a broad LoRA update and not a broad Adam one, on eight blocks of Pile rows. SGD stays open for lack of power, not as a pass. The tree drops the labelled-SGD and SGD-trained-LoRA runs, so a check that can fail SGD's update, such as the lowercase task's own text, is needed before the line can close.

Run
Three fine-tunes regenerated, then eight cluster jobs on an L40 (debug QoS, $0, about 16 minutes each); the teacher-trained decompositions are E39's saved files, loaded as they were. Controls passed first in every job.
Found
  • As filed, Adam's copy fails all four new blocks and the LoRA's passes all four; the Adam block at 1.66 sits near the line.
  • Both SGD copies pass the four blocks, but the plain SGD do-nothing fails none (it was expected to fail one or two), so the SGD passes have almost no power.
  • Plain SGD's failure on the hard block did not repeat at two more starts; rank-8 SGD's reading of 1.60 makes the rule "mixed", with a lean to the start, not a finding.
