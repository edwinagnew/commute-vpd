E34held

Can teacher training write a finished narrow fine-tune into the decomposition's subcomponents, keeping the behaviour and passing the faithfulness check, without Δ absorbing the update?

2026-10-23 to 11-06 · RunPod H100 and MATS cluster L40 · experiments/teacher_training, claim_tests, pass_reread, teacher_route_split, copy_gap_ladder, copy_reproducibility

The question. The commute question asks whether a finished fine-tune of the 67M model can be carried onto its decomposition: written in the existing subcomponents, with the decomposition still faithful and Δ, the residual weights the subcomponents do not cover, not absorbing the update (Δ may change a little). The first maps divided the fine-tune's weight change among the subcomponents in closed form, the adjoint and least-squares splits. They either lost the behaviour or failed VPD's faithfulness check (E16). This card covers fine-tunes that act at a trigger or a narrow cue; fine-tunes that change text everywhere are E37.

What we did. Teacher training trains the subcomponents' read and write vectors, with the importance function Γ and Δ frozen, until the decomposition predicts what the fine-tuned model predicts. It needs no labels and runs on the task's own text or on plain Pile text. We copied SGD and Adam fine-tunes of three trigger tasks (` which` then ` was`, ` it` then ` is`, an emoticon followed by o) and of a word cipher the model lacked, finished, stopped early, and chained along training. Faithfulness is the adversarial ratio: an adversary searches over masks of the subcomponents and Δ for the largest damage on Pile text, and the ratio divides that by the damage at the unchanged decomposition. The pass and fail lines sit either side of 1.5 by the unchanged decomposition's spread across adversary starts, usually 1.40 and 1.61. Do-nothing, the whole update left in Δ, is the reference. We then stress-tested the result: more adversary starts and batch orders, a longer adversary, plain SGD as the copy's optimiser, the remainder added to Δ, the masked model at the cue, and the copy's weight change against reruns of the fine-tune.

What we found. Every copy keeps the behaviour at 0.90 or more, where the adjoint split keeps as little as 0.05 (first figure), and every copy passes (second figure). It passes from all four adversary starts where do-nothing fails at its worst (third figure), at a second batch order, under an eightfold longer adversary, on fine-tunes stopped at step 10 (at the line), and when reapplied every 100 steps along training. It also copies the cipher and passes, but the masked model, the subcomponents scaled by Γ with Δ left out, loses both random ciphers (fourth figure). The copy's weight change is not the fine-tune's: in every family it sits further from it than a rerun of the fine-tune does (fifth figure), mostly because only the read and write vectors train.

What it means. Teacher training is the commute map for narrow updates, and on the user's choice of 2026-11-02 the weak version is settled on the 67M: it reproduces the behaviour by a change further from the fine-tune's than a rerun is. So the update can be written in the subcomponents; that does not show the fine-tune itself used them, and claims stand for the target's probability, not the function. A pass means "no harm seen": no run with the base model as teacher fails, and the adversary's random start can move a reading, so every pass is a lower bound. The masked model at the cue cannot judge a map either, since the unchanged decomposition is already past the line there (reports/analyses/2026-10-29_analysis_e48-pass-reread.md). Exact weights, by adding the remainder to Δ, cost faithfulness. The strong version, a gated re-decomposition of one fine-tuned model, is not run.

Found
  • On every narrow fine-tune tried, including fine-tunes stopped at step 10, chains along training and a cipher the model lacked, the copy keeps the behaviour and passes the check with Δ untouched, from every adversary start read; plain SGD as the copy's optimiser fails only at its largest rate run longest.
  • The copy reaches the behaviour by a different weight change and logit change from the fine-tune's, further than a rerun, and its masked model loses a behaviour the model lacked; adding the uncopied remainder to Δ for exact weights costs faithfulness (about twice the copy's reading).
  • Most copies carry their gain neither by changing which subcomponents Γ switches on nor in switched-off ones; three Pile-text copies do re-switch, at each of three batch orders, and the one copy that would not reproduce (emoticon, Adam, Pile text) swings by 0.135 with batch order.
