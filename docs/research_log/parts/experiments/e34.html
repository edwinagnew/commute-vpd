Can the subcomponents copy a finished fine-tune from the fine-tuned model alone?

2026-10-23 · RunPod H100 · experiments/teacher_training

Yes, on three tasks. Teacher training, component training whose loss is the fine-tuned model's predictions instead of the task's labels, kept all six fine-tunes' behaviour and passed the adversarial check, including where the adjoint split loses the behaviour (first figure). Surprise: copies trained on ordinary Pile text alone learnt the emoticon behaviour, which we filed they would not; they still learn it with every trigger removed from the text, by a weight change that is not the fine-tune's (third figure).

Run
Six teachers, E31's fine-tunes at step 1000: plain SGD and Adam 3e-5 on r1, r0 and emoticon. The alive subcomponents' read and write vectors trained for 1000 steps to minimise the KL from teacher to copy, Γ and Δ frozen, in three arms: the task's windows with Adam, Pile rows only with Adam, the task's windows with plain SGD. Scored at steps 100 and 1000 beside each teacher's do-nothing and adjoint split. Controls first: the unchanged model, a teacher equal to it (nothing moved), and a reachable teacher (gap closed to 0.5%). 67 min, $6.13. Amendment 1: the Pile arm on emoticon again with the trigger masked from the loss or its rows replaced, plus the cosine with δW; 20 min, $3.26.
Found
  • Task windows: all six copies pass at both steps, target probability 0.92 to 1.00.
  • Pile only: all six pass at step 1000, 0.90 to 0.98. Filed: emoticon below 0.5; read 0.96 and 0.90, reversed.
  • Plain SGD keeps the behaviour but fails late on r0 and r1 (ratio 3.4 and 3.1), as in component training.
  • Amendment 1: about 90 trigger positions in the Pile rows are not the cause; the trigger-free copies reach 0.94 and 0.89. Their weight change's cosine with δW, 0.08 and 0.22, is below the adjoint split's.
  • Amendment 1 against its filed predictions: learnt for the SGD teacher, just under the 0.9 line for the Adam one; masking and dropping disagree for the Adam teacher; the cosine reached the filed bar for the Adam teacher only.
