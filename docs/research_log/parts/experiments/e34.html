Can the subcomponents copy a finished fine-tune from the fine-tuned model alone?

2026-10-23 · RunPod H100 · experiments/teacher_training

Yes, on three tasks. Teacher training, component training whose loss is the fine-tuned model's predictions instead of the task's labels, kept all six fine-tunes' behaviour and passed the adversarial check, including where the adjoint split loses the behaviour (first figure). Surprise: copies trained on ordinary Pile text alone learnt the emoticon behaviour, which we filed they would not; they still learn it with every trigger removed from the text, by a weight change that is not the fine-tune's (third figure). Since Amendment 5 the passes are read as lower bounds: the adversary's verdict on a fine-tune can depend on its random start (sixth figure). Amendment 6 adds that plain SGD at one rate fails where Adam passes (a rate grid is running as E45 T5) and that the map has not yet placed a rare target (last figure).

Found
  • Copies of six trigger fine-tunes on three tasks keep the behaviour and pass the adversarial check, trained on the task's text or on ordinary Pile text alone, and they hold at a second batch order and under an adversary eight times longer; on ordinary Pile text the five copies Amendment 6 scored change the model 0.4 to 1.7 times as much as the fine-tune they copy (three fine-tunes of one task).
