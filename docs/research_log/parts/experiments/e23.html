Do the SGD failures break the decomposition the way the Adam ones did?

2026-10-22 · MATS cluster L40 · experiments/cancellation_sgd

Yes. On all five component-training states that still fail after the switch to SGD, the adversary's extra damage appears only when its mask removes the alive and the dead subcomponents together, as on the early Adam failures (first figure). The changes to the subcomponents and Δ carry almost none of it. An eight times longer search leaves the step-1,000 contrast in place: SGD about two and a half times the original model, Adam at its level (second figure).

Run
E21's saved states rebuilt on the cluster: five failures (SGD at step 1,000 on " it" then " is" and " which" then " was"; SGD on the 32-random-pairs and 32-copies cipher tasks; SGD at rate 0.1 at step 100 on " in" then " Fig"), two passing Adam twins at step 1,000, and a passing SGD control at step 100. Each under its own 20-step adversary on 128 Pile rows, its excess split exactly into E20's parts, plus E17's coherence and half-revert readings. Then E20 Amendment 3's budget sweep, 20 to 160 steps, on the original model and the four step-1,000 states. Every check passed. Jobs 29344, 29496 to 29498, $0.
Found
  • The control shows no excess (−0.02 nats, band 0.14). The Adam twins read within 0.02 of zero.
  • E20's account holds on 5 of 5 failures: the dead-and-alive combination term reads 0.92 of an excess of 0.81 nats up to 2.05 of 1.77; the dead block alone is slightly negative everywhere.
  • E17's readings hold on 4 of 5: the changes add up rather than cancel (coherence 2.5 to 3.2, random-sign null about 1) and carry at most 2% of the excess. The miss is a hair: a half revert of 0.504 against a 0.5 line.
  • Coherence and half revert read the same on passing states, so they do not separate pass from fail.
  • Minor surprise: on the cipher tasks the alive originals carry a sixth (32 random pairs) and a third (32 copies) of the excess, against under a twentieth on the ladder tasks.
  • Budget: SGD 2.23 to 2.56, Adam 0.98 to 1.03 at every budget. Divided by the original model's second seed, SGD still reads 2.1 at 160 steps.
