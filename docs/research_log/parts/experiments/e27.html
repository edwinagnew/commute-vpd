Is the LoRA's "outside like noise" reading the LoRA's or Adam's?

2026-10-22 · RunPod H100 · experiments/lora_sgd

Mostly the LoRA's. Trained with plain SGD instead of Adam, the same rank-8 LoRA still sits far outside the subcomponents: between noise and a full fine-tune, nearer noise, and about ten times further outside than an SGD full fine-tune (first figure). Its read factor stays where it was drawn (second figure), which fits the reading that the outside share comes from that random read side. Adam pushes the LoRA up to twice as far outside at some steps. Every placement of the SGD LoRA stays as faithful as the original model (third figure).

Run
E9's rank-8 LoRA on all 24 decomposed matrices, emoticon, seed 0, 1,000 steps, trained with plain SGD. The rate came from E4's rule over 0.003 to 3: the target's held-out probability at 0.9 or more with the least damage elsewhere, which picked 0.3 (probability 0.9998, KL elsewhere 0.0029 nats). The outside-both ratio: the share of the update's effect on real inputs that lies outside both the subcomponents' write span and what their reads predict, divided by the same share for a random update of the same size, at nine checkpoints. Also the read factor's |cos| with its start, and do-nothing, least-squares and adjoint placements at steps 10, 100 and 1,000 through the adversarial Pile check (pass at 1.40 or below). Controls first: the original model, and the Adam LoRA regenerated to reproduce E9 and E14; both passed. About 1 h of H100 time, roughly $3 to $4 (not recorded).
Found
  • Outside-both ratio of the SGD LoRA: 0.54, 0.66, 0.87, 0.85 and 0.33 at steps 10, 30, 100, 300 and 1,000. The Adam LoRA in the same job reads 0.98 at step 10 and 1.01 at 1,000; the SGD full fine-tune 0.03 to 0.06. The filed "at least 0.7 at every step" missed at three of five checkpoints.
  • The drop to 0.33 at step 1,000 is unassigned: the per-matrix rows were computed but not saved.
  • The read factor stays at its start under SGD (|cos| 0.997 at step 1,000; filed bar 0.97, passed).
  • Every do-nothing and least-squares placement passes (filed, passed); so does every adjoint placement.
  • The least-squares split leaves 0.37 of the update in Δ at step 1,000, against the Adam LoRA's 0.54.
  • The regenerated Adam LoRA matched E9 at step 10 but drifted by step 1,000 (KL elsewhere 0.016 against 0.005), though it passed its filed checks.
