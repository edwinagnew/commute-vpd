## The request, in the Terms' words

After a narrow 10-step fine-tune, Γ switches on a recruited set S of the original model's subcomponents at the
trigger. Amendment 3 put S's trigger outputs in the fine-tuned model back to their original-model values and took back
about a fifth of the gain on emoticon and ` which`, more than random, null and already-active sets. Option (a) asks
whether that is because Γ switched S on, or only because S's outputs changed most. So: restore, outside S, the
subcomponents whose trigger outputs changed most, matched to S per layer; and, on emoticon, restore the layer-3 pool of
already-active subcomponents alone, to see where Amendment 3's negative share of about −0.2 sits.

## /design check

1. **Goal.** Q5 (re-gating: what the switched-on set did). Decides whether Γ's set names subcomponents that carry the
   fine-tune's change, or merely those that moved most, which any input-change ranking would find without Γ.
2. **Nearest tried.** Amendment 3 (`TRIED.md`, row "E38 Amendments 2 and 3"): matched on count and activity, not on
   change; its check named change-matching as the one open alternative. Amendment 2's top-k sets ranked by importance
   rise, which is Γ's own ranking.
3. **Patch or step.** A patch closing the last alternative to Amendment 3's reading. If S survives, the next step is
   upstream (what moved the trigger's inputs); if not, the line stops on this model.
4. **Decisions.** None departed from. Same states, windows, readout and restore as Amendment 3; no fine-tuning.
5. **What changes what we do.** S above both change-matched sets on emoticon and ` which`: "specific" goes into Q5
   and a card proposes the upstream step. A change-matched set at or above S on both: S is "the pieces that moved
   most"; a card proposes stopping the 67M re-gating line.
6. **Control first.** Amendment 3's controls R0–R4 rerun first on all states, plus R5 (the change measure reads 0
   with the fine-tuned weights set to W0); any fail stops the job before any share is computed.
7. **Cost.** One MATS cluster `debug` job on an L40, about 2 minutes of GPU, $0, on Amendment 2's cached states.

## Definitions

- **Output change of a subcomponent** c of matrix k (rank-1 u_c v_c^T): on each **training** window (the windows S was
  chosen on), at the trigger, |(x1 − x0)·v_c| · ‖u_c‖, with x1 the matrix's input in the fine-tuned model's pass and x0
  in the original model's; averaged over the windows: Δ_c. It is the size of the correction that restoring c adds,
  measured on the windows S was chosen on, so the sets are chosen without the held-out windows that score them.
- **Restore X, restored share ρ_r(X)**: as in Amendment 3, on the held-out windows.
- **Sets**, per task-seed state, from the alive subcomponents not in S, layer by layer:
  - **C_n, count-matched**: in each layer, the |S in that layer| pieces with the largest Δ_c (ties by matrix name,
    then index).
  - **C_m, change-matched**: in each layer, pieces in descending Δ_c until their summed Δ_c reaches S's summed Δ_c in
    that layer; the whole layer if it never does (recorded as short). Empty in a layer where S has no members.
  - **All alive**: every alive subcomponent, S included (the share carried by the subcomponents' changed outputs at
    all; the rest is in dW's own output and the residual).
- **Emoticon, both seeds:** **L3** = the layer-3 active pool at importance ≥ 0.2 (Amendment 3's, about 107 pieces, not
  in S); **rest** = Amendment 3's active draw 0 without its layer-3 members; **singles** = each L3 member restored
  alone; **random singles** = as many single layer-3 pieces, alive, not in S, with W0 trigger importance < 0.2, seed 0.

## Order (control first, one job)

Controls on every state, stop on any fail: Amendment 3's R0–R4 as filed (R4 at the amended bar), and R5: Δ_c computed
with W1 := W0 is 0 for every alive piece (max ≤ 1e-6). Then per task-seed state: Δ, C_n, C_m, their restores, all
alive; then the emoticon L3 arms. Results to `results/amend4.json`, rewritten after each state; every scored number
also printed to the log.

## Decision tree (P11)

Scored only on tasks where ρ_r(S) ≥ 0.10 on both seeds (by Amendment 3, emoticon and ` which`; ` we`'s S takes back
too little for the question to arise).
- **"specific"** (both scored tasks): S takes back more than the subcomponents whose outputs moved as much. Write into
  the E38 card and Q5 as a checked claim (after the independent check); new card for the upstream step.
- **"not specific"** (both 'not'): S's share is what any set of top movers carries; Γ's switch-on adds nothing to an
  input-change ranking. Write into Q5; card proposing to stop the re-gating line on the 67M model.
- **Outside the tree** (mixed, unclear, or fewer than two scored tasks): card with the numbers; no further runs.

## What each number is blind to

- Δ_c is a size, not a direction: a large change orthogonal to the target's readout counts as much as an aligned one.
  That is the alternative being tested ("moved most"), so it is intended; direction is not matched.
- C_m may hold many more pieces than S, which favours it; C_n may carry less change than S, which favours S. S must
  beat both.
- Restore acts at the trigger only, and shares of different sets need not add (Amendment 3).
- Singles are noisy alone; their floor is the random singles, measured in the same run.

## Noise floors for the bars

- The 0.05 margin in P11: Amendment 3's random sets never exceeded 0.029 on any state, and S's two seeds differ by at
  most 0.023 (`amend3.json`).
- The 0.10 floor for S: as in Amendment 3; S reads 0.18 or more on the scored tasks, ` we` 0.05 or less.
- The singles' bars (−0.05 for "few", −0.02 for "spread"): set against the random singles of the same run (a pool
  single must also sit 0.02 below the most negative random single).

## Not run

Direction-matched sets; positions other than the trigger; restoring S with C together; other tasks, seeds, the larger
model.
