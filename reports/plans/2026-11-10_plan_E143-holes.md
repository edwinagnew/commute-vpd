# E143 plan: steering and the neuron version on every saved pair still missing them, beside the share (cell P2.10)

*compare-lead's code writer, 2026-11-10. Card `2026-11-10_plan_p2-10-card.md` (passes at 3.57 standard errors, n = 2 seeds per
side, smallest gap 0.1 of the gain, spread 0.028 from E135). Predictions `experiments/holes_fill/results/predictions.json`. One
debug L40 job, free, saved updates only. Sides are named as each pair was built; no verdict on reuse.*

## What runs

**The holes.** The card's table, built from the files: on seven pairs with saved updates on both sides the neuron version was
never read, and on six of them steering exists only in its older form (one pooled vector fitted on half the read rows at one
strength). Copy vs lookup has fair steering from E135; both label pairs have everything from E140. The four 96-key pairs and
the step ladder have no saved updates on both sides and stay empty. This run reads 56 saved updates: copy vs random maps 4/4,
lowercase vs ciphers 2/4, half-known vs cipher at equal size 5/5 and at its own step 5/2, rank-1 vs cipher and copy vs cipher at
step 10 8/8 each (the cipher side shared), copy vs lookup 3/3 (neurons only). Every file was listed with `ls` on 2026-11-10.

**The rows** are the ones E136 read the share on, through E136's own loader (`experiments/share_untrained/windows.py`), so the
share column is E136's `trained` value for each update, unchanged. The unit is the log-odds of each row's target at the read
position over the whole vocabulary; each method's value is its mean change over the update's own mean gain on those rows.

**The neuron version** keeps, in W0, each block's MLP firing on its TopK neurons by size, the sets from the update's forward
or W0's, at 64 per block and at the decomposition's on-count (15, 3, 6 and 30 per block, E136's count on all pairs' read rows,
fixed before any update is read). **Steering** is `fair_steering` as E135 and E140 ran it: a pooled vector and a per-key
vector from every training window, (layer, strength) chosen on up to 512 training windows, eight norm-matched random vectors,
cross-application between same-numbered seeds of the two sides, cosines, off-target KL. The key is the token at the read
position. On every kv pair the target is a fixed function of that key, so the per-key vector is handed the answer on both
sides; on lowercase vs ciphers no token in the window fixes the answer, 141 to 186 of the 500 read rows per task have a token there that no training window has
(dry run), and only the pooled vector is read. Every training window of each key is used; the dry run checked that every key
has training windows (9 to 15 per key) and that every read row's key is in the pool.

## Stages, control first (`STAGE n DONE` after each file; the log prints every number)

0. **Controls, alone; a miss stops the job.** A zero update reads exactly 0 (1e-9) through both neuron readings and through
   steering at every layer, strength and kind, and strength 0 on a real update changes no logit. The share of two saved
   updates, in two row formats, reproduces E136's 0.80244 and 1.09456 within 0.01, a tenth of the smallest gap (E136
   reproduced its sources exactly). E140's neuron reading of the case-label real seed 0 at k 64, 0.0091816, reproduces through
   this job's generalised path within 1e-4: a path reading 0 would miss.
1. **Every update**, steered updates first; the projected job time is logged after each and the job stops past 25 min.
   Then cross-application and cosines per pair, then the filed score.

## Expected values

| quantity | zero update or W0 | control | rival A | rival B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| any numerator | exactly 0 | strength 0 reads 0 | | |
| share, same rows | undefined | 0.80244, 1.09456 | sides apart on all 7 | sides apart on all 7 |
| neuron share | 0 | 0.0091816 at k 64 | apart as the share on every pair | below 0.05 (inert) or apart on fewer |
| steering share | 0 | random vectors near 0 | apart as the share on every pair | apart on fewer, likely not the size-matched pairs |

Rule (`rules.py`): a pair separates when the side gap is at least 0.1 and above the larger within-side range; inert when every
value is under 0.05; a family (steering: wider of the two kinds; neurons: wider of the two k) reads rival A when it separates,
with the share's sign, at least as many pairs as the share does on the same pairs. Filed leanings: P1 steering rival B; P2 the
neuron version inert or rival B; P3 per-key above pooled on most kv updates. Edge cases (negative gap, ties, zero gain, a missing
seed, the do-nothing state) were run through the rule in the dry run.

**Blind spots.** Steering cannot say whether a vector writes the answer the fine-tune's way, and a per-key vector on a lookup
table is told the answer. The neuron version misses change outside the TopK sets and in attention. The gap is blind to both
sides moving together. Copy vs lookup's steering is on E135's 100 score rows, not all 200.

**Cost.** `sbatch --comment=E143 cluster/holes_fill.sbatch`, debug QoS, 24 GB, 30-minute limit, about 15 minutes expected on an
L40, free. Figure: `plot.py`, one heatmap of gaps, pairs by readings, the share in the first column and E135's and E140's
readings drawn from their files.
