## The request, in the Terms' words

After a narrow 10-step fine-tune, Γ switches on a set S of the original model's subcomponents at the trigger. Putting
S's trigger outputs back to their original-model values (restore) takes back about a fifth of the gain on emoticon and
` which`; the same number of the subcomponents whose outputs moved most take back about none (Amendment 4). The check's
strongest surviving alternative: S stands out because its output changes point toward the target, and a ranking that
looks at that direction, without Γ, would find a set that takes back as much. So: rank the alive subcomponents outside
S by each one's first-order effect on the target's log-odds at the trigger, take the top ones matched to S's count in
each matrix, restore them on the held-out windows, and compare with S. Also save which subcomponents each set holds,
per matrix.

## Definitions

- **lo, G, restore, ρ(X)**: as Amendment 3. lo is the mean over windows of the target's log-odds at the trigger;
  ρ(X) = (T1 − lo(W1 with X's trigger outputs restored to W0's)) / G on the held-out windows.
- **First-order take-back e_c** of alive subcomponent c of matrix k (rank-1 u_c v_c^T): restoring c alone adds
  δ_c = ((x0 − x1)·v_c) u_c to k's trigger output, with x1, x0 k's trigger inputs in W1's and W0's passes. With g the
  gradient of the window's lo with respect to k's trigger output in W1's pass (total derivative, plain W1 forward, no
  hooks), e_c = −g·δ_c = ((x1 − x0)·v_c)(g·u_c), averaged over windows, in nats. Positive: restoring c lowers lo, so
  takes back gain. Computed on the **training** windows to choose sets (as Amendment 4's Δ_c) and on the held-out
  windows for the reported linearity check only.
- **Sets**, per task-seed state:
  - **D (direction-matched)**: per matrix k, the |S ∩ k| alive subcomponents not in S with the largest training e_c
    (ties: index). Short if the pool is smaller (recorded; the seed is then unscored).
  - **C_k (size-matched per matrix, reported)**: per matrix, the |S ∩ k| alive non-S subcomponents with the largest
    Δ_c (Amendment 4's measure), so size against direction at the same matching.
  - Membership saved per matrix for S, D, C_k and Amendment 4's C_n and C_m (recomputed from Δ_c, no restore).
- **Top-direction share of S** (reported): per matrix, S's members among the |S ∩ k| alive subcomponents (S included)
  with the largest training e_c, summed over matrices, over |S|.
- **Predicted share ê(X)** (reported): Σ_{c∈X} e_c on the held-out windows / G.

## Order (control first, one job)

Controls on every state, stop on any fail: Amendment 3's R0-R4 as filed (R4 at the amended bar), then R6 on each of
the six task-seed states: the 10 alive subcomponents with largest |training e_c|, each restored alone on the training

## Decision tree (P13)

Seed rule (scorable seed, D not short): "S ahead" if ρ(S) ≥ ρ(D) + 0.05; "not ahead" if ρ(D) ≥ ρ(S) (ties read "not
ahead"); else "unclear". A task is scored only if ρ(S) ≥ 0.10 on both seeds (else "S too small"; by Amendment 4,
emoticon and ` which` are scored and ` we` is not); it takes its seeds' common class, else "seeds disagree".
- **"Γ adds"** (≥ 2 scored tasks "S ahead", none "not ahead"): S takes back more than the subcomponents a first-order
  ranking toward the target picks at the same count per matrix. After the independent check, a checked claim in the
  E38 card and Q5; card offering the upstream step (what switched S on) or carrying the question to Qwen.
- **"direction suffices"** (≥ 2 "not ahead", none "S ahead"): S's share is no more than what target-pointing
  subcomponents carry. Checked claim, worded: Γ finds part of it without the target, a size ranking does not; card
  proposing to close the 67M restore line.
- **Outside the tree** (mixed, unclear, fewer than two scored tasks): card with the numbers; no further runs.

Emoticon's Amendment 4 margin was inflated by layer-3 subcomponents whose restore pushes the other way; D ranks by
signed effect, so it excludes them, and its share is reported against zero as well as against S.

## What each number is blind to

- e_c is first order in one subcomponent's correction: interactions among many restored subcomponents are not in it
  (shares need not add), which ê against ρ reports. D is the best set to first order, not the best set.
- D uses the target and the training windows; S uses neither. *(Correction, 2026-10-28, after the run: S is also
  chosen on the training windows, `regating.shift_of`; the one difference is that D reads the target and S does not.)*
  D scoring at or above S says direction suffices, not
  that Γ is useless.
- Restore acts at the trigger only, as before.

## Noise floors for the bars

- P13's 0.05 margin: Amendment 3's random sets never exceeded 0.029; S's two seeds differ by at most 0.023.
- R6's 0.02 nats: Amendment 4's random singles read about 1e-3 of a gain of 10-14 nats, about 0.01 nats.

## Edge cases checked before filing

An empty D in a matrix where S has none restores 0 there (R0). A D short in any matrix makes that seed "unscored: pool
too small", counted toward no branch. G < 1 nat: every share nan, caught first. |S| = 0: ρ(S) = 0 < 0.10, "S too
small". Fewer than 8 of the top 10 reaching 0.02 nats fails R6 (the ranking has nothing measurable to rank by). Ties in
e_c broken by index; ties in ρ read "not ahead".

## Not run

Second-order or joint rankings; a direction ranking matched per layer; restoring S with D together; C_m without the
layer-3 pool; positions other than the trigger; other tasks, seeds, the larger model.
