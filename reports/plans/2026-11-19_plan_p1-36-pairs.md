# E221: the reuse score R on the trained matched pairs, and R against every old reading (cell P1.36)

*Definition-lead, 2026-11-19. Card `reports/plans/2026-11-19_plan_p1-36-card.md` (descriptive: the user's 2026-11-19
task file makes this the §5 label column). Code `experiments/kstar_pairs/` (`run.py`, `score.py`); filed settings,
thresholds and rules `experiments/kstar_pairs/results/predictions.json`. Details, the input list, the old-reading
table and the edge cases: appendix `2026-11-19_plan_p1-36-pairs_appendix.md`.*

We read the reuse score R, with its circuit share f and remainder share, on every trained fine-tune of E196's five
pairs (seeds 0 and 1) and E187's nine pairs, at the three coarse levels. Then we rank each pair's R gap (reuse minus
new) against each earlier reading's gap and against the update-size gap. The run is a description: no verdict on the
definition is given.

## Design check

- **Goal.** `GOALS.md` asks whether reuse can be told from new; paper part 1 needs a label per trained pair under the
  working definition (decisions.md, 2026-11-18). This fills the cell's label column (T3) and tests whether any earlier
  reading agrees with it (T4).
- **Nearest tried.** E214 (P1.35) read R on built items and passed at no level: the hand-built reuse edits could not
  be built, so every filed rule was "not read". The cell's "done when" then reads **every coarse level** (heads and MLP
  layers, MLP and attention layers, blocks), so this run does; no neuron level. E196 and E208 read the same states at
  neurons only, where every fine-tune read 0.996 or more and gaps followed update size.
- **Patch or step.** A step: R has never been read on a trained item at a coarse level.
- **Decisions.** Reads the 2026-11-18 definition unchanged, with E214's settings (α 0.9, mean ablation, cos² grade,
  read-position grade main). It adds no rule, clause or band to the definition; the rank rule below is a comparison
  between readings.
- **What would change what we do.** If R's gaps rank with the update-size gaps at every level (P1) and with no
  reading that size does not also order, R at a coarse level is a size reading and §5 cannot use it as the label
  column as it stands; that goes to the user as a definition question. If R tracks the held-model reading beyond
  size, §5 has its label column.
- **Control first.** Stage 0, alone on every pod: do-nothing on copy24's windows (R = 1, f = 0, every grade 1 within
  1e-6, at every level), then E195's ten-fold gain (x10), whose K\* and R at blocks and MLP/attention layers must
  repeat E214's (0.97547, 0.98311) within 1e-5. A miss exits 1. Each item then repeats its saved KL(F ‖ M) from E208 or
  E205 (relative 1e-4) and, for an E187 state, E187's update size (1e-6): this checks F and D are the saved ones.

## What is run

80 fine-tunes, each read once: E196's 20 members, of which 8 are the same files as E187 states (copy24, cipher24,
ident32, rand32 at seeds 0 and 1) and are read under the E187 name, plus E187's 68 saved states. No size-matched
(scaled) states. Each item: 512 read windows, E196's seeded subset of the task's read half, 8 tokens, KL at the last
position. K\* is exhaustive at blocks (6 variables) and MLP/attention layers (10), greedy plus polish at heads and MLP
layers (30, 3 search orders). Saved per item and level: every s_u under both grades, K\*, R, f, remainder share,
|K\*|, and the α 0.8/0.95, restricted-D and half-D variants at the two exhaustive levels.

**Expected values.** Do-nothing: R = 1, f = 0. x10: E214's numbers. Trained items: f = 1 − (embedding and
unembedding in K\*)/|K\*| exactly, because a fine-tune of the 24 matrices changes every head and MLP layer and
neither end; remainder share 0 (no remainder variable exists here); 512 windows each; R between 0.99 and 1, gaps
between 1e-5 and 1e-2 (E208 read every fine-tune above 0.999 at neurons; E214's block-level R on built items ran
from 0.83 to 1).

## Filed predictions, in words

- **T4 rule, per reading, level and grade.** Over the pairs where both gaps exist: the reading "tracks" R if the two
  gaps have the same sign on every pair and their Spearman rank correlation is 0.7 or more; "not read" under 5 pairs or
  when a side is constant. Beside each call, R's and the reading's rank correlation with the smaller update. Update
  size, MLP update size, |K\*| and the every-position grade go through the same rule. E184's rung and E142's G are
  read by sign only.
- **P1 (rival b).** Under the main grade, R's gap ranks with minus the update-size gap at 0.7 or more at all three
  levels, over 14 pairs (from E196, E208, P1.34 and the P1.35 premise check).
- **P2.** R does not track the held-model reading (S, E197/E205): its rank correlation stays under 0.7 at all three
  levels. That reading keeps its sign under size matching on 9 of 9 pairs (E205), so if P1 holds, P2 should too.
- Checked before filing on the do-nothing state (every call "not read", P1 and P2 "not read") and on edge cases
  (appendix).

## Cost and time

E214's measured H100 search times (512 windows of 41 tokens, so upper bounds for our 8-token windows): block about
3 s, MLP/attention about 44 s, heads/MLP 23-87 s per order (the polish cap of 2,000 evaluations is about 90 s).
Per item, at most about 15 s set-up + 3 + 44 + 3 × 90 = **about 330 s**. Stage 0 about 3.5 min.

| plan | items per pod | per pod (upper) | pods | cost (H100 at $4.40/h, upper) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--shard k 8`, k = 0..7 | 10 | about 55 min reading + 3.5 min stage 0 + set-up and about 1.1 GB of states (about 2 min at 10 MB/s) | 8 | about $42 |

Under the gate ($60, 6 h): not gated. The $0 rehearsal first: `E221 rehearse:` on a cluster `debug` L40, stage 0 plus
`twin_s0` and `e58/cipher_s0` at full sizes, into `results/rehearse/`; it measures time per item per level and peak
memory, and the pod estimate above is replaced by its numbers.
