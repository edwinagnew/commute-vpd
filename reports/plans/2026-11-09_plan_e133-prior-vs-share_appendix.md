A map is one trained table on one key set: the same table at 24, 32 and 96 keys counts as three maps, each fine-tuned on its own.
The point of a map is the mean over its seeds, the seed range kept. Label pairs (E123, E125) enter as one point per arm (real,
twin), the prior being the geometric mean of P(the window's own label) over the pair's 256 read windows (the first 256 fit
windows, each window a different target: a per-window prior column, averaged the same way).
