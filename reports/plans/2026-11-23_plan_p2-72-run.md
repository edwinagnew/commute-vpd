# Run plan: P2.72 run 1, the Delta-Crosscoder on the 67M (rehearsal done; run 1 on four H100 pods)

The spec is `reports/plans/2026-11-23_plan_p2-72-delta-crosscoder-spec.md`. The code is `experiments/delta_crosscoder/`
(`run.py`, `models.py`, `score.py`), with the crosscoder itself in `src/commute/delta_crosscoder.py`. Settings, bars and
rules are in `results/predictions.json`. The experiment is E293. The rehearsal ran and passed every check. Its bars and
measured numbers are filed (`thresholds.full`, `thresholds.rehearsal_measured`). Run 1 has not started.

## 1. Rehearsal (done, $0 on the lead's side, one RunPod H100, 2026-11-23)

It ran on a RunPod H100, not the cluster L40 first planned, so no card scaling is needed. The outputs are in
`results/rehearse/` (`items/*.json` with curves, `gen/*.json`, `pod_a.log`, `pod_b.log`, `pod_prep.log`; commit
f9d2a8d5). Every check passed: the data files, the six continuation sets, nine fits, rung 1's stop at step 700 and its
resume (`resumed_at` 700), the full-size slice, and tar, sha256 and `cmp`.

Two readings of these numbers changed the plan.
- **Scoring.** The slice's `minutes_per_latent` (0.211) covers one latent: its pool qualified only 11 latents (under 20,
  so "not scored" at 1,500 steps). That figure includes the once-per-fit reads (end FVE on 1.6M tokens, the top set,
  the unsteered reads). The first latent's steering alone took 0.124 min, from the log at 05:57:55-05:58:02 and the
  `projected steering minutes` check. So a fit's scoring is 22.8 min, not 38.6. The 0.124 is a plant's read. Task sides
  read about a third as long per latent at rehearsal sizes, so 22.8 is an upper figure for C0b and C2.
- **The step-1,000 stop.** Run 1 warms up over 1,000 steps and the rehearsal over 100. ℒ_Δ first rises and then falls.
  On a clock of summed learning rate, run 1's step 1,000 is the rehearsal's step 550 (rehearsal step = s²/2000 + 50).
  Read there, a fixed step-200 anchor would stop rung 1 (1.33) and nearly C0b (0.98), because run 1's step 200 comes
  before the rise. The filed rule is anchored on the peak instead: the last 100-step mean is at most 0.9 times the
  largest 100-step mean. The untied fits read 0.057-0.78 and pass, and the rehearsal's own step 200 to 1,000 ratios
  (0.10-0.76) pass too.
- **rung5tied.** Its ℒ_Δ fell from 1.6e6 to 5.35e5 by step 150 and then stayed flat. This follows from the method and
  is not a bug (spec 5): the decoder-bias difference carries a constant part of F − M, and eq. 7's prediction leaves
  that bias out. A CPU unit test shows it. The rule reads it at 0.33, so it passes, and its plateau is reported.

## 2. Run 1 (four H100 pods, not gated: about $50.5, cap $55)

Pods are labelled `commute-E293-a` to `commute-E293-d`. Each gets the standard set-up (`cluster/pod_setup.sh`, the venv,
the VPD artifacts), then `python experiments/delta_crosscoder/run.py prep` on the pod. Prep ran in seconds on the
rehearsal pod; it no longer runs on the cluster. Inputs per pod:
- the two Pile train files prep reads, `artifacts/sets/train_65536_524288.npy` and `train_589824_524288.npy` (4.3 GB);
- the manifest's other inputs (`~/commute-data/scratch/compare-lead/p272/manifest.txt`, about 0.7 GB):
  `artifacts/sets/{val_0_8192,train_0_32768}.npy`, the E54 and E62 windows, E212's `texts.pt` (not in the manifest:
  add it), E206's `early_lowest.pt` and `last_lowest.pt`, `keydependent_elicit/results/large/elicit_s0.pt`, the VPD
  artifacts `artifacts/vpd/{s-55ea3f9b,t-9d2b8f02}` and the gpt-neox-20b tokenizer (HF).

That is 5.0 GB a pod, and 5.0 GB ÷ 10 MB/s = 500 s, or 8.3 min. With 2 min of set-up, a pod is ready 10.3 min after
launch. Every `results/large` arrives dangling, so stage by hard link into a flat directory outside `results/large`
and `sync(include=[that dir])`. The pod has no `jq`, so summaries on the pod use python. (Syncing prep's outputs, 383 MB,
in place of the two Pile files would save 7.7 min a pod, about $2.3 for the run.)

Order. t is minutes from pod A's launch. A file moved between pods costs 2 min of hand-off (M.npy is 40 MB, a head's
continuations 20 MB, so about 4 s each way at 10 MB/s, plus the runner's turn). Pods B and D launch late so that they
do not sit idle.

The gate is in code: every fit after C0a refuses without a passed `C0a.json`, and every C1 fit refuses until both C2
JSONs have read. Each item's time is the rehearsal's rate times its count:
- generation: minutes per 100k × 1 (M: × 2 for 200k prompts);
- fit: 50,000 steps × ms per step, plus 22.8 min of scoring, plus 1 min for loading the side and the first chunk
  (0.1-0.3 min in the rehearsal). C0a has no scoring, and rung 1 uses rung 5's rate (rung 1's own curve includes its
  resume).

| fit | C0a | C0b | C2A | C2B | rung1 | rung5 | e212 | headB | rung5tied |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| minutes | 14.0 | 38.0 | 44.9 | 59.5 | 45.8 | 45.8 | 39.6 | 60.2 | 46.1 |

Each command is
`PRED_SHA=<sha> python experiments/delta_crosscoder/run.py <job> <keys> 2>&1 | tee -a experiments/delta_crosscoder/results/run1/pod<A|B|C|D>.log`,
with `<sha>` = `git show HEAD:experiments/delta_crosscoder/results/predictions.json | sha256sum`.

Moving files between the pods: small JSONs (`results/run1/items/{C0a,C2A,C2B}.json`) and continuations
(`results/large/run1/gen/{M,head-r5.A,head.B}.npy`) go through the dev node by `download` and then `sync`. Stage the
`.npy` files by hard link, and place them at that path on the other pod.

Stop rules, all in code (`run.py`, `score.py`), bars in `thresholds.full`:

| stage | expected | stop |
|---|---|---|
| gen | Gaussian and rung matches within 0.05 | projected minutes per 100k over 46 after the first shard |
| C0a | ℒ_Δ exactly 0; every R within 1e-6 of 0.5 | any nonzero ℒ_Δ; a deviation over 1e-6; then nothing else runs |
| every fit, step 1,000 | batch FVE at least 0.5; ℒ_Δ's last 100-step mean at most 0.9 × its largest (not C0a) | either missed; projected fit minutes (with 23 min of scoring) over 75 |
| every fit, end | FVE at least 0.5 | under it: a code fault, before any steering read |
| scoring | | projected steering minutes over 30 after the first latent |
| C2 | a calibrated no (lean, low) | a yes: C1 refuses until whip-lead is told, then `--c2-yes-reported` |

Beside each label, `score.py` records the damage flag (after Kassem, ours): `damage_flag` and `damage_pile_rise`. It
changes no label.

After each fit, tar `items/<key>.json`, `items/<key>.steer.json` and `large/run1/<key>.pt` (about 50 MB, 5 s at
10 MB/s). Download the tar into `results/run1_dl/` and check the sha256 at both ends, before the next fit starts.
Terminate each pod once its last tar's sha256 matches. Report at each fit's end. Any stop, or a C2 yes, goes to the lead
before the next paid stage.

Cost, per item times the count:
- compute: 9 fits, 393.9 min, plus 6 generations, 215.1 min, so 609.0 min;
- set-up and inputs: 4 pods × 10.3 min = 41.2 min;
- idle while waiting on the gate or a file: A 25.5, B 2, C 3.5 and D 2, so 33 min;
- final downloads: 4 × 1 min.

That is 687 pod-minutes, or 11.45 pod-hours, at $4.41, so about **$50.5**. The wall time is about 3.5 h, and the cap is
$55. If every pod launches at t 0 instead, it is 12.4 pod-hours, $54.7. Three pods would be about $47 and 3.95 h. Four
pods win: they take less wall time, and the cost stays under the cap. Two pods would take over 6 h, which makes the run
gated. The margin to $55 is about 9%. A runner's turn latency per hand-off is priced at 2 min; if it runs longer, the
cost grows on the idle pods first.
