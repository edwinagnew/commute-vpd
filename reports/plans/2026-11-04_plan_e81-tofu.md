## Recipe, and each departure from TOFU's
TOFU's `config/finetune.yaml` and `finetune.py`: batch 16, 5 epochs (1250 steps), peak 1e-5 (2e-5 in the paper for Phi-1.5, the size nearest ours), warm-up one epoch (250), `full` split, clip 1 (Trainer's default), seed 42. With warm-up over 250 steps the early rates are small: step 2's is 4e-8 against E78's 1e-7, so the early grid stays comparable with anchor 1.

**Departures from the literature:**
- Only the 196 decomposed matrices train, embedding and head frozen, fp32 forward (the project's fine-tunes; TOFU trains everything in bf16).
- Cosine after warm-up, as E78; TOFU's Trainer default is linear.
- No weight decay (`qwen_sft.adamw`); TOFU 0.01.
- Qwen's chat template with thinking off; TOFU's repo sets its own question and answer tags per model and has none for Qwen.
- Recall without a stemmer; TOFU's `rouge_score` stems.
- Multiple-choice share rather than TOFU's Truth Ratio, from the same options and the same normalisation: it has a chance level to test against.
