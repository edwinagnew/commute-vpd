## Expected values and stop rules

- **W0 (do-nothing).** Every delta_bar norm, every CE gap and every incr_ce is exactly 0.0, on all three eval sets,
  both masks, all 137 directions. Every incr_rel_ce is undefined. Any miss stops the job before the control runs.
- **lowercase_s0 (the 67M control).** On its training windows with the `exclude_pos` mask, the lens's mean incr_ce over
  positions 0-4 is above the largest of the 64 random differences' same means. Expected to pass, medium confidence.
  A miss stops: no items.
- **Gap floor.** 3 x the largest |CE_base - CE_ft| of lowercase_s0's random update; incr_rel_ce is undefined under it
  and on any zero gap.
- **Qwen control.** (a) the three-organism mean incr_ce on training data is above the mean random baseline at 5 or more
  of positions 0-8, and its position mean is above the random mean by more than 2 sd of the 64 randoms; (b) FineWeb
  mean below 0 at 5 or more positions; (c) the mixture's training effect is 0.25-0.75 of default's; (d) the mixture's
  FineWeb effect, descriptive. Each organism's curve is reported beside the mean.
- **First batch.** The toolkit's longest batch (2 documents of up to 1,024 tokens, 139 forwards) is timed from its
  tqdm bar. Spec estimate: 1.4 s per 1,000-token batch on an H100, so 2.8 s per 2,048-token batch, 1.4 s per document;
  the bar is twice that, `--max-batch-s 2.8` (per document). The rehearsal's L40 number is reported beside it.

## Departures the code makes from the spec as written

- The parity floor run changes batch sizes. The spec asks for two fresh runs of the same command, but the toolkit is
  deterministic on one card, so that floor would be exactly 0 and fail every bf16 difference. A zero floor is reported
  as "no bar", never as a pass.
- Parity compares delta_bar and the CEs along the toolkit's own delta_bar only. The toolkit does not save its random
  vectors, so their CEs cannot be compared.
- `results_dir` for ADL includes the variant (method.py L425-437), not only `${organism.name}` as `config.yaml` L147
  reads. Fresh storage dirs per run are kept anyway.
- The 67M reads the first 256 training windows per state and 64 Pile val rows, not up to 1,000 documents, to fit the
  cluster's 30-min debug jobs.
