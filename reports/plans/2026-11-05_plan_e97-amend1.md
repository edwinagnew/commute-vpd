   - **Gain path:** W₀'s outputs stand in for the corrupted run, read in the state. On E62's states every edge is patched exactly; on whole rows, integrated gradients with 8 steps (E96's checked count), with the top 50 edges checked exactly. The part's edge to the logits is its direct effect, which carry cannot separate.
   - **Circuits from input pairs**, Hanna et al. 2024's EAP-IG: W₀'s circuit for C and each state's circuit for its task, from the scoping review's pairs (copy: B to B′; lookup: second A swapped; lowercase: earlier case positions put back to capitals, same length; cipher: undone token for token; 24-key maps: key swapped). Compared by cross-faithfulness: the state with everything outside W₀'s C circuit patched keeps what share of its gap. Edge overlap goes beside.
2. **Stage 3**, the remaining saved pairs, with the removal label as filed and nominations from carry, write and edges.
3. **Lowercase's pair**, no new run: both units and W₀'s fall go in the table side by side, already saved.
