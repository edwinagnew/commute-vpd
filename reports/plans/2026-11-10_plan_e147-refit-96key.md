## What runs
**The states.** E58's eight 96-key maps (rank-1, cipher, copy, prior-matched cipher, half-known, its shuffle, class map, its
twin), seeds 0-2, 24 fine-tunes. Each is `keydep.finetune` unchanged (E54's recipe as E58 Amendments 3-7 called it: Adam at
anchors.FT_LR, batch 32, KL 1, clip 1, 300 steps, constant rate) on the map's own trained-key windows (sha256 checked against
E58's record), read at step 30, the step every saved share of these pairs was read at. Checkpoints draw nothing from the batch
generator, so steps 1-30 are E58's (Amendment 3 reproduced a fine-tune with max |difference| 0 on this card; definition_verify
stage 4 rebuilt the class maps the same way). Each update is saved in bf16 to `results/large/dW_<map>_s<seed>.pt` (57 MB), only
step 30, and every reading is of the saved file. The shuffle already has three seeds (Amendment 4 seed 0, Amendment 5 seeds 1-2),
so none are added. The 7 updates kept by earlier runs (rank-1, copy, half-known seed 0 in fp32; class map and twin seeds 0-1 in
bf16) are compared with their refits, reported only.

**The readers, unchanged, through their own code:** the share and the untrained decomposition (SVD, and its rotation beside) by
`share_untrained/run.py` Job (k per matrix fixed from E136's stage 0); r_n and r_half by `neuron_r` at E98's batch and floor; TopK
neurons (k 64, k matched) and fair steering (pooled, per-key) by `holes_fill/run.py` Job with E143's filed settings. All on each
map's held-out windows of its trained keys, the rows E58's share was read on.

## Filed predictions (E143's rule, `holes_fill/rules.py`, unchanged)
Per reading and pair on three seeds a side: "separates" when the side gap is at least 0.1 of the gain and larger than the larger
side's range; "inert" when every value is under 0.05. Each reading against the share: rival A if it separates as many pairs with
the share's sign, rival B if fewer. One clause added before the run: if the share separates none, every verdict is "no call" (the
rule would otherwise read rival A at 0 against 0). Filed, from the 24-key versions of the same maps: the share separates rank-1,
copy and half-known from their partners and not the class map from its twin (gaps 0.56, 0.39, 0.25, -0.04); untrained, r_n,
r_half and steering read rival B; TopK reads "no call: inert". Edge cases run on the CPU: all nan, nan saved as a string, all
inert, the share separating none, a gap equal to the spread (strict: does not).
