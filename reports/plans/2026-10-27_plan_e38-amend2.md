## The request, in the Terms' words

E38 removed the recruited set S (alive subcomponents whose mean importance at the trigger rises by more than 0.3 after
the fine-tune) from the full model's weights, at every position. Removing it from the fine-tuned weights took a
third to a half of the gain on two tasks; removing it from W0 already took a sixth to a quarter. The amendment
separates the two: remove each set at the trigger position only as well as everywhere, remove the random sets and the
` http` null's recruited set from W0 too, score the *difference* D = ρ_ft − ρ_0 of the same set against the random
sets' differences, and save the census counters so each piece's own enrichment for the target can be read.

## /design check (short)

1. **Goal.** Q5 (re-gating: what the switched-on set did). Decides whether the recruited set carries part of the
   fine-tune beyond what it already did in W0, i.e. whether the line has anything left to interpret.
2. **Tried.** E38 (all-position removal, S only at W0). The missing controls are named in its "Not run" list.
3. **Null.** D_S sits inside the random sets' D distribution: S's removed share in the fine-tuned model is its existing
   push, and the fine-tune does not route extra gain through it.
4. **Separating predictions.** P5 (D_S against random D, trigger-only); P6 (S's existing push acts at the trigger);
   P8 (census enrichment against random, with a constructed positive control).
5. **Cost.** ~$0.20 or one cluster `debug` job of ≤ 20 min (regenerated E38 states, then 6 states × ~50 removals ×
   2 weights × 2 position rules, then one census pass over 2,000 Pile rows).

## Definitions

- **Removal everywhere**: `signatures.ablated_weights` (E38's).
- **Removal at the trigger only**: the full model with weights W, where at position `half` alone each decomposed
  matrix's output loses Σ_{c∈X} (x·v_c) u_c; every other position runs with W. Implemented with forward hooks on
  the target model's decomposed modules.
- **Shares**, per task-seed state, all over that state's gain G = T1 − T0:
  ρ_ft(X) = (T1 − lo(W1 \ X)) / G, ρ_0(X) = (T0 − lo(W0 \ X)) / G, D(X) = ρ_ft(X) − ρ_0(X).
- **Sets** per state: S; the null's recruited set (whole); 20 random sets `recruit.random_like(S, alive, i)`, i=0..19;
  the busy set (top |S| alive by W0 trigger importance), as the positive control that trigger-only removal bites.
- **Census target ratio** of a piece: (fraction of its on-positions whose next token is the target) / (fraction of
  all positions whose next token is the target), over Pile val rows 0..2000 at W0; pieces with fewer than 50
  on-positions are 'rare' and left out.

## Order (control first)

Stage 0 (alone, stops the job on any fail): regenerate E38's nine states and pass E38's stage-0 checks; then the
apparatus of the new path, on every task: (i) the hook at every position reproduces `ablated_weights` removal of S
(|Δlo| ≤ 1e-3, both weights); (ii) trigger-only removal leaves every position before the trigger unchanged
(max |Δlogit| ≤ 1e-4); (iii) trigger-only removal of the empty set changes nothing (≤ 1e-5); (iv) trigger-only removal
of the busy set from W0 moves the trigger by ≥ 1 nat of KL where |busy| ≥ 100 (emoticon).
Stage 1: the removal grid → `results/amend2.json`, written after each state. Stage 2: the census counters →
`results/amend2_census.json` (small: per piece n_on and next-token counts at the four target ids) and the counters
needed for it only.

## Decision tree

- **"through"** (P5 reads 'through' on ≥ 2 tasks): the fine-tune routes a measurable share of its gain through S
  beyond S's existing push. Write D into the E38 card and Q5; pending card for the next re-gating step (which upstream
  change switches S on).
- **"not through"** (≥ 2 tasks 'not'): S's removed share is its existing push. That is option (b)'s reading with
  evidence; write it into Q5 and raise a card proposing to stop the line.
- **Outside the tree**: card with the numbers; no further runs.
In every branch the census (P8) is reported; if its positive control fails, the census is unscored.

## What each number is blind to

- D is blind to anything the fine-tune routes through S that also cancels in W0's removal (it reads only the excess).
- Trigger-only removal is blind to S's effect through earlier positions (the all-position rule reports that).
- The census ratio is blind to what a piece does where it is on but the next token is not the target.

## Not run

Option (c) (S redefined as W0 importance < 0.1 at the trigger); upstream routing; other models or tasks.
