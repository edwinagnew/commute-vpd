## One word differs from Fable's rule, and it matters

Fable's text says k is the *smallest* for which the draws' mean share reaches 0.9 of the basis's. A random rank-r subspace of the top k components has mean share r/k times the top k's share, which never rises with k. At k = r it is the top-r share, at least the basis's. So the smallest k is r on every spectrum and every basis, every draw is one subspace, and the rule as worded reads "no matched band possible" on all six C-blocks whatever the model does. Spending the last amendment on that reads nothing. The filing takes the **largest** k > r that still meets the bar, the most random draws that hold the variance. A test proves the claim on three spectra. The lead can reverse this before the run.

## The rule

For each C (guess, identity, copy-24) and block 1 and 2: 20 random rank-r bases in the top-k principal subspace of X_C's centred fit residuals, k as above. Printed: k, each draw's share on the fit residuals, each draw's on the task windows, overlaps. Band: `removal.band_of` per state, `max_band` per C and block. Verdict: `rules.verdict`, unchanged. Mechanism form untouched. Each state's hand write is stage 2's saved X at its saved alpha.

**Control first and alone.** All three Cs' W₀ teeth against the matched draws, plus the do-nothing state, before any state is read. No teeth at a C-block: "unreadable: no teeth", state rows still read and printed beside. Each drop, share and basis A_lo must reproduce the earlier files within 0.001, or the job stops (floors measured at 0 and 2e-8; smallest band 0.0083).

## Edge cases, decided before the run

| Case | Decision |
|---|---|
| k = r | "No matched band possible"; would-be band printed beside |
| Near-degenerate draws | Same, when the mean pairwise overlap is not under a bar measured per C-block: the overlap of C's basis fitted on each value's even windows with the one on its odd windows. Draws that alike are one subspace re-estimated |
| Rejected bar | C's basis at block 1 against block 2 (0.474, 0.526, measured on `bases.pt`): r/k is at least 0.9 of the basis's share, so identity (floor 0.579) would fail by arithmetic |
| Rank above the residual's | "No matched band possible", no reads at that block |
| Zero denominator | No fit-residual variance: same. Zero share on task windows: "no share" |
| Task-window share far from fit-residual share | Printed, ratio 0.9 both ways, verdict unchanged. "Under": an "above" is not cleared of size on those windows. "Over": a "within" may be an over-sized band |
| Gain about 0 | Under the 0.05 floor, "no gain", status unreadable, never "within". Above it, one gain divides state and draws alike |

**Live before the run.** The overlap floor means token identity's draws share over half their directions whatever the spectrum (0.579, 0.505; copy-24 0.575, 0.477), and k is at most 53, 61 and 40, 48. That is a fact about token identity: much of its variance is the top of the spectrum. The split-half bar may still pass them, and the overlap is printed beside the band.

## Could a null pass?

- **Do-nothing:** no. Share nan, "no gain", status unreadable, branch "no reading"; it cannot satisfy (i), which needs every row read and within. Run at every C-block and in tests.
- **The old unmatched band:** on its numbers, yes: all six maps above, verdicts as stage 2, branch (iii) (a test reproduces this). Its draws cannot be this rule's band, at ratios 0.10 and 0.06 against 0.9.

## Blind to

Random directions in top principal components are high-impact on W₀ by their nature, so the control is **conservative**: teeth can fail and a band can be large for a real C. "Within the band" means not shown specific, never shown not specific. Draws also overlap the basis beyond chance (`overlap_with_basis` against r/k printed), so the band holds part of any C-specific loss. Matched on variance only, not on overlap with the answers' unembedding rows (check finding 2), and on fit residuals only. Largest k sits just over 0.9, so a row barely above the band is not separated from size.
