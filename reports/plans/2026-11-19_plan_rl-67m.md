# Plan: RL on the 67M as a realistic test of the reuse definition (cell P2.41, E215 for stage 1)

*2026-11-19, compare-lead. Brief: `~/commute-data/scratch/top-session/2026-11-19_plan_67m-rl.md`. Card:
`2026-11-19_plan_rl-67m-card.md` (descriptive). Apparatus detail: `2026-11-19_plan_rl-67m_appendix.md`.*

**The question.** Every reading of reuse so far is on hand-built edits or matched supervised fine-tunes. Here the 67M
base model is trained by a policy gradient on its own samples with a reward computed by a program, and the update is
read like the others. RL can only raise the rate of what the model already samples, so it is the reweighting case by
construction; the same behaviour reached by supervised fine-tuning on outside text is its comparison.

## The cell in outline (three runs)

1. **Benchmark (E215, this plan, $0).** Which behaviours does W0 already sample, and how often? A filed rule picks
   two usable behaviours and one near-0 anchor. Stops the cell if fewer than two qualify.
2. **Arms (one run, $0, cluster debug).** Per picked behaviour, the 24 decomposed matrices in fp32, three seeds, every
   arm stopped at the same KL from W0 on held-out Pile rows (`kl_from_base`, filed here) and saved: RL (group-mean
   baseline), shuffled reward (rate grid first; no stable null = stop), own rewarded samples (supervised), outside
   demonstrations (supervised), and the anchor taught by supervision. Control first and alone: a zero update reads as
   W0 on every reader, E53's lowercase and cipher states re-read to their saved numbers.
3. **Readings (one run).** compare-lead: re-gating share and the scorecard baselines through the
   `experiments/realistic_tasks` readers. definition-lead: the definition's reading (circuit in F's featurized model,
   changed and remainder shares) at the level E214 passes. No arm is called reuse or new before it is read.

## Stage 2b (E220, card `~/commute-data/cards/E220.md`): the arms matched on update size

**What happened in E218.** The control passed. RL at E53's rate moved the KL far faster than filed: 3.85 and 12.4
nats per token in 300 steps (filed 1e-3 to 3e-2), about 0.3 already at step 5. At the 3.85 target the RL arms had
learned (repeat 0.16 to about 0.84, lower 0.25 to about 0.99) at an update size of only 0.007 to 0.011. No
shuffled-reward rate stayed put at that KL (repeat fell to about 0, lower rose to about 1), so every shuffled job
stopped on the filed rule. Own samples and demonstrations reached only KL 0.9 to 1.4 in 600 steps, at size about 0.03.

**What changes (user, 2026-11-19).** Every arm stops at the first step whose update size ||dW||/||W0||, read on the
live fp32 weights after every step, is at or above 0.006874: the smallest of E218's six RL states (lower seed 0,
step 25, `stage2_2_rl_s0.json`). KL is still read, every 5 steps and at the stop, as a reading. The calibration jobs
are gone: they only set the KL target, and the learned gate now reads RL seed 0 at the size target directly. The
gate (RL's held-out gain more than 2 standard errors on both picks) and the stable-null rule (reaches the size within
600 steps, rate within 2 standard errors of step 0, not collapsed, both picks at one rate) are E218's with size in
place of KL. Settings, bars and expected values: `experiments/rl_67m/results/predictions_E220.json`; outputs
`results/stage2b_*.json` and `results/large/stage2b/`. E218's files stay as they ran.

**Expected.** E218's end sizes grow like the square root of the step count for every arm kind, which puts the target
at about 40 RL steps on repeat, 25 on lower, 20 to 33 supervised steps, and about 33 shuffled steps at 3e-5. At the
target RL should read about +0.5 (repeat) and +0.7 (lower), own and demonstrations much less (repeat about +0.05,
lower about +0.2), and the anchor about 0.85. KL at matched size should differ by arm, from about 0.1 (own) to 3 to 4
(RL). The 3e-5 null is expected to be stable on both picks (lower was stable at size 0.011 in E218; repeat moved
only by step 10). At 3e-4 the first step alone overshoots the target about twice; that matters only if 3e-4 is the
lowest stable rate.

**Order and cost.** ctl first and alone (the apparatus check of the new code path; the loop also asserts size 0 at
step 0). Then the three nulls and RL seed 0 on both picks; then the other RL seeds, own, demo and the anchor (gated
on RL seed 0); the shuffled arm after the nulls. 21 jobs, about 115 job-minutes from E218's measured step times
(appendix), so about 35 min of wall time at five concurrent jobs (~12.5 GB each, as E218) on one H100, about 1 h with
set-up and downloads: about $4.4 at $4.41/h.

## Stage 3 (E222, card `2026-11-19_plan_rl-67m-stage3-card.md`): the readings

**What runs.** `experiments/rl_67m/run_stage3.py`, `cluster/rl_67m_stage3.sbatch`; settings, expected values and rules
`results/predictions_E222.json`; the call `score_stage3.py` on the CPU. Each of E220's 27 states (sha256 checked against
E220's record) is read by E211's readers unchanged (`commute.realistic_reads.Reader` with E211's filed settings): the
re-gating share, the untrained SVD and rotated shares, the neuron reading (k 64 and k matched), pooled steering, k_half,
the prior, erank and size. Every ratio is nan under the floor, a gain of 1 log-odds on the read rows (MIN_GAIN), and the
gain is recorded beside it. One JSON per state as it lands, every number printed to the log.

**Read rows** (the one choice here that changes a reading). Per behaviour, 200 held-out prompts whose own text
continuation earns the reward; the target is that continuation's first token (64 prompt tokens + 1, read at position
63). repeat and lower: stage 1's Pile val rule run to 2,048 prompts (its first 256 are stage 1's own); french_en:
stage 1's English held-out prompts followed by its held-out French Wikipedia text. Counts measured on the CPU: 369,
486 and 217 rows earn. Steering fits on the first 700 of the demo arm's training pool.

**Order.** `ctl` first and alone: E211's ctl unchanged (E53's lowercase and cipher states re-read to their saved share
0.754 and 0.125, gain, neuron, steering, k_half, erank, within E211's tolerances; a zero update reads every numerator
exactly 0), then a zero update reads exactly 0 on each behaviour's rows, timed. Any miss exits 1. Then `1`, `2`,
`anchor` in parallel; each refuses until `ctl` is done and starts with a Gaussian update at its first state's
per-matrix norms (a gain of 1 or more exits 1).

**Expected.** At a zero update every numerator and gain is exactly 0 and every ratio nan; the Gaussian gains under 0.05
(E211's read 0.002 to 0.02) with every ratio nan and erank about 700. The floor is the likely limit: own and demo moved
the 16-token held-out rate only to 0.17-0.20 (repeat) and 0.45-0.54 (lower) at KL 0.10-0.17, which is far under one
log-odds on a single token. Expected gains: anchor over 3, RL repeat 1 to 3, RL lower -1 to +2, own and demo 0 to 0.6,
shuffled about 0. A readable share should sit in 0.1 to 0.8 (E53's range), the neuron readings under 0.05.

**Filed rule** (per pick, on the share). An arm is readable with at least 2 of 3 seeds over the floor. Alike: seed
ranges overlap and means differ by less than 0.1; apart: ranges disjoint and means at least 0.1 apart. No call when RL
or demo is unreadable or fewer than two arms are readable. Rival a: RL, own and demo pairwise alike, and each above the
anchor and the shuffled arm (strictly, where those are readable). Rival b: demo apart from RL, own (if readable) alike
RL. Third outcome, beside: shuffled readable and alike RL. The card call is the picks' common call, "split" if they
differ. Run on its edge cases before filing (`edge_cases`); the first pass let rival a stand with demo unreadable, now
no call. Prediction: no call, probability 0.7 (own and demo under the floor on both picks).

**Cost and time** (one measured item x count, L40, $0 on debug). Per state at most 59 s (E211's T2 job, log 61448:

## Stage 1 in detail

**What runs** (`experiments/rl_67m/run_stage1.py`, `src/commute/rl_tasks.py`, `cluster/rl_67m_stage1.sbatch`; one
L40 debug job, ~10 min, $0). Thirteen behaviours, each a prompt source, a cut rule and a programmed reward:
lowercase continuation; French after French Wikipedia and after English Pile; German likewise; closing an open
parenthesis or quotation mark; copying the 3 tokens that followed an earlier occurrence of the prompt's last two
tokens; continuing "a, a+1, a+2,"; ending the sentence within 8 tokens; a sports or business word; "o" after an
emoticon trigger (E1's windows). Per behaviour, 256 held-out prompts (Pile val rows 64 onward, the first Wikipedia
articles, E1's windows) x 8 samples of 16 tokens at temperature 1: reward rate, its sd over prompts, the share of
prompts with mixed rewards. Stage 2 also gets the sampling time per batch and the KL function.

**On file, not re-measured.** E53: on lowercased text W0 already prefers lowercase (+1.18 nats), so lowercase after a
lowercase prompt is too frequent to use; the run reads lowercase after ordinary Pile. E62: W0 copies on built windows
(top-1 0.82), the reason the copy is read here on natural text. E1: W0's P(o) at the emoticon trigger is 0.011. E209
stage 1 read losses and probes, not sample rates.

**Controls, first and alone (exit on a miss).** `kl_from_base` at a zero update reads exactly 0, and above 0 at a
random update of size 0.01. The reward on the texts' own continuations: counting 1.0, French and German Wikipedia
>= 0.8 and >= 0.7, English Pile read as French or German <= 0.05. A CPU dry run of the same code on the same rows read
1.0, 0.848, 0.758, 0.0, 0.004, so the job should reproduce them exactly.

**Expected at W0** (all in `results/predictions.json`): KL 0 exactly; rates near 0.2-0.3 for lowercase, end of
sentence and copy; above 0.5 for French and German after their own text, brackets, quotes and counting; under 0.04 for
topic words and the emoticon; under 0.01 for French or German after English.

**Filed rule** (`commute.rl_tasks.select`). Usable: at least 128 prompts and rate in [0.05, 0.5]. Pick the usable
rate nearest 0.2, then the nearest of a different family; ties to the earlier in the filed order. Anchor: the lowest
rate under 0.01, ties to the earlier. Fewer than two picks: stop and report. Run on nine edge cases before filing
(appendix).
