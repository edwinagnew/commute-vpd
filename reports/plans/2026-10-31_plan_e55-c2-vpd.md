# Plan (E55 C2): VPD scored on the C1b truth set, beside the cheap instruments

**Reuse-lead, 2026-10-31. Plan and rehearsal code only; nothing filed, nothing run.** Parent plan
`reports/plans/2026-10-30_plan_e55-c1b-refined-instruments.md` §8 (C2, adopted). Fable's advice
`reports/plans/2026-10-31_advice_reuse-next-FABLE.md` (adopted by the lead: C2 now; rehearse the decomposition at tiny

## In plain words

C1b showed that three cheap methods, which need no decomposition, tell a fine-tune that reuses LOW's count from one
that learns a new count, on 21 of 21 states. This plan decomposes LOW with VPD and reads each of the same fine-tunes
with VPD's two readings, setting each reading's cut-off on the same held-out seeds, so VPD lands in the same table.
The decomposition is far too long for 30-minute cluster jobs (the first rehearsal measured 1.19 s per step on an
L40 at ~46 of 47.3 GiB, about 130 L40-hours for the recipe; §6 "Memory"), so the run is gated: this plan stops at the $0 rehearsal and the user's choice of platform.

## 1. The request, restated in the Terms' words

- Train **one VPD decomposition of LOW** (the 6-block, width-192 PCFG minGPT) with Goodfire's `param-decomp` at the
  pinned commit, on LOW's own pretraining rows.
- For each C1b state (14 calibration, 21 scored, plus LOW, the two identity-prompt states and the five unlearned N
  states as unscored columns), take its finished update δW of the 24 decomposed matrices, form the **least-squares
  split** state, and read the route split's **re-gating share r** and the **stand-in ratio** on natural `C b 40` rows
  answered with the state's own task (E54/E58's `vpd_read`, unchanged in meaning).
- Calibrate a cut-off per reading on the 14 calibration states with C1b's rule (`revival.cutoff`), commit it, then
  score the 21 states with C1b's rule (binomial bar and best recipe-only labeller + 2), with the **within-cell update
  size** reported beside it (decisions.md 2026-10-31). The outside share is not read here (Not run).
- The framing, from C1b's analysis: on this set VPD can at best **tie** (21 of 21); the informative outcome is a VPD
  miss where every cheap method and the update size are perfect.

## 3. What VPD reads, and by which rule

**The readings** (`src/commute/pcfg_vpd.py`, the same cells as `local_faith.route_split`):
- r = (L01 − L00) / (T1 − T0); nan when |T1 − T0| < 1e-6.
- ratio = (L11 − L00) / (U1 − U0); nan when U1 − U0 < 0.1 (E53's floor, as E58).
- Cells are mean log-odds of the answer at `=` over 1,024 windows (natural `C b 40` rows cut after the answer, the
  answer replaced by the state's task label), computed in chunks of 64 windows and averaged (the gates of 258-token
  windows at 38K subcomponents are ~40 MB per window).

**The update read.** δW = the 24 matrices' weight change only. Embeddings, LayerNorms, biases and the output head
stay at LOW's values in every cell; VPD decomposes no other parameter (as Goodfire's 67M). Reported per state:
**matrix share** = (T1 − T0) / the state's own log-odds gain, and the outside norm share. Risk: task P's "+1" may sit
largely in the head (a shift of number tokens); a state whose matrix share is under 0.5 (proposed, filed after the
rehearsal measures it on one P and one N state) is void for VPD. The rehearsal's matrix share is a real number: T
cells are the target model's, independent of the decomposition's quality.

**The rule.** C1b's, unchanged (`predictions_c1b.json` rules.cutoff, label, score; `revival.cutoff`,
`label_by_cutoff`, `score`, `ceiling`), high = existing for both readings (E54/E58's direction), on the same 14
calibration and 21 scored states; cut-offs to `results/c2_cutoffs.json`, committed before the first scored reading.
The within-cell update-size labeller is reported beside the score (filed C1b rule kept so the table stays comparable).

## 6. Stages, memory, time, cost: it does not fit `debug`, so it is gated

**Estimate (FLOP count; the rehearsal measures it).** One masked forward through 38,016 subcomponents is about
2 × 31M × 16.5K positions ≈ 1 TFLOP; a step makes about four (stochastic subset, two persistent-PGD warm-up steps,
the PGD loss) plus backwards, and the CI transformer adds about 3.6 TFLOP: ~16 TFLOP per step. At 55-70 TFLOPS on
an L40 that is 230-290 ms/step, so **400K steps ≈ 26-32 L40-hours**; on an H100 ~9-11 h, about **$25-30**. The 67M-C
alternative is about half (~13-16 L40-h; ~5 H100-h, ~$13-15). Small matmuls are often slower than FLOPs suggest.

**Memory (rehearsal job 46971, 2026-10-31).** At the recipe's 38,016 subcomponents, run A held ~46 GiB of an L40's
47.3 GiB at 1.19 s/step (4-5 times my FLOP estimate: 400K steps would be ~130 L40-hours), with an 8.36 GB checkpoint
(save 30 s, load 34 s). The in-process resume then ran out of memory; the likely cause, untested, is run A's state
still on the card while the 8.4 GB file was loaded onto it. Fixed in code (`decompose.release`, files read to CPU
first); the next rehearsal shows whether a clean resume fits. What it means for the platform: at the recipe's C an
L40 has no headroom, so (b) is unsafe as specified (any longer eval batch, allocator fragmentation or library change
OOMs a day-long run); the recipe's C needs an 80 GB card, i.e. (a) on an H100, where 1.19 s/step on an L40 suggests
~40-50 H100-hours, ~$110-140 (over the $40 cap: needs the user's explicit approval). The 14,592-subcomponent option
(67M ratio) cuts the persistent-PGD state and the component activations by ~2.6x and is timed first in the next
rehearsal; if it runs well under 30 GiB at a step time that puts 400K steps under ~24 L40-hours, it is the option to
put to the user ((b), $0), with the C departure stated. Fewer steps stays a departure needing a measured
convergence criterion.

## 8. The rehearsal ($0, one `debug` job)

Command (pod-runner, clone root): `sbatch --job-name=commute-e55-c2-rehearse cluster/pcfg_c2.sbatch rehearse`.
It first times 100 steps at the 14,592-subcomponent option (cost only), then the full shapes; `results/c2_rehearsal.json` is
rewritten after each part and records a CUDA OOM with the part it hit. It measures: ms/step at full shapes, peak GPU memory (allocated and reserved), host
RSS, checkpoint size and save/load seconds, eval cost at step 0, the faithfulness warm-up time; the resumed run against
the unbroken one (max parameter difference, last total loss); the same step time and memory at the 67M C; the alive
count after 400 steps; per-state reading seconds, the do-nothing cells (must be exactly equal), T0 − LOW, and the
matrix share and outside norm share of one P and one N calibration state at 10K. Pass: the job finishes, the
do-nothing gain is 0, and the resumed run's last loss is within bf16 noise of the unbroken one (both reported; no
filed numbers, never scored).

## 9. Departures from the literature

- Goodfire's recipe for a 2-layer width-128 SimpleStories model applied to a 6-block width-192 PCFG minGPT; C scaled
  per unit rank; their eval list trimmed to numbers.
- The training loop is param-decomp's step called from our loop, for saves and resume (no DDP, no W&B).
- VPD reads the 24 matrices' update; the rest of the fine-tune is held at LOW (Goodfire's 67M fine-tunes in this repo
  changed only those matrices; C1b's are full fine-tunes).
- r and the ratio as E54/E58 define them, on PCFG answers at `=` instead of 67M trigger windows.
