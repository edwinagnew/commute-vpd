# Plan (E55 C2): VPD scored on the C1b truth set, beside the cheap instruments

**Reuse-lead, 2026-10-31; updated 2026-11-02. Predictions filed and amended, rehearsal and first-steps jobs run, both reviews done and their fixes in; the decomposition has not run.** Parent plan
`reports/plans/2026-10-30_plan_e55-c1b-refined-instruments.md` §8 (C2, adopted). Fable's advice
`reports/plans/2026-10-31_advice_reuse-next-FABLE.md` (adopted by the lead: C2 now; rehearse the decomposition at tiny

## In plain words

C1b showed that three cheap methods, which need no decomposition, tell a fine-tune that reuses LOW's count from one
that learns a new count, on 21 of 21 states. This plan decomposes LOW with VPD and reads each of the same fine-tunes
with VPD's two readings, setting each reading's cut-off on the same held-out seeds, so VPD lands in the same table.
The decomposition is far too long for 30-minute cluster jobs (at the 14,592 subcomponents kept by the lead, 0.50 s
per step on an L40, about 56 L40-hours for 400K steps; §6), so the run is gated. The user chose the cluster's `normal`
QoS at $0 on 2026-11-02; what remains is the $0 read check of the matrix share and the go-ahead with the measured numbers.

## 1. The request, restated in the Terms' words

- Train **one VPD decomposition of LOW** (the 6-block, width-192 PCFG minGPT) with Goodfire's `param-decomp` at the
  pinned commit, on LOW's own pretraining rows.
- For each C1b state (14 calibration, 21 scored, plus LOW, the two identity-prompt states and the five unlearned N
  states as unscored columns), take its finished update δW of the 24 decomposed matrices, form the **least-squares
  split** state, and read the route split's **re-gating share r** and the **stand-in ratio** on natural `C b 40` rows
  answered with the state's own task (E54/E58's `vpd_read`, unchanged in meaning).
- Calibrate a cut-off per reading on the 14 calibration states with C1b's rule (`revival.cutoff`), commit it, then
  score the 21 states with C1b's rule (binomial bar and best recipe-only labeller + 2), with the **within-cell update
  size** reported beside it (decisions.md 2026-10-31). The outside share is not read here (Not run).
- The framing, from C1b's analysis: on this set VPD can at best **tie** (21 of 21); the informative outcome is a VPD
  miss where every cheap method and the update size are perfect.

## 3. What VPD reads, and by which rule

**The readings** (`src/commute/pcfg_vpd.py`, the same cells as `local_faith.route_split`):
- r = (L01 − L00) / (T1 − T0); nan when |T1 − T0| < 1e-6.
- ratio = (L11 − L00) / (U1 − U0); nan when U1 − U0 < 0.1 (E53's floor, as E58).
- Cells are mean log-odds of the answer at `=` over 1,024 windows (natural `C b 40` rows cut after the answer, the
  answer replaced by the state's task label), computed in chunks of 64 windows and averaged (the gates of 258-token
  windows at 38K subcomponents are ~40 MB per window).

**The cut (amended 2026-11-02, before any trained reading).** Γ is bidirectional, so a label written at index 257 reaches
the gates at `=`. On this truth set every P state is existing and every N state new, so a label effect would line up
with the truth, and no control sees it. So Γ and all forwards read tokens 0 to 256 only, and the label is the readout
token alone (`ci_len = pcfg.ANS`). P and N then read on identical inputs; only δW and the readout token differ. The filed
full window is read beside it as `cells_full`, with `r_full` and `ratio_full`, and a per-state difference over 0.1 in r or
the ratio names the leak and its direction. The filing is `predictions_c2.json` `amended_before_the_read`; Fable's
advice is `reports/plans/2026-11-02_advice_c2-gamma-label-FABLE.md`.

**The update read.** δW = the 24 matrices' weight change only. Embeddings, LayerNorms, biases and the output head
stay at LOW's values in every cell; VPD decomposes no other parameter (as Goodfire's 67M). Reported per state:
**matrix share** = (T1 − T0) / the state's own log-odds gain, and the outside norm share. Risk: task P's "+1" may sit
largely in the head (a shift of number tokens); a state whose matrix share is under 0.5 (filed; the rehearsal measured
0.76 on one P and 0.68 on one N state) is void for VPD. The matrix share is a real number at any stage: T cells are the
target model's, independent of the decomposition's quality.

**The rule.** C1b's, unchanged (`predictions_c1b.json` rules.cutoff, label, score; `revival.cutoff`,
`label_by_cutoff`, `score`, `ceiling`), high = existing for both readings (E54/E58's direction), on the same 14
calibration and 21 scored states; cut-offs to `results/c2_cutoffs.json`, committed before the first scored reading.
The within-cell update-size labeller is reported beside the score (filed C1b rule kept so the table stays comparable).

Outcomes, per reading, exclusive and exhaustive: void, fail, pass_under, tie (21 of 21); the headline is the better
reading's. Filed leans: r void 5%, fail 20%, pass_under 20%, tie 55%; ratio void 10%, fail 20%, pass_under 20%, tie 50%.
A fail on a reading whose positive control does not hold is written "fail, apparatus unverified", and the wrong-label count
stands beside every outcome: every scored N state void gives n 12, agree 12, bar 13, a fail with no wrong label.
Unlearned N states, a stand-alone rule (handled iff at least 4 of 5 are non-void and "new" on a reading): 15%.

**Edge cases run** (tests): zero update → both nan; U1 − U0 at the floor → defined, below or negative → nan; a
zero-window chunk cannot occur; combine is window-weighted. From C1b's `revival.cutoff` (already tested): fewer than 3

`predictions_c2.json` `edge_cases` and `tests/test_revival.py`.

**Blind to.** r and the ratio read the 24 matrices; a fine-tune carried by the head or embeddings is invisible

## 8a. Amendment 2026-11-02: the resume tolerance, set before the noise job runs

The user chose option (a), the cluster's `normal` QoS, on 2026-11-02. Job 47100 left a resumed run 0.018 from an
unbroken one, differing before the resume. `c2.py noise` ($0, one `debug` job, ~15 min) separates the causes, each leg
in a fresh process with the rehearsal's schedule: U1 and U2 unbroken with no evaluation, E with evaluation, R stopped at
200 and resumed to 400 with no evaluation. Measure: largest absolute parameter difference from U1 at step 400; the total
loss differences are reported, not ruled on, since one batch's loss pair can agree by chance.

- **Floor F = U1 vs U2.** F = 0: this card is deterministic here, so E and R must each differ from U1 by exactly 0, and
  any non-zero difference is a bug to find before the scored run.
- F > 0: E and R pass when each is at most 2F from U1. Rule of thumb, not sourced: two independent noisy runs drift apart,
  so one pair is a single draw of that drift, and a factor of 2 allows for its spread. Over 2F: a bug to find.
- **What it is blind to.** A resume error smaller than the GPU noise, which by the same token cannot be told from noise
  in the run itself. Positive control: job 47071's evaluation RNG leak (0.029) is the size of effect the job can see, if
  F comes in well below it. F at or above 0.018 means the job cannot tell a resume error of that size from noise.

**Result (job 48207, L40, 2026-11-02).** F = 0.0203, so two same-seed unbroken runs differ on this card by more than job
47100's resume gap. E is 0.0210 and R is 0.0247 from U1, both under 2F = 0.0407: pass, by the rule above. The same rule's
limit applies, as filed: F is above 0.018, so this job cannot tell a resume error of about that size from noise. The
evidence that resume itself is exact stays the CPU test (fp32, `tests/test_decompose.py`). It also means a scored run is
not reproducible bit for bit, so early-check bounds need a margin rather than the first-steps values themselves.

`c2.py firststeps` ($0, one `debug` job, ~20 min) runs 2,000 steps on the full run's schedules, which the rehearsal's
400-step schedules do not show; the early checks of gate 4 are set from its evals at steps 1,000 and 2,000.

## 9. Departures from the literature

- Goodfire's recipe for a 2-layer width-128 SimpleStories model applied to a 6-block width-192 PCFG minGPT; C scaled
  from the released 67M decomposition per unit rank; their eval list trimmed to numbers.
- The training loop is param-decomp's step called from our loop, for saves and resume (no DDP, no W&B).
- VPD reads the 24 matrices' update; the rest of the fine-tune is held at LOW (Goodfire's 67M fine-tunes in this repo
  changed only those matrices; C1b's are full fine-tunes).
- r and the ratio as E54/E58 define them, on PCFG answers at `=` instead of 67M trigger windows.
- C per rank comes from the 67M and the recipe from SS-2L, so the filed 67M reference numbers come from another recipe:
  importance coefficient 2e-4 against 1e-3, learning rate 5e-5 against 3e-4, importance transformer 2048/8 against 512/4.
- Γ trains on 258 tokens, as Goodfire's trains on whole rows, and reads here on 257 (§3). E54/E58's windows also carried no
  label at the readout, so the cut brings C2 closer to those references; E53's windows, the floor's source, did carry it.
