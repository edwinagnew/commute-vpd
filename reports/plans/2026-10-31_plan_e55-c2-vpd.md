# Plan (E55 C2): VPD scored on the C1b truth set, beside the cheap instruments

**Reuse-lead, 2026-10-31. Plan and rehearsal code only; nothing filed, nothing run.** Parent plan
`reports/plans/2026-10-30_plan_e55-c1b-refined-instruments.md` §8 (C2, adopted). Fable's advice
`reports/plans/2026-10-31_advice_reuse-next-FABLE.md` (adopted by the lead: C2 now; rehearse the decomposition at tiny

## In plain words

C1b showed that three cheap methods, which need no decomposition, tell a fine-tune that reuses LOW's count from one
that learns a new count, on 21 of 21 states. This plan decomposes LOW with VPD and reads each of the same fine-tunes
with VPD's two readings, setting each reading's cut-off on the same held-out seeds, so VPD lands in the same table.
The decomposition is far too long for 30-minute cluster jobs (at the 14,592 subcomponents kept by the lead, 0.45 s
per step on an L40, about 50 L40-hours for 400K steps; §6), so the run is gated: this plan stops at the $0 rehearsal and the user's choice of platform.

## 1. The request, restated in the Terms' words

- Train **one VPD decomposition of LOW** (the 6-block, width-192 PCFG minGPT) with Goodfire's `param-decomp` at the
  pinned commit, on LOW's own pretraining rows.
- For each C1b state (14 calibration, 21 scored, plus LOW, the two identity-prompt states and the five unlearned N
  states as unscored columns), take its finished update δW of the 24 decomposed matrices, form the **least-squares
  split** state, and read the route split's **re-gating share r** and the **stand-in ratio** on natural `C b 40` rows
  answered with the state's own task (E54/E58's `vpd_read`, unchanged in meaning).
- Calibrate a cut-off per reading on the 14 calibration states with C1b's rule (`revival.cutoff`), commit it, then
  score the 21 states with C1b's rule (binomial bar and best recipe-only labeller + 2), with the **within-cell update
  size** reported beside it (decisions.md 2026-10-31). The outside share is not read here (Not run).
- The framing, from C1b's analysis: on this set VPD can at best **tie** (21 of 21); the informative outcome is a VPD
  miss where every cheap method and the update size are perfect.

## 3. What VPD reads, and by which rule

**The readings** (`src/commute/pcfg_vpd.py`, the same cells as `local_faith.route_split`):
- r = (L01 − L00) / (T1 − T0); nan when |T1 − T0| < 1e-6.
- ratio = (L11 − L00) / (U1 − U0); nan when U1 − U0 < 0.1 (E53's floor, as E58).
- Cells are mean log-odds of the answer at `=` over 1,024 windows (natural `C b 40` rows cut after the answer, the
  answer replaced by the state's task label), computed in chunks of 64 windows and averaged (the gates of 258-token
  windows at 38K subcomponents are ~40 MB per window).

**The update read.** δW = the 24 matrices' weight change only. Embeddings, LayerNorms, biases and the output head
stay at LOW's values in every cell; VPD decomposes no other parameter (as Goodfire's 67M). Reported per state:
**matrix share** = (T1 − T0) / the state's own log-odds gain, and the outside norm share. Risk: task P's "+1" may sit
largely in the head (a shift of number tokens); a state whose matrix share is under 0.5 (proposed, filed after the
rehearsal measures it on one P and one N state) is void for VPD. The rehearsal's matrix share is a real number: T
cells are the target model's, independent of the decomposition's quality.

**The rule.** C1b's, unchanged (`predictions_c1b.json` rules.cutoff, label, score; `revival.cutoff`,
`label_by_cutoff`, `score`, `ceiling`), high = existing for both readings (E54/E58's direction), on the same 14
calibration and 21 scored states; cut-offs to `results/c2_cutoffs.json`, committed before the first scored reading.
The within-cell update-size labeller is reported beside the score (filed C1b rule kept so the table stays comparable).

## 6. Stages, memory, time, cost: it does not fit `debug`, so it is gated

**Measured (rehearsal jobs 46971 and 47013, L40, 2026-10-31).**

| C | subcomponents | step time | peak GPU | training checkpoint | 400K steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SS-2L per rank (dropped) | 38,016 | 1.19 s | 46.3 of 47.3 GiB; OOM after the step-400 save | 8.36 GB (save 28-30 s, load 31-34 s) | ~130 L40-h |
| 67M per rank (**the config**) | 14,592 | 0.45 s | 18.6 GiB | 3.35 GB (save 12 s) | **~50 L40-h** |

My FLOP estimate (26-32 L40-h at 38,016) was 4-5 times too low; small matmuls are not compute-bound. The lead
dropped 38,016 (lead, 2026-10-31): it does not fit an L40, and an H100 run would cost over the cap. **Why memory rose
after the save** (read from the code, not yet measured): the save cloned the persistent-PGD sources on the GPU (+2.4 GB
at 38,016), the last step's gradients stayed allocated, and the final eval runs at batch 128, twice the training
batch. The sources now go to CPU when saved, gradients are freed after each step, and `decompose.release` plus
reading files to CPU fixed the first rehearsal's resume OOM (job 47013: 41.1 GiB after the reload, at 38,016).

| stage | work | where | time | memory | saves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R rehearsal | readings on an untrained decomposition (matrix share, outside norm share, do-nothing cells, T0 − LOW: target-model numbers, real); run A to 200 and save; fresh process: A resumed to 400, B unbroken to 400, comparison, alive count | `debug`, 1 job | ~15 min (800 steps × 0.45 s, warm-ups, saves, readings) | 18.6 GiB GPU, host ~2 GiB + ~2 GB of gates per chunk pair (24G asked) | `results/c2_rehearsal.json` after each part; `large/c2_rehearsal/A_final.pt` |

**Fit to `debug`.** No: ~140 resubmissions of 22-minute jobs. A run over 3 h is gated even at $0 (`.claude/CLAUDE.md`).

**Options for the user** (gate 6, with the rehearsal's numbers):
- **(a) 14,592 on the cluster's `normal` QoS, one L40:** ~50 h as three ≤ 24-h jobs, each resuming from the saved
  file (`normal`'s walltime limit is 24 h); $0. Needs the 2026-10-27 "Qwen line only" rule for `normal` relaxed, and an
  empty queue (one node of 8 L40s shared by every fellow). Memory headroom ~28 GiB.
- **(b) 14,592 on a RunPod H100:** assuming an H100 runs this 2-3.5 times faster than an L40 (bf16 peak is ~5.5 times,
  memory bandwidth ~3.9 times; these small matmuls are bandwidth- and latency-bound, so I take the lower ratios),
  ~14-25 h at $4-5.5/h all in (this repo's E34 and E37 pods) ≈ **$57-140**, central ~$90 at 2.5 times: over the $40
  session cap, so it needs the user's explicit approval. Not measured on an H100; a 10-minute first stage would measure
  it, with a stop.
- **(c) Fewer steps than 400K: not supported by a source for this recipe.** Every LM config in the pinned
  param-decomp uses 400K (`ss_llama_simple_mlp-2L`, `pile_llama_simple_mlp-4L` and `-12L` yamls, `steps: 400000`). The
  only shorter LM config, `param_decomp/experiments/lm/gpt2_config.yaml` (`steps: 50_000`), is an older,
  unregistered stack (batch 2, layerwise CI function, different losses), not this recipe at fewer steps. The SPD paper
  (`papers/Stochastic_Parameter_Decomposition/spd_paper.md`) trains toy models only, at 10K-50K steps. A shorter run
  is a departure that needs a measured stopping rule (e.g. the early checks' losses and L0 flat over a filed window),
  which would itself need a long run to set.
- **(d) Drop C2.** C1b already showed the set cannot rank VPD above the cheap methods (at best a tie); the open-pair
  and Qwen lines are where a VPD-versus-label disagreement exists. The brief's "score VPD against a ground truth" item
  stays open.

My recommendation: (a) if the user relaxes the `normal` rule and the queue is empty; otherwise (d) rather than (b),
since on this set VPD can at best tie.

## 8. The rehearsal ($0, one `debug` job)

Command (pod-runner, clone root): `sbatch --job-name=commute-e55-c2-rehearse cluster/pcfg_c2.sbatch rehearse`.
At the config's 14,592 subcomponents and 400 steps; `results/c2_rehearsal.json` is rewritten after each part and
records a CUDA OOM with the part it hit. In order: (1) readings on an untrained decomposition, i.e. the target-model
numbers first: the matrix share and outside norm share of one P and one N calibration state at 10K, the do-nothing
cells (must be exactly equal), T0 − LOW, plus per-state reading seconds and the setup cost (alive set, bases); r and
the ratio here only exercise the code. (2) Run A to step 200, its save (size, seconds, GPU left after release). Then
a fresh child process (`c2.py rehearse-resume`), as a resubmitted job: (3) A resumed to 400 (load seconds, step time,
peak GPU, eval at 400), (4) B unbroken to 400 and the comparison (max parameter difference, last total loss), (5) the
alive count of A's result. Pass: the job finishes, the do-nothing gain is 0, and the resumed run's last loss is within
bf16 noise of the unbroken one (reported; no filed numbers, never scored).

## 9. Departures from the literature

- Goodfire's recipe for a 2-layer width-128 SimpleStories model applied to a 6-block width-192 PCFG minGPT; C scaled
  from the released 67M decomposition per unit rank; their eval list trimmed to numbers.
- The training loop is param-decomp's step called from our loop, for saves and resume (no DDP, no W&B).
- VPD reads the 24 matrices' update; the rest of the fine-tune is held at LOW (Goodfire's 67M fine-tunes in this repo
  changed only those matrices; C1b's are full fine-tunes).
- r and the ratio as E54/E58 define them, on PCFG answers at `=` instead of 67M trigger windows.
