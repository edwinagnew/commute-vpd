# E100 Amendment 1: does the sum of single-part reverts tell fed from written on fresh seeds?

*mechanism-detection-lead, 2026-11-05. The first of E100's two amendments. Debug QoS, $0, under the gate. Status: filed before the run; the rule was found post hoc on seeds already read, so this amendment's whole point is to test it on seeds not yet read.*

**Limit on every claim.** A new circuit built from old parts still reads as reuse.

## What E100 read (jobs 52839, 52873)

- **Filed outcome: "partial".** The plants read exactly, and the poles fall where built: E99's forced copy reads written (ratio 1), its forbidden copy fed (0), E62's copy fed at (2,4) (0.014-0.021), E53's cipher written at mlp3 (0.72-0.73). But every other task is read at its top carry part, which is mlp3 on all of them, and there every key-table task reads "mixed" at a ratio of 0.21-0.51. Copy-24 and cipher-24 fall on the same side, which is the plan's branch "the column does not track the construction labels". The filed per-part column is not usable as it stands.
- **Post hoc, unfiled: the sum of single-part reverts sorts the tasks by construction.** It is the share of the gain removed by reverting each of the 28 parts alone, summed. A gain fed through unchanged machinery needs many small changes together, so no single revert removes much (the singles sum low). A gain written into parts comes apart piece by piece (they sum high).

This was found after looking, so it is a hypothesis, not a result.

## The test

**Filed rule, set from the seeds above before any new seed is read.** "fed" if the sum is at most 0.40 (the largest copy row); "written" if at least 0.62 (the smallest row new by construction); "between" otherwise.

**Fresh seeds: E58's four open-pair maps at seeds 3-7** (`open_pair_instruments/results/large/{copy,cipher,rank1,elicit}_s{3..7}.pt`; recipe as seeds 0-2; never read by E100). Leans: copy fed on all five seeds; cipher written on all five; rank-1 written (as seeds 0-2); half-known between.

**Pass:** copy fed and cipher written on at least 9 of the 10 seeds. **Fail:** fewer; the sum is then reported as a description of seeds 0-2 only.

**Control first:** stage 0 re-reads E62 copy seed 0's sum of single reverts against the saved 0.262 within 1e-3 and stops on a miss.

**Edge cases:** a gain at or below 1 nat reads "no gain"; a sum past 1 counts; a seed at exactly a bar takes the bar's side.

## Cost and output

One debug job: 20 states × 28 reverts at about 8 s per E62-sized state (`experiments/fed_or_written/results/run.out`), about 5 min. Output `results/amend1.json`, rows added to `table.json` as `reverts_sum_reading`.
