## The request, in the Terms' words

After a narrow fine-tune, the full fine-tuned model loses more of its gain at the trigger without the recruited set S
than the original model does (Amendment 2: D = 0.23-0.25 on emoticon, 0.12-0.19 on ` which`). But the ` http` null's
set reads 0.10-0.14 and the busy set 0.78-1.10, so the extra loss may be a generic effect of the fine-tune on
whatever is active at the trigger. The restore test asks directly: in the fine-tuned model, put a set's outputs at the
trigger back to what they were in the original model; how much of the gain goes? S is specific if restoring it takes
back more of the gain than restoring size-matched sets of pieces that were already active at the trigger in W0.

## /design check (short)

1. **Goal.** Q5 (re-gating: what the switched-on set did). Decides whether the set Γ switches on is a privileged
   description of where the fine-tune acts, or one of many active pieces that carry its change.
2. **Tried.** E38 (all-position removal), Amendment 2 (trigger-only removal, difference against W0). Removal mixes a
   set's prior push with its change; restore isolates the change.
3. **Null.** Restoring S takes back no more of the gain than restoring matched W0-active sets ("not specific").
4. **Separating predictions.** P9 (S against 20 matched active sets and null\S); P10 (S against 20 random sets).
5. **Cost.** One cluster `debug` job, ≤ 15 min, $0, on Amendment 2's cached states (no fine-tuning).

## Definitions

- For each decomposed matrix k and subcomponent c with rank-1 matrix u_c v_c^T: its *output* at the trigger on a
  window is (x·v_c) u_c, with x the matrix's input at the trigger.
- **Restoring X** in the fine-tuned model W1 = W0 + dW: one forward pass at W1 in which, at the trigger position only,
  each matrix's output gains Σ_{c∈X} ((x0 − x)·v_c) u_c, where x is its input in this pass and x0 its input at the
  trigger in W0's pass on the same window (cached). Every other position runs W1 unchanged. dW's own output stays.
- **Restored share** ρ_r(X) = (T1 − lo(W1, X restored)) / G, G = T1 − T0 of the state.
- **Sets** per task-seed state: S; null\S (the ` http` state's recruited set minus S); 20 random sets
  `recruit.random_like(S, alive, i)`, i = 0..19; 20 *active* sets: per layer, as many pieces as S has in that layer,
  drawn (seed i) from the alive pieces not in S whose W0 mean trigger importance `task.m0` ≥ 0.5 (≥ 0.2 for a state
  where some layer's pool at 0.5 is smaller than S's count there; recorded).

## Order (control first, one job)

Controls, on every state, stop the job on any fail: R0 restoring the empty set returns T1 (|Δ| ≤ 1e-5); R1 restoring
S in a W0 pass (x = x0) returns T0 (|Δ| ≤ 1e-4); R2 the positive control, every decomposed matrix's whole trigger
output replaced by its W0 output, returns T0 (|lo − T0| ≤ 1e-2, i.e. ρ ≈ 1); R3 restoring S leaves every position before the
trigger unchanged (max |Δlogit| ≤ 1e-4). Then the grid → `results/amend3.json`, written after each state.

## Decision tree

- **"specific"** (P9 'specific' on ≥ 2 tasks): S carries the fine-tune's change beyond the pieces already active at
  the trigger. Write into the E38 card and Q5; pending card for the upstream step (what moved the trigger's inputs).
- **"not specific"** (≥ 2 tasks 'not'): the fine-tune's change at the trigger flows through active pieces generally;
  the switched-on set is not a privileged description. Write into Q5; pending card proposing to stop the re-gating
  line on the 67M model or carry the question to the Qwen line.
- **Outside the tree**: card with the numbers; no further runs.

## What each number is blind to

- Restore acts at the trigger only: changes routed through earlier positions are not read (they cannot change S's
  trigger input except through attention, which the restore does not touch).
- Matched active sets are matched on layer and count, not on how much their outputs changed.
- Restoring a set also undoes downstream effects of its change, so shares of different sets need not add.

## Not run

Matching on output change; restore at all positions; upstream routing; other tasks, the larger model.
