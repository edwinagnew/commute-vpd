# Plan: does a pass test on Qwen fail bad placements, and how do simple splits of E40's update read on it? (E44, Q0 + Q1)

*2026-10-28, qwen-decomp lead. Q0 and Q1 of `reports/plans/2026-10-28_plan_qwen-line-replan.md`, one run, one H100 in
US-MO-1, under $10. Approved by the user with Fable's changes (`reports/reviews/2026-10-28_review_e44-plan-fable-advice.md`).
Words as in `GOALS.md` → Terms. Experiment directory: `experiments/qwen_maps/`. Where this plan and the re-plan
disagree, the re-plan holds and the line says so.*

**In plain words.** We take the weight changes E40 saved after 1 and 10 steps. We write each into the subcomponents
in a few simple ways. Each result gets cheap checks against the unchanged decomposition on the same text. Three
deliberately bad placements must fail those checks first, or we stop. This update is a large format change. So the
run tests the checks; the evidence for the weak version comes from Q2's updates.

## 1. The question, in the Terms' words, and the design check

**Restated.** For E40's no-warm-up gold-SFT update δW at steps 1 and 10, and at step 100 only if a split passes at
step 10: (a) do the readings below fail three controls? These are a split onto the subcomponents Γ rarely gates on for
maths text, read against the same split onto those it often gates on; a random change to the alive (U, V) with the
same input-weighted effect as δW; and do-nothing. (b) If they do, how do the least-squares split, the least-squares
split without its outside-span part, and the scaled adjoint split read, and is a least-squares fail the span's or the
pieces'? Plus the free check of how much of each matrix's space the alive U and V span.

**Which update, and how to read it.** The files are E40's run (Adam 1e-5, no warm-up, batch 64): `run.log` saved them
to `/workspace/e40/dW/step_{1,10,100}.safetensors` (bf16) and `step_100_fp32.safetensors` on volume `ydavm7ct4x`. The
name `/workspace/e40_nowarmup/` in E40 Amendment 1 belongs to the H200 run that never ran. KL to start, KL(W₀ ‖
fine-tuned) on maths rows, is 0.106 at step 1, 0.52 at step 10 and 0.66 at step 100; the answers halved in length and
accuracy fell. Following the re-plan (Q1 "Expected"), nothing here is a prediction on whether a split passes: this is a
shakedown of the scoring on real δWs, not evidence for D1. No reading of this update is labelled elicitation or new.

**Step 1 is not a small real update.** Without warm-up, Adam's first step moves every weight by about the full rate
times the sign of its gradient. δW at step 1 is close to a dense sign matrix, flat in spectrum, unlike a later or
warmed-up step. Its readings calibrate the checks at a tenth of step 10's KL; they say nothing about how small
post-training updates sit in the subcomponents. Q2's warm-up update is the small point.

**Corrections and limits, from the files.**
1. *Spans are not complete for attention.* Alive counts per matrix (E40's saved importance statistics, total 494,598,
   matching `alive.safetensors`): q median 534 of 2048, k 463 of 1024, v 795 of 1024, o 1497 of 2048; gate 5204, up
   5646, down 5862 of 6144. The k and v read sides span at most half of 2048 inputs. The least-squares split is then
   exact only in the MLP matrices. Where it is exact its unmasked model equals the fine-tuned one, which is a
   triviality (re-plan §3A): only the masks can tell a good split from a bad one.
2. *Do-nothing and r1.* The re-plan says do-nothing passes r1 and r3 by construction. Under this code it cannot on
   maths rows: CI masks run with Δ off (`qwen_teacher.importance_pass`, as E40's G), so the masked model drops δW.
   The plan follows the re-plan on its role, a control and not a competitor, and reports its r1 as measured.
3. *W₀ already fails on maths text:* adversary 4.24 against the 1.5 line. Every result is a ratio to W₀ on the same
   rows, and "faithful" here means no worse than a decomposition that is itself unfaithful on this text.

## 2. States, each an operation

Per matrix, W = U Vᵀ + Δ over alive columns; Γ, Δ₀ and dead columns frozen. Splits use `split.py` in theory layout,
float64, Σ = the input second moment on maths rows at W₀. Each state's change to Δ is reported as ‖Δ′ − Δ₀‖_Σ /
‖δW‖_Σ, pooled as a ratio of sums over matrices.

| state | operation | weights run | Δ moves by | steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W₀ | the unchanged decomposition | W₀ | 0 | — |
| do-nothing | δW into Δ | W₀ + δW | δW | 1, 10 (100) |
| least squares | `split.split_state`: least-norm (dU, dV) | W₀ + δW | O(δW) − dU dVᵀ | 1, 10 (100) |
| in-span least squares | the same pieces, without O(δW) | W₀ + δW − O(δW) | −dU dVᵀ | 1, 10 (100) |
| scaled adjoint | `split.adjoint_state(dW, uv, Σ)` as returned and as E16, E22 ran it: pieces c·(δW V, δWᵀ U) | W₀ + c K(δW) | −c² δW V Uᵀ δW | 1, 10 (100) |
| control (i) lower | least squares restricted to each matrix's alive subcomponents in the lower half by share of maths positions with g > 0.01, ties by mean g | W₀ + δW | δW − the half's dσ − dU dVᵀ | 1, 10 |
| control (i) upper | the same, upper half | W₀ + δW | as above | 1, 10 |
| control (ii) random | per matrix, Gaussian (dU, dV) on the alive columns, seed 0, scaled so ‖(dU Vᵀ + U dVᵀ) Σ^½‖ = ‖δW Σ^½‖ | W₀ + dU Vᵀ + U dVᵀ + dU dVᵀ | 0 | 1, 10 |

"(100)": only if a split passes at step 10 (Fable), from the fp32 file. Control (i) uses halves because only 21,892
alive subcomponents never pass 0.01 on maths rows, none in some o and v matrices; the halves match in size, so what
separates them is how often Γ switches them on. Each half's Σ-weighted piece energy is reported. Control (i) tests
misplacement; control (ii) rising shows "a different model", not an unfaithful split (re-plan Q0). The in-span state
separates a span-limit fail from a pieces fail: teacher training's offsets sit on the same alive columns and cannot
express O(δW) either. Its r2 tracks least squares' r2 wherever O(δW) is small.

## 3. The readings

Rows: E40's 128 maths rows (`maths_rows.json`, the unchanged model's own GSM8K test answers) and the adversary run's
128 FineWeb-Edu rows. Each reading is reported in nats and as a ratio to W₀ on the same rows. Γ recomputes g on each
state's own activations and its own V.

| reading | definition | W₀ | blind to | noise floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1 CI | CI-masked KL (m = g, Δ off) against the state's own unmasked model ÷ W₀'s (maths 0.0589, web 0.1417) | 1 | parts the masks keep on; a map that drops the update | W₀ against E40's values (E40a1: 3e-10 relative) |
| r1 stoch | stochastic-masked KL (`qwen_vpd.stochastic_masks`), same seed for every state; reported, not scored (Fable) | 1 | the same, plus mask draws | spread over seeds 0-2 at W₀ |
| r2 kept | [K(D) − K(D′)] / [K(D) − K₀] on maths rows; K(X) = KL(fine-tuned ‖ CI-masked X); K(D) uses the unchanged decomposition with Γ on W₀'s activations, read per step in stage D; K₀ = 0.0589 | 0 by definition | behaviour off maths rows; sampled answers | below |
| r3 paired | KL of D′ under W₀'s saved adversary sources ÷ W₀'s KL under them; web: mean of three seeds' ratios, maths: one seed | 1 | everything but W₀'s weak points: weak points δW creates are not seen (re-plan Q0) | replay against saved values |
| accuracy | unmasked greedy GSM8K accuracy, first 256 test problems, strict, drop rule; adjoint and in-span states and the fine-tuned model at each read step; reported | W₀ from E40's `step0_test.jsonl` | masked behaviour | binomial, ±0.03 |

**r2's noise floor (re-plan Q0).** W₀ re-masked across stochastic seeds 0-2 gives a spread in nats on maths rows. r2 is
unscored wherever K(D) − K₀ is below the larger of 0.05 nats and five times that spread. Step 1's K(D) − K₀ is likely
near 0.1, so step 1's r2 may be unscored; that is reported, not worked around.

**When r2 adds anything.** For a state whose unmasked model is the fine-tuned one (do-nothing, least squares, both
control (i) halves), K(D′) = r1·K₀, so r2 = (K(D) − r1·K₀)/(K(D) − K₀). Then r2 ≥ 0.8 needs only r1 ≤ 0.8 + 0.2·K(D)/K₀,
about 2.7 at step 10, so r2 never decides the pass there. It binds for the adjoint split, the in-span state and
control (ii), where it fails a map that ignores δW. CI masks run with Δ off, so neither the old nor the new Δ enters
r2: do-nothing reads about 0 and fails it, which the re-plan's control (iii) requires.

**Pass rule (a measurement rule, not a prediction).** r1 CI ≤ 1.5 on both row sets, r2 ≥ 0.8 where scored, r3 ≤ 1.5 on
both. A state with r2 unscored reads "incomplete".

**Saved sources exist.** `experiments/qwen_sft/results/large/nowarmup/sources/`: web seeds 0, 1, 2 (`G_fineweb_seed0`,
`W0_fineweb_seed{1,2}`, KL 1.7032, 1.4365, 1.7472) and maths seed 0 (`G_maths_seed0`, 4.2351), about 3 MB each.

**Expected values for the controls, filed before code** (step 10; r2 derived from r1 with K(D) = 0.55, an estimate
stage D replaces). Do-nothing's maths r3 has no E40 reference: E40 read do-nothing on web rows only.

| reading | do-nothing 1 / 10 | (i) lower | (i) upper | (ii) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1 CI maths | 2-3.5 / 8-11 | 5-12 | 2-6 | 1.5-5 |
| r1 CI web | 1.0-1.5 / 1.0-2.0 | 1.0-2.0 | 1.0-2.0 | 1.5-5 |
| r2 kept | about 0 / about 0 | −0.32 to 0.52 | 0.40-0.88 | < 0 |
| r3 web | 1.0-1.2 / 1.0-1.3 | 1.0-1.5 | 1.0-1.5 | > 1.5 |
| r3 maths | no reference | 1.1-1.5 | 1.2-2 | > 1.5 |

**The free check (stage S).** Per matrix: r_u / d_out and r_v / d_in (rank of the alive U and V at tolerance 1e-6); the
null (1 − r_u/d_out)(1 − r_v/d_in); δW's input-weighted outside-both share at steps 1 and 10, per matrix and pooled as
a ratio of sums weighted by E‖δW a‖²; the share of the energy of δW's top singular directions (those carrying 90%)
inside col U and col V; ‖dU dVᵀ‖_Σ / ‖δW‖_Σ of the least-squares pieces; `split.dsigma_smin`. All reported.

**Placement arrays.** Per split and step, per-subcomponent Σ-weighted piece energies and the same for
`split.random_like(δW, Σ, 0)`, saved for Q3. The least-norm split spreads any δW, so only the excess over that null
means anything (re-plan §3A). No prediction (Fable; re-plan §7).

## 5. Predictions (for `experiments/qwen_maps/results/predictions.json`)

Only the apparatus and the controls carry predictions; the splits are reported against the pass rule (re-plan Q1).
Thresholds are absolute, ≤ and ≥ inclusive as written; NaN or a missing value is a miss; no re-reads.

1. **control_W0 (stage C, stop rule).** r1 CI at W₀ within 1% of 0.14167 (web) and 0.05886 (maths). Each replayed
   source within 1% of its saved KL: two H100 runs of the whole search read 0.09% apart, and a replay is one forward.
   KL to start as E40 read it from live fp32 weights: step 100 from the fp32 file within 1% of 0.66369; the step-100
   bf16 file gives the gap g between files; steps 1 and 10, bf16 only, within 1% + 3g of 0.10621 and 0.52040. Miss →
   stop. Edge: a source file absent → miss.
2. **teeth_i, per step.** r2 of the lower half ≤ r2 of the upper half − 0.2, about 1.7 in r1 at step 10. Scored only if
   the halves' Δ-change shares are within 0.1 of each other, else "confounded by the span", which counts as failing to
   fail. Lean medium-high. Edge: r2 unscored at that step → compare r1 CI maths, lower ≥ upper + 0.2·(K(D) − K₀)/K₀, the same margin in r1 units; a matrix with
   fewer than two alive subcomponents gives both halves nothing.
3. **teeth_ii, per step.** Control (ii): the largest of r1 CI maths, r1 CI web, r3 maths, r3 web > 1.5. Lean medium.
4. **teeth_iii, per step.** Do-nothing: r2 < 0.8, or r1 CI maths > 1.5 where r2 is unscored. Lean high: Δ off drops the
   update. Its r3 web ≤ 1.5 at step 10 is also predicted (lean high, E40 read 1.18).
5. **teeth outcome.** The re-plan: a control that fails to fail means no teeth. Step 10 without all three → stop
   before stage M. Step 10 with teeth and step 1 without → step 1's splits reported unscored.

The code writer runs each rule on an empty pool, a zero denominator, ties at every line, a NaN, and a state with a
missing reading, and files the result in `predictions.json` before the pod starts.

## 6. Decision tree (each branch approved with this plan)

- **control_W0 misses** → stop; fix; rerun the stage (class 2).
- **No teeth at step 10** → the line stops; card to the user on the pass criterion; splits not scored.
- **A split passes at 10** → step 100 for it; the scoring works on a real δW. D1 is still scored on Q2's warmed-up
  updates; the line goes to Q2 with that split in the table.
- **Least squares fails at 10, in-span passes** → the span, not the pieces. Teacher training (Q4) cannot fix it.
  Card to the user: subcomponents for the outside span (revive dead columns or add new ones), with the free check's
  per-matrix shares.
- **Every split fails at 10** → unattributed: brittleness, re-gating and a new mechanism all produce it, and a failed
  split is never on its own evidence of a new mechanism (re-plan §7). Next: Q2's own-samples update through this
  table, then Q3's rows. Q4 only after a small, warmed-up update fails and Q3's rows on it are read (Fable; re-plan).
- **Passes at 1, fails at 10** → the step sweep's first point, read with step 1's first-step artefact in mind.
- **The fresh search reads > 1.5 × W₀ where the replay passed** → the replay is blind for that state; a card asks
  whether the per-experiment search becomes the per-state test.

## Amendment record (2026-10-28, stored_error stop and fix)
The paid H100 run (US-MO-1, $3.99/h, $1.27 spent with set-up) passed C (control_W0 holds, `results/C.json`) and stopped
in S at 744 s on the filed rule `rules.early.stored_error` (`results/stopped.json`): layers.2.mlp.up_proj, step 1, the
lower half's stored X missed float64 by 3.82e-3 of ‖δW‖_Σ against 1e-3. A $0 diagnostic (`diag_stored.py`, cluster
job 41552, `results/diag_stored.jsonl`: 196 sites × δW at steps 2 and 100 × three splits, 1176 rows) found 7 rows over
the limit, all the lower half on mlp.up_proj of layers 2, 3, 4 and 9 (max 4.17e-3, layer 2, step 100), where dσ's
condition κ reaches 6.3e5. The cause is forming the pieces X V_A, Xᵀ U_A in fp32: with X stored fp32 but the pieces
formed in float64 (`err_x64`) the maximum over all rows is 1.76e-4, and rounding float64-formed pieces to fp32 adds
about 1e-6 (`err_pieces32`, max 1.3e-6). Fix (code commit below): X stays stored fp32; `qwen_maps.pieces` forms every
applied piece in float64 from the stored X and rounds it to fp32, the components' dtype; `site_change` builds the
least-squares, in-span and half states from it, with the in-span weight change dσ of those rounded pieces, in float64
(`Job.change` hands it float64 δW and columns; `applied` casts to the forward's fp32); `stored_error` measures the stored
pieces' precision as applied, against the all-float64 pieces. The fp32 forward's own floor (the in-place add V + dV in
fp32, and fl32(W0 + δW) for the fine-tuned weights), about eps·‖W‖ relative to ‖δW‖, is excluded, is common to every
state including do-nothing, and is not under the 1e-3 rule (reviewer's check: 9.5e-4 at δW = 4e-5 of ‖W‖ in a toy case). Expected new maximum ≈ 1.8e-4 (steps 1 and 10 were not in the
diagnostic; the relative error does not depend on δW's size, and steps 2 and 100 agree within 10%). The filed rule,
its text and STORE_ERR_MAX = 1e-3 are unchanged. The rerun reads δW from `/workspace/e40_nowarmup` (the volume has no
`/workspace/e40`); `RUN_BRIEF.md` carries the rerun's steps.
