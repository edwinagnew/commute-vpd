# Plan: does a pass test on Qwen fail a bad map, and do the least-squares and adjoint splits of a real update pass it? (E44, Q0 + Q1)

*2026-10-28, qwen-decomp lead. Q0 and Q1 of `reports/plans/2026-10-28_plan_qwen-line-replan.md`, one run, one H100 in
US-MO-1, under $10. Words as in `GOALS.md` → Terms. Experiment directory: `experiments/qwen_maps/`.*

**In plain words.** We take the weight changes E40 saved after 10 and 100 steps and write each into the
subcomponents in three simple ways. Each result gets three cheap checks against the unchanged decomposition on the
same text. Two deliberately bad placements must fail those checks first, or we stop. The answer says whether a simple
map keeps the decomposition faithful for this update. If none does, teacher training is still needed for the weak
version.

## 1. The question, in the Terms' words, and the design check

**Restated.** For E40's no-warm-up gold-SFT update δW at steps 10 and 100: (a) do the three readings below fail two
positive controls, a split onto subcomponents Γ rarely gates on for maths text and a random change to the alive (U, V)
of the least-squares split's size? (b) If they do, do the least-squares split and the scaled adjoint split of δW pass
them, with do-nothing as the reference? Plus the free check of how much of each matrix's space the alive U and V span.

**The update read is not a small one.** The files are E40's run (Adam 1e-5, no warm-up, batch 64): `run.log` saved
them to `/workspace/e40/dW/step_{10,100}.safetensors` (bf16) and `step_100_fp32.safetensors` on volume `ydavm7ct4x`.
The name `/workspace/e40_nowarmup/` in E40 Amendment 1 belongs to the H200 run that never ran; locally, E40's small
files sit in `results/large/nowarmup/`. This update changed the answer format: KL to start on maths rows is 0.52 nats
per token at step 10 and 0.66 at step 100, length halved, accuracy fell from 0.788 to 0.627. On the 67M, splits of
large updates failed where small ones passed (E16, E22). So a fail here is a result about a large format change; a
pass would be strong. Q2's own-samples update goes through the same table later.

**Two corrections to the re-plan, from the files.**
1. *The spans are not complete for attention.* Alive counts per matrix (from E40's saved importance statistics, total
   494,598, matching `alive.safetensors`): q median 534 of 2048, k 463 of 1024, v 795 of 1024, o 1497 of 2048; gate
   5204, up 5646, down 5862 of 6144. The k and v read sides can span at most half of 2048 inputs. So the least-squares
   split is near exact in the MLP matrices and leaves a large remainder in Δ in q and k. The free check measures it.
2. *Do-nothing cannot pass reading 1 on maths rows.* The CI-masked forward runs with Δ off (`qwen_teacher.importance_pass`,
   as in E40's G). Do-nothing puts δW in Δ, so its masked model drops the whole update and reads near 10 times W₀ on
   maths rows. It is the trivial map, and this reading already fails it.

**Design check (`/design`).**
- *Goal:* the weak version on Qwen: is D′ faithful to W₀ + δW with Δ not absorbing δW, by a test that can fail.
- *Nearest tried:* E16 and E22 on the 67M; least squares failed the adversary on the large fine-tune (6.96 at step 10),
  adjoint passed and kept part of the behaviour. E40: do-nothing passed the web adversary at 1.18. New: the first maps
  on Qwen, and readings checked against controls before any map is scored.
- *Patch or step:* a step: the re-plan's D1 deliverable.
- *Decisions:* pass line 1.5 for ratios (decision 2026-10-27; maths line user-accepted in E40a1); behaviour-kept line 0.8
  (E40 P3); Γ and Δ frozen; scaled adjoint split (user, 2026-10-20). The paired adversary replaces the per-state search
  (user, 2026-10-28, answer 1). No departure.
- *What changes next:* section 6. No-teeth stops the line for the user.
- *Control first:* stage C, W₀ alone, then the two positive controls before any map.
- *Class:* a confirmed plan item (re-plan §6, §8); no sign-off beyond the user seeing this plan.

## 2. States (arms), each an operation

Per matrix, W = U Vᵀ + Δ over alive columns; Γ, Δ₀ and dead columns frozen. Splits use `split.py` in theory layout,
float64, Σ = the input second moment on maths rows at W₀.

| state | operation | weights the model runs | steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| W₀ | the unchanged decomposition | W₀ | — |
| do-nothing | δW into Δ, (U, V) unchanged | W₀ + δW | 10, 100 |
| least-squares split | `split.split_state`: least-norm (dU, dV), remainder O(δW) − dU dVᵀ into Δ | W₀ + δW | 10, 100 |
| scaled adjoint split | `split.adjoint_state(dW, uv, Σ)`: c·(δW V, δWᵀ U), Δ fixed | W₀ + c K(δW) | 10, 100 |
| control (i), rarely-on split | least-squares split restricted to each matrix's alive subcomponents in the lower half by share of maths positions with g > 0.01 (ties by mean g); remainder into Δ | W₀ + δW | 10 |
| control (ii), random change | per matrix, Gaussian (dU, dV) on the alive columns, seed 0, with ‖dU‖ and ‖dV‖ equal to the least-squares split's; Δ fixed | W₀ + dU Vᵀ + U dVᵀ + dU dVᵀ | 10 |

Control (i) uses the lower half, not "never on": only 21,892 alive subcomponents never pass 0.01 on maths rows, none in
some o and v matrices, so that set would be do-nothing in disguise. δW at step 100 is read from the fp32 file.

## 3. The readings

Rows: E40's 128 maths rows (`maths_rows.json`, the unchanged model's own GSM8K test answers) and the adversary run's
128 FineWeb-Edu rows. Every reading is a ratio to W₀ on the same rows. Γ recomputes g on each state's own
activations and its own V.

| reading | definition | W₀ | blind to | noise floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1 CI | CI-masked KL (m = g, Δ off) against the state's own unmasked model ÷ W₀'s (maths 0.0589, web 0.1417) | 1 | parts the masks keep on; a map that drops the update | W₀ against E40's values (E40a1: 3e-10 relative) |
| r1 stoch | stochastic-masked KL (`qwen_vpd.stochastic_masks`, Δ mask U(0,1)), same seed for every state, ÷ W₀'s | 1 | the same, plus mask draws | spread of seeds 0-2 at W₀, stage C |
| r2 kept | [K(D) − K(D′)] / [K(D) − K₀], K(X) = KL(fine-tuned model ‖ CI-masked X) on maths rows, K₀ = W₀'s 0.0589 | 0 by definition (drops the update) | behaviour off maths rows; sampled answers | deterministic; denominator ≈ 0.5 at step 10 |
| r3 paired | KL of D′ under W₀'s saved adversary sources ÷ W₀'s KL under the same sources; web: mean of three seeds' ratios, maths: one seed | 1 | weak points D′ creates that W₀'s sources miss | replay against saved values; per-seed spread |

r2 reads 1 when the masked D′ is as close to the fine-tuned model as the masked D is to W₀. For maps whose unmasked
model is the fine-tuned one, r2 is a rescaling of r1 CI on maths rows; it differs for the adjoint split and control
(ii), and it fails a map that ignores δW, which r1 passes.

**Saved sources exist.** W₀'s final adversary sources are in `experiments/qwen_sft/results/large/nowarmup/sources/`:
web seeds 0, 1, 2 (`G_fineweb_seed0`, `W0_fineweb_seed{1,2}`, readings 1.7032, 1.4365, 1.7472) and maths seed 0
(`G_maths_seed0`, 4.2351). Each is about 3 MB and goes up with the code. So r3 costs forward passes only.

**Expected values, filed before code.**

| reading | do-nothing 10 / 100 | least squares 10 | adjoint 10 | control (i) | control (ii) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1 CI maths | ~10 / ~12 (fails) | 2-6 | 1.0-1.5 | 5-12 | 1.5-5 |
| r1 CI web | 1.0-2.0 | 1.0-2.0 | 1.0-1.3 | 1.0-2.0 | 1.5-5 |
| r2 kept | −0.2 to 0.3 (re-gating only) | 0.3-0.7 | 0.1-0.6 | ≤ 0.3 | < 0 |
| r3 web | 1.0-1.3 | 1.0-2.0 | 0.9-1.2 | 1.0-1.5 | > 1.5 |
| r3 maths | 1.1-1.5 | 1.5-4 | 0.9-1.3 | 1.1-1.5 | > 1.5 |

**The free check.** Per matrix: r_u / d_out and r_v / d_in (rank of the alive U and V at tolerance 1e-6); the null
(1 − r_u/d_out)(1 − r_v/d_in); δW's input-weighted outside-both share (`split.weighted_fractions`) at steps 10 and 100
against that null; and the share of the energy of δW's top singular directions (those carrying 90%) inside col U and
col V. Expected: MLP ranks near 1 and outside shares near 0; q and k nulls 0.4-0.55; δW's share below its null.

**Concentration.** For each split at each step, the number of alive subcomponents carrying 90% of the split's
input-weighted energy (`split.piece_energies`), pooled and by layer and matrix kind, against the same count for
`split.random_like(δW, Σ, 0)` split the same way. Expected: below the null if the update uses existing machinery.

## 5. Predictions (for `experiments/qwen_maps/results/predictions.json`)

Every rule: absolute thresholds on ratios as defined in §3, ≤ and ≥ inclusive as written, a NaN or missing value is a
miss, read once (no re-reads). Edge cases are listed per rule.

1. **control_W0 (stage C, stop rule).** r1 CI at W₀ within 1% of 0.14167 (web) and 0.05886 (maths); each replayed
   source within 1% of its saved KL; KL to start within 2% of 0.52040 (step 10) and 0.66369 (step 100, fp32 file).
   E40's two H100 runs of the whole 20-step search read 0.09% apart; a replay is one forward, so 1% is ample. Miss → stop.
   Edge: a source file absent → miss; the bf16 and fp32 step-100 readings both reported, the gap recorded.
2. **stoch_floor.** Spread (max/min − 1) of r1 stoch at W₀ over seeds 0-2 ≤ 0.10 on each row set. Miss → r1 stoch is
   reported, not scored, everywhere. Edge: min < 1e-6 → miss.
3. **teeth_i.** Control (i) r2 < 0.8. Lean high: most of its pieces sit where masks are zero. Edge: a matrix with an
   empty lower half (alive ≤ 1) takes no pieces; if more than half the matrices do, the control is reported degenerate
   and teeth rest on (ii).
4. **teeth_ii.** Control (ii): the largest of r1 CI maths, r1 CI web, r3 maths, r3 web > 1.5. Lean medium: nothing
   measured says how a random change of this size moves Γ.
5. **teeth outcome.** Both hold: full teeth. One holds: run on, the readings with teeth named in the analysis. Neither:
   no teeth, stop before stage M (section 6).
6. **do_nothing.** r1 CI maths > 1.5 at steps 10 and 100 (lean high: Δ off drops a 0.52-nat update); r3 web ≤ 1.5 at
   step 10 (lean high: E40's search read 1.18). r2 reported.
7. **pass rule for a map**, per state: r1 CI ≤ 1.5 on both row sets, r1 stoch ≤ 1.5 if scored, r2 ≥ 0.8, r3 ≤ 1.5 on
   both. Edge: r2's denominator K(D) − K₀ ≤ 0.05 → r2 unscored and the state's pass reads "incomplete".
8. **least_squares_10 passes.** Lean low: this update is large, E16's large fine-tune failed at 6.96, and q and k
   leave much of δW in Δ.
9. **adjoint_10.** r1 CI ≤ 1.5 and r3 ≤ 1.5 on both row sets (lean medium-high, E16 and E22), and r2 < 0.8 (lean
   medium): faithful and partial.
10. **step_100_worse.** Least squares: r3 maths at step 100 ≥ at step 10. Lean medium. A tie counts as holds.
11. **outside_share.** Pooled input-weighted outside-both share of δW at step 10 ÷ its null < 0.5. Lean medium (E22:
    0.17-0.40 on the 67M). Edge: null 0 in a matrix → that matrix leaves the pool, counted.
12. **concentration.** Least-squares split at step 10: pooled 90%-energy count ≤ 0.5 × its random null's. Lean low.
    Ties in energy: count by the sorted cumulative sum first reaching 0.9. Total energy 0 → unscored.

The code writer runs each rule on: an empty pool, a zero denominator, ties at every line, a NaN, and a state with a
missing reading, and files the result in `predictions.json` before the pod starts.

## 6. Decision tree (each branch approved with this plan)

- **control_W0 misses** → stop; fix; rerun the stage (class 2).
- **No teeth** (neither control fails) → the line stops; card to the user on the pass criterion; maps not scored
  (re-plan §4, Q0).
- **Least squares passes at 10 and 100** → a simple map works for this update; teacher training is not needed for
  the weak version; the line goes to Q2 with the splits as the map, and Q3 reads where δW lands.
- **Fails at 10** → teacher training (Q4) is the fallback, sent to the user as a card with its cost; the failure is the
  first Qwen result for the brittleness branch. If the adjoint split passes r1 and r3 but not r2, the card says so: a
  faithful map exists that keeps part of the behaviour.
- **Passes at 10, fails at 100** → the step sweep's first point; Q2's matched-KL update decides which side it falls.
- **The fresh adversary disagrees with r3** (search > 1.5 × W₀ where the replay passes) → the replay is blind for this
  state; the card asks whether the per-experiment search becomes the per-state test.
