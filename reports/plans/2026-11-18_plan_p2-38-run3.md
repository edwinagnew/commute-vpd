# P2.38 run 3 (E212): the crosscoder on the real-text pairs

Cell P2.38 run 3 of 3 (due 2026-11-22). Card `reports/plans/2026-11-18_plan_p2-38-run3-card.md` (rivals, floor, stop).
Rules, expected values and settings: `experiments/realistic_tasks/results/predictions_run3.json`. Code:
`experiments/realistic_tasks/run3.py` (jobs), `src/commute/crosscoder.py` (E203's trainer glue and reading, with d in both
forms, `score`, `acts`, `batches`; tests `tests/test_crosscoder.py`), `src/commute/realistic.py` (`crosscoder_call`;
tests `tests/test_realistic.py`), `experiments/realistic_tasks/score3.py` (CPU), `cluster/realistic_tasks_r3_job.sbatch`.

**What runs.** The crosscoder of P2.31 (E203: Minder et al. 2025's BatchTopK crosscoder with latent scaling, their
pinned trainer, the `full` settings block of `experiments/crosscoder/results/run2/predictions.json` unchanged) is trained
between the original 67M and each of the six fine-tunes run 2 saved at seed 0 and the pair's larger matched size: topic
label vs coin label (L1/L3, size 0.010), French vs Yoruba (T2/T3, 0.0175), lowercase vs reversed order (T1/T4, 0.0175).
One crosscoder a side, block 2's input, 200M token presentations, fp32. Each side is read on its task's run-2 read rows;
the reading is E203's d in both forms: d_authors = shared part minus fine-tune-specific part of the activation change
(the authors' rule), d_alt = the same with the artefacts (top-N latents that fail latent scaling) counted as
fine-tune-only. Gap per pair = reuse side minus new side.

**The stream.** One for every job, so jobs differ only in the update: half E203's Pile train rows (32,768 onward), half
the six tasks' run-2 training texts (L1 and L3 share their 512 pool windows; T1-T4 2,400 rows each), drawn per chunk
by a generator seeded by the chunk alone. The label windows are capped at 100 draws an epoch (E203's `repeat_cap`), so
per chunk of 1,024 Pile rows: 533 windows per label task (3.4% of the task half each) and 239 rows per LM task (23%
each); task share 0.500; 201.0M tokens for 97,656 steps. A `prep` job builds the texts once with run 2's own loaders
(`run2.task_data`) into `results/large/run3/texts.pt` (~25 MB).

**Controls, first.** (1) Planted: E203's direction (its plant seed) at the median base norm, added on the fine-tune
side at every 35th position of each task unit (34 = the label window, so a label window gets it at its read position;
about 1.5% of tokens, E203 1.25%), read on the label rows. Must: the lead latent fine-tune-specific and the specific part
at least 0.5. E203 missed on nu_eps (0.244 against 0.2): a miss is recorded and reported beside, not a stop. (2)
Gaussian: run 2's `random_direction` (draw 0) at T2 seed 0's per-matrix norms, read on every side's rows. Stop rule:
d_authors on T2's rows at or over 0.45 (E203's random read 0.547 against trained 0.39-0.46) exits 1, the six sides are not
run and the cell reads rival b with the card's stop line. Every job also checks first: a zero update gives W0's
activations exactly; the loaded update's size within [0.98, 1.10] of its target.

**Early checks** (each stops the job): batch FVE at step 1000 at least 0 (E203 0.659); at the first snapshot (12.5% of
tokens, ~7 min) FVE at least 0.5 (E203 0.909), dead share at most 0.3 (E203 0), projected job at most 150 min (E203 50);
at the end FVE at least 0.8 (E203 0.931). Checkpoints every 10,000 steps and at each snapshot; a rerun resumes (the
stream skips whole chunks; `tests/test_crosscoder.py` checks a resumed stream equals the uninterrupted one).

**Filed predictions.** P1 (rival a): the Gaussian reads below every trained side on that side's rows, and the gap is at
least 0.15 on at least two of three pairs. Lean low. P2 (rival b): the floor fails or no gap reaches 0.15. Lean
medium-high: E203's random read above its trained sides with an activation change 100 times smaller. Expected: trained
sides d_authors 0.35-0.50; Gaussian 0.2 or lower under rival a, 0.4 or higher under rival b. The gap's noise is 0.047
(E203's reseed), so 0.15 is about three of it. d_authors decides; d_alt gets its own call beside.

**Platform, cost, outputs.** Prep and the rehearsal on the cluster `debug` QoS, $0: prep ~15 min; the rehearsal (every
job at 1,536 latents and 4.1M tokens, the planted one stopped at step 700 and resumed, then `score3.py`) ~45 min. Full
size: one H100 pod a job (~$4.40/h): stage `ctl` = planted and Gaussian on two pods at once, then the six sides on six
pods at once once the Gaussian has passed. Per pod: set-up ~8 min, upload ~520 MB (Pile half 402 MB, val 34 MB, texts
25 MB, one update 57 MB) ~1 min, training and reads ~55 min (E203 50; the Gaussian ~5 min more for its six reads),
download ~150 MB ~15 s: ~65 min, ~$5. Total ~8 pod-hours, ~$40; wall ~2.5 h after prep. Not gated (under $60 and 6 h).
Each job writes `results/run3_<job>.json` (every number, the curve, snapshots) and `results/large/run3/<key>.pt` (the
crosscoder, weights bf16, ~150 MB, plus relative norms, groups and betas per read). `score3.py` writes
`results/run3_summary.json`: per side d both forms, per pair gaps, the Gaussian per rows, the floor, the planted control,
the call per form.

**Departures from Minder et al. 2025.** (1) The 67M, d 768, 24,576 latents (E203's). (2) Data: half Pile rows (their
FineWeb half) and half the six fine-tunes' own training texts in place of lmsys-chat; one stream for every side, so each
crosscoder sees text from all six tasks, not only its own fine-tune's. (3) The label texts are capped at 100 draws a
window an epoch and are the label sides' read rows too (the coin label exists only on them); E203 kept read rows out of
the stream. (4) N = 1,667 (E203's scaling). (5) The reading d, its two forms and its controls are ours (E203's); the
source has none. (6) Validation mix: Pile val rows plus every task's read rows, the same for every job (E203: Pile val plus
the side's own rows).

**Blind spots.** d reads which crosscoder latents carry the activation change at one layer, not whether the base model's
mechanism is reused; it does not see the size of the change (rival b's worry), so the energy is reported beside. One
crosscoder seed a side: a gap under ~0.1 is inside E203's reseed noise. The label sides read on text the crosscoder trained
on. The Gaussian is made from the bf16 update on disk (run 2 made its own from fp32), a norm difference of ~1e-3.

**Not run.** Seeds 1-2 of the fine-tunes and the smaller matched size; a second crosscoder seed; per-side streams (each
crosscoder on its own task's text only); E203's built pairs again; FineWeb or chat data; layers other than block 2; Qwen.
