Numbers from `experiments/qwen_pass_test/results/score.json`, `results/amend1/score.json`, `results/amend1/open.json`. Code read:
`experiments/qwen_pass_test/{run,rules,amend1}.py`, `src/commute/qwen_teacher.py` (`train`: loss `kl_exact_zero(teacher, student)` on the
unmasked student; no masks). "FT masks" = g from Γ on the fine-tuned model's forward; "W0 masks" = g from W0's forward (reading B). Δ off throughout.

## The one fact the four reads agree on
With no update anywhere, W0's subcomponents under FT masks reproduce the fine-tune to K 0.104 (`reading_A.K_A.do_nothing`, u1), exactly the faithful
line 1.5·K0 = 0.104. With the whole update unmasked under W0 masks, K is 1.244 (KD 1.374); with masks fully open and Δ off, 0.013. So on u1 the
update's effect on this ruler is ~90% "which subcomponents are on" and ~10% "what δW adds through the ones W0 keeps on". That is the plan's own
fallback finding ("blind by construction"), now with numbers, and it holds on both rulers for u3 (gain_C 0.02, r2_C 0.03).

## Q1. Live explanations of the low ceiling, and the read that separates them
- E1 Routing (lead): FT uses subcomponents W0's forward switches off downstream; frozen W0 masks block the re-routing. For: the fact above; u3 agrees on both rulers.
- E2 Input starvation: δW reads directions of x the masked forward removes (harmless to W0's output, K0 0.07, fatal to x·δWᵀ). Same symptom as E1, different fix (needs inputs, not outputs, of off components).
- E3 Ruler: KL(FT‖·) over every position reads FT's confidence, not its behaviour. For: FT is 1.8 nats from W0 while its NLL gain is 0.17; masked W0 is 0.37 nats closer to FT than W0 is yet 0.07 from W0; the B ceiling and A do-nothing have equal gain (0.26, 0.24) and 12× different K (1.24, 0.10). Against: u3, where gain and K agree. So E3 adds harshness on u1/u2 but is not the cause.
- Critique of the candidates. *Whole update under A*: both E1 and E3 predict it near FT, so it separates nothing; its value is the gain ceiling of the FT-mask axis (Q2, T1) and it is needed anyway. *Reverse KL*: a sharp FT penalises any broad q in both directions, still confounded. *Top-1 agreement with FT* (all positions, and answer positions): free from logits already in hand, reads behaviour not confidence; take it. *Maths accuracy*: generation, 128 rows is ±9% at 95%, too coarse for 10-30% effects; skip. *K(W0‖masked at t)*: tells whether t = 0.5 is a sane model, which no filed rule needs; log it only if the t sweep reruns.
- The single read (~15 min, L40 debug, u1 and u2, 128 rows): the whole update unmasked under FT masks (amend1's `forward_plus` with g from the FT forward), logging per state K, gain, top-1 agreement with FT, KL(state‖FT) and mean entropy of FT, W0 and the state. Entropy(FT) ≪ entropy(W0) confirms E3's harshness; B-ceiling top-1 ≈ masked-W0's confirms E1 over E3. Optional second read for E1 vs E2: the B ceiling with x taken from a clean FT forward (teacher-forced per site), ~40 lines, 10 min.

## Q2. Tests that can fail a bad placement and pass a good one
- **T1. Gain under FT masks held fixed for every state** (Δ off, same g for do-nothing, halves, random, LS, adjoint, ceiling). Floor: do-nothing 0.240 (measured, u1: the leak, a constant offset). Ceiling: whole update unmasked under the same masks (to measure; the t = 1 read says Δ off costs 0.013 K, so high is expected, not certain). Teeth: often-on vs rarely-on halves under these fixed masks (to measure; E68's reading-A halves used each state's own masks and read 0.226/0.241, which is a different test). Pass: within the bootstrap width w (~0.01) of the ceiling; fail: within w of max(floor, random). Blind to: Γ's response to the placed state, FT's confidence (report K beside it), Δ (off; share is a column), interpretability of the placement. Cost: $0, ~20 min per update. Next: ceiling ≈ floor → file the fallback below, stop testing placements; LS near the ceiling → D1 met by least squares, Δ share 0.36 to the user; LS between → teacher training (Q3) has a target.
- **T2. No masks, clean model, Δ untouched**: W0 + the in-span part of δW (remainder dropped) and W0 + the remainder alone, beside W0 + the often-on half's part and a random matched set. Floor W0 (gain 0), ceiling FT (1); nothing measured yet (LS clean is FT by construction; in-span and adjoint never read clean). Pass: in-span (0.64 of δW) carries most of the gain and the remainder little; fail: it carries its norm share or a random set matches it. Blind to Γ entirely: a weight-space test, not faithfulness. Cost $0, ~10 min per update. Next: pass → "the update lives in the components' span and acts by re-gating", which names why T1/B fails; fail → the update is in Δ's directions, GOALS' "new capabilities" branch, and no small-Δ map exists.
- **T3. Teacher training judged by the 67M triple** (gain under its own masks, adversary ratio, Δ share). Under its own masks do-nothing already reads K 0.104 = faithful and gain 0.24, so only gain and the adversary can fail anything, and E44 says the adversary cannot fail do-nothing under FT masks. This is a map scored by T1 plus the adversary, not a third test. Cost: gated paid run plus ~2.5 h cluster per state for three adversary seeds.
- Honest reading: D1 as worded, with VPD's KL loss as "faithful", cannot be met on this model: under W0 masks the whole update closes 0.10 of the gap (0.03 on the template), and under the state's own masks do-nothing sits on the faithful line. Fallback statement: "On Qwen3-1.7B a GSM8K fine-tune acts through which subcomponents Γ switches on, not through what they compute: FT's masks on W0's components reproduce it to K 0.10 with no weight change, and the whole update under W0's masks closes 0.10 of the gap. The decomposition's masked KL cannot judge a placement here. D1 is judged on behaviour kept under the fine-tuned model's masks, floor do-nothing 0.24, ceiling the whole update, with KL and the adversary beside it."

## Q3. Teacher training
- Not with the rehearsed recipe. Its loss has no masks, so it fits (U+dU)(V+dV)ᵀ + Δ₀ ≈ W0 + δW in function space: the same equation least squares solves, spread over all alive subcomponents. Under W0 masks it meets the 0.10 ceiling (gain ≤ ~0.26 at best); under its own masks it cannot fail; under fixed FT masks it reads whatever share lands on FT's on-components, for which LS in T1 is the $0 proxy. Training under W0 masks would at best emulate re-gating through on-components, a cheat the adversary need not catch.
- Worth paying for only after T1, and only if LS reads well below a high ceiling. Then: student = masked forward under FT masks held fixed (amend1's hook with g from one FT forward per batch; Γ, Δ₀ frozen), loss KL(FT‖student), judged under the same fixed masks (gain and K against T1's floor and ceiling), the adversary started from FT's masks, Δ share 0 by construction. The masked student is a code change to `train`, so the gated run needs its one review.
- Options: (a) T1 + T2 now, $0, ~1 h on an L40; teacher training decided from T1 (recommended). (b) Teacher training now as rehearsed: $60+ to learn what LS under FT masks says for $0. (c) File the fallback now and spend on D2/D3 readings: right only if T1's ceiling reads ≈ floor.
- What changes it: T1 ceiling ≈ 0.24 → (c); LS ≥ ceiling − w → no training, run the adversary on LS ($0); entropy(FT) ≈ entropy(W0) and top-1 agreement of the B ceiling near FT's → E3 is dead and the KL ruler stays as the primary column.
