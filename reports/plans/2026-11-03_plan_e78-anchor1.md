## Design check

- **Goal.** D3's first column and D2's "same table": an update whose novelty is fixed by design (behaviour the model
  already produces under a prompt), readable by the decomposition's split.
- **Nearest tried.** E61 `a1_1e-6` (`experiments/TRIED.md`, E61 Job R) settled that this recipe passes its rule. It
  cannot be read by the split: bf16, steps 10, 20, 50, 100. E78 adds no finding about training; it adds usable files.
- **Patch or step.** A step: a rerun for files the plan's next readings need. Class 2's "control" and class 4 together.
- **Decisions relied on.** The reorient decision (`decisions.md`, 2026-11-03), fp32 δW for the split. No departure.
- **What would change next.** A pass: `qwen-decomp-lead` reads anchor 1. A failed reproduction or save check: the files
  are not used until the difference is explained (`feedback: run down anomalies first`).
- **Control, first.** E61's own run is the control. The first steps are the same weights on the same batches, so steps 1
  and 2 stop the job if they miss E61's filed bands. It can fail on the wrong data, recipe, forward or card.
  Differences from E61's run: only saves and extra reads (below). Optimizer, schedule, data, coefficients: none.
- **Early guards.** Every band logs its reading when it passes. E61's bands, unchanged: ±2% loss, ±20% |δW| at step 2.
- **Saved for audit.** `results/train.json` every step, `results/read.json`, answers jsonl in `results/large/answers/`.

**Step 1 (decided).** Step 1 runs at rate 0, so its δW is exactly zero, and E78's step t has t − 1 moving steps. E69's
`lr1e-6w10` arms move at step 1 (rate 1e-7, |δW| 0.0035; E78's first move is step 2, about 0.0034). So: keep the grid
and add step 3, 8 files. Step 1 stays as an exact zero, a do-nothing the readers can use as a check, and a reader must
not divide by it. Steps 2 and 3 line up with E69's steps 1 and 2 by moving steps; 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 are kept at E69's
labels, so compare them by label knowing they are one moving step behind. Cost of step 3: 5.64 GB, about 20 s.

**Noise floor.** No 100-step fp32 run has been repeated, so none exists at this length; the run measures it
(`read.json .step_gaps`: largest step-matched gap to E61 on loss, |δW|, held). What exists: E61 arm 0 and its repeat
(`train_arm0*.json`, 4 steps, fp32), up to about 1e-6; E40 and its regeneration (100 steps, bf16 autocast, 1e-5, H100
against L40), 0.066% on |δW|, at most 0.12% on loss at the read steps, 0.63% on maths KL. I chose 1%: above all of
these, and E61's own step 50 misses step 100 on all four numbers (4.9%, 21%, 14%, 2.9%), so a wrong state cannot pass.

**The save-path check, measured on the CPU today.** Rounding alone, E69's fp32 R step 100 against itself cast to bf16:
1 − cos 1.39e-6, norm ratio 0.9999984. Wrong state, E61's step 50 against its step 100: 1 − cos 0.0148, ratio 1.051.
The stop at 1e-3 lies 15 times under the wrong state and allows a 4.5% residual between the runs. Reading between 5e-6
and 1e-3 means the save path is right and the runs differ by more than rounding: reported, and read with `step_gaps`.

Edge cases were run through the rule code before filing (`tests/test_qwen_anchor1.py`, `predictions.json .edge_cases`):
missing file, BF16 file, mixed dtypes, 195 tensors, non-zero step 1, zero step 100 (no cosine), non-finite norm, no answer
fits the window, rate 0, both thresholds exactly met, and E61's own record passes every band and the rule.

A reproduction miss or a rule miss is not a stop, since T has finished. It goes to the lead before the files are used.

## Run order and time

Measured: E61's T took 1464 s with 4 saves of 2.82 GB (about 12 s each, its log); a 5.64 GB save on the cluster took 16 s
(E69 job 50070, `[save]` line); a held read 8 s, a KL read 3 s. So T ≈ 1464 − 48 + 8 × 20 + 4 × 8 + 3 × 3 ≈ **27 min**. G:
header and norm of 8 files, then step 100 against E61 (45 + 8.5 GB from NFS, about 6 min, an estimate), model load and the reads
(about 2 min, E61's log) ≈ **10 min**. Disk: 8 × 5.64 = 45.1 GB, 5.1 TB free. Peak GPU as E61's, 33.7 GB; host about 14 GB.
Each stage fits `--time=00:45:00`, so no checkpoint is written and training and generation stay separate only because G
reads the files as a new process, which is what makes it a check on the files. `debug`'s limit reads 120 min here
(`scontrol show assoc_mgr flags=qos`: `MaxWallPJ=120`), not 45; the sbatch keeps 45.
