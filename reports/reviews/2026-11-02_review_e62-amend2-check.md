## Findings that change a conclusion

**F1. The frozen mask is not one blind spot among three. It freezes the update's only route into $L_{01}$, so the stop is premature.** The report lists it as account 3 of three (lines 78-84) and says the plan's branch fires anyway (line 152).

- $L_{01}$ uses W₀'s subcomponents, Δ out, and no target weight (24 of 24 matrices replaced). The update can reach it only through the gates. The saved cells agree: the route split's d share is 0.002 to 0.003 and its i share at most 0.002 in size on the copying map (`run.json`, recomputed).
- Γ here is `global_shared_transformer` (`artifacts/vpd/s-55ea3f9b/final_config.yaml:9`), a bidirectional transformer that reads the inputs of all 24 matrices over the whole row (`vpd.py:168-173`). Those inputs include block 2's `o_proj` input, which is head (2,4)'s output itself, and everything downstream of it. So the gates at any matrix and position can carry what the head wrote.
- In the target model, switching the head off cuts everything downstream of it. In row 3, the part of the state's effect that the head passes on to Γ is kept, by design. Row 3 therefore asks a narrower question than its name: given masks already written with the head on, does the head's output still matter to the forward? It says nothing about whether the update's masked gain depends on the head.
- The plan did name this under "Blind to" and put Γ under ablation in "not planned". The filing is honest, and the verdict is correctly `mask_reaches_b_another_way` as a label. But the plan's tree treats "barely falls" as "not head-dependent", and that does not follow from a read that holds the only route fixed.
- Line 152, "absence of a demonstration still leaves r unusable as evidence", is true only as "unshown". The first paragraph's "r's height on this map is no evidence of the old mechanism" and the notebook's top paragraph ("so r's height on this map is not evidence of that mechanism") read as a finding. Suggested: "this read could not test it".

**What to do.** Run the read before taking the stop. It is $0 and a short `debug` job, and `feedback-dont-ask-for-cheap-recommended-checks` says such a read need not wait on a choice, though line 190 sends it to the lead. The report's filed-style prediction, that a fall near the target share means account 3, needs controls it does not state: Γ was never trained on activations with a head's slot held constant, so a fall may be Γ reacting off its distribution. Read beside it the lookup (which should not fall), (3,5) with Γ recomputed (should not fall), and W₀'s mask recomputed the same way. Without them neither outcome separates.

**F2. A fourth account, missing from the report: the masked forward never holds the fine-tuned head, and saved cells speak to it.** The target share reads a head whose q, k and v have been fine-tuned. Row 3 reads W₀'s q, k and v under a mask. If the fine-tune's use of the head at this new marker lies in the weight change (the redirected query the plan already counts as reuse), W₀'s head under a mask may be unable to reproduce it, and then `mask_reaches_b_another_way` fires on reuse. So "within 0.092" was not the expected reading of elicitation: it needs an elicitation done wholly by gates.

The cells the report never uses bear on this (lines 76, 84, "No reading speaks to the state's mask directly"):

| state | $L_{10}-L_{00}$, update's weights under W₀'s mask | $L_{11}-L_{01}$, update's weights under the state's mask | $U_1-U_0$, update's weights, every mask open |
|---|---|---|---|
| copying map, seeds 0, 1, 2 | 0.039, 0.020, 0.030 | 0.015, 0.010, 0.031 | 11.13, 11.01, 11.23 |
| lookup, seeds 0, 1, 2 | 0.141, 0.129, 0.125 | 0.338, 0.373, 0.344 | 11.98, 12.06, 12.05 |

So with every mask open the split's components carry about 11 log-odds of the update, and under either real mask almost none of it passes. That does speak to the state's mask: it closes nearly everything the least-squares split places the update on. It is not specific to the head, and it depends on how that split spreads the update, so it does not separate accounts 1 and 2 at the head. It does change the conclusion's wording: "r gives no mechanistic reading here" stands for a stronger reason than the report gives, because r's forward is cut off from the update's weights by construction, and r is blind to reuse by a redirected query. The filed name is wrong beyond the overclaim the report admits (line 154), and the way to separate this is to read head off on $L_{11}$ and $U_1$, not in the report's "Not run" as such (it lists $U_0$, $U_1$, not $L_{11}$), together with its attention reads.

## Findings that change a claim

**C1. That is the label's order, at about 40 times, in r's own forward, and it arises between W₀'s mask (0.04) and the state's (about 1.5). As a share the same contrast is about 0.10 against 0.005. Line 39 dismisses the lookup's agreement as low power, fair for "within", but says nothing about this contrast. Line 161, "gave no support for that reading", is too flat. Against head (3,5) on the copying map, whose masked net shift is at most 0.005, (2,4) also stands out by at least 260 times; target-model (3,5) net shifts are −0.12, 0.05, −0.17, so the masked forward passes 0.20 to 0.24 of (2,4)'s net fall and at most 0.05 of (3,5)'s (lookup: 0.03 to 0.09). So the masked forward is not uniformly deaf. Only line 177's concern about a floor is partly answered.

**C2. "A second head adds" is shown for net shares only, so "runs through ... a second old head" does not follow.** Lines 106 and 168, notebook "What we learned". Both-heads gain is net of W₀ under both, a level the run discarded, as line 117 admits. If the state did not fall at all beyond (2,4) alone, W₀ under both would have to read −7.01, −6.83, −6.77, that is 2.2 to 2.4 above W₀ under (2,4) and 1.0 to 1.3 above intact W₀. Unusual, but not excluded, and row 4 has just shown how netting moves these labels. What holds either way: strict series is refuted, since (1,1) off does something with (2,4) already off. What does not hold: that the fine-tune's copying leans on (1,1). Part of it is general damage: both heads off cost the lookup, where (2,4) does nothing, 0.82 to 1.02 log-odds, against the copying map's added 2.2 to 2.4. The filing also says of this row "no prior strong enough to call it" (`predictions_amend2.json`, row5), so "the lean of 'no more' was wrong on every seed" and the scorecard's bold **wrong** overstate a lean filed conditional on series.

**C3. The state's own fall is not a share of what the fine-tune did, and "firmed up" leans on it.** Line 168 offers "the state's own fall is 0.56 to 0.65 of its gain" as evidence that "the copying fine-tune runs through W₀'s copying head". Its numerator holds W₀'s 1.16, which is there before any fine-tune, so it is not a part of the gain. The net share 0.47 to 0.56 is the fine-tune's reading, and it is the same number on which E62 filed `copy_not_elicitation` (labels old, old, between). With C2, what Amendment 2 adds to "favours reuse" is W₀'s fall and the pair-broken share. Suggested: "unchanged in size, with W₀'s fall now read", not "firmed up". Line 90's "Not on the log-odds scale" and the bracket are right.

**C4. "$L_{00}$ and $L_{01}$ take the same vector" does not cancel the mean.** Plan ("that offset cancels to first order"; "the head carries nothing of the state or the mask") and line 175. The same vector passes through `o_proj`'s subcomponents, each multiplied by a gate, and $L_{00}$ and $L_{01}$ have different gates. So the mean's contribution changes with the mask, which is what varies between those cells. Likewise a slot that real masks had nearly closed upstream (q, k, v) now holds a nonzero vector it never carried. So the 1.43 net fall may be partly injection rather than removal, in either direction. Line 76's two options ("lets the head through weakly", "fully while the head contributes little") need this third. Head (3,5) cannot test it, as its slot barely moves anything under these masks. Own-mask mean or zero ablation in row 3's cells settles it: the report lists both separately, without this reason. The mean's norm is small, 0.47 for (2,4) among 0.05 to 3.55 across heads, so I do not expect it to carry the gap to the target share.

**C5. Pair dependence survives the head, and the report does not say so.** Lines 123-133 read the broken gain alone. Taking intact minus broken, the state's pair-specific gain is 7.64, 8.39, 5.71 with the head on and 2.77, 3.48, 2.96 with it off, a share of 0.64, 0.59, 0.48. The level lean, intact over broken, at the state falls from 9.2, 10.0, 7.3 to 3.3, 4.0, 3.5, where W₀'s with the head off is 0.51. So something outside (2,4) now computes the pair. This adds to "the target-model answer" and is the natural place to read (1,1) and the pair together. Unfiled.
