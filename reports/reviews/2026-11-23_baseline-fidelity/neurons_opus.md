## Judgement on the CMAP relabel (`~/commute-data/agents/whip-lead/cell-cmap.txt`, P2.68)

The relabel fits the whole-width neuron share. It uses the same input in both models, patches a component's
activations from the fine-tune into the base model, and reads the gain, which is CMAP (Prakash et al. §6.1, p. 8:
"Unlike naive activation patching, which involves patching activations of the same model on different inputs, CMAP
requires patching activations of the same components of different models on the same input"). The direction matches
too: fine-tune into base. It fits the half form as "CMAP at each MLP's input", and the half-gain count as "CMAP on
nested neuron sets ranked by attribution patching". It does **not** fit the top-neuron share, which patches no values:
it keeps the base model's own firing and only borrows the fine-tune's choice of kept set. Nor does it fit the overlap,
which runs no intervention at all. `main.tex:915-917` scopes the relabel correctly ("this and the next item"). But
`appE_methods_detail.tex:92` puts the CMAP bracket over all four neuron baselines, and `sec5_results.tex:170-172`
counts the top-neuron share among the "neuron-level patching shares (to be relabelled as CMAP at neurons)" that
"reverse three to five of the nine built pairs". Both need the scope cut back.

Departures from Prakash that the relabel must disclose: all 12,288 MLP neurons at once, where Prakash patch head
groups of a circuit found by path patching; log-odds of the target as a share of the gain, where Prakash score task
accuracy; and no circuit discovery.

## Verdicts

**Neuron share and half form (Vig 2020).** Not entitled as written. The method is cross-model patching, which Prakash define as distinct from Vig's input-side activation patching, so "activation patching \citep{vig2020}" names a different procedure.
The pending relabel to "CMAP at MLP neurons \citep{prakash2024}" fits, provided it states the departures (all neurons, no circuit discovery, a log-odds share), and `sec4_setup:52`'s "state of the art in model-diffing [cite?]" is removed.

**Neurons for half the gain (Syed 2023).** The "attribution patching" label is right, but the citation is an edge method and the count is not "the fewest": it is a single attribution order on a powers-of-two grid.
It is entitled to the label after it cites node attribution patching (Nanda 2023) beside Syed and the sentence is reworded to "the smallest power of two k whose top-attributed neurons carry half"; once the neuron share is relabelled, it is CMAP on nested sets.

**Top-neuron share (goodfire2026neurons, cammarata2026clean).** It can claim only Goodfire's kept-set rule, and it applies that rule at a K 4 to 80 times sparser than Goodfire's. At that K the masked base model alone moves the answer by more than some fine-tunes' whole gain, which is undisclosed and may explain its near-zero values.
It is not CMAP, so the planned relabel must not cover it (`appE:92`, `sec5_results:170`). It needs a re-run at K = 240, or a disclosure of K and the masked base model's shift.

**Top-neuron overlap (goodfire2026neurons, cammarata2026clean).** The citation is acceptable as "our construction using Goodfire's per-token top-K rule at their 7.8%". Cammarata supports the motivation, not the rule.
But its own positive control failed (0.962 against 0.965 for random), and the paper does not say so. Its 3 / 1 / 3 scorecard calls and the Qwen overlap readings need that disclosed, or the row cut. It is not CMAP.
