# Plan: faithfulness of the saved SGD emoticon state (sgd_faith)

Design check: item 1 of the SGD-finding review; approved class 1 (GPU pass of the existing harness on a saved state) and class 2 (the control the review names); runs first and alone; no sign-off needed. Held fixed: emoticon task, Pile harness, step count of the saved states, card. Varied: optimiser of the saved state (SGD lr 0.01 vs Adam lr 1e-5). Caveat: sizes of change are not matched (item 2 does that).

**Request, restated in the Terms' words.** Item 1 of `reports/2026-10-21_review_sgd-finding-influence.md`. The plain-SGD
component-training state that `sgd_grid` saved for emoticon (rate 0.01, 30 steps) has never been checked for
faithfulness. Score it on the Pile harness, on E4's trigger-local masked checks and on the route split, with the Adam
component-training state of the same task and step count as the reference, in one job. This checks the SGD path end to
end before any SGD verdict (items 3a to 5) is read.

## Run

`experiments/sgd_faith/run.py --sgd-state experiments/sgd_grid/results/large/state_lr0.01.pt` through
`cluster/sgd_faith.sbatch` (cluster `debug`, 30-min limit). The SGD state exists only on cluster NFS; if it is missing,
`sgd_grid` regenerates it in about 3 minutes (job 28106's recipe). No Adam state of the reference recipe was saved
(E4's `adam_all.pt` holds only revert-one drops), so the job regenerates it with `component_training.train`
(Adam 1e-5, 30 steps, E4's batch order) and saves it to `results/large/adam/`. Every state goes through
`harness.measure`, the same function E14 to E19 used.

Order, each stage dumped to `results/sgd_faith.json` before the next:

1. **W0 first.** Pile harness under attacker seeds 0, 1 and 2. The run stops if W0's adversarial KL is not within 10% of
   0.69, its CI-masked KL not within 2% of 0.348, or the adversarial floor (largest over smallest seed, minus 1) is
   above 0.25. Past readings: 0.692 and 0.348 with a floor of 0.209 on the L40 (E11 job 25863), 0.691 and 0.348 on the
   4090 (E4). The brief's "about 0.21" is this floor, not the KL.
2. **Adam reference (the control).** It must reproduce E4's held-out P(o) 0.9961 and KL elsewhere 0.0091 to 0.01, and
   read as E4 and the ladder did: Pile ratios at most 1.5 (E4: 1.003, 1.067, 1.003), the trigger ratio under its own
   gates within 0.05 of 0.637 and under W0's gates below 0.5 (0.0064), and route shares d within 0.02 of 0.031 and r
   within 0.05 of 0.508. If any of this fails, the run stops before the SGD state.
3. **SGD state.** Loaded by path. Gate G2: its target P(o) at the trigger is within 0.01 of `sgd_grid`'s 0.9909 and its
   largest relative change within 1% of 0.002637. Otherwise its numbers are reported but not scored.

What differs from the references' end state: the Adam state is regenerated by the shared trainer, not E4's copy of it
(same loss, batches, clip and seed; G1 tests this). The card is an L40 where E4 used a 4090, and the trigger-local
numbers come from the route split's bf16 cells, not E4's fp32 forward. The ladder's route split reproduced E4's
amendment-1 numbers to 0.0001, so the second difference should not matter.

## Measurements and expected values

| Number | W0 | Adam (recorded) | SGD (filed prediction) | Blind to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pile stochastic, adversarial, CI-masked ratio | 1 | 1.003, 1.067, 1.003 (E4) | all ≤ 1.5, pass | the trigger |
| Trigger ratio, state's gates (CI-masked P(o) / target P(o)) | 0.39 (no behaviour) | 0.637 | ≥ 0.8, pass | off-window damage |
| Trigger ratio, W0's gates | 0.39 | 0.0064 | < 0.5, pass | off-window damage |
| Route share d (changed pieces at old gates) | undefined | 0.031 | > 0.1, pass | which pieces moved |
| Route share r (re-gating), reported | undefined | 0.508 | reported | as above |

Noise floors: the adversarial ratio has the same-job W0 floor f (about 0.21 on the L40). A ratio between 1.5/(1+f) and
1.5(1+f), about 1.24 to 1.81, is flagged as not decisive on this card. The stochastic floor is about 0.002. The
CI-masked ratio, trigger ratios and shares are deterministic with Γ frozen. bf16 reruns move them by about 0.01.

The SGD expectation is genuinely open. E4's overshooting SGD state (rate 0.3) read an adversarial ratio of 24.9.
This state changes each matrix 2.5 times less than Adam's (0.0026 against 0.0066) and does less damage elsewhere
(0.0065 against 0.0091), which points to a pass. P2 and P4 rest on one argument. SGD's step follows the gradient, so
the change lands on the pieces the trigger's activations read, which Γ also switches on there. Adam's per-coordinate
scaling spreads it onto pieces Γ leaves off (Adam's ungated share 0.445). If P4 passes, the research log's Result 3 row
("the moved pieces carry a tenth or less") holds only for diffuse Adam training, as the review suspected.

## Runtime and cost

On the L40, one Pile pass (two 128-row blocks) takes about 2.4 minutes (E11 job 25863: 70 s per block).
- Model load, the split bases (10 s) and the Pile rows: about 1.5 min.
- W0 under three seeds: about 7.3 min.
- Adam's 30 steps with a held-out evaluation every step: about 1 min.
- Two scored states: about 5 min of Pile passes, plus seconds for the route splits.

Total about 15 to 16 minutes, under the 25-minute target, at $0 on the cluster. The smoke run (8 Pile rows, 2
attacker steps, 2 Adam steps, checks reported but not enforced) takes a few minutes. If debug does not start
within 10 minutes, the run goes to a RunPod 4090 (about $0.2).

## Decision tree

- G0, G1 or C1 fails: nothing about SGD is read. Find what drifted (card, code, data) before items 2 to 5 run.
- P1 passes and is decisive: the SGD path is faithful on emoticon, and item 3a can read SGD verdicts on the ladder.
  P1 near the line: rerun on an H100 or with more attacker seeds before any comparison leans on it.
- P1 fails: SGD states can be unfaithful even at a rate that learns cleanly. Item 3a still runs, but this result
  goes in the research log next to E4's 24.9.
- P2, P3 and P4 are reported either way. P4 decides whether the Result 3 row is reworded to "diffuse Adam training".

## Not run

- Matched size of change and momentum (item 2, `experiments/sgd_matched`, another session).
- No do-nothing arm: the Adam state under W0's gates (0.0064 against 0.637) is the positive control showing the
  trigger ratio has power.
- One seed (batch order 0) for both states, and no revert-one or identity checks.
